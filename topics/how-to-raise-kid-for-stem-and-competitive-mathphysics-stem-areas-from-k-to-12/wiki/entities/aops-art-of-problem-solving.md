## Overview

AoPS is the dominant ecosystem for competitive math preparation in the United States. It encompasses textbooks, online courses, a free adaptive practice tool (Alcumus), an elementary curriculum (Beast Academy), community forums, and an olympiad training course (WOOT). AoPS Online School is accredited by the Western Association of Schools and Colleges (ACS WASC). Most AoPS subject classes are NCAA approved, and two courses (Calculus and Physics 1: Mechanics) are College Board-approved AP courses.

## Products

### Beast Academy (Grades 2-5)

According to Art of Problem Solving, Beast Academy curriculum costs approximately $118 per level (online subscription or physical books) and covers grades 2-5 (Levels 2A-5D) in a comic-style format.

Beast Academy is the recommended entry point for gifted elementary students. It develops number sense, logic, and mathematical reasoning through an engaging comic-book format. See [[k12-stem-roadmap|Grade K-5 section]] and [[enrichment-programs-guide]].

### Textbooks (Introduction, Intermediate, Olympiad)

According to the AoPS store, book prices include: Introduction to Algebra ($59), Introduction to Geometry ($63), Introduction to Counting & Probability ($59), Introduction to Number Theory ($59), Precalculus ($59), Intermediate Algebra ($63), and Intermediate Counting & Probability ($63).

According to the AoPS store, The Art of Problem Solving Vol 1 costs $59, Vol 2 costs $63, and Competition Math for Middle School costs $45. MATHCOUNTS competition handbooks are available free from the MATHCOUNTS website.

See [[book-resources-by-level]] for the full book progression.

### Online Courses

According to a review of training providers, AoPS online courses cost approximately $415-$895 per course depending on level and duration. WOOT (Worldwide Online Olympiad Training) costs $845-$895.

Courses are typically 7 months long, with live class sessions and homework. They cover the same material as the textbooks but with instructor feedback and peer interaction.

### Alcumus (Free)

According to AoPS, Alcumus is a free, adaptive online math practice tool. It covers topics from prealgebra through AMC 12 level and adjusts difficulty based on student performance.

### Community Forums

The AoPS forums are the largest online community for competition math students. They include problem discussions, competition result threads, and general advice.

## Which AoPS Product Should You Use?

| If your child is... | Start with... | Why |
|---------------------|---------------|-----|
| Grades 2-5, gifted, needs engaging enrichment | Beast Academy at current level | Builds problem-solving foundations in a comic format the child seeks out voluntarily |
| Finishing Beast Academy Level 5 or equivalent | AoPS Prealgebra (textbook or course) | Bridge to competition math; the textbook ($59) works for self-motivated students; the online course ($415+) adds instructor feedback |
| Starting AMC 8/MATHCOUNTS prep | Introduction to Algebra (textbook or course) | Core first competition math text; choose the online course if the student benefits from peer discussion |
| Preparing for AIME | Intermediate series (textbook or course) + WOOT consideration | WOOT ($845-$895) is designed for AIME-qualified students only; do not purchase before reaching AIME level |
| Wanting daily practice at any level | Alcumus (free) | Best used as daily supplement alongside a textbook or course, not as a standalone curriculum |

**Key decision:** Textbook vs. online course. The textbooks provide identical mathematical content at 1/7 the cost. The online courses add live instructor feedback, peer interaction, and structured pacing. For students who need external accountability or proof-writing correction, the course is worth the premium. For self-motivated students with a parent who can check work, the textbook is sufficient.

**Accreditation note:** Because AoPS Online School is accredited by ACS WASC and most courses are NCAA approved, AoPS courses can satisfy high school graduation requirements and appear on official transcripts. This matters for students using AoPS to replace school math courses.

Some parents on gifted education forums characterize AoPS as targeting gifted students pursuing competition math, while RSM is described as catering to "high average to moderately gifted kids" preparing for standardized testing and AP Calculus readiness.

## Common Mistakes

- **Starting AoPS Introduction to Algebra before completing Beast Academy Level 5 or equivalent.** The Introduction series assumes arithmetic fluency and comfort with multi-step reasoning that students who skip Beast Academy often lack, leading to frustration and abandonment of the AoPS ecosystem entirely.
- **Purchasing WOOT before reaching AIME qualification.** WOOT (Worldwide Online Olympiad Training) is designed for students who can already solve AIME problems and need olympiad-level proof training. Students below AIME level will not understand the material and may become discouraged.
- **Treating the AoPS textbook as equivalent to the online course for proof writing.** The textbook provides no feedback mechanism. For students who need proof-writing correction (especially at the Intermediate level and above), the online course section is necessary — the instructor reads and responds to student solutions, which self-study cannot replicate.

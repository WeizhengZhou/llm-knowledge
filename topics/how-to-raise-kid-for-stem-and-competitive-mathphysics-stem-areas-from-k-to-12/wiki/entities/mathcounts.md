## Overview

MATHCOUNTS is the premier middle school math competition in the United States. According to MATHCOUNTS, the Competition Series is open to students in grades 6-8 only, with participation limited to 3 years per student. No student who has completed grade 8 may compete.

According to MATHCOUNTS, the Competition Series has four levels (school, chapter, state, national) and four rounds (Sprint, Target, Team, Countdown).

## Competition Format

MATHCOUNTS Sprint Round: 30 problems in 40 minutes, individual, no calculator. Target Round: 8 problems in 4 pairs with 6 minutes per pair, calculator permitted. Team Round: 10 problems in 20 minutes for a team of up to 4 students.

According to MATHCOUNTS, the Countdown Round is a head-to-head oral competition with 45 seconds per problem, used at chapter, state, and national levels.

## Participation

According to MATHCOUNTS, a school may send up to 14 competitors to chapter: 4 for team plus up to 10 individuals. There are no additional fees for advancing to state or national level.

According to MATHCOUNTS, the top 4 individuals from each state (224 competitors total) receive an all-expenses-paid trip to the National Competition.

## Is MATHCOUNTS Right for My Child?

Students with AoPS Prealgebra-level skills and who enjoy problem-solving challenges are ready for MATHCOUNTS in grade 6. MATHCOUNTS is team-based and school-organized (unlike [[amc-competitions|AMC 8]], which is individual and nationally administered); it rewards both speed and collaboration. Students who thrive in team environments and enjoy fast-paced competition often prefer MATHCOUNTS. Students who prefer individual performance and untimed work often prefer AMC 8 or [[entities/usamts|USAMTS]].

MOEMS (see below) is a lower-pressure stepping stone: team-based, monthly contests with 30 minutes per 5 problems. Students unsure about competition math should try MOEMS Division E (grades 4-6) before committing to MATHCOUNTS.

## Also: MOEMS

MOEMS (Mathematical Olympiads for Elementary and Middle Schools) serves a similar age range and is a common stepping stone to MATHCOUNTS.

According to MOEMS, more than 120,000 students from every US state and 39 additional countries participate each year.

According to MOEMS, Division E covers grades 4-6 and Division M covers grades 6-8. Grade 6 students may participate in either division but not both.

MOEMS holds 5 monthly contests per year (November through March), each with 5 problems in 30 minutes. Scoring is 1 point per correct answer with no partial credit, for a maximum of 25 points per year.

According to MOEMS, teams may enroll up to 35 students per team. The program is open to public, private, parochial, and home schools.

According to MOEMS, the George Lenchner Medallion is awarded for perfect scores (25/25).

## Common Mistakes

- **Registering a student who has not yet completed AoPS Prealgebra or equivalent.** MATHCOUNTS Sprint Round problems require algebraic reasoning; students without pre-algebra foundations are often overwhelmed and may disengage from competition math entirely. Complete AoPS Prealgebra or equivalent before competing.
- **Waiting to start until 7th grade, losing one of three allowed years.** According to MATHCOUNTS, participation is limited to 3 years per student. Skipping 6th grade to "wait until more ready" wastes one of three attempts. Grade 6 is for building experience; grades 7-8 are for peak performance.
- **Focusing only on Sprint Round speed at the expense of Team Round strategy.** Many parents drill their child individually without preparing for the Team Round collaborative format. Team Round (10 problems in 20 minutes for up to 4 students) requires communication and strategic problem assignment — skills that solo practice does not develop.

# Recommended Books and Resources by Level

## Overview

This guide organizes recommended books and free resources for competitive math and physics, from elementary through olympiad level. Resources are ordered by difficulty within each stage.

## Math Books

### Stage 1: Pre-Competition Foundation (Grades 2-6)

**Start with:** Beast Academy at the child's current level (Level 2 for gifted K-2, Level 3 for grade 3). Complete all Beast Academy levels before moving to AoPS Prealgebra. Singapore Math is a complement, not a replacement — use it alongside Beast Academy for word problem fluency.

| Book | Price | Notes |
|------|-------|-------|
| [[aops-art-of-problem-solving\|Beast Academy]] Levels 2-5 | ~$118/level | Comic-style; engaging for gifted elementary |
| Singapore Math (Primary Mathematics) | ~$122/level | Strong bar model approach |
| AoPS Prealgebra | $59 | Bridge from elementary to competition prep |

### Stage 2: Introduction Level (Grades 5-9, AMC 8 / MATHCOUNTS)

**Start with:** Introduction to Algebra, then add Introduction to Counting & Probability. These two books cover the core skills for AMC 8 and early MATHCOUNTS. Introduction to Geometry and Introduction to Number Theory follow. Competition Math for Middle School (Batterson) is a MATHCOUNTS-focused supplement that bridges Beast Academy to the AoPS Introduction series. AoPS Vol 1 is supplementary for students who want additional broad problem-solving practice.

According to the AoPS store, book prices include: Introduction to Algebra ($59), Introduction to Geometry ($63), Introduction to Counting & Probability ($59), Introduction to Number Theory ($59), Precalculus ($59), Intermediate Algebra ($63), and Intermediate Counting & Probability ($63).

### Stage 3: Intermediate Level (Grades 8-11, AMC 10/12 / AIME)

**Start with:** Intermediate Algebra (AoPS), then Intermediate Counting & Probability. These are the core AoPS continuation. Add Precalculus when AMC 12 content is needed. Zeitz (*The Art and Craft of Problem Solving*) and Engel (*Problem Solving Strategies*) are supplementary once the AoPS Intermediate series is complete — they teach meta-strategies and olympiad techniques that go beyond the AoPS curriculum.

### Stage 4: Olympiad Level (USAMO / IMO Aspirants)

**Where to start:** For geometry, begin with EGMO (Evan Chen) before *Geometry Revisited*. For algebra, start with the AoPS *Inequalities* volume. For number theory, Evan Chen's OTIS materials (free) provide the most accessible modern entry point. For combinatorics, *A Path to Combinatorics* (Andreescu) is the standard starting text. The general olympiad books (Zeitz, Gelca) are best used after topic-specific preparation.

**Geometry:**
- *Euclidean Geometry in Mathematical Olympiads* (EGMO) by Evan Chen — modern standard text; start here
- *Geometry Revisited* (Coxeter and Greitzer) — classic

**Algebra:**
- *Inequalities* (AoPS/Andreescu)
- *Olympiad Inequalities* (Thomas Mildorf)
- OTIS Excerpts by Evan Chen (free online)

**Number Theory:**
- *Modern Olympiad Number Theory* (Evan Chen OTIS)
- *An Introduction to the Theory of Numbers* (Niven, Zuckerman, Montgomery)
- *104 Number Theory Problems* (Titu Andreescu)

**Combinatorics:**
- *A Path to Combinatorics for Undergraduates* (Andreescu)
- *Problems in Combinatorics, Number Theory, and Geometry* (Andreescu)

**General Olympiad:**
- *Putnam and Beyond* (Gelca/Andreescu)
- Past IMO Problems (annual collections)
- USAMO Historical Problems (MAA, free)

## Common Mistakes

- **Buying all the books at once.** Purchase one level at a time. Complete each book before advancing.
- **Skipping the AoPS Introduction series.** Even strong students benefit from the problem-solving approach in these books.
- **Using only books without doing past competition problems.** Real competition practice is essential — books teach concepts, past tests build speed and pattern recognition.
- **Ignoring free resources.** The combination of Alcumus + past tests + Expii covers most needs through AIME level at zero cost.

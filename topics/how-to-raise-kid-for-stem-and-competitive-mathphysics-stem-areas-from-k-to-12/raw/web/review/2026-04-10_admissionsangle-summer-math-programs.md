# Best Math Summer Programs for High Schoolers — Admissions Angle

## S-Tier Programs

**MOSP (Math Olympiad Summer Program)**
- Selectivity: Extremely selective — only top 60 US high school students annually
- Eligibility: Rising 9th-12th graders who qualify via AMC 10/12 → AIME → USAJMO/USAMO
- Cost: Free
- Location: Carnegie Mellon University
- Curriculum: Olympiad preparation for IMO competition
- Application: Qualification based solely on math competition scores; no essays/interviews

---

## A-Tier Programs ("The Big Four")

**PROMYS (Program in Mathematics for Young Scientists)**
- Cost: $6,100 (financial aid available)
- Dates: June 29–August 9, 2025
- International: Yes
- Focus: Number theory emphasis with intensive academics
- Deadline: March 3, 2025
- Acceptance rate: ~10-13%
- Enrollment: ~80 students per year (60 first-year, 20 returning)

**The Ross/USA Program**
- Cost: $7,000 (financial aid available)
- Dates: June 9–July 19 or June 16–July 26, 2025
- International: Yes
- Focus: Similar number theory focus to PROMYS
- Deadline: March 15, 2025

**SUMaC (Stanford University Math Camp) — Residential**
- Cost: $8,575 (financial aid available)
- Dates: June 22–July 18, 2025
- Programs: Abstract Algebra & Number Theory (I) or Algebraic Topology (II)
- International: Yes
- Deadline: February 3, 2025

**Canada/USA Mathcamp**
- Cost: $6,600 (financial aid available)
- Dates: June 29–August 3, 2025
- Curriculum: "Flexible" with "no set curriculum" and student choice in classes
- International: Yes
- Deadline: February 26, 2025
- Acceptance rate: 5-7%
- Enrollment: 65 new students + 55 returning alumni per summer

## B-Tier Programs

**HCSSiM (Hampshire College Summer Studies in Mathematics)**
- Cost: $5,882 (financial aid available)
- Dates: June 29–August 9, 2025
- Style: "Flexibility and wide scope" similar to Mathcamp
- Application: Rolling; no problem set required

**MathILy**
- Cost: $5,300 (financial aid available)
- Dates: June 22–July 26, 2025
- Curriculum: Discrete math, combinatorics, graph theory, geometry, topology
- Deadline: April 29, 2025

**SAMS (Summer Academy for Math and Science at Carnegie Mellon)**
- Cost: Free
- Dates: June 21–August 2, 2025
- Eligibility: Underrepresented groups, low SES, first-generation students
- Focus: Quantitative and Computational Skill seminars with college coaching
- Deadline: February 1, 2025 (preferred)

**SUMaC Online**
- Cost: $3,580 (financial aid available)
- Sessions: June 16–July 4 and July 7–July 25, 2025
- Note: Ranked lower due to online format despite strong curriculum

## C-Tier Programs

**Mathworks at Texas State**
- Cost: $6,600
- Dates: June 15–July 26, 2025
- Application: No problem set; "much more accessible"
- Benefit: Direct interaction with professors for mentorship

**MathILy-Er**
- Cost: $5,300 (financial aid available)
- Dates: June 22–July 26, 2025
- Target: Younger/earlier students building toward competitive programs
- Deadline: April 29, 2025

**AwesomeMath (Online)**
- Cost: $1,275–$1,575 per course
- Sessions: Three concurrent options (June–August 2025)
- Focus: "Competition focus" on AMC/AIME/USAMO preparation
- International: Yes

**MathPath**
- Cost: $6,300 (financial aid available)
- Dates: June 29–July 27, 2025
- Target: Middle school students (ages 11–14)
- Application: Rolling; recommended by April 1
- Acceptance rate: ~30%

## Key Distinctions

- A-Tier programs offer strongest college admissions recognition
- Supplemental recommendation letters from program instructors enhance value at any tier
- The article notes "across-the-board increase in tuition fees"
- Financial aid is widely available across most programs
- Programs NOT covered in this article: RSI, SPARC, ARML

## RSI (from other sources)

- Research Science Institute — acceptance rate under 10%
- Selects ~100 world's most accomplished high school juniors
- Six-week program
- Most impactful for college admissions among all research programs

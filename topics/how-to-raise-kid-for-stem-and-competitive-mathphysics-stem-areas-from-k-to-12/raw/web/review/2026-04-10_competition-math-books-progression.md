# Competition Math Book Progression by Level

## AoPS Level Definitions

- **Elementary:** Elementary school through possibly early middle school
- **Getting Started:** AMC 8/10/12 and MATHCOUNTS participants
- **Intermediate:** Students who can pass or nearly pass AMC 10/12
- **Olympiad:** Students who can qualify or nearly qualify for USA(J)MO

## Stage 1: Pre-Competition Foundation (Grades 2-6)

**Beast Academy (AoPS)** — Grades 2-5
- Comic book style, self-contained elementary curriculum
- Teaches deep conceptual understanding vs. procedure
- Online + print versions
- Cost: ~$15/month online, ~$32 per book set (print)
- Develops: number sense, logic, patterns, mathematical play

**Singapore Math (Primary Mathematics series)**
- Concrete-Pictorial-Abstract approach
- Strong number sense and bar model problem solving
- Better for systematic skill-building than competition-specific prep

**Art of Problem Solving: Prealgebra**
- Bridge from elementary to competition prep
- More rigorous than standard prealgebra textbooks

## Stage 2: Introduction Level (Grades 5-9, AMC 8 / MathCounts level)

**AoPS Introduction Series** (each ~$60-67 print):
1. Introduction to Algebra (Richard Rusczyk) — 656 pages
2. Introduction to Counting & Probability
3. Introduction to Geometry
4. Introduction to Number Theory

**Other intro resources:**
- Competition Math for Middle School (Batterson)
- MathCounts preparation materials
- AMC 8 previous tests (free from MAA)

## Stage 3: Intermediate Level (Grades 8-11, AMC 10/12 / AIME level)

**AoPS Intermediate Series:**
1. Intermediate Algebra
2. Intermediate Counting & Probability
3. Precalculus
4. Calculus (AoPS)

**Other key books:**
- The Art and Craft of Problem Solving (Paul Zeitz) — best all-around problem-solving book
- Problem Solving Strategies (Arthur Engel) — comprehensive olympiad techniques
- AMC 10/12 previous tests (free from MAA)
- AIME previous problems (free from MAA)

## Stage 4: Olympiad Level (USA(J)MO / IMO aspirants)

**Geometry:**
- Euclidean Geometry in Mathematical Olympiads (EGMO) by Evan Chen — modern standard text
- Geometry Revisited (Coxeter & Greitzer) — classic

**Algebra:**
- Inequalities (AoPS/Andreescu)
- Olympiad Inequalities (Thomas Mildorf)
- OTIS Excerpts by Evan Chen (free online)

**Number Theory:**
- Modern Olympiad Number Theory (Evan Chen OTIS)
- An Introduction to the Theory of Numbers (Niven, Zuckerman, Montgomery)
- 104 Number Theory Problems (Titu Andreescu)

**Combinatorics:**
- A Path to Combinatorics for Undergraduates (Andreescu)
- Problems in Combinatorics, Number Theory, and Geometry (Andreescu)

**General olympiad:**
- Putnam and Beyond (Gelca/Andreescu)
- Recommended Past IMO Problems (annual collections)
- USAMO Historical Problems (MAA, free)

## Physics Book Progression

### F=ma Level (Algebra-based mechanics)
- Problems and Solutions in Introductory Mechanics (David Morin) — most important F=ma prep book
- Halliday/Resnick/Krane Physics — comprehensive reference
- Past F=ma exams (free from AAPT)

### USAPhO Level (All introductory physics topics)
- Introduction to Mechanics (Kleppner & Kolenkow) — MIT standard
- Electricity and Magnetism (Purcell & Morin) — strong EM coverage
- Classical Mechanics (Taylor) — accessible mechanics
- Irodov Problems in General Physics — classic comprehensive problems

### IPhO Level (University-level topics)
- Classical Mechanics (Morin) — includes Lagrangian mechanics
- Introduction to Electrodynamics (Griffiths) — standard undergrad EM
- Kevin Zhou's Physics Olympiad Handouts (free PDF, knzhou.github.io) — comprehensive syllabus
- Theoretical Minimum series (Susskind) — conceptual depth

### Free Online Physics Resources
- Kevin Zhou handouts (knzhou.github.io/handouts/) — comprehensive IPhO syllabus with problems
- AAPT past F=ma and USAPhO exams (free)
- IPhO past problems (ipho.info)

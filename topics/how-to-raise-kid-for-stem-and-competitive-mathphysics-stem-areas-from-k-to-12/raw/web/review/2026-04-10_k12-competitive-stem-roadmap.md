# K-12 Competitive STEM Roadmap by Grade Band

Synthesized from multiple sources: AlphaStar Academy, Davidson Institute, Math Project, Think Academy, AoPS, UB Gifted Math Program.

---

## K-2 (Ages 5-8): Foundation Years

**Goal**: Develop number sense, mathematical curiosity, and love of problem-solving before introducing competition structure.

**Indicators of readiness for enrichment**:
- Testing most reliable and predictive between ages 6-9
- Students younger than 13 who score 500+ on SAT math section show readiness for algebra
- Watch for: unusual interest in number patterns, ability to understand mathematical relationships intuitively, can skip steps to arrive at correct answers

**What to do**:
- Focus on depth and curiosity over speed and drill
- Avoid hasty acceleration into abstract topics (algebra, number theory prematurely)
- Math Kangaroo (grade 1+) is an appropriate low-pressure entry
- Beast Academy Level 1-2 or Singapore Math for enrichment
- Play-based mathematical exploration
- Do NOT rush into formal competition prep at this stage

**What to avoid**:
- "Mathematics as a stunt to please parents or educators is not likely to inculcate in the doer a love for the subject" — Davidson Institute
- Kumon-style drill as sole enrichment

## Grades 3-5 (Ages 8-11): Early Enrichment

**Goal**: Build problem-solving foundations; introduce competition mathematics as fun exploration.

**Competitions appropriate at this level**:
- Math Kangaroo (grades 3-5)
- MOEMS (grades 4-8; five monthly 30-min contests Nov-Mar)
- Math League (online, monthly)
- National Trimathlon (grades 3-6)
- MathCON (grades 4-12)

**Curriculum options**:
- Beast Academy Level 3-5 (AoPS heritage; strongly competition-oriented)
- Singapore Math (grade-appropriate, builds systematic thinking)
- AoPS Introduction to Problem Solving (for truly advanced students)
- RSM (Russian School of Math) — structured after-school program

**Key principle**: Students who start by 3rd or 4th grade can develop the depth of thinking needed to pursue Distinguished Honor Roll on AMC 8 and, for some, a perfect score.

---

## Grades 6-8 (Ages 11-14): Core Middle School Competition Years

**Goal**: Establish competition math foundation; primary competition window for MATHCOUNTS; begin AMC 8 and AMC 10 prep.

**Primary competitions**:
- MATHCOUNTS (grades 6-8): School → Chapter → State → National. "Largest in-person middle school math tournament in the USA."
- AMC 8 (grade 8 and below): "Earliest entry point to the Olympiad pathway." 25 questions, 40 minutes.
- MathPath summer program (ages 11-14): residential, ~30% acceptance rate

**Competition pathway context**:
- AMC 8 → (some skip directly to) AMC 10 → AIME → USAJMO/USAMO
- Gifted students often take AMC 10/12 in middle school (eligible if under 17.5/19.5 years old)

**Preparation resources**:
- AoPS online courses (Introduction to Algebra, Number Theory, Counting & Probability, Geometry)
- AlphaStar Academy AMC 8 prep
- RSM competition track

**Davidson Institute benchmark**: Students younger than 13 who score 500+ on SAT-M are ready for algebra-level competition prep.

---

## Grades 9-12 (Ages 14-18): High School Competition and College-Prep

**Goal**: Progress through AMC/AIME/USAMO pipeline; explore physics competition pathway; target selective summer programs for enrichment and college preparation.

**Math competition pipeline**:
- AMC 10 (grades 10 and below) → AIME → USAJMO
- AMC 12 (grades 12 and below) → AIME → USAMO → MOP → IMO
- New 2025-2026 index: (AIME × 20) + AMC score
- ~300,000 AMC participants; ~10,000 reach AIME; ~500 reach USAMO

**Physics competition pipeline**:
- F=ma (January) → USAPhO semifinal → Training camp → IPhO team (5 students)
- Physics Bowl (March-April, AAPT): accessible, covers full HS physics

**Selective summer programs** (application window: December-March):
- MOSP/MOP: Free, invitation-only (top 60 USAMO/USAJMO performers)
- Ross Program: $7,000, number theory focus
- PROMYS: $6,100, ~10% acceptance
- Canada/USA Mathcamp: $6,600, 5-7% acceptance
- RSI: ~10% acceptance, research focus, strongest college impact
- SUMaC (Stanford): $8,575 residential

**College admissions connection**:
- USAMO qualification: MIT acceptance rate >50% vs. overall <7% (from CollegeBase analysis)
- AIME qualification: valued for engineering/science programs
- USAMO winners/IMO team members: near-certain top-university admission
- RSI: among the most impactful programs for college applications

**Key principle**: From 9th to 11th grade, each step should build on the last, showcasing depth, progression, and impact. Elite universities seek coherent academic growth paths.

# RSM Math Competitions Calendar for 2025-2026

Source: Russian School of Mathematics (RSM) blog

## Competition Listings

**IMC (International Math Contest)**
- Date: February 3, 2026 (Registration opens October 1, 2025)
- Grades: 1-8
- Description: "a celebration of the beauty of mathematics and the joy of learning it" featuring 75-minute competitions with international problem sets and prizes

**MOEMS (Math Olympiads for Elementary and Middle Schools)**
- Dates: Five monthly contests from November 2025–March 2026
- Grades: 4-8
- Description: Popular annual mathematics competition with one test administered monthly; administered at individual schools

**AMC 8**
- Date: January 22-28, 2026
- Grade: 8
- Description: Prestigious national competition with 25 problems administered by the Mathematical Association of America (MAA)

**AMC 10/12 (Contest A and B)**
- Dates: November 5, 2025 (A versions); November 13, 2025 (B versions)
- Grades: 10-12
- Description: "25-question, 75-minute, multiple-choice examinations" promoting problem-solving skills; AMC 10 covers through 10th grade curriculum; AMC 12 covers full high school curriculum

**AIME (American Invitational Mathematics Examination)**
- Dates: February 5, 2026 (AIME I); February 11, 2026 (AIME II)
- Grades: 10-12
- Description: "15-question, 3-hour examination" with integer answers 0-999; invitation-only from top AMC performers

**Math Kangaroo**
- Date: March 19, 2026 (Registration opens September 2025)
- Grades: 1-12
- Description: Offers 24 problems (75 minutes) for grades 1-5 and 30 questions for grades 5+

**The Purple Comet! Math Meet**
- Date: April 14-23, 2026
- Grades: 6-12
- Description: "free, annual, international, online, team mathematics competition" featuring variable difficulty levels; ten-day competition window

## Grade Band Entry Points (implied by RSM placement)

- Elementary (Grades 1-5): Math Kangaroo, MOEMS
- Middle School (Grades 4-8): MOEMS, AMC 8, MATHCOUNTS
- High School (Grades 9-12): AMC 10/12, AIME (by qualification)

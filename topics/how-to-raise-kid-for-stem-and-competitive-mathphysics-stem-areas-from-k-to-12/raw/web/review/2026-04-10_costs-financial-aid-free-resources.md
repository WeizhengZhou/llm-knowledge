# Competitive STEM Preparation: Costs and Financial Aid

## Cost Tiers by Engagement Level

### Casual / Entry-Level (AMC 8, basic competition exposure)
Annual estimated cost: **$0-$500**

- AMC 8 registration: ~$10-30 (administered through school)
- Beast Academy online: ~$15/month ($180/year)
- Books (AoPS Introduction series): ~$60-70 per book
- Math circle or school math team: often free
- AoPS free resources (Alcumus, forums, Wiki): free

### Serious / Middle School (MathCounts, AMC 8/10 prep)
Annual estimated cost: **$500-$3,000**

- RSM (Russian School of Math): ~$1,000/semester, $2,000/year
- AoPS Academy (in-person): ~$1,000-1,800/semester
- AoPS online courses: ~$500-900/course (7-month courses)
- Competition registrations (AMC 8, MathCounts): $50-100/year
- Books: ~$200-400

### Advanced / High School (AIME level, serious competition prep)
Annual estimated cost: **$2,000-$8,000**

- AlphaStar Academy year-round: ~$2,000-4,000/year
- AoPS WOOT (olympiad): ~$600/year
- Private tutoring (if used): $100-300/hour × sessions
- Competition travel (state/national MathCounts, AMC): $500-2,000
- AMC 10/12 registration: ~$30
- Summer program (Mathcamp, Ross, PROMYS): $6,000-7,500 before financial aid

### Elite Track (USAMO level, summer programs, travel)
Annual estimated cost: **$8,000-$20,000+**

- All above plus
- RSI: FREE (if selected)
- International competition travel (if selected for IMO team): covered by MAA
- Private coaching at high level: $150-300/hour, 2-3 hours/week = $15,000+/year
- Multiple summer programs

## Scholarship and Financial Aid Sources

- **Individual programs:** All major summer programs offer need-based aid; most will make programs free for low-income families
- **Davidson Institute:** Fellowships and scholarship resources for profoundly gifted students
- **State universities:** Multiple states offer merit scholarships to USAMO qualifiers
- **Math Prize for Girls:** Cash prizes for top-performing female students
- **MATHCOUNTS Foundation:** Scholarship for national-level competitors
- **Intel/Regeneron STS:** Significant prize money ($25,000 for finalists, $250,000 for first place)

## Cost-Effectiveness Analysis

**Highest ROI for cost:**
1. AoPS books + free problem archives + AoPS forum community ($200-400 total)
2. Free resources (Alcumus, past exams, Evan Chen's OTIS)
3. One well-chosen summer program with financial aid (transformative experience, $0-3,000 after aid)

**Lower ROI:**
- Intensive private tutoring for students who are self-directed (expensive, often matches what free self-study provides)
- Multiple overlapping programs simultaneously
- Programs without strong peer community component

**Key insight:** The top performers at USAMO and IMO level rarely had expensive private coaching. Most succeeded through self-study with the right books and the AoPS community. The biggest investment that pays off is summer programs (Ross, PROMYS, Mathcamp) which provide the peer community and intensive exposure that's hard to replicate otherwise.

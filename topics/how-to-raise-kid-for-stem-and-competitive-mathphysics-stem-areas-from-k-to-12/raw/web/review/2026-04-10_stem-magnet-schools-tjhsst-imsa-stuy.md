# Top STEM Magnet High Schools in the US

## Thomas Jefferson High School for Science and Technology (TJHSST)

**Location:** Alexandria, Virginia (Fairfax County Public Schools)

**Founded:** 1985

**Mission:** Intensive, specialized STEM education for academically gifted students throughout Northern Virginia.

**Admission Requirements:**
- Students from Fairfax, Arlington, Loudoun, Prince William counties, and Cities of Fairfax and Falls Church
- Must be enrolled in Algebra 1 or higher in 8th grade
- Minimum GPA: 3.5
- Holistic process: GPA + math/science problem-solving essay + student portrait sheet + socioeconomic context

**What it offers:**
- Specialized research labs
- Unique electives in cutting-edge fields
- Senior research program (real-world research projects)
- Strong alumni network (includes tech industry leaders, researchers)

**College outcomes:** Excellent — high rate of admission to MIT, Caltech, Ivies, top engineering schools

**Scale:** ~1,800 students total (grades 9-12)

## Illinois Mathematics and Science Academy (IMSA)

**Location:** Aurora, Illinois (residential)

**Type:** State-funded residential school (Illinois students only, grades 10-12)

**Admission Requirements:**
- Must be Illinois resident
- Must have completed at least Algebra 1 and high school science equivalent
- Application includes: transcripts, teacher/counselor evaluations, essays, SAT/ACT scores
- Holistic process: academic performance + extracurriculars + leadership + test scores

**What it offers:**
- All students live on campus (required, even for local students)
- Highly accelerated, interdisciplinary curriculum
- Emphasis on inquiry-based learning and independent research
- STEM-focused but strong humanities component

**Selectivity:** Approximately 300 students admitted per class; competitive

**College matriculation:** Strong placement at top universities; many go to Ivies, MIT, Caltech

## Stuyvesant High School

**Location:** New York City (Manhattan)

**Type:** NYC Specialized High School (public, free)

**Admission:** Solely based on score on the Specialized High Schools Admissions Test (SHSAT). No essays, no teacher recommendations — pure test score.

**SHSAT Scale:**
- About 27,000 students take the test annually
- ~950 admitted to Stuyvesant (~3.5% of applicants)

**What it offers:**
- 30+ AP courses
- Unique electives
- Strong alumni network (many Nobel laureates, scientists, business leaders)
- Competitive academic culture

**Note:** Does NOT require relocation (NYC students commute)

## Other Notable STEM Magnet Schools

**North Carolina School of Science and Mathematics (NCSSM)**
- Residential (Durham, NC); also has Charlotte campus + online options
- Free for NC residents (state-funded)
- Grades 11-12 (junior/senior years only)
- Strong research program

**Bergen County Academies (BCA)**
- Hackensack, New Jersey (public magnet)
- Multiple specialized academies including science and engineering
- Admission based on exam; free public school

**Louisiana School for Math, Science, and the Arts (LSMSA)**
- Residential, grades 10-12
- Free for Louisiana residents

**Texas Academy of Mathematics and Science (TAMS)**
- Denton, TX (University of North Texas campus)
- Grades 11-12 only
- Students take college courses, earn both HS diploma and ~60 college credits
- Free for Texas residents

## Is It Worth Relocating For?

**Arguments for:**
- Peer cohort of similarly motivated students is powerful for development
- Access to specialized labs, research programs, and expert teachers
- College counseling infrastructure for top-tier admissions
- Network effects (alumni)

**Arguments against:**
- Disruption of existing social network
- Quality of experience depends heavily on fit
- Many top universities (MIT, Caltech) explicitly value students who thrived at ordinary schools
- Private and homeschool options can achieve similar academic outcomes
- Competition-focused kids can reach USAMO/IMO without attending these schools

**Consensus view:** These schools add real value, but are not required for top STEM outcomes. For families already near TJ/Stuyvesant/IMSA, they are strong choices. Relocating specifically for these schools is a major life decision that should weigh family factors heavily.

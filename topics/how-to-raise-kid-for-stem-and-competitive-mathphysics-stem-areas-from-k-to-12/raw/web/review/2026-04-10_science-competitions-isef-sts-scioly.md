# Science Competitions: ISEF, Regeneron STS, Science Olympiad

## Regeneron ISEF (International Science and Engineering Fair)

**What it is:**
The largest pre-college science fair in the world. Widely regarded as the most prestigious pre-university scientific and engineering research competition in the world.

**How to Enter:**
Students in grades 9-12 must first compete in a Regeneron ISEF affiliated science fair (regional or state) and win the right to attend Regeneron ISEF. The first step is finding an affiliated fair in your area through the Society for Science website.

**Team vs. Individual:**
Both individual and team projects compete. Team projects must have no more than 3 members.

**Scale:**
Thousands of students from 60+ countries compete annually.

**Awards:**
Grand awards, special awards from organizations, and significant monetary prizes including the Gordon E. Moore Award ($75,000).

## Regeneron Science Talent Search (STS)

**What it is:**
The nation's oldest and most prestigious science research competition for high school students. Started in 1942 as the Westinghouse Science Talent Search.

**Eligibility:**
High school seniors only (12th grade, enrolled in U.S. high school at time of application).

**Application Process (different from ISEF):**
Application-based — not through an in-person fair competition. Students submit:
- A formal research report explaining experiments and conclusions
- Application packet

**Scale:**
About 1,900 students enter annually.
- 300 recognized as "Scholars"
- 40 invited as "Finalists" to Washington, D.C. for final round

**Key difference from ISEF:**
ISEF requires winning a local affiliated fair first; STS is direct application with a research paper.

## Science Olympiad

**What it is:**
A nationwide team competition held in the spring where students compete in 23 events spanning earth science, biology, chemistry, physics, and engineering.

**Format:**
Team-based (15 students per team). Events include building events and knowledge/test events.

**Structure:**
Invitational → Regional → State → National competitions.

**Entry:**
School teams register. No individual application — must represent a school team.

**Grade bands:**
Division B: grades 6-9
Division C: grades 9-12

## Key Distinctions Between the Three

| Feature | ISEF | Regeneron STS | Science Olympiad |
|---------|------|---------------|------------------|
| Individual vs. Team | Both | Individual | Team only |
| Entry | Win local/regional fair | Direct application | School team |
| Eligible grades | 9-12 | Seniors only | 6-12 |
| Research required | Yes | Yes | No |
| Format | Science fair judging | Research paper + application | 23 diverse events |
| Prestige | Very high (global) | Very high (oldest) | High (national) |

# Competition Math Training Providers: Comparison

## Art of Problem Solving (AoPS)

**What it is:** The dominant platform for competition math in the US. Founded by Richard Rusczyk (USAMO winner).

**Products:**
1. **AoPS Online School** — 7-month courses; AMC 10/12, AIME, WOOT (olympiad), Precalculus, Calculus
2. **AoPS Books** — Introduction through Olympiad series; best competition math texts available
3. **Beast Academy** — Elementary grades 2-5 comic/puzzle format
4. **AoPS Academy** — In-person centers (CA, MD, VA, WA, TX, WI + expanding)
5. **Alcumus** — Free adaptive problem system

**Cost:**
- Online courses: ~$500-900/course (7 months)
- WOOT (olympiad): ~$600/year
- Books: ~$60-70 each
- Academy in-person: ~$1,000-1,800/semester

**Strengths:**
- Industry standard materials; nearly all top competitors use AoPS resources
- Strong online community (forums — used by USAMO/IMO competitors)
- Comprehensive coverage of AMC 8 through olympiad level
- Self-paced online books available

**Limitations:**
- In-person centers only in select cities
- Online courses require self-discipline (no in-person accountability)

**Best for:** Anyone from AMC 8 level through olympiad; the default choice

---

## AlphaStar Academy

**What it is:** Competition-focused training programs, in-person (Santa Clara/Fremont, CA) and online.

**Focus:** National and international competitions — MathCounts, AMC, USAMO. Strong competition results.

**Products:**
- Weekly fall/spring courses
- Summer and winter intensive camps
- Proprietary curriculum aligned with AMC/AIME/USAMO topics

**Cost:**
- Year-round program: ~$2,000-4,000/year
- Summer camp: additional

**Strengths:**
- Highly competition-focused
- Strong track record of producing AMC/AIME/USAMO qualifiers
- Intensive format produces fast progress

**Limitations:**
- Primarily serves Bay Area (in-person) and online
- Competition-focused orientation may not suit all students

**Best for:** Serious competition track students, especially in Bay Area

---

## Russian School of Mathematics (RSM)

**What it is:** Multi-center after-school program (primarily Northeast, CA, expanding nationally).

**Philosophy:** Soviet-style rigorous mathematics; systematic foundational skills; problem-solving focus at all levels.

**Products:**
- Grade K-12 programs with differentiated levels (accelerated/advanced/honors competition tracks)
- In-person classes 2x/week

**Cost:** ~$1,000-1,200/semester (~$2,000-2,400/year)

**Strengths:**
- Strong foundational curriculum
- Good for students who need systematic instruction
- Community (in-person, consistent cohort)
- Teachers with strong math backgrounds

**Limitations:**
- Not as competition-specifically oriented as AoPS or AlphaStar
- Geographic limitations (not available everywhere)
- More about foundational rigor than creative problem-solving at lower levels

**Best for:** Students who need structured in-person instruction; K-5 gifted students before AoPS level

---

## AwesomeMath

**What it is:** Summer programs and year-round online courses by Titu Andreescu (USAMO trainer).

**Products:**
- Summer camps (3 weeks): Dallas TX, San Jose CA, Hofstra NY
- Online courses

**Cost:** ~$2,500-3,500 for summer program

**Strengths:**
- Strong competition focus
- Andrescuu's materials are widely respected
- Residential summer program provides immersive experience

**Limitations:**
- Smaller scale than AoPS
- Less community infrastructure year-round

---

## Areteem Institute

**What it is:** Live and self-paced online courses, AMC through olympiad; also runs ZIML competition.

**Products:**
- Live online courses
- ZIML monthly problems
- Competition prep at all levels

**Cost:** Varies by course

**Strengths:**
- Accessible online format
- Good for AMC 10/12 level prep
- Competition-focused curriculum

## Recommendation by Situation

**"My child is new to competition math (grades 4-7)"**
→ Start with Beast Academy + AoPS Introduction series + AMC 8 past tests. If in-person wanted: RSM or AoPS Academy.

**"We're serious about AMC 12 and AIME (grades 8-10)"**
→ AoPS online school (Intermediate series courses) + AlphaStar if in Bay Area.

**"I want to reach USAMO (grades 10-12)"**
→ AoPS WOOT + Evan Chen's OTIS + past USAMO/IMO problems. Self-study is the primary path at this level.

**"I'm looking for in-person community"**
→ RSM (if available) or AoPS Academy center.

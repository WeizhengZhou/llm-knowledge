## About Po-Shen Loh

Po-Shen Loh is a professor at Carnegie Mellon University and the US IMO Team Coach who led the US team back to first place at the IMO in 2015 (for the first time in 21 years), and repeated in 2016 and 2018. He founded Expii as a mission to democratize mathematics education globally.

His philosophy: use technology to give everyone access to the equivalent of a personal tutor, regardless of ZIP code or wealth.

## Expii.com — Free Personalized Learning Platform

**URL:** https://www.expii.com/

**What it is:** A free personalized learning platform that reinvents the middle school math curriculum with a focus on creative thinking.

**Key features:**
- Thousands of math and science lessons through interactive explanations and video
- Adaptive: adjusts to student level
- Aligned with MATHCOUNTS and AMC 8
- Topics span all areas of beyond-curricular math: Algebra, Geometry, Combinatorics, Number Theory, Probability
- Each module contains 150+ specially crafted problems
- Includes homeworks and practice tests

**Goal:** Turn smartphones into virtual tutors. Make the personalized-tutoring experience available to everyone.

**Target audience:** Middle school level, but useful for grades 5-9 working toward AMC 8 / MATHCOUNTS level competitions.

**Cost:** Free

## Daily Challenge with Po-Shen Loh

## LIVE — Live Math Classes by Student Teachers

**URL:** https://live.poshenloh.com/

**What it is:** A math education site where "student teachers" (high school and college students who are strong competition math participants) teach small courses surrounding middle and high school competition math via livestreaming and video chat.

**Structure:**
- Classes run 60–90 minutes
- Structured around "big questions" that students discuss collaboratively
- Streamers improvisationally lead students to discover solutions before comprehensive coverage
- Covers MATHCOUNTS, AMC 8, AMC 10/12 level content

**Philosophy:** Combines the Socratic method with competitive math content. Creates a two-way learning experience rather than passive lecture.

**Cost:** Not specified; platform appears low-cost or free for students

## Po-Shen Loh's Philosophy on Competition Math (from Quanta Magazine profile)

- His goal as IMO coach was not to maximize current wins but to "maximize 20-year future outcomes" for students
- Believes competition math should develop curiosity and deep thinking, not just problem-solving speed
- On the US IMO team's success: built culture of intellectual community and genuine mathematical curiosity
- On Expii: "We want to teach people how to think about math, not just solve problems"
- Against pure drill/rote learning approaches: wants students to understand the 'why'

## Limitations and Criticisms

- Expii platform has received less attention than AoPS for serious competition preparation at AIME/USAMO level
- More valuable at middle school level (MATHCOUNTS, AMC 8) than for advanced competition prep
- Some parents note the platform has had inconsistent update cadence
- LIVE classes depend on student-teacher quality which can vary

## Relevance to K-12 STEM Path

For families starting competition math in grades 5-7, Expii offers a free, accessible entry point with strong pedagogical design. For serious AMC/AIME students, Expii is a supplement rather than a primary resource. AoPS books and courses remain the primary recommendation for that level.

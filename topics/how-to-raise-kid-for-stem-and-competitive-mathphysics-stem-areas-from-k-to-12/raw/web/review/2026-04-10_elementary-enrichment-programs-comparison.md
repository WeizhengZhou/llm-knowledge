# Elementary Math Enrichment Programs — Comparison Summary

## Beast Academy (Art of Problem Solving)

Source: https://beastacademy.com/

- **Grade Levels**: Levels 1-5, covering approximately ages 6-13 (elementary through early middle school)
- **Creator**: Art of Problem Solving (AoPS) — recognized leader in competition math education
- **Curriculum Approach**:
  - Combines digital and physical components
  - Online platform with interactive lessons and digital guidebooks
  - Physical books: separate Guide and Practice books for each level (subdivided A-D)
  - Narrative-driven content with comic/graphic novel style teaching
  - Emphasizes problem-solving and mathematical thinking over procedures
- **Cost**: ~$118 per complete grade level (bundled); annual online subscriptions with bundled books
- **Competition Connection**: Created by AoPS, strongly aligned with competition mathematics principles
- **Target Audience**: Mathematically advanced learners seeking enrichment beyond standard curricula
- **Difficulty**: Usually a grade beyond respective age/grade level; company recommends starting a grade below or taking placement test

## Singapore Math

- **Grade Levels**: Pre-kindergarten through grade 8
- **Approach**: Based on Singapore national primary mathematics curriculum; sequential and mastery-based
- **Cost**: ~$122 per complete grade level
- **Format**: Primarily printed; recently introduced digital subscription videos
- **Audience**: Broader range of student abilities; less specifically competition-oriented than Beast Academy
- **Notable**: The model method for word problems is distinctive and develops systematic thinking

## Key Differences: Beast Academy vs Singapore Math

| Feature | Beast Academy | Singapore Math |
|---------|--------------|----------------|
| Primary audience | Gifted/advanced | Broader ability range |
| Problem-solving emphasis | Very high | High |
| Difficulty | Significantly above grade level | At/above grade level |
| Format | Digital + physical, narrative | Primarily print |
| Competition prep | Strong (AoPS heritage) | Moderate |
| Cost | ~$118 | ~$122 |
| Age range | ~6-13 | PreK-14 |

## Russian School of Mathematics (RSM)

Source: https://www.mathschool.com/ and community reviews

- Brick-and-mortar after-school program with locations primarily in metro areas
- Three levels per grade: accelerated, advanced, and honors (all more advanced than school)
- Strong competition preparation track
- Forum discussion (DCUrbanMom): "RSM or AoPS — which one?" suggests both are respected, RSM for structured in-person, AoPS for online/self-directed

## Other Elementary Enrichment Options

- **Kumon**: Drill-based, repetitive computation focus; develops speed and automaticity but not competition problem-solving depth
- **Math Olympiads for Elementary and Middle Schools (MOEMS)**: Competition program for grades 4-8, team-based through school
- **AlphaStar Academy**: Competition math prep specifically, grades 3-12

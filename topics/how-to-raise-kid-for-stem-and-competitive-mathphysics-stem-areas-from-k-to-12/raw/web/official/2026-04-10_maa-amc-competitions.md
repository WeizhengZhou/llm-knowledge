# AMC Competition Series Overview — Mathematical Association of America

## Structure

The MAA AMC comprises three competitions designed for different grade levels:

**AMC 8**: A 25-question, 40-minute competition for students in grade 8 and below (maximum age under 15.5 years). It emphasizes "middle school mathematics topics, including counting and probability, estimation, proportional reasoning, elementary geometry."

**AMC 10**: A 25-question, 75-minute multiple-choice test for grades 10 and below (maximum age under 17.5 years). Content includes elementary algebra, basic geometry, area/volume formulas, and elementary number theory, excluding trigonometry and advanced topics.

**AMC 12**: A 25-question, 75-minute competition for grades 12 and below (maximum age under 19.5 years). It "covers the full high school mathematics curriculum, including trigonometry, advanced algebra, and advanced geometry. Calculus is excluded."

## Competition Dates (2025-26)

- AMC 10 A & AMC 12 A: November 5, 2025
- AMC 10 B & AMC 12 B: November 13, 2025
- AMC 8: January 22-30, 2026

## Eligibility & Participation

Students participate through schools, colleges, math circles, or learning centers. Educators register as competition managers and administer tests to interested participants.

The AMC 10 serves as the first step in qualifying for the USA team in prestigious international mathematics competitions, including the International Mathematical Olympiad.

## Organization

Administered by the Mathematical Association of America (MAA). The MAA began holding the American High School Mathematics Examination (AHSME) in 1950.

## Pipeline

AMC → AIME → USAMO/USAJMO → MOP → IMO

AIME qualification: top 2.5% of AMC 10 participants or top 5% of AMC 12 participants (approximately 6,000–7,000 students annually). As of August 2025, international students no longer affect AIME cutoffs.

USAMO/USAJMO qualifying index (2025-2026): (AIME score × 20) + AMC score. [Changed from AIME × 10 + AMC in prior years.]

Approximately top 11% of all AIME participants are selected for USAMO or USAJMO (~500 total).

## AIME Qualification Structure
- Pre-2020: Fixed cutoff scores (AMC 10: 100 or 120; AMC 12: 86 or 100)
- Post-2020: Top 2.5% of AMC 10 participants and top 5% of AMC 12 participants
  (or a fixed cutoff, whichever is lower — guaranteeing those percentiles qualify)
- 2025-2026: No cap of 120/100 points — cutoffs can go up to 150

## Note on Index Formula Change (2025-2026)
The change from AIME×10 to AIME×20 significantly shifts the relative weight of AIME vs AMC score.
Under old formula: An AIME 10 score with AMC 130 → index 230
Under new formula: Same scores → index 330
This means AIME performance matters roughly twice as much as before.

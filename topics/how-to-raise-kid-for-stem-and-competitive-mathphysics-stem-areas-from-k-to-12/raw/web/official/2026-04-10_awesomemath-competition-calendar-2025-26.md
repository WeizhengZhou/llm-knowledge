# Physics and Math Competition Calendar 2025-2026

## Fall Competitions (September – December 2025)

**USAMTS Round 1**
- Organization: USA Mathematical Talent Search
- Timing: Opens September; solutions due mid-October
- Format: Proof-based competition, one month to solve problems
- Eligibility: Free for all U.S. high school students
- Note: "around 68/75 points or higher" qualifies for AIME

**MATHCOUNTS School Competition**
- Timing: November or December
- Grade Level: 6th-8th graders
- Format: Sprint, Target, and Team rounds
- Advancement: Top performers advance to Chapter Competition (February)

**AMC 10/12 A-Date**
- Timing: Early-to-mid November (November 5, 2025)
- Registration: 2-3 weeks before test
- Format: 75-minute exam, 25 multiple-choice questions
- Purpose: Pathway to AIME qualification

**AMC 10/12 B-Date**
- Timing: Approximately one week after A-Date (November 13, 2025)
- Format: Different problems, identical difficulty
- Note: Students can take both dates to maximize opportunities

**USAMTS Round 2**
- Timing: Mid-November; solutions due December

## Winter Competitions (January – February 2026)

**AMC 8**
- Timing: January 22-30, 2026
- Eligibility: Grade 8 and below
- Format: 25 multiple-choice questions, 40 minutes
- Registration: Late October deadline

**MATHCOUNTS Chapter Competition**
- Timing: Early-to-mid February
- Format: Sprint, Target, Team, and Countdown rounds
- Advancement: Top scorers advance to State Competition

**F=ma Exam (Physics)**
- Timing: Late January
- Organization: American Association of Physics Teachers (AAPT)
- Format: 75-minute exam, 25 questions
- Purpose: Qualifying round for USA Physics Olympiad
- Focus: Mechanics, conceptual understanding, problem-solving

**AIME I and AIME II**
- Timing: February 5, 2026 (I); February 11, 2026 (II)
- Format: 15 questions, 3 hours, integer answers (0-999)
- Eligibility: Invitation-based through AMC qualification
- Note: Two separate date options available

## Spring Competitions (March – April 2026)

**MATHCOUNTS State Competition**
- Timing: Early March
- Advancement: Top four individuals plus winning team advance to Nationals
- Format: Includes prestigious Countdown Round

**USAMO/USAJMO**
- Timing: Late March (typically two days)
- Format: Three proof-based problems, 4.5 hours per day
- Eligibility: Invitation based on combined AMC and AIME scores
- Level: Most challenging American high school competition

**Purple Comet! Math Meet**
- Timing: April 14-23, 2026
- Format: Free online team competition (up to six students)
- Levels: Middle or high school
- Cost: Free

**USAPhO (USA Physics Olympiad)**
- Timing: Late March or early April
- Eligibility: Qualification through F=ma exam
- Format: Free-response competition
- Advancement: Top performers invited to training program

## Late Spring/Summer (May – June 2026)

**MATHCOUNTS National Competition**
- Timing: May
- Participants: Approximately 224 students (four per state)
- Significance: Pinnacle of middle school mathematics competition
- Format: All standard rounds plus special mathematical events

**MOP (Mathematical Olympiad Program)**
- Timing: June (3-4 weeks)
- Location: University campus (Carnegie Mellon)
- Eligibility: Top USAMO and USAJMO performers
- Purpose: Advanced training and IMO team selection

**IMO (International Mathematical Olympiad)**
- Timing: July
- Format: Two-day competition, six problems
- Team: Six-person U.S. team
- Significance: World's premier high school mathematics competition

## Other Competitions (various timing)

**Math Kangaroo**
- Timing: March 19, 2026
- Grades: 1-12
- Format: 24 problems (grades 1-5) or 30 questions (grades 5+) in 75 minutes

**MOEMS (Mathematical Olympiads for Elementary and Middle Schools)**
- Timing: November through March, monthly
- Grades: 4-8

**IMC (International Math Contest)**
- Date: February 3, 2026
- Grades: 1-8

## Key Planning Notes

- Registration deadlines typically occur 2-3 weeks before exams
- Year-round preparation recommended rather than competition-specific cramming
- Eligible grade 10 and below students may take both AMC 10 and AMC 12 on different dates
- Multiple competition participation is standard for serious competitors

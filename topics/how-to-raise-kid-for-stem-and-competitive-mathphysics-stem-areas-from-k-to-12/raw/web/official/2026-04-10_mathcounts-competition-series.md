# MATHCOUNTS COMPETITION SERIES

Source: https://www.mathcounts.org/programs/mathcounts-competition-series
Supplemented by: https://www.mathcounts.org/programs/competition-rules-faq
Supplemented by: https://www.thethinkacademy.com/blog/2026-mathcounts-ultimate-guide-levels-timeline-prep-resources/

## Program Overview

MATHCOUNTS Competition Series is a national program that provides students in grades 6-8 the opportunity to compete in live math contests against and alongside their peers.

## Competition Structure: Four Levels

The Competition Series has 4 levels of competition — school, chapter, state, and national. Each level comprises 4 rounds: Sprint, Target, Team, and Countdown Round. Altogether the rounds are designed to take about 3 hours to complete.

### Round Formats

**Sprint Round**
- 30 problems given all at once
- 40 minutes to complete
- Individual, no calculator

**Target Round**
- 8 problems given 2 at a time (4 pairs)
- 6 minutes to complete each set of 2 problems
- Calculator permitted
- Individual

**Team Round**
- 10 problems for a team of up to 4 students
- 20 minutes to complete
- Calculator permitted
- Top 4 chapter competitors from a school form the team

**Countdown Round**
- Head-to-head oral competition
- Top individual scorers compete one-on-one
- 45 seconds to answer each problem
- Used at chapter, state, and national levels

## Competition Level Details

### School Competition (August 2025 – January 2026)
- Schools register and coaches prepare students using MATHCOUNTS resources
- Starting in November 2025, coaches administer the School Competition (can be official or unofficial)
- Purpose: select students who will represent the school at chapter competition
- Up to 14 competitors can advance from a school: 4 for the team round + up to 10 individuals

### Chapter Competitions (February 1-28, 2026)
- Local schools compete in approximately 500 chapter competitions across the country
- Schools can send up to 14 competitors: 4 for team + individual rounds, plus 10 competing individually only
- Non-school competitors (NSCs) compete as individuals
- Top students advance to state

### State Competitions (March 1-31, 2026)
- Top students from each chapter (including NSCs, who can advance as individuals) advance
- Held in all 50 US states plus territories
- No additional fees required for competitors to advance to the state level

### National Competition (May 10-11, 2026)
- The top 4 individuals from each state (including NSCs) receive an all-expenses-paid trip
- Held at RTX MATHCOUNTS National Competition
- 224 competitors total form 4-person state teams
- Competitors also compete individually for the title of National Champion
- 2026 national sponsors include Northrop Grumman Foundation, Lockheed Martin, NSPE, 3M, Art of Problem Solving

## Eligibility

**Student eligibility:**
- Enrolled in grades 6-8
- Participation in MATHCOUNTS Competition Series is limited to 3 years per student
- No student who has completed grade 8 may compete

**School eligibility:**
- All types of schools: public, private, charter, virtual, and home schools
- Tutoring/enrichment centers or other groups that do not function as students' official school of record are NOT eligible to register

**Non-School Competitors (NSCs):**
- A student may register as an NSC only if their official school of record is not registering for the Competition Series
- NSCs compete individually (cannot form a team)
- NSCs can advance through chapter and state to national as individuals

## Registration and Cost

- Schools register in the fall (August-December timeframe)
- Registration fee applies at the school/chapter level
- State and national advancement is at no additional cost to the student

## Upcoming Changes
- MATHCOUNTS is developing a standalone grades 3-5 program (not yet launched as of 2026)
- No changes to the existing grades 6-8 Competition Series rules

## Preparation Resources Provided
- MATHCOUNTS School Handbook with practice problems and solutions
- Online coach resources dashboard
- Past competition tests available

## Notable Alumni Quote
"The lesson I got from MATHCOUNTS is one that I have cherished more than any math lecture I have ever received: The best part of doing math is doing it with others." — BRIAN, COMPETITION ALUMNUS

## Competition Math Content Areas
Problems span: arithmetic, algebra, geometry, probability, statistics, number theory, combinatorics — all at a middle school level but requiring problem-solving skills beyond standard curriculum.

## Prep Strategy Notes (from Think Academy guide)
- Grade 6: start with AMC 8 prep, Beast Academy, or Competition Math for Middle School (Jason Batterson)
- Grade 7: AoPS Volume 1, targeted MATHCOUNTS practice handbooks
- Grade 8: timed practice on past MATHCOUNTS school/chapter tests; target top-10 finish at chapter to advance to state
- Sprint Round requires speed + accuracy; Target Round allows more thought + calculator use
- National qualifiers typically score 30/38+ on Sprint + Target combined at chapter level

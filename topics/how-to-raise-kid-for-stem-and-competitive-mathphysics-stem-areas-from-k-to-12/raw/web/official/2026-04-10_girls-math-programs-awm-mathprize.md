# Girls-Specific Math Programs and Competitions in the USA

## AMC Young Women in Mathematics Prize and Certificate Program (MAA)

- Honors the top-performing, self-identifying girl students on AMC 8, AMC 10, and AMC 12.
- Top 5 scorers on each competition (AMC 8, 10, 12) split a $5,000 scholarship.
- Top 5 scorers from each MAA Section receive a certificate.
- Total annual impact: 20 scholarships and approximately 580 certificates distributed.
- Purpose: to increase visibility and recognition of high-achieving girls in math competitions.

## Math Prize for Girls (MP4G)

**Organizer:** Advantage Testing Foundation and Jane Street (global trading firm), hosted at MIT Department of Mathematics.

**Scale:** The largest math prize for girls in the world. Approximately 250 competitors annually.

**Prize Pool:** $100,000 total; first-place winner receives $50,000.

**Format:** Challenging test of mathematical creativity and insight. Held each fall at MIT in Cambridge, Massachusetts.
- 2026 date: Sunday, October 11, 2026.

**Eligibility:**
- Must be female (self-identifying).
- Must be in 11th grade or below as of March 1 of the competition year.
- Cannot have graduated high school or begun college before the competition.
- Residents of the United States or Canada; non-residents may apply with citizenship/permanent residency documentation.
- Must have taken an official administration of the AMC 10 or AMC 12 in November of the prior cycle.

**Application Process:**
- Submit official AMC 10/12 scores (both A and B if taken).
- Optional: AIME score if applicable.
- Deadline: May 31 (no late submissions).
- Selection: Foundation invites approximately 300 applicants based on AMC scores, with separate cutoffs for each exam variant.
- Notification: By July 1.

**Community Connection:** Math Prize for Girls alumnae frequently reconnect at Mathcamp reunions, creating lasting peer networks.

## Association for Women in Mathematics (AWM) K-12 Programs

AWM was founded in 1971; its purpose is to "create a community in which women and girls can thrive in their mathematical endeavors, and to promote equitable opportunity and treatment of women and others of marginalized genders."

### Programs for Girls / K-12 Students:

**GirlsGetMath@ICERM**
- One-week day program for high school students.
- Focus: mathematics and computing.
- Hosted at ICERM (Institute for Computational and Experimental Research in Mathematics, Brown University).

**Sonia Kovalevsky (SK) Days**
- AWM-supported program bringing together students and female mathematicians.
- Held at universities across the country.

**Epsilon Camp**
- Serves exceptional young mathematicians ages 7–12 and their families.
- Designed for children who are highly gifted and passionate about math.
- Includes both student program and parent workshop.

**Julia Robinson Math Festival**
- Free puzzles and virtual/in-person events.
- Focus on joyful, non-competitive math exploration.

**AWM Student Essay Contest**
- Annual biographical essay competition for K-12 students.

**Expanding Your Horizons Conferences**
- One-day conferences at 100+ U.S. locations.
- Features women in STEM.

**Math Circles (AWM-supported)**
- Informal after-school programs combining exploration with mathematical discovery.

### Summer Programs Listed by AWM for Girls (Middle/High School):

Middle school:
- BEAM: Free 5-week residential program in NYC/LA for 6th graders from low-income backgrounds.
- National Math Camps: 2-week residential, ages 11–14.
- MSSM Summer Camps (Maine, ages 10–14).

High school:
- GirlsGetMath@ICERM (one-week day program).
- Girls Who Code (7-week summer immersion, not math-competition-focused).
- Mathcamp (5-week intensive; ~47% girls and non-binary students).
- MathILy (5-week program at Bryn Mawr College).
- PROMYS (6-week program at Boston University).

## All Girls Math Tournament (STEAM for All)

- STEAM for All organization runs an All Girls Math Tournament.
- 2024 edition confirmed.
- Enables participants to explore STEM disciplines and sharpen skills in preparation for larger competitions like AMC 8/10/12 and MATHCOUNTS.
- Designed as an entry-level, encouraging environment for girls.

## Who AoPS Serves

AoPS curriculum is specifically designed for high-performing math students in grades 5-12. Provides much broader and deeper exploration of challenging mathematics than typical curricula. Teaches critical problem-solving skills for MATHCOUNTS, AMC, top universities, and competitive careers. AoPS is an ACS WASC Accredited School.

In 2015, the US won IMO for the first time in 21 years; all 16 students on the 2015/2016/2018 winning teams had collectively enrolled in more than 100 AoPS classes.

## BOOK SERIES

### 1. Introductory Curriculum (Grades 6-10)
Covers the standard Prealgebra-Algebra-Geometry sequence at an advanced level, plus discrete math topics usually underrepresented in middle/high school.

**Prealgebra** — Richard Rusczyk, David Patrick, Ravi Boppana
- Text: 608 pages. Solutions: 224 pages.
- Price: $59.00 (text + solutions set)
- Targets: MATHCOUNTS, MOEMS, AMC 8
- Prepares students for the rigors of algebra; teaches problem-solving techniques for prestigious middle school math contests

**Introduction to Algebra** — Richard Rusczyk
- Text: 656 pages. Solutions: 312 pages. (2nd edition)
- Price: $67.00
- Target grades: 6-9
- Topics: linear equations, ratios, quadratic equations, complex numbers, logarithms, sequences/series, and more
- Deeper and more challenging than typical Algebra 1 curriculum

**Introduction to Counting & Probability** — David Patrick
- Text: 256 pages. Solutions: 120 pages. (2nd edition)
- Price: $49.00
- Target grades: 7-10
- Topics: permutations, combinations, Pascal's triangle, geometric probability, Binomial Theorem, basic combinatorial identities

**Introduction to Geometry** — Richard Rusczyk
- Text: 557 pages. Solutions: 226 pages. (2nd edition)
- Price: $65.00
- Target grades: 7-10
- Topics: similar/congruent triangles, quadrilaterals, polygons, circles, power of a point, 3D geometry, transformations, introductory trigonometry

**Introduction to Number Theory** — Mathew Crawford
- Text: 336 pages. Solutions: 144 pages.
- Price: $55.00
- Target grades: 7-10
- Topics: primes/composites, multiples/divisors, prime factorization, base numbers, modular arithmetic, divisibility rules, linear congruences

### 2. Intermediate Curriculum (Grades 9-12, advanced)
For advanced high-school students; more challenging than most standard textbooks. Lays foundation for university-level math, physics, engineering, or computer science.

**Intermediate Algebra** — Richard Rusczyk and Mathew Crawford
- Text: 720 pages. Solutions: 336 pages.
- Price: $74.00
- Covers Algebra 2 + Precalculus topics, plus advanced AMC/AIME problem-solving strategies
- Topics: complex numbers, conics, polynomials, identities, inequalities, exponents/logarithms, functional equations

**Intermediate Counting & Probability** — David Patrick
- Text: 400 pages. Solutions: 208 pages.
- Price: $56.00
- Target grades: 9-12
- Topics: inclusion-exclusion, recursion, conditional probability, generating functions, graph theory, Pigeonhole Principle, Catalan numbers

**Precalculus** — Richard Rusczyk
- Text: 528 pages. Solutions: 272 pages. (2nd edition)
- Price: $62.00
- Topics: trigonometry, complex numbers, vectors, matrices
- Includes many AIME and USAMO problems

**Calculus** — David Patrick
- Text: 336 pages. Solutions: 128 pages. (2nd edition)
- Price: $59.00
- Single-variable calculus: limits, continuity, derivatives, integrals, power series, plane curves, differential equations

## Recommended Learning Path (from AoPS)

1. **Grades 3-6 (Beast Academy):** BA 2A–5D series before moving to AoPS curriculum
2. **Grades 6-8 (Intro track):** Prealgebra → Intro Algebra A/B → Intro C&P + NT + Geometry
3. **Grades 9-10 (Intermediate track):** Intermediate Algebra → Intermediate C&P → Precalculus
4. **Grades 10-12 (Competition prep):** AMC 10/12 Problem Series → AIME Series → WOOT (if targeting USAMO/IMO)
5. **Physics:** F=ma Problem Series → PhysicsWOOT

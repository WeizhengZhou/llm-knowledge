# European Girls' Mathematical Olympiad (EGMO): US Team Selection and Details

## What is EGMO

- Founded in 2012, held annually in April.
- Inspired by the China Girls Mathematical Olympiad.
- Started with 19 countries; grew to approximately 57 countries by the 11th edition (2022).
- Although the competition is held in Europe, it is "open to female participants from all over the world."
- The US leads the all-time EGMO medal table with 39 gold medals, 12 silver, and 5 bronze medals through 2025.

## Competition Format

- Two papers on consecutive days.
- Each paper: three problems, 4.5 hours per paper.
- Total: six problems, each marked 0–7 points (maximum 42 points total).
- Same format and difficulty level as the IMO.

## Eligibility

- Participants must be female (self-identifying girl students in MAA language).
- Must be under age 20.
- Must not yet be enrolled at a university.

## US Selection Pathway

According to the MAA:

1. **AMC**: Selection begins each fall with the AMC exam series. Top-scoring girl-identifying students on AMC 10 and AMC 12 advance.
2. **AIME**: Top AMC scorers are invited to AIME.
3. **USAMO/USAJMO**: The MAA explicitly invites "top-scoring AMC girl-identifying students to USAMO and USAJMO each year."
4. **MOP (Mathematical Olympiad Program)**: A summer training camp. Female USAMO/USAJMO qualifiers and strong performers are invited to MOP.
5. **Team Selection Tests (TSTs)**: At MOP, top scorers are invited to TSTs — 4.5-hour, 3-question olympiad-style exams. TSTs determine the teams for both IMO and EGMO.
6. **EGMO Team**: Based on EGMO selection index, the MAA invites four students to comprise the US EGMO team.

Key note from Evan Chen's MOP website: the EGMO selection index considers "top scorers in the previous year's EGMO selection indices, or those who attended EGMO."

## Is It Invitation-Only or Application-Based?

The pathway is competitive/qualification-based, not an open application. Students must qualify through the AMC/AIME/USAMO pipeline. There is no separate EGMO application — the MAA selects based on competition performance.

## 2025 US EGMO Team Results

- Competition held in Prishtina, Kosovo (14th EGMO).
- US Team finished 2nd place.
- All four US members earned gold medals — the only team at the competition where all members earned gold.
- Two team members ranked in the top 10 scorers overall.

2025 US EGMO Team members:
- Shruti Arun (Colorado, age 17)
- Hannah Fox (California, age 17)
- Alansha Jiang (Washington, age 17)
- Angela Liu (California, age 17)

Team leaders: Rachel Zhang and Serena An.

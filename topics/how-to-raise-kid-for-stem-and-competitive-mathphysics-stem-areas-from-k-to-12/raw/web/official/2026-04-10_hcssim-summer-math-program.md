# Hampshire College Summer Studies in Mathematics (HCSSiM)

Source: https://hcssim.org/
Source: https://hcssim.org/faq/
Source: https://www.lumiere-education.com/post/the-hampshire-college-summer-studies-in-mathematics-hcssim-our-review
Source: https://www.hampshire.edu/summer-academic-programs/hampshire-college-summer-studies-mathematics-hcssim

## Program Overview

HCSSiM is an intensive six-week encounter with college-level mathematics for talented and highly motivated high school students. The program emphasizes: "It is demanding and expanding." Students spend a major portion of each day actively engaged in doing mathematics — not simply learning results of mathematics.

This is the 54th session (as of 2026 application cycle).

## Location and Duration

- **Location:** Hampshire College, Amherst, Massachusetts
- **Duration:** Six weeks (residential)
- **2026 session dates:** June 28 – August 8, 2026
- Students live in college dormitories on Hampshire College campus

## Daily Schedule

- **Morning:** 4 hours of classes (Monday through Saturday)
- **Evening:** 3-hour problem sessions
- **Daily:** "Prime Time Theorem" — a significant result presented each day
- Faculty accessible at meals and in the program dormitory
- Recreational activities interspersed throughout

**Total contact time:** approximately 7+ hours of active mathematics per day, 6 days/week

## Eligibility and Who Attends

**No hard age/grade cutoffs.** The program states: "Mathematical age would be more important than chronological age."

**Typical profile:**
- Most students come to HCSSiM after their sophomore or junior year (grades 10-11)
- Some students have completed only 9th grade if they have advanced math preparation
- Occasionally even younger students are accepted
- Gender balance: "Typically, there are as many girls and non-binary students as boys"
- No requirement for competition math experience specifically — mathematical curiosity and capability matter more

**What HCSSiM is NOT looking for:**
- The program does not select purely based on competition performance
- Values mathematical depth and genuine intellectual engagement over trophy collection

## Mathematical Content

The program covers "courses, usual and unusual" — meaning standard advanced topics plus exploratory, non-standard mathematics. Known topics include:

- Number theory
- Combinatorics
- Abstract algebra (groups, rings)
- Topology and geometry
- Analysis / calculus concepts
- Recreational and exploratory mathematics
- Humor: "We've usually covered all the math there is by the middle of the 5th week"

The curriculum is emergent — driven partly by student interest and instructor creativity.

## Application Process

1. **Letter of application:** A friendly-and-informative letter about reasons and motivations for applying, and why spending 8 hours/day doing math during summer is reasonable
2. **Sponsor letter:** Someone who knows the student in a mathematical context (math teacher, coach, mentor)
3. **Interesting Test:** An email is sent sometime in March with the "Hampshire College Summer Studies in Mathematics Interesting Test" — a non-standard math problem set that serves as the core of the application
4. **Rolling decisions:** No strict deadline, though a deadline for "full consideration" is in late April

**Application is free, online, and obligation-free.**

## Cost and Financial Aid

- **2026 tuition:** $7,208 (six weeks, residential)
- **Financial aid:** "There is ample financial aid available"
- The program actively works to make HCSSiM accessible regardless of financial circumstances

## Program Philosophy

HCSSiM emphasizes doing mathematics as a creative, collaborative, joyful act. It explicitly contrasts with test-prep approaches. The program is known for its unique culture: mathematically intense but also irreverent and community-focused.

Unlike competition-focused programs, HCSSiM is often described as revealing what professional mathematics actually feels like — open-ended exploration rather than racing toward known answers.

## Notable Aspects

- One of the longest-running summer math programs in the US (54th year in 2026)
- Produced many mathematicians, scientists, and academics
- Smaller and more intimate than Mathcamp or RSI
- Strong alumni community

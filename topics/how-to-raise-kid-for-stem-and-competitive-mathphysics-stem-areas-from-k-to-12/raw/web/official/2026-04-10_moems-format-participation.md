# Math Olympiads for Elementary and Middle Schools (MOEMS)

Source: https://www.moems.org/pages/about-the-program
Source: https://moems.org/information/
Source: https://moems.org/general/

## Program Overview

MOEMS® is one of the most influential and fun-filled math competition programs in the United States and throughout the world, with more than 120,000 students from every state and 39 additional countries participating each year.

## Divisions and Grade Levels

| Division | Eligible Grades |
|----------|----------------|
| Elementary (Division E) | Grades 4, 5, and 6 |
| Middle School (Division M) | Grades 6, 7, and 8 |

**Key rule:** No team may have any student who has completed grade 8.

Grade 6 students may participate in either division, but not both.

## Competition Format

**5 monthly contests per year**, each with **5 non-routine problems** (25 problems total per school year).

**Per contest:**
- 5 problems
- 30 minutes to complete
- Students work alone (no collaboration during the contest)
- 1 point for each correct answer (maximum 5 per contest; maximum 25 per year)
- No partial credit

**Contest administration windows (2025-2026):**
| Contest | Administration Window | Score Deadline |
|---------|----------------------|----------------|
| Contest 1 | Nov 10 – Dec 1, 2025 | Mar 31, 2026 |
| Contest 2 | Dec 8 – Dec 29, 2025 | Mar 31, 2026 |
| Contest 3 | Jan 5 – Jan 26, 2026 | Mar 31, 2026 |
| Contest 4 | Feb 2 – Feb 23, 2026 | Mar 31, 2026 |
| Contest 5 | Mar 2 – Mar 23, 2026 | Mar 31, 2026 |

## Administration Options

- **MOEMS On Paper** — traditional paper-based administration
- **MOEMS Online** — same contest problems, administered digitally
- Both options have the same administration window
- Contests may be administered at the PICO's (Person In Charge of Olympiads) convenience during the window

## Team Registration

- Teams may enroll up to **35 students** per team
- Open to: public schools, private schools, parochial schools, home schools, institutes
- Individuals (with restrictions) may also participate
- The PICO is the adult coordinator responsible for administering contests

## Awards and Recognition

- **Individual certificates** for all participants
- **Performance-based patches and pins** awarded at certain score thresholds
- **Team plaques** for top-performing teams
- **George Lenchner Medallion** — awarded for perfect scores (25/25)
- End-of-year awards recognizing top individual and team scores nationally

## Resources Provided to Teams

- Monthly contests with detailed solutions
- Solution videos accompanying each contest
- 100 practice problems from the previous two years with step-by-step video explanations

## Program Philosophy and Goals (Eight Core Objectives)

1. Stimulate enthusiasm and love for mathematics
2. Introduce important mathematical concepts
3. Teach major strategies for problem solving
4. Develop mathematical flexibility in solving problems
5. Strengthen mathematical intuition
6. Foster mathematical creativity and ingenuity
7. Provide for the satisfaction, joy, and thrill of meeting challenges
8. Build a student's appreciation for mathematics

## How MOEMS Differs from MATHCOUNTS

| Feature | MOEMS | MATHCOUNTS |
|---------|-------|------------|
| Grade levels | 4-8 | 6-8 |
| Format | Monthly contests, untimed-ish (30 min) | Live competition with timed rounds |
| Team size | Up to 35 | Up to 4 (team round) |
| Frequency | 5 per year (November–March) | Single annual competition season |
| Advancement | No elimination ladder | School → Chapter → State → National |
| Problems per contest | 5 | 30+ (Sprint) |
| Level | Entry-level competition math | More demanding, nationally competitive |

## Why MOEMS as an Entry Point

MOEMS is commonly recommended as the first formal math competition for grades 4-6. It introduces non-routine problem solving without the high-pressure environment of MATHCOUNTS. Problems require creative thinking but are accessible to well-prepared elementary students.

# U.S. Physics Team Competition Details

## F=ma Exam

**Format & Content:**
The F=ma exam is a 75-minute exam with 25 multiple choice questions that focuses on mechanics. Coverage includes kinematics, statics, Newton's laws, momentum, energy, oscillations, orbital mechanics, rotational dynamics, and fluids. Problems are solvable without calculus, though some may have shorter calculus-based solutions.

**2026 Test Date:**
The 2026 F=ma test was held February 12, 2026.

**Preparation:**
Students should practice using past exams available on the AAPT website and collaborate in study groups. Most concepts are covered in algebra-based high school physics courses.

**Eligibility:**
- U.S. Citizens, permanent residents, or students currently attending U.S. schools
- Must be physically located within the U.S. during testing

## Score Cutoffs (Historical)

The qualifying score to move on to the USAPhO exam is typically between 14-18. The 2025 cutoff was 15. Approximately 400 students advance to USAPhO based on F=ma scores.

## USAPhO Exam

**Format & Content:**
A 3-hour free response exam divided into two 90-minute sections requiring justified answers. It covers all topics in introductory physics, including mechanics, electromagnetism, thermodynamics, relativity, nuclear, atomic, and particle physics, waves, optics, and data analysis.

**Advancement & Awards:**
Approximately 400 students advance to USAPhO based on F=ma scores. Medal distribution:
- Gold: top 10-12%
- Silver: next 14-16%
- Bronze: next 20-22%
- Honorable mentions: next 24-26%
Cutoffs vary annually.

**Eligibility:**
- U.S. Citizens or permanent residents only
- Must be located within the U.S.

## IPhO Selection & Training Camp

**Process:**
Approximately 20 students are selected for a ten-day training camp at the University of Maryland, based on combined USAPhO and F=ma scores. At the end of the camp, 5 students are selected to represent the U.S. at the International Physics Olympiad.

**2026 Schedule:**
- U.S. Physics Team Training Camp: Late May and early June 2026
- 56th International Physics Olympiad: Bucaramanga, Colombia, July 4-12, 2026

**Requirements for IPhO:**
- Must be U.S. Citizens or permanent residents
- Cannot exceed age 20 by June 30 of competition year
- Must attend full 10-day camp

## Pipeline Summary
F=ma (25 multiple choice, 75 min) → Top ~400 → USAPhO (3-hour free response) → Top ~20 → Training Camp (10 days, University of Maryland) → Top 5 → IPhO

# U.S. Physics Team / USA Physics Olympiad — AAPT

## Administering Organization

The American Association of Physics Teachers (AAPT) sponsors the U.S. Physics Team program. The American Institute of Physics (AIP) co-sponsors.

## Competition Pipeline: F=ma → USAPhO → IPhO

### F=ma (Stage 1)
- Annual physics competition for high school students in the United States
- Sponsored by AAPT
- Format: 75-minute exam, 25 multiple choice questions
- Focus: Classical mechanics exclusively
- Timing: Late January each year
- Purpose: Selects approximately top 400 students for USAPhO

### USAPhO Semifinal (Stage 2)
- "USA Physics Olympiad" semifinal exam
- Format: 3-hour free-response exam
- Structure: Two parts of 90 minutes each
- Students required to justify their answers
- Eligibility: Top ~400 students from F=ma exam

### U.S. Physics Team Training Camp (Stage 3)
- Location: University of Maryland
- Duration: Intensive training program
- Selection: Approximately top 20 students from USAPhO semifinal
- Students receive intensive theoretical and experimental preparation
- Take selection tests during the camp

### IPhO Team (Stage 4)
- Top 5 students from training camp represent USA
- Compete at International Physics Olympiad (held in July)
- IPhO is held internationally at rotating location

## Physics Bowl
- Annual international physics competition
- Administered by AAPT
- Timing: March–April each year
- Format: 40 multiple-choice questions
- Content: Wide range of high school physics topics
- Note: Less selective than F=ma/USAPhO pathway

## Science Competitions (Separate from Physics Olympiad)

### Regeneron ISEF (International Science and Engineering Fair)
- World's premier science competition for high school students
- Awards nearly $9 million in prizes each year
- More than 1,600 finalists from over 70 countries
- Students qualify through local and regional ISEF-affiliated fairs
- Format: Students present original research completed within 12-month period
- Evaluation: Creativity, scientific rigor, communication across 22 categories

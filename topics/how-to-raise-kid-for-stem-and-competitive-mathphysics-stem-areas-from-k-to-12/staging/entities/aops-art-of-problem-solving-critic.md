## Must-Fix Before wiki/ Graduation

1. **No Common Mistakes section.** Entity articles require this section. Three specific, non-obvious mistakes belong here: (a) enrolling in AoPS Introduction to Algebra before completing Beast Academy Level 5 — the Introduction series assumes arithmetic fluency that students who skipped Beast Academy often lack, leading to frustration and abandonment; (b) choosing WOOT before reaching AIME qualification — WOOT targets students who can already do AIME problems and need olympiad-level training; buying it earlier results in students not understanding the material; (c) treating the AoPS textbook as equivalent to the online course — the textbook provides no feedback mechanism; for students who need proof-writing correction, the online course section is necessary rather than optional.

## Should-Fix

1. **The "Online Courses" section does not help the parent choose a starting course.** The section says courses cost $415-$895 and cover the same material as the textbooks. A parent with a grade 6 student who just finished Beast Academy cannot determine whether to start Introduction to Algebra as a self-study textbook or online course from this entry. A one-sentence recommendation — "For students beginning competition prep, Introduction to Algebra is the standard starting course; choose the online section if the student benefits from instructor feedback and peer discussion" — would convert this from a description to a decision.

2. **Alcumus section lacks a "how to use it" note.** The section says Alcumus is free and covers prealgebra through AMC 12 level. A first-time parent does not know whether Alcumus should replace courses, supplement them, or serve as a diagnostic. One sentence — "Alcumus is best used as daily practice alongside a textbook or course, not as a standalone curriculum" — would make this entry actionable.

3. **Accreditation and NCAA approval details are listed but their relevance is not explained.** Parents reading "ACS WASC accredited" and "NCAA approved" need to know why this matters: AoPS courses can satisfy high school graduation requirements and be listed on transcripts. Adding one sentence of context would make these facts useful rather than just present.

## Graduation Decision: NEEDS-REVISION

The missing Common Mistakes section is a must-fix that blocks graduation. The three specific mistakes identified are non-obvious and directly relevant to P2 and P3 parents making program decisions.

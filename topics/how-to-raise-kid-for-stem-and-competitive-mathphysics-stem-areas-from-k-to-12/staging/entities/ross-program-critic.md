## Must-Fix Before wiki/ Graduation

1. **No Common Mistakes section.** Three specific mistakes identified: (a) applying without taking the qualifying problem set seriously; (b) choosing Ross for a breadth-seeking student who will be frustrated by six weeks on number theory; (c) applying to only Ross and not also to PROMYS and Mathcamp.

## Should-Fix

1. **Application requirements are absent.** A P3 parent reading this article as the final step before applying to Ross cannot determine what to submit. At minimum, add: "The Ross application requires a qualifying problem set (available on rossprogram.org in late winter/early spring) and teacher recommendations. The problem set, not prior competition scores, is the primary admission criterion."

2. **Financial aid mechanism is vague.** The article says "Financial aid is available" and cites sponsors, but does not say how to apply or what income levels are served. Compare to PROMYS (free under $80K income threshold) — Ross's financial aid terms are less clear from public sources, but noting "contact the program to discuss financial aid" is more actionable than listing sponsors.

3. **L4 synthesis opportunity.** Even one community observation — e.g., "Some alumni on mathematics forums describe the six-week number theory immersion as the most intellectually intense experience of their pre-college years" — with the required epistemic note block would give P3 parents context for what they are selecting.

## Graduation Decision: NEEDS-REVISION

Missing Common Mistakes section is a must-fix. The other gaps are should-fix but the article needs the Common Mistakes section before graduating.

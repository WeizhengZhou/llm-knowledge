# Critic Report — Research Science Institute (RSI) — 2026-04-10

## Overall Assessment

This entity article is the tightest of the summer program entity articles. Quick Facts are complete with precise data (acceptance rate, cost, eligibility, deadlines). The "Why RSI Matters for College Admissions" section adds value that no other article in the set provides for this program. The article is appropriately short because it correctly defers to `summer-programs-guide.md` and `college-admissions-strategy.md` for context. The main gaps are the absent Common Mistakes section and the absence of application content guidance.

## Must-Fix Before wiki/ Graduation

1. **No Common Mistakes section.** Three specific mistakes: applying without a research narrative, missing the December deadline, and not discovering/planning for RSI until 12th grade (the only eligible year).

## Should-Fix

1. **Application preparation guidance is absent.** The article lists required materials but not what a strong application looks like. Adding one sentence — "Strong RSI applications typically describe a specific research question the student wants to investigate, supported by evidence of independent curiosity (completed projects, self-directed study, or relevant coursework)" — would help P3 parents prepare rather than just apply.

2. **The "Why RSI Matters" section would benefit from honest qualification.** The section cites Rise Global Education's claim that "STS alumni have a notably high college admissions success rate at elite schools." This is attributed correctly, but the article could add: "Note that RSI applicants are already among the most academically strong high school students in the country; the correlation between RSI and elite college admissions reflects this pre-selection, not a causal admissions boost." This mirrors the epistemic discipline in the college admissions strategy article and prevents P3 parents from over-indexing on RSI as an admissions formula.

3. **L4 synthesis would meaningfully differentiate this article.** What makes RSI different from Ross/PROMYS/Mathcamp in terms of student experience is the research — working independently with a mentor on a question that has no known answer. A single community-sourced observation with the epistemic note block would help P3 parents understand this distinction.

## Graduation Decision: NEEDS-REVISION

Missing Common Mistakes section is the must-fix. All other gaps are should-fix. The revision is additive.

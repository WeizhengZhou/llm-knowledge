# Critic Report — MATHCOUNTS — 2026-04-10

## Overall Assessment

This entity article covers MATHCOUNTS with official L1 source data and is factually complete. Quick Facts, format details, key dates, and participation logistics are all present. The MOEMS section is a useful addition that helps P2 parents understand the feeder competition landscape. However, the article has no preparation guidance (beyond wikilinks), no Common Mistakes section, no L4 synthesis on what the competition experience is like for students, and the article does not help a parent decide whether or when MATHCOUNTS is the right first competition for their child.

## Must-Fix Before wiki/ Graduation

1. **No Common Mistakes section.** Entity articles require this. Three specific mistakes identified above: entering a student without pre-algebra foundations, waiting to start until 7th grade and losing one of three allowed years, and over-focusing on Sprint Round individual speed without Team Round preparation. All three are specific consequences that a parent making a MATHCOUNTS enrollment decision needs to know.

2. **Decision framing is absent.** A parent who has never heard of MATHCOUNTS and wants to know whether their 5th-grade student showing math aptitude should start in 6th grade cannot answer this question from the article. The "Preparation" section needs at minimum one sentence: "Students with AoPS Prealgebra-level skills and who enjoy problem-solving challenges are ready for MATHCOUNTS in grade 6." This enables the parent decision rather than just describing the competition.

## Should-Fix

1. **RO3 differentiation from AMC 8/Math Kangaroo/MOEMS is absent.** The article should include a brief differentiation note: "MATHCOUNTS is team-based and school-organized (unlike AMC 8, which is individual and nationally administered); it rewards speed and collaboration in equal measure. Students who thrive in team environments often prefer MATHCOUNTS; students who prefer individual performance often prefer AMC 8." Two sentences would answer the RO3 must_answer item.

2. **The MOEMS section notes that grade 6 students may participate in "either division but not both" — this is a useful fact but the practical implication should be stated.** Which division is appropriate for a 6th grader who is strong in math? Add a recommendation: "Grade 6 students with competition math preparation typically benefit more from Division M (grades 6-8 content) than Division E."

3. **L4 synthesis on preparation start age should be added.** The forum observation that MATHCOUNTS preparation typically begins in grade 6 with AoPS Prealgebra and MATHCOUNTS handbooks is in the roadmap article body — bring it into this entity article in a "What Parents Observe" section with the required epistemic note block.

## Graduation Decision: NEEDS-REVISION

Two must-fix items: missing Common Mistakes section and absent decision framing. Both are additive and require modest revision.

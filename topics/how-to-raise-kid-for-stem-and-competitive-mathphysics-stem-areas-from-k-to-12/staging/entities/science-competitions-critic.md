## Must-Fix Before wiki/ Graduation

1. **No Common Mistakes section.** Three specific mistakes identified: the ISEF multi-year pipeline timing (students who start in 11th grade have one shot), the STS independent-contribution requirement, and the Science Olympiad team-building lead time. All three are non-obvious to P3 parents approaching these competitions for the first time.

2. **ISEF qualification pathway (regional fair requirement) is mentioned in the Quick Facts but not explained in the body.** A parent reading the body text of the ISEF section encounters: "ISEF eligibility requires grades 9-12 students to first compete in a Regeneron ISEF-affiliated science fair (regional or state) and win the right to attend." This is present but only as a subordinate clause. The body text does not explain how to find an affiliated regional fair, that regional fair registration is separate from ISEF, or that the research project must be substantially complete before the regional fair (typically February-March). This is the critical information gap between "I want my child to do ISEF" and "here is what to do this year."

## Should-Fix

1. **D2 decision framing: add a "Which Competition and When" section.** A two-paragraph section distinguishing when each competition is appropriate — "ISEF: for students who want to do independent research starting in grades 9-11; STS: the senior-year capstone for students who have ongoing research; Science Olympiad: for students who prefer team-based events across multiple science domains" — would convert this from a reference article to a decision guide.

2. **Science Olympiad section mentions 23 events but does not name any.** A parent who has never heard of Science Olympiad cannot evaluate whether their child would enjoy it. Naming four or five representative events ("Anatomy and Physiology, Dynamic Planet, Robot Tour, Experimental Design, Bridge Building") would give parents and students a concrete sense of what participation involves.

3. **JSHS section should be collapsed.** Four sentences about a suspended program inflates the article without serving any current reader need. Reduce to one sentence as suggested above.

## Graduation Decision: NEEDS-REVISION

Two must-fix items: missing Common Mistakes section and absent ISEF qualification pathway explanation. Both are additive revisions.

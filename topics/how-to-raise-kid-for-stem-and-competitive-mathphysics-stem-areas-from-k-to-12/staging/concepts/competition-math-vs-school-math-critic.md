## Must-Fix Before wiki/ Graduation

1. **No "Common Mistakes" section.** This concept article serves the same decision-enabling function as a guide for RO2/RO3 parents. The two mistakes already identified in the article body — (a) treating AMC 8 first-attempt low scores as a talent signal rather than a format-unfamiliarity signal, and (b) adding competition math on top of school math rather than replacing routine work — are specific and non-obvious. They need to be surfaced in a dedicated Common Mistakes section so they are not buried in body prose that skimming parents will miss.

## Should-Fix

1. **RO2 age guidance is absent.** The "How to Bridge the Gap" section tells parents what to use (Beast Academy, AoPS Prealgebra, USAMTS) but does not say at what age each step applies. A P1 parent (grades K-2) reading this article cannot extract a concrete starting point. Adding a one-sentence age note per step (e.g., "Beast Academy is appropriate starting grade 2; AoPS Prealgebra when Beast Academy Level 5 is complete, typically grades 5-6") would fully answer RO2's "when is it too early" must_answer item.

2. **The "USAMTS" wikilink is to `[[entities/usamts|USAMTS]]` in two places but USAMTS does not appear to have a dedicated entity article in the staging directory.** This is technically a lint issue, but the consequence is that a parent following this link will find nothing. The recommendation to "Use proof-based competitions early" loses its actionability if the reader cannot learn more. The wiki-compiler-agent should verify whether a USAMTS entity article exists or is needed.

## Graduation Decision: NEEDS-REVISION

The missing Common Mistakes section is a must-fix. The article is otherwise clean and requires only additive work.

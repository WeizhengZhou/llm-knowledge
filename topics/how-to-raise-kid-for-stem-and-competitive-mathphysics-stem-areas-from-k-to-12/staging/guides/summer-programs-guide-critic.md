# Critic Report — Summer Math Programs Comparison Guide — 2026-04-10

## Overall Assessment

This guide serves RO4 well. Cost data, acceptance rates, dates, and program-fit guidance are present. The "How to Choose" decision table is genuinely useful. The Common Mistakes section is almost entirely non-obvious and specific. One must_answer item from RO4 is missing: the article does not address "when does a student need a private coach vs. self-study vs. group classes," which RO4 explicitly requires. L4 synthesis is absent despite community data existing — though the fact-sheet has no L4 claims tied specifically to summer program experiences, so this absence is defensible.

## Must-Fix Before wiki/ Graduation

1. **RO4 must_answer item "when does a student need a private coach vs. self-study vs. group classes" is unaddressed in this article and not clearly covered in any wikilinked article.** The "How to Choose" table covers program types but does not address the coaching vs. self-study question. The `enrichment-programs-guide.md` has a section on private coaching but it is framed as program comparison context, not as a decision framework. At minimum, this article should add one sentence pointing readers to the `enrichment-programs-guide.md` section on private coaching, or the `enrichment-programs-guide.md` needs to explicitly answer this question. The wiki-compiler-agent should verify that `enrichment-programs-guide.md` fully covers this must_answer item and add a wikilink note here if it does.

## Should-Fix

1. **HCSSiM has no "Who It Is For" section while all other programs do.** The HCSSiM subsection describes format and application but does not tell the reader what type of student it suits vs. Ross or Mathcamp. Adding "Best for: students who want intensive immersion similar to Ross/PROMYS but prefer a broader initial topic range and 6-days-a-week schedule" would match the depth of other program profiles and help the parent differentiation decision.

2. **Application fee absence for Mathcamp is noted, but the "Step 2: Apply Early" section does not mention which programs have application fees.** This matters for budget-constrained families. A parenthetical noting "Mathcamp has no application fee; most other programs charge $0-$50" would make the cost information more complete.

3. **The AwesomeMath row in the comparison table says "Open enrollment" under Acceptance Rate, but the prerequisites section says "AMC 10 score of 100+ or AIME qualification" for most programs.** A parent reading only the table might not understand that AwesomeMath is a different tier. Adding a footnote or brief differentiation note to the AwesomeMath row — "No admission threshold; self-paced placement" — would prevent misreading of the table.

## Graduation Decision: NEEDS-REVISION

The must-fix item (unaddressed RO4 coaching vs. self-study question) requires either a direct answer here or verification that `enrichment-programs-guide.md` covers it with a cross-link added. The revision is minor.

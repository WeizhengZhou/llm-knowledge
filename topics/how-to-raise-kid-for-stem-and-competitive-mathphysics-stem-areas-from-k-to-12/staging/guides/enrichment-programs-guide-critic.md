## Should-Fix

1. **RSM profile is thinner than all other programs due to L4-only sourcing.** The RSM section contains two L4 community observations and no official source data (no website, no tuition, no enrollment size). Every other program in the guide has official source attributes. The result is that RSM appears in the "Comparison: When to Use What" table as a recommendation ("Wanting structured in-person classes → RSM") without enough information for a parent to act on that recommendation. At minimum, add the RSM website and a note that pricing and locations vary by franchise. If no official source data was available, add a note: "RSM operates as a franchise; contact a local center for pricing and schedule details."

2. **Pricing data scattered across article and cost guide creates maintenance fragility.** The article quotes AoPS course prices ($415-$895/course), Beast Academy ($118/level), and Kumon ($150-$200/month). The same figures appear in `cost-and-financial-aid.md`. Recommend removing price quotes from this article and replacing with "see [[cost-and-financial-aid]] for current pricing" — especially for Kumon, whose range is location-dependent and will become stale.

3. **OTIS entry lacks an application/access note.** Evan Chen's OTIS is described as targeting AIME and USAMO level students but the article does not say how to apply, how selective it is, or what it costs. Parents at the AIME+ level for whom OTIS is the recommended program cannot act on this recommendation. Adding "Application required; see web.evanchen.cc for current cycle details" would make this entry actionable.

## Graduation Decision: READY

No must-fix items. Three should-fix items are improvements but do not block graduation.

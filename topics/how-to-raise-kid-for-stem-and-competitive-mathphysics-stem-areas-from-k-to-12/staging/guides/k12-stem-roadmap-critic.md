## Must-Fix Before wiki/ Graduation

1. **RO2 must_answer item "How do I foster mathematical thinking without drilling or pressure?" is unanswered.** The K-2 "Parent Role" section gives three bullet points ("Provide access to materials; let the child lead," "Do NOT assign extra math homework," "Celebrate curiosity") that are direction-correct but not instructive. A parent reading this cannot translate these into specific activities. The section needs 3-4 concrete examples of what non-drilling mathematical thinking looks like: e.g., "count steps during walks," "ask 'how many ways can you arrange these blocks?'," "play Set or Blokus," "read _Bedtime Math_." These are the specific behaviors RO2 P1 parents need.

## Should-Fix

1. **Common Mistakes section needs two replacements.** "Starting formal competition prep too early (before grade 5)" should be replaced with a specific mistake: e.g., "Enrolling a grade-3 student in MATHCOUNTS-style training when they haven't completed Beast Academy — leading to frustration with multi-step problems they lack the foundations for." "Treating the roadmap as mandatory" should either be dropped or replaced with a concrete failure mode: e.g., "Forcing a student to do AMC 10 in 8th grade because the roadmap says grade 8 is the milestone, when the student has not completed the AoPS Introduction series."

2. **RO3 competition differentiation is implicit.** The tables show all four competitions (MATHCOUNTS, AMC 8, Math Kangaroo, MOEMS) at the 3-5 and 6-8 grade bands, but the article does not distinguish what each measures or which to prioritize. A P2 parent (grades 3-8) cannot decide which competition to enter first. Two sentences differentiating them — e.g., "MOEMS is team-based and lower-pressure than AMC 8; Math Kangaroo is the lowest-commitment first exposure" — would answer the must_answer item.

3. **D5 physics paragraph.** The four-word "Physics Pipeline" subsection in the 9-12 grade band adds almost nothing beyond the wikilink. Collapse to: "Students typically start F=ma preparation in grade 10 after completing algebra-based physics. See [[physics-competitions-pathway]] for the full pipeline."

## Graduation Decision: NEEDS-REVISION

One must-fix item (unfostered mathematical thinking guidance). Common Mistakes improvement is a should-fix. The structural revision required is additive and modest.

## Must-Fix Before wiki/ Graduation

1. **RO8 must_answer item "What is the opportunity cost of heavy competition focus vs. broader extracurriculars?" is completely absent.** RO8 is explicitly about P4 parents "weighing whether competitive STEM is right for their child at all." The financial cost is only one dimension; the opportunity cost in time, social activities, non-STEM exploration, and college application breadth is what P4 parents are actually weighing. A section addressing: "At AIME level (8-15 hrs/week), a student typically cannot maintain a varsity sport, and college applications show a narrower extracurricular profile" — even with appropriate caveats about individual variation — directly enables the RO8 decision in a way the current article does not. This section can draw on the time commitment estimates from c111 (with their required caveats) and can reference the burnout article for the personal development cost.

## Should-Fix

1. **The fourth Common Mistakes entry is generic.** "Equating spending with outcomes" is a platitude. Replace with: "Paying $895 for WOOT (AoPS Worldwide Online Olympiad Training) before reaching AIME — WOOT requires AIME-level preparation to benefit from; students below that threshold do not get value from the investment and may become discouraged."

2. **The "Competition Registration" cost section could note that travel costs for Nationals are not trivial.** The section mentions "$500-$2,000/year for serious competitors" for travel to state and national competitions, but this appears as a single sentence after the table. For MATHCOUNTS National, the top 4 per state travel is fully covered. For AMC/AIME, there is no travel involved. The ambiguity about which competitions involve travel costs should be resolved.

3. **Financial aid application timing is not addressed.** The article tells families to "apply regardless of income" but does not say when financial aid applications are due or how to apply. Adding "Financial aid applications are typically due with program applications (January-March for most summer programs; December for RSI)" would make Step 3 actionable.

## Graduation Decision: NEEDS-REVISION

One must-fix: the opportunity cost dimension is entirely absent despite being an explicit RO8 must_answer item. The revision requires one substantive section and is additive.

## Should-Fix

1. **AIME "one per cycle" rule could be stated more prominently.** Currently it appears as a "Key rule:" callout in the AIME section body. A parent who skims the article looking at headings only will miss this. Moving it to the Common Mistakes section as "Attempting AIME II when you already took AIME I — students may take the AIME only once per cycle; taking both A-date and B-date AMC tests does not give two AIME attempts" would make this important rule impossible to miss and align with the article's otherwise excellent Common Mistakes section.

2. **Historical cutoffs note could clarify usability.** The article provides historical cutoffs under the "old formula" (AIME x 10 + AMC) but does not tell the reader these numbers are no longer applicable for planning purposes. Adding one sentence — "These historical figures are not directly comparable to 2025-2026 cycle targets, which use the new formula (AIME x 20 + AMC)" — would prevent a parent from misusing old cutoff data.

3. **The AMC 8 section lacks an AIME/pipeline connection sentence.** A parent reading Stage 1 does not know whether AMC 8 score affects AIME eligibility. One sentence clarifying "AMC 8 scores do not qualify for AIME; it is a standalone entry-level competition used to prepare for AMC 10/12" would prevent a common misunderstanding and complete the pipeline story from the very first stage.

## Graduation Decision: READY

No must-fix items. Three should-fix items are minor improvements that can be made opportunistically.

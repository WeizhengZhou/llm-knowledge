## Must-Fix Before wiki/ Graduation

1. **"Book Progression for Physics Competitions" section duplicates `book-resources-by-level.md` content.** The section spans lines 79-95 and reproduces F=ma, USAPhO, and IPhO book lists with no additional context beyond what `book-resources-by-level.md` already provides. This creates a maintenance problem (two places to update if books change) and violates scope discipline. Replace the entire section with: "For the full book progression from F=ma through IPhO level, see [[book-resources-by-level]]. The single most important F=ma preparation book is *Problems and Solutions in Introductory Mechanics* (David Morin)."

## Should-Fix

1. **USAPhO advancement to Training Camp is opaque.** The funnel table shows ~400 USAPhO participants advance from F=ma and ~20 advance to Training Camp, but the article does not explain what the selection criterion is for Training Camp — is it a score cutoff, medal level, or invitation? A parent of a USAPhO qualifier needs to know whether Gold medal = Training Camp, or whether it is more selective than that. One sentence stating the selection basis would resolve this.

2. **Eligibility restriction for USAPhO deserves earlier placement.** The citizenship/permanent residency requirement for USAPhO appears in Stage 1 (F=ma section) but only as a subordinate sentence after the general eligibility paragraph. International students at US schools who can take F=ma but cannot advance to USAPhO would benefit from a clearer callout. The prerequisite section is the right place for this note.

3. **Physics Bowl is mentioned only in "Other Physics Competitions" and in Common Mistakes but not in the Steps section.** The Steps section says "Take the Physics Bowl for practice" in Step 1 but Physics Bowl is not explained until Stage 4 "Other Physics Competitions." Reversing the order — introducing Physics Bowl in Stage 1/Step 1 — would make the steps self-contained.

## Graduation Decision: NEEDS-REVISION

The duplicate book section is a must-fix scope violation. The revision is simple: remove the "Book Progression" section and add a one-sentence redirect wikilink.

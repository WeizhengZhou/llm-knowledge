## Parent Notes

- K-Pop dance is a growing trend among Asian-American youth in the Bay Area; many studios specifically market to this demographic
- K-Pop classes tend to emphasize choreography replication (covering idol group dances) rather than improvisation or technique-building
- Hip-hop street styles at studios like In the Groove include authentic house, footwork, and vogue — distinct from commercial hip-hop taught at competitive studios
- EDS Dance hosts competitive K-Pop random dance events and showcases where students can perform

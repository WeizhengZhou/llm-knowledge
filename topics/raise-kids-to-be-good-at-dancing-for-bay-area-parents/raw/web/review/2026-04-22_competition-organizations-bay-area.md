# Dance Competition Organizations Operating in the Bay Area

## California Dance Classics (CDC)

**Focus:** California's premier youth ballet & contemporary competition
**What it includes:** Master classes, scholarships, expert feedback

**Bay Area 2026 Schedule:**
- March 25-29, 2026: Mountain View Center for the Performing Arts
- Ballet and contemporary styles only (not commercial/jazz/hip-hop)
- Entry open to studios across California and beyond

**Los Angeles 2026:**
- March 4-8, 2026: Downey Theatre

**Key features:**
- Scholarship opportunities for young dancers
- Expert adjudicator feedback
- Classical-focused (distinguishes it from commercial competitions)
- Appropriate for pre-professional track students

## NYCDA (New York City Dance Alliance)

**Focus:** Full range of dance styles (jazz, contemporary, hip hop, lyrical, tap, musical theater, ballet)
**Type:** Regional competition series leading to national finals

**Bay Area Location:** Santa Clara regional competition
**Season:** 2025-2026 season

**How it works:**
- Studios compete routines throughout the season
- Routines performed at regional competition
- Top-scoring routines advance toward nationals
- Age divisions: Mini (ages 3-8), Junior (ages 9-11), Teen (ages 12-14), Senior (ages 15-19)
- Competition categories: Group, Small Group, Duo/Trio, Solo

**Scholarship Opportunities:** NYCDA Foundation offers scholarships at nationals

## How Competitions Are Structured (general format)

**Age Divisions:** Mini (under 8) through Senior (15+), sometimes Elite/Senior Elite
**Style Categories:** Ballet, Tap, Jazz, Lyrical, Contemporary, Hip Hop, Musical Theater, Acro, Open
**Competition Formats:**
- **Solo:** One dancer performing alone (highest cost; most prestigious)
- **Duo/Trio:** 2-3 dancers
- **Small Group:** 4-9 dancers
- **Large Group:** 10-14 dancers
- **Line/Production:** 15+ dancers

**Scoring:** Usually judged 1-10 or 1-100 scale; rankings within age and style categories
**Awards:** Typically: Platinum/Diamond/Gold/Silver at high-score end; Overall titles for highest scores

## The Competition Calendar (typical annual structure)

**September-October:** Season begins; studios form competition teams; choreography and costume planning
**November-January:** Intensive rehearsals; local/regional competitions may begin
**February-April:** Peak regional competition season (most Bay Area competitions in this window)
**May-June:** National competitions (e.g., NYCDA Nationals, Starpower Nationals)
**July-August:** Summer conventions; off-season

Note: Families considering competitive dance should understand that the competition calendar heavily impacts weekends from November through June. Missed competitions are typically not refunded.

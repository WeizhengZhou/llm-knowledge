# Dance Competitions Explained: Everything You Want to Know

Source: danceparent101.com — dedicated parent resource site for competitive dance families

## How Dance Competitions Work

Dance competitions are events where "individuals and groups of dancers from all different studios come together for the purpose of showcasing their routines." Events typically span 1-4 days, most running Friday afternoon through Sunday evening at auditoriums, theaters, or hotel ballrooms.

Routines perform before three qualified judges who provide numerical scores and written feedback. Every participant receives an award trophy based on their adjudication score, plus additional recognitions for top placements.

## Scoring System

Judges evaluate routines out of 100 points each. Three judges combined = 300 total. Typical scoring categories:

- Technique (40 points)
- Performance (30 points)
- Choreography (15 points)
- Presentation (10 points)
- Overall Impression (5 points)

Competition-specific software flags suspicious scoring patterns.

## Competition Categories

**Age Divisions:**
- Petite: 8 & Under
- Junior: 9-11
- Teen: 12-14
- Senior: 15-19
- Adult: 20+

**Dance Styles:** Ballet, tap, jazz, contemporary, lyrical, hip-hop, acro, modern, and more

**Group Size:** Solo, duo/trio, small group, large group, line, production

**Skill Levels:**
- Novice/Primary (beginner)
- Intermediate (mid-level)
- Advanced/Pre-Professional (highest)

Level placement depends on technical skill, competition experience, and weekly training hours.

## Types of Competitions

**Regional Competitions:** January-June across cities, primarily local dancers

**National Competitions:** Summer events; now sometimes primarily local entrants despite "national" label

**Title Category:** Separate soloists competition for titles like "Miss Dance of the Year"

**Convention Competitions:** Attached to conventions (NYC Dance Alliance), highly competitive, level-free

## Cost Overview

Competitive dance requires significant financial investment. Budget for:
- Entry fees
- Costumes
- Travel and accommodations for multi-day events
- (Specific pricing varies by competition and routine count)

Some competitions offer cash prizes; many now provide credits toward future entries.

## Key Notes for New Parents

- Competition season pauses around Easter and Mother's Day weekends
- Dancers ages 3+ can participate; upper age limits rare
- Some competitions accept independent entries; others require studio enrollment
- Level placement is studio-determined; competitions may adjust mis-leveled routines
- No overarching governing body exists across competitions

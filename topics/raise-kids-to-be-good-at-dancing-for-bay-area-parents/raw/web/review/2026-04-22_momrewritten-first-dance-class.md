# First Dance Class: Complete Guide for Parents

## Class Structure
A typical dance class includes:
- Warm-ups and stretching
- Center work or barre exercises
- Across-the-floor movements like leaps and turns
- Skill instruction broken into manageable steps
- Musicality and free movement
- Learning dance routines

The teacher links individual steps into sequences set to music.

## Dress Code & What to Bring

**Clothing:**
- Leotard or unitard in breathable fabric
- Dance tights (without underpants)
- Optional short skirt or lightweight sweater
- Hair pulled back with elastics and bobby pins
- Avoid princess dresses, jeans, or clothing covering hands/feet

**Footwear & Essentials:**
- Properly fitted dance shoes (ballet, jazz, or tap as required)
- Regular shoes for travel to class
- Labeled water bottle
- Hair brush and extra elastics
- Thin socks if not wearing tights

## Parent Behavior Guidelines

Key advice: "Leave the teaching to the teacher" — avoid correcting your child during class. Parents should step in if misbehavior occurs. Most studios don't permit parents in the classroom to minimize distractions.

## Preparation Tips

- Visit a class before your child's first session so they know what to expect
- Discuss that class isn't playtime—encourage focus and participation
- Ensure bathroom use before class begins
- Prepare children for parent separation if classes are drop-off only
- Explain that teacher corrections help children learn and improve
- Bring a fed, happy, and rested child to class

## Readiness Indicators

Children should be able to:
- Follow instructions
- Wait their turn
- Work independently
- Cooperate with teachers
- Participate without parental presence

## Separation Anxiety

Especially for toddlers and preschoolers, separation anxiety can happen. Teachers are experienced at gently guiding children through this. Most kids are smiling by the end of the first class.

# Dance Styles for Children: Benefits & Characteristics — Cole Academy of Dance

## Ballet
**Key Benefits:** Develops "impeccable posture, balance, and poise" while instilling discipline and concentration. Emphasizes flexibility, strength, and body awareness. Children gain self-confidence through mastering structured movements.
**What It Teaches:** Grace, discipline, and focus that extends beyond the studio into academic and personal pursuits.
**Foundation role:** Often considered the foundation of all dance training.

## Tap Dancing
**Key Benefits:** "Fosters creativity in children" by engaging their brains uniquely while "improving coordination and motor skills." The rhythmic nature enhances cognitive development.
**What It Teaches:** Self-expression through sound and movement, allowing young dancers to convey emotions and stories via footwork patterns.

## Jazz Dance
**Key Benefits:** Encourages embracing individuality and freely expressing oneself. Exposes children to diverse movement vocabulary through mixed traditional and contemporary approaches.
**What It Teaches:** Improvisation skills that help children "think on their feet, adapt to changes, and creatively solve problems."
**Balance:** Strikes balance between the discipline of ballet and the energy of hip-hop.

## Hip-Hop
**Key Benefits:** Helps channel boundless youthful energy positively while developing "a strong sense of timing and musicality." Fosters rhythm and coordination.
**What It Teaches:** Self-expression by "communicating feelings, experiences, and stories through dynamic movements" using modern, urban-inspired choreography.

## Contemporary
**What It Teaches:** Freedom of expression, improvisation, innovation and creative exploration of movement. Uses improvisation games to inspire the student's own creative movement.
**Origin:** Emerged in the mid-20th century; combines jazz and ballet elements.

## Cross-Style Benefits (All Dance Forms)
- Teamwork and cooperation
- Confidence
- Perseverance
- Goal-setting abilities
- Social skills through group performances and activities

# Choosing Between Vaganova, RAD or Cecchetti: Why It's Not as Important as You Think

Source: balancedballerinas.com

## Key Finding for Parents

The instructor matters far more than the methodology. As stated in the article: "More important than the specific style of ballet you're learning is the quality of the teacher guiding your journey." Prestigious ballet companies and pre-professional programs don't prioritize which methodology students learned. Success depends on "natural ability, aesthetic, features and work ethic"—not syllabus choice.

## Key Methodologies

**Russian (Vaganova)**
- Emphasizes "fluidity of movement, musicality, and expressive port de bras"
- Described as "big, bold, elongated and dramatic"
- Features strong back mobility, flexibility, and expressive head/facial positioning
- Known for "impeccable technique, breathtaking jumps, and emotive performances"

**English (RAD - Royal Academy of Dance)**
- A "blend of everything—Vaganova, Bournonville, Cecchetti, French, Italian"
- Offers "free movement so students can be incredibly musical"
- Provides both recreational and vocational pathways
- Aims for "strong, healthy dancers without losing discipline and excellence"
- Structured with graded exams: Pre-Primary through Grade 8, then vocational tiers up to Advanced 2
- Examiners travel to participating studios to assess students

**French/Italian (Cecchetti/ISTD)**
- Emphasizes "lightness, fluidity, and refinement"
- Features "poised and elegant" movement
- Focuses on "precise, intricate footwork"
- Via ISTD: structured grades evaluated and certified by accredited assessors
- Has standardized curriculum with specific requirements for each grade/level

**American (Balanchine)**
- Features "speed and precision, expansive use of space"
- Known for "intricate footwork, daring lifts and rapid directional changes"
- Distinctive hand shapes and wrist positioning
- Associated with New York City Ballet style

## Implications for Parent Studio Selection

- Methodology should not be the primary factor in studio selection
- Teacher quality, studio environment, and student fit matter more
- RAD and Cecchetti offer external exam certifications that provide benchmarks
- Vaganova does not use external exams; progress measured internally
- Students transitioning between systems may need to relearn technical elements

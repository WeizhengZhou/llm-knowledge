# Summer Dance Intensives Available to Bay Area Youth (2025-2026)

## Bay Area Programs

### San Francisco Ballet School — Summer Programs
- Source: sfballet.org/the-school/summer-programs/
- Offerings: Summer session with audition-based placements
- Summer Auditions/Audition Tour: National touring auditions for summer intensive placement
- Directed at intermediate to advanced students
- Contact school directly for 2026 dates and fees

### Bay Area Ballet Conservatory (BABC) — Summer Dance Intensive
- Source: bayareabc.com/summer-dance-intensive
- **Eligibility:** Intermediate through advanced level dancers accustomed to minimum 3 ballet classes/week
- **Audition required** for visiting or new students to ensure readiness
- **Daily curriculum:** Ballet, contemporary, and pointe/pre-pointe every day
- **Format:** Both ballet and contemporary dance training combined

### New Ballet (San Jose) — Summer Intensive
- Source: newballet.com/summer-intensive
- Designed for New Ballet students placed in Levels 1B and up for 2025-26 school year
- Also open to dedicated ballet students ages 10-23 from other studios
- **Scholarship auditions:** Bay area dates to be announced; easy video audition option for scholarships
- Email info@newballet.com for requirements or to schedule zoom/in-person audition

### AileyCamp Berkeley/Oakland
- Source: calperformances.org/community/aileycamp/
- **Format:** Joyful and rigorous six-week program
- **Cost: FREE** (selected youth only)
- **Ages:** 11-14
- **Summer 2026 dates:** June 15–July 24, 2026, 8:30am–3:30pm daily
- **Curriculum:** African dance, Ballet, Jazz, Modern dance, Creative Communication, Personal Development; field trips, guest workshops, special events
- **Host:** Cal Performances, Berkeley/Oakland
- **How to apply:** Contact Cal Performances community programs
- **Note:** Highly competitive free program; limited spots

### ODC Summer Programs
- Source: odc.dance/summer
- Summer weekend dance classes: June 6-July 27, 2026
- Eight-week session
- Wide variety of dance styles and levels
- Ages 2-18
- Scholarship application deadline: May 29, 2026

## National Programs with Bay Area Auditions

### San Francisco Ballet School — Summer Audition Tour
- Source: sfballet.org/the-school/summer-programs/summer-auditions-audition-tour/
- Touring auditions at multiple cities nationally
- Students accepted gain access to summer intensive at SF Ballet's facilities
- Prestigious pipeline program for serious ballet students nationally and internationally

### School of American Ballet (SAB) — Summer Intensive
- Source: sab.org/summer-auditions-admission/
- National and international auditions; one of the most prestigious summer programs
- Bay Area students can audition at regional auditions
- Highly competitive; for advanced students with professional aspirations

### ABT Summer Intensives
- Source: abt.org/training/dancer-training/summer-programs/summer-intensives/
- National locations; regional audition sites include California
- Part of ABT National Training Curriculum system
- New Ballet (San Jose) as ABT Certified School can help students prepare for ABT auditions

## How to Evaluate a Summer Intensive

Key questions to ask:
1. What level of experience is required? (Beginner programs vs. audition-based)
2. How many hours per day? (Intensives range from 4 to 8+ hours)
3. What styles are included?
4. Are scholarships available and when do applications open?
5. What is the teacher-to-student ratio?
6. Are there performance opportunities at the end?
7. Does the intensive lead to any year-round program invitations?

## Timing: When to Apply

Most summer intensive applications open January-February for programs running June-August. Scholarship deadlines often 1-2 months earlier. AileyCamp and free programs have the most competitive admissions — apply as early as possible.

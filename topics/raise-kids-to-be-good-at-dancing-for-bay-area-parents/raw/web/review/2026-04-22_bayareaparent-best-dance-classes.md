# Best Dance Classes in the Bay Area — Bay Area Parent Magazine (2025 Best of the Best Reader Poll)

Published: July 29, 2025

These businesses were voted the Best Dance Classes in the Bay Area in Bay Area Parent's 2025 Best of the Best reader poll.

## EAST BAY

**Gold: Mr. D's Music Club**
All about creativity without pressure, this center features dancing, singing, acting and art after school programs and camps. Oakland.
Website: mrdsmusicclub.com

**Silver: B*Dazzled Dancers**
Two locations in San Leandro.
Website: bdazzleddancers.com

**Bronze: In Motion Dance Center**
Martinez.
Website: inmotiondancer.com

## PENINSULA

**Gold: San Carlos Children's Theater**
Offers dance classes for toddlers to teens, as well as specialized workshops and full-scale productions. San Carlos.
Website: sancarloschildrenstheater.com

**Silver: Principal Creative & Performing Arts**
San Mateo.
Website: principalarts.com

**Bronze: Broadway Babies & Kids**
Belmont and Foster City.
Website: broadwaybabiesandkids.com

## SAN FRANCISCO/MARIN

**Gold: ODC School Youth & Teen Program**
Students ages 2-18 enjoy dance technique classes and performance opportunities in a supportive and body-positive environment. San Francisco.
Website: odc.dance

**Silver: Love 2 Dance**
Novato.
Website: love2dance.biz

**Bronze: San Francisco Youth Ballet Academy**
San Francisco.
Website: sanfranciscoyouthballet.org

## SILICON VALLEY

**Gold: West Valley Dance Company**
This dance company has been in operation for over 35 years, offering classes for children aged 18 months and above in ballet, contemporary dance, hip-hop, jazz, musical theater, tap and more. Multiple locations in San Jose and Mountain View.
Website: westvalleydanceco.com

**Silver: San Jose Dance Theatre**
San Jose.
Website: sjdt.org

**Bronze: Dance Academy USA**
Cupertino.
Website: danceacademyusa.com

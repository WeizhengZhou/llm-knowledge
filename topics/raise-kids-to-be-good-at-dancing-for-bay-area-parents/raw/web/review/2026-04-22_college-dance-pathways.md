## Juilliard Dance Program Details

**Source:** Juilliard.edu official admissions page

**Program:** Bachelor of Fine Arts (BFA) in Dance
**Annual enrollment:** Approximately 20–24 students per year
**Techniques taught:** Ballet, modern, and contemporary

**Requirements:**
- Minimum 3 years experience in both ballet and modern dance vocabulary
- Currently training minimum 10 hours per week
- Prescreening video submission → invitation to in-person audition in New York

**Juilliard Summer Dance Intensive:**
- Three-week program for high school dancers
- Designed to introduce dancers to Juilliard and college dance programs
- Opportunity to meet faculty and Dance Division leadership one-on-one
- Strong pipeline tool for Juilliard applicants

## What Bay Area Pre-Professional Training Contributes

Students from SF Ballet School, New Ballet San Jose, ODC, and similar Bay Area institutions typically enter college dance auditions with:
- Strong technical foundation (especially for SF Ballet School alumni: classical ballet at a professional level)
- Performance experience (Nutcracker, spring shows)
- Summer intensive attendance at nationally recognized programs
- YAGP (Youth America Grand Prix) competition experience — relevant for ballet-track students

**SF Ballet School college pipeline:** Per SF Ballet School materials, 70%+ of SF Ballet company members are alumni of the school. Students who do not enter a company often attend college dance programs; the school's alumni placement is nationally respected.

## Key Timeline for College Dance Applications

- **Junior year (11th grade):** Begin researching programs; attend summer intensives affiliated with college programs (Juilliard Summer Intensive, USC summer programs)
- **Senior year fall:** Submit prescreening videos for selective programs (Oct–Dec)
- **Senior year winter:** In-person auditions (Jan–Mar)
- **Dance resume building:** Competition experience, summer intensive attendance, varied training history, teacher recommendation letters from recognized faculty

---

## Scholarship Pathways

- **YAGP (Youth America Grand Prix):** Competition designed specifically to create scholarship pathways to pre-professional and college programs worldwide
- **Princess Grace Awards:** Grants for emerging dance artists (college and post-college)
- **NYCDA scholarships:** Competition scholarships for college dance programs
- **Direct merit scholarships:** Most private university dance programs offer merit-based scholarships at audition; public university systems (UC, CSU) less common for dance-specific merit aid

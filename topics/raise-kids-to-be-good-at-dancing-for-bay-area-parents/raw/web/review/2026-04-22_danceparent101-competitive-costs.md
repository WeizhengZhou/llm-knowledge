# Competitive Dance Costs: Annual Breakdown for One Family

## Major Cost Categories

**Academy/Technique Classes (Monthly Tuition)**
- Southern California example: $280/month for 7 classes weekly (September-June)
- Summer session: $380 for 5 classes weekly over 7 weeks

**Competition Dances & Entry Fees**
One family's annual breakdown for five dances:
- Studio production: $725
- Large group: $950
- Small group: $1,050
- Duo/trio: $1,270 (costume not included)
- Solo: $2,250 (costume not included)
- Team fee: $150
- **Subtotal: $6,395** (5% upfront discount savings: ~$270)

**Dance Conventions**
- Master classes and competitions: $200-$275 for two days

**Master Classes & Private Lessons**
- Master classes: approximately $20 for 60-90 minutes
- Private lessons: $45 for 30 minutes; semi-private: ~$22.50

**Competition Costumes**
- Solo/duo/trio costumes: $100-$500 each
- Group costumes: often included in dance package pricing

**Hidden & Miscellaneous Costs**
- Competition bag (Dream Duffel): $50-$300+
- Garment bags: $73
- Makeup requirements: ~$60
- False eyelashes: $1.25-$15 for multiple sets
- Team spirit wear jacket: $70
- Dance shoes (annual): $23-$135+ per type
- Dancewear (various): $40+ per item

**Audition Fees**
- Audition workshops: $50-$200
- Audition fees: $20-$200

**Recital Costumes**
- $70 per costume (separate from competition costumes)

**Travel Expenses**
- Nationals hotel/meals: minimum $150/night plus food
- Weekend competition meals: ~$100+ per competition season

## Overall Annual Investment

"The cost of Competitive dance in the USA easily starts with prices in the $1,500 range and can far exceed $10,000 annually." This represents competition-specific expenses only, excluding regular academy tuition.

More commonly cited range: $8,000–$20,000+ per season for a mid-level to serious competitive dancer, depending on training level, number of competitions, costumes, and travel to nationals.

## Second Source: Second Stage Dance — True Cost Article
Source: https://secondstagedance.org/true-cost-of-competitive-dance/

For a mid-level regional competitor with 2–3 routines, regular regional travel, and 1 private lesson per month, rough monthly average is $300–700 (above and beyond base tuition).

Full annual estimate breakdown:
- Base tuition (technique classes): $2,000-$5,000/year
- Competition fees (entry, costumes, team fees): $3,000-$8,000/year  
- Travel and accommodations: $1,500-$7,500 per competition season
- Conventions and workshops: $500-$2,000/year
- Private lessons: $500-$2,000/year
- Miscellaneous (makeup, gear, spirit wear): $300-$800/year

**Grand total range: $7,800 to $25,300+ annually**

From ages 3 to 18, a competitive dancer could easily rack up over $100,000 in total expenses.

# Dance Studio Roadmap: How to Choose the Right Studio for Your Dancer

Source: thebrainyballerina.com — dance education parent resource blog

## Studio Categories (Match to Family Goals)

- **Competition studio:** 4-6 competitions yearly, possible contracts
- **Recreational studio:** Multiple classes, 1-2 showcases annually
- **Curriculum-based studio:** Progressive training with performance opportunities
- **Pre-professional studio:** 4-6 days/week with audition guidance

## Green Flags (What to Look For)

**Teacher Qualifications**
- College/graduate degree in dance or education
- Professional dance background with certifications
- Mentorship opportunities balanced with experienced staff

**Quality Training Standards**
- Student-to-teacher ratio of 10-12:1 is a good sign
- Age-appropriate class lengths: 30-45 minutes (young children), 60-90 minutes (advanced)
- Minimum 2-3 ballet classes weekly for intermediate/advanced students

**Wellness & Safety**
- Injury prevention policies and workshops
- Staff certified in dancer wellness programs
- Collaboration with Dance Physical Therapists
- Standardized pointe readiness criteria

**Inclusive Environment**
- Welcoming atmosphere within 30 seconds of entry
- Inclusive dress codes and employment practices
- Role models promoting healthy mindsets

**Well-Rounded Education**
- Regular performance opportunities
- Multiple dance styles offered
- Encouragement to attend summer intensives

## Red Flags (Warning Signs)

**Inadequate Training**
- Insufficient technique classes relative to rehearsal hours
- Dancers on pointe without 4+ years training
- Lack of 2-3 weekly 90-minute technique + pointe classes

**Safety Concerns**
- Overstretching practices or extended hyperextended poses (oversplits, back bends)
- Strength training without proper alignment focus
- Pointe work without meeting safety criteria

**Problematic Culture**
- Exclusive atmosphere or bullying tolerance
- Contracts preventing training elsewhere
- Studio prioritizes business interests over student success

**Unhealthy Mindsets**
- Perfectionism culture without balance
- Discouraging listening to one's body
- Pressure to dance through injuries
- Body shaming in any form

## Key Questions to Ask

1. What are instructor credentials and experience?
2. What's the student-to-teacher ratio?
3. What are class lengths and weekly frequency?
4. What pointe readiness criteria do you use?
5. How do you support injury prevention?
6. Can dancers train at other studios?
7. What performance opportunities exist?

## Core Principle

"Is the studio a tool for my dancer's success or is my dancer a tool for the studio's success?"

**Website:** https://healyirishdancers.com/
**Phone:** (415) 924-9100
**Location:** Larkspur, California (Marin County)
**Founded:** 1870 (also cited as 1902 in some sources for Bay Area operations); now in its **5th generation** of family operation

**Age range:** 4–15 for intro classes (competitive dancers continue beyond 15)
**Levels:** Beginner through competitive
**Tuition:** Not published on website; contact school directly at (415) 924-9100
**Registration:** Inquire via contact form on website; new sessions open every 8–10 weeks

### What Healy Offers

- Recreational Irish dance instruction for beginners
- Competitive Irish dance training
- Participation in Feiseanna (Irish dance competitions)
- Céilí dance (traditional social dance forms)

### About Feiseanna

Feiseanna are competitive Irish dance events. The United Irish Cultural Center (UICC) in San Francisco hosts several official Feiseanna throughout the year, in partnership with Comhaltas Ceoltóirí Éireann Cooley-Keegan Branch of San Francisco.

## Style Characteristics

Irish step dance is characterized by:
- Upright torso with arms held close to the body
- Intricate, rapid footwork
- Hard-shoe (jig, reel, hornpipe) and soft-shoe (light jig, slip jig, reel) forms
- Competitive structure with levels: Beginner → Novice → Prizewinner → Preliminary Champion → Open Champion
- Distinctive costume elements: elaborate embroidered dresses (girls) or kilts/dress pants (boys)

## Why Bay Area Parents Seek Irish Dance

- Strong Irish-American cultural community in San Francisco (historically significant Irish immigration)
- Combination of athleticism, discipline, and cultural heritage
- Active competitive circuit with Feiseanna as regular events
- No partner required (solo discipline)

## Cost Considerations

Tuition for Irish dance is not published publicly by Bay Area schools. Industry norms: competitive Irish dance involves significant costume costs (costumes can reach $500–$2,000+ for solo costumes at higher competitive levels). Competitive wigs are also required at advanced levels (additional cost).

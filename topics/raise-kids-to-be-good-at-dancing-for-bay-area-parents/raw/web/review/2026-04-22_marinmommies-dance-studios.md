# Ballet and Dance Studios for Kids in Marin County and San Francisco

Source: Marin Mommies (local Bay Area parent community resource)

## Studio Directory

**Marin Dance Theatre** — San Rafael & Corte Madera
- Ages: 18 months to 9+ years
- Programs: Early ballet, nurturing environment
- Contact: (415) 499-8891, info@mdt.org, www.mdt.org

**Marin Ballet** — San Rafael
- Ages: 3–18 years
- Programs: Pre-ballet through level nine across six studios in 20,000 sq ft facility
- Contact: (415) 453-6705, www.marinballet.org
- Note: "Your first class is free"

**Just Dance Academy** — San Rafael
- Ages: 4+ years
- Styles: Ballet, jazz, tap, contemporary
- Programs: Twinkle Toes program (ages 4–5), performances, competitions
- Contact: (415) 451-1734, justdance.biz

**Ballet Attitude Academy** — Greenbrae
- Programs: Classical ballet using Vaganova method, contemporary, character dance
- Founded by Svetlana Siplatova, former Mariinsky Theatre dancer
- Contact: balletattitude.org

**Love2Dance** — Novato
- Ages: 14 months and up
- Styles: Ballet, hip-hop, contemporary, jazz, break dancing
- Contact: (415) 898-3933, love2dance.biz
- Note: Silver winner, Bay Area Parent 2025 Best of the Best (SF/Marin category)

**Marin Conservatory of Dance** — Mill Valley
- Ages: 6+ years
- Programs: Classical ballet, Nutcracker performance
- Contact: (415) 326-5117, marinconservatoryofdance.org

**PAAM Arts (Performing Arts Academy of Marin)** — Mill Valley
- Ages: 3 through high school
- Styles: Ballet, Broadway, hip-hop, jazz, tap
- Contact: (415) 380-0887, www.paamarts.com

**Presidio Dance Theatre** — San Francisco
- Ages: 5–18 years
- Programs: 20+ years, "high-quality training in Vaganova style ballet, character and folk dance"
- Contact: presidiodance.org

**Rhythms Performing Arts** — Novato
- Ages: 3+ years
- Styles: Ballet, tap, jazz, hip-hop, Broadway
- Contact: (415) 898-9717, www.rhythmsperformingarts.com

**RoCo Dance** — Mill Valley
- Ages: 4+ years
- Styles: Hip-hop, ballet, modern, jazz, breaking
- 100+ classes weekly spanning all levels
- Contact: (415) 388-6786, www.rocodance.com

**San Francisco Ballet School** — San Francisco
- Ages: 2–7 years (youth entry programs)
- Programs: Pre-Ballet, Dance with Me classes (younger children)
- Full school extends to pre-professional training
- Contact: www.sfballet.org

**Splitz Dance Academy** — Novato
- Ages: 3+ years
- Programs: Tiny Toes through advanced ballet
- Contact: (415) 598-7732, www.splitzdance.com

**Stapleton School of the Performing Arts** — San Anselmo
- Ages: Early childhood through teens
- Programs: Creative movement, pre-ballet, full ballet curriculum, hip-hop, musical theater, Nutcracker performance
- Operating since 1988
- Contact: (415) 454-5759, www.stapletonschool.org

**Tutu School** — Larkspur, San Francisco, East Bay (multiple locations)
- Ages: 6 months to 8 years
- Programs: Tutu Toddler to Primary Ballet Prep
- Contact: (415) 380-0887, www.tutuschool.com

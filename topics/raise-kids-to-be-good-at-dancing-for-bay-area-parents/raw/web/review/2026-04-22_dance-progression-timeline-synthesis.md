# Children's Dance Progression Timeline: Beginner to Advanced

## Age-Based Dance Progression (Synthesis from Multiple Sources)

### Ages 18 months – 2 years: Parent-Child Classes
- "Mommy & Me" or "Wee Dance" programs
- Focus: rhythm, music exposure, basic motor skills
- Format: parent accompaniment required
- Example: Marin Ballet Wee Dance ($96 for 4 weeks)

### Ages 3-4: Creative Movement / Pre-Dance
- Independent creative movement classes (no parent required)
- Focus: body awareness, listening, imaginative play, group participation
- No formal technique; play-based learning
- Typical class length: 30-45 minutes

### Ages 5-6: Pre-Ballet / Dance Combo
- Introduction to formal structure
- Combo classes common (ballet + tap, ballet + jazz)
- Focus: basic positions, simple sequences, musicality
- Can start competitive training at some studios

### Ages 7-9: Beginning Technique
- Formal technique by style begins
- Skills: basic technique in chosen style, flexibility, strength development
- Ballet: barre work, center work, across-the-floor patterns
- In most cases, formal ballet training should not begin before age 6 (bone structure consideration)
- Competitive: ages 7-10 considered optimal start for competition

### Ages 10-12: Intermediate / Pre-Pointe
- More complex technique
- Ballet: pre-pointe readiness evaluation
- Transition to pointe typically around age 12 or Level 5 (after 4+ years of solid training)
- Multiple weekly classes become common

### Ages 12-14: Pointe Work / Advanced Technique
- Pointe work begins (for ballet students meeting criteria)
- Multiple days per week of training standard
- Competitive dancers may begin serious competition circuit
- Pre-professional programs become relevant for serious students

### Ages 14-18: Serious / Pre-Professional Track
- Most professionals began around age 8 and reached this level after 6-10 years
- Multiple days per week mandatory
- Summer intensives at regional/national programs
- Auditions for pre-professional companies or conservatories
- College dance program audition preparation

## Key Milestones

| Milestone | Typical Age Range |
|-----------|------------------|
| First dance class | 18 months – 4 years |
| Formal technique training | 5-7 years |
| Competitive dance entry | 5-10 years (varies by child) |
| Pre-pointe assessment | 9-11 years |
| Pointe work begins | 11-13 years (ballet only) |
| Summer intensive pursuit | 12-16 years |
| Pre-professional auditions | 14-18 years |

## Style-Specific Notes

- **Ballet:** Most structured progression; pointe is a key milestone; can begin formal training at 6-7
- **Hip-hop:** More flexible entry; less formal age structure; can start later and progress quickly
- **Contemporary/Jazz:** Often begins after ballet foundation; ages 8+ for serious study
- **Tap:** Can begin as early as 3-4 for rhythm development; progresses well alongside other styles

# Affordable, Scholarship-Supported, and Sliding-Scale Dance Programs in the Bay Area

## San Francisco Programs

### ODC School (Mission District, SF)
- Need-based scholarships for students ages 8+
- Criteria: financial need, motivation, commitment
- **Fall 2025 application deadline:** August 29, 2025
- **Summer 2026 application deadline:** May 29, 2026
- **Dance Jam Scholarship Fund:** Full year of tuition + participation fees + stipend for ancillary expenses (travel, dancewear)
- Apply: odcdance.wufoo.com/forms/z1qc2mjy0us3o3f/
- Contact: registration@odc.dance
- Note: ODC is body-positive, contemporary/hip-hop focused; not ballet track

### JCCSF Dance School (SF)
- Financial assistance available; apply separately
- 10% community membership discount
- Visit: jccsf.org/financial-assistance
- Ages 3-16; Ballet, Tap, Musical Theater, Combo

### Sambaxé (SF)
- Brazilian samba and dance forms
- **Sliding scale pricing:** Youth and Students $5; Adults & Families $10
- "No one turned away for lack of funds" policy
- Classes Fridays 5:30pm-7pm

### San Francisco Youth Ballet Academy
- Source: sanfranciscoyouthballet.org
- Tuition and calendar info available on website
- Financial assistance information listed on Policies page
- Note: Not SF Ballet School (different organization)

## East Bay Programs

### Shawl-Anderson Dance Center (Berkeley)
- Longstanding community center with accessible pricing
- Various class levels for all ages
- Known for inclusive philosophy
- Worth contacting directly for scholarship/sliding scale info

### Luna Dance & Creativity (Berkeley/Oakland)
- Community-focused studio
- Contact for financial assistance options

### AileyCamp Berkeley/Oakland (Cal Performances)
- **COMPLETELY FREE for accepted students**
- Ages 11-14 only
- Six weeks, full-day (8:30am-3:30pm daily)
- June 15-July 24, 2026
- Includes African dance, Ballet, Jazz, Modern, Creative Communication, Personal Development
- Highly competitive admission; limited spots
- Contact: Cal Performances community programs

- Need-based financial aid (FACTS platform)

- Applications open January 1; May 1 deadline for current families
- New family deadline: upon acceptance notification

### Bay Area Ballet Conservatory
- 501(c)(3) non-profit organization
- Contact directly for scholarship information

## How to Find More Scholarship Options

1. **Check the studio's financial assistance page** — most studios with non-profit status offer some form of aid
2. **Ask directly:** Many studios have undisclosed scholarship funds or payment plans; asking directly at intake is appropriate
3. **Apply early:** Most scholarship applications have deadlines 4-8 weeks before session start
4. **Cal Performances community programs:** Covers AileyCamp and other subsidized youth arts programming
5. **National scholarships:** NYCDA Foundation (for competitive dance) and Living Arts Dance Foundation offer scholarships
6. **Arts grants for youth:** Check local community foundations and arts councils for supplementary funding

## Cost Benchmark for "Affordable" Dance in Bay Area

Recreational dance at community centers or non-profit studios: approximately $80-$180/month for one class/week
Sliding scale or scholarship programs: $5-$150/month depending on income
Competitive dance: $500-$2,000/month all-in (not considered affordable without significant family budget)

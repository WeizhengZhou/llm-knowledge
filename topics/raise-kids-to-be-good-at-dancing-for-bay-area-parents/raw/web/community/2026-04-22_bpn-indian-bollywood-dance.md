## Thread: Bollywood Style Indian Dancing for 3 year old (July 2011)

**Original question:** Looking for Bollywood style dance class for a 3-year-old in Berkeley/Oakland area, ideally group class not one-on-one, for a half-Indian daughter to connect with heritage.

**Parent response (Sylvie):**

> "You should try Chhandam school of Kathak. They are fantastic and they have a branch in Berkeley that my daughter has attended for the past 2 years. She loves the teachers, the dance, the school and the community. There are kids (girls and boys!) from all backgrounds and they teach you dance but also singing, rhythm, math, philosophy and they really help the kids of Indian descent connect to their heritage. I believe classes start at age 4 but you can always email them/talk to the teachers. www.kathak.org look on the Chhandam school tab."

**Parent response (Monika):**

> "I've been doing a similar search as we recently moved to the area and also have a half Indian daughter who wants to do Indian dance... In my searches, I've found the Kalanjali School of Dance in Berkeley which I hope to call this week so here's the number if you'd like to try as well: (510) 526-2183. Also, I read that the YWCA of Berkeley/Oakland has Bollywood classes."

**Parent response:**

> "You should also try the Ashkenaz in Berkeley: http://www.ashkenaz.com/html/classes.html. It looks like you will have to call for more detailed info."

## Thread: Bollywood style Dance Class for 4 Year Old (June 2009)

**Original question:** Looking for Indian/Bollywood style class in Berkeley/Albany/El Cerrito area for a 4 year old girl.

**Parent response (Megan):**

> "My daughter has loved Danceversity — very Bollywood and other Middle Eastern influenced dances. She has done it now for several years and started with Hannah, who runs the program, in the summer camp program... Hannah is very encouraging and welcoming. She has terrific props - scarves and sparkly costumes that capture the spirit. For the camps there is a final performance, most kids want to do it but Hannah is very accommodating. All abilities and body types are truly welcome. Check out www.danceversity.com."

## Thread: Indian/Bollywood dance lessons for grown ups (Dec 2015)

**Parent response:**

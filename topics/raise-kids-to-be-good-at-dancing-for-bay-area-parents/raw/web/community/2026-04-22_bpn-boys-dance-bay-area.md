## Key Parent Insights on Boys in Dance

1. **Main concern:** Boys being the only boy in class creates discomfort
2. **Solution:** Look for studios with male instructors, which provide role models
3. **Best styles for boys:** Hip hop, circus arts, musical theater, African dance, Kathak — styles without strong "girl" associations
4. **Age consideration:** Most parents noting boys start later (12-14) because they don't get enrolled young, putting them behind peers who started at 8-9
5. **Parent framing matters:** One parent noted "when a boy arrives to his first class and loves it, sometimes the parent will bring up that he is the only boy — and when the parent asks if it bothers him, he says yes — but the parent made it an issue when there was no issue to begin with"
6. **Ballet-specific:** Berkeley Ballet Theater (now Berkeley City Ballet) historically gave discounts to male students to encourage enrollment
7. **Male instructor effect:** Multiple parents specifically sought out male instructors as important for boys feeling validated in dance

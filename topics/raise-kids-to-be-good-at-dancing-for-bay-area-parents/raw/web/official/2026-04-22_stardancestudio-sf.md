# Star Dance Studio — San Francisco

Location: 300 Moraga Street at 9th Avenue, San Francisco, CA 94122
Phone: (415) 564-1200
Established: 1950 (75+ years serving the community as of 2025)

## Programs & Age Groups

- Tots (youngest students)
- Kids (children's classes)
- Teens (adolescent instruction)
- Adults (evening and adult classes)

## Dance Styles Taught

- Ballet
- Tap
- Jazz
- Hip-hop
- Contemporary
- Musical theater

The studio's founder historically taught Armenian folk dancing and other folk forms.

## Studio Philosophy

"The joy of movement, teamwork, achievement and discipline in a supportive, loving environment." Curriculum emphasizes strength, confidence, artistry, technique, and expression.

## Performance Opportunities

Students participate in dance productions held at San Francisco's Herbst Theater.

## Registration

Online registration available. Multiple class schedules: regular, summer, adult/teen summer.

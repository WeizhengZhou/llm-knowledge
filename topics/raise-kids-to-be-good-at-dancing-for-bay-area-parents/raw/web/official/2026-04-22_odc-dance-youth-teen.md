# ODC School Youth & Teen Programs

Source: odc.dance (official ODC School website)
Location: San Francisco, CA

## Age Ranges & Program Structure

ODC School serves ages 2-18 across three divisions:
- **Young Creative Classes**: Ages 2-7
- **Youth Classes**: Ages 7-13
- **Teen Classes**: Ages 12-18

The program operates on a fall/spring session basis for older students (ages 8-18), with year-long enrollment. Young Creative students can register per session.

## Dance Styles Offered

"Creative movement, contemporary, hip hop, ballet, tap, composition, choreography and more" comprise the curriculum. Additional offerings include specialized intensives and dance across world styles.

## Program Philosophy

ODC emphasizes a "supportive and body-positive environment" where students are "challenged and delighted" through age-appropriate instruction. The organization states it aims to "inspire audiences, cultivate artists, engage community, and foster diversity and inclusion."

## Key Features

- Performance opportunities integrated throughout the year
- Scholarship applications available (Fall deadline: August 29, 2025)
- Placement evaluations for continuing ballet/contemporary students
- Parent observation days scheduled in advance
- "Beyond the Classroom" $65 registration fee covering field trips, evaluations, and production costs

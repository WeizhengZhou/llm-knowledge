# Shawl-Anderson Dance Center (SADC) — Berkeley

Established: 1958 (Berkeley institution)
Tagline: "A home for dance"
Location: Berkeley, CA

## Mission & Philosophy

SADC is "a Berkeley institution, proudly serving the community since 1958" that aims to foster an inclusive environment for dancers of all backgrounds. Emphasizes inclusivity, serving "movers of all ages and styles" and supporting local artists while partnering with community organizations.

## Programs Offered

**Youth & Teen Classes**
- Dedicated youth and teen programming
- Summer registration available for 2026 season
- Specific age ranges and class schedules on individual program pages

**Adult Classes**
- Year-round adult programming with scheduled closures

**Community Events**
- Queering Dance Festival (QDF)
- "Dance Up Close" events
- Rehearsal and performance space for community use

## Access & Financial Support

- Financial aid available for both youth and adult programs
- Class registration through Momence platform
- Weekly newsletter for class/event updates
- Gift certificate packs available

## Notes

Specific dance styles, detailed age ranges, class structures, and tuition information require visiting individual program pages at shawl-anderson.org.

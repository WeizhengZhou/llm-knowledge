# San Francisco Ballet School — Programs Overview

Founded: 1933 (oldest professional ballet school in the United States)
Location: San Francisco, CA
Contact: 415-865-6700
Website: sfballet.org/school

## Programs Offered

**Pre-Ballet (Ages 2-7)**
Introduces young children to movement in a structured, nurturing environment.

**School Year Program (Ages 8-18)**
Eight progressive levels from foundational technique through pre-professional preparation.

**Trainee Program**
- Selective, immersive one- to two-year program
- Designed to prepare dancers for professional career demands
- Top tier of the school pipeline

**Summer Programs**
Offerings ranging from discovery-level to advanced students pursuing professional paths.

**Adult Classes**
For dancers of all levels; builds strength, flexibility, and artistry.

## Professional Pipeline

Nearly 70% of San Francisco Ballet company dancers trained through the School's programs. This demonstrates effectiveness in preparing "dancers capable of joining a professional company."

## Admissions

- Inquiry form available on website

## Significance for Bay Area Families

SF Ballet School is the pre-eminent pre-professional ballet institution in the Bay Area. It is associated with San Francisco Ballet, a top-5 ballet company in the US. Entry to the School Year Program is selective and competitive.

# Dance Academy USA — 2025-2026 Season Programs

Contact: 408-257-3211 | officeteam@danceacademyusa.com

- Creative Movement (age 3)
- Intro to Dance (ages 4-6)

- Ballet/Jazz and Tap/Jazz Combos (ages 5-7)
- Beginner Acro (ages 6-9)

**Technique Classes (Ages 7-17) — Multiple levels (1, 1B, 1C, 2, 3, 4)**

- Jazz (various levels)
- Tap
- Hip Hop and Femme Hip Hop
- Lyrical/Contemporary
- K-Pop (Intro, Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced)
- Breakdance
- Acro
- Musical Theater Jazz
- Stretch/Flexibility
- Leaps/Turns
- High School Dance Team Training

**Team Classes (By Audition/Application)**
- Competition teams: Mini through Senior level
- Performance teams: Ages 5-6 and up

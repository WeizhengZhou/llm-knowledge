# East Bay Dance Company — Tuition & Policies (San Ramon Valley)

Location: San Ramon Valley, East Bay, CA
Website: eastbaydancecompany.com

## Monthly Class Tuition (Single Student)

| Classes per Week | Monthly Rate |
|---|---|
| 1 class/week | $88/month |
| 2 classes/week | $149/month |
| 3 classes/week | $198/month |
| 4 classes/week | $240/month |
| 5 classes/week | $280/month |
| 6 classes/week | $300/month |
| 7 classes/week | $340/month |
| 8+ classes/week | $340 + $12 per additional class |

## Additional Fees

- **Annual Registration:** $35 per dancer
- **Drop-in/Trial classes:** $25 each
- **Private lessons:** $60 (30 min), $75 (45 min), $100 (60 min)

## Discounts

- **Sibling discount:** 10% off additional dancer at same address

## Payment Terms

- Monthly tuition charged via ACH on the 1st
- Credit card payments incur 2.49% convenience fee
- Late payments after the 7th: $25 late fee
- "East Bay Dance Company does not provide refunds of any kind, for any reason" (including illness, injury, or missed classes)
- Yearly tuition is divided into 10 equal monthly payments (not true monthly billing)
- Tuition cannot transfer between seasons

## Studio Rental

- $130/hour

## Notes for Parent Cost Planning

For a typical recreational dancer attending 1 class/week: $88/month + $35 annual registration = approximately $915/year before costume and recital fees. For 2 classes/week: approximately $1,525/year base tuition.

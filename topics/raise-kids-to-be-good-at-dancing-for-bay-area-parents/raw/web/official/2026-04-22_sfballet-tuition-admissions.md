## Additional Mandatory Fees

- **Registration Fee:** $250 (non-refundable)
- **Wellness Fee:** $250 (Levels 4-5); $500 (Levels 6-8 & Trainees)
- **International Student Fee:** $75 (non-refundable)

## Housing Costs (for out-of-area students)

- **Housing Registration Fee:** $500 (non-refundable)
- **Housing Annual Fee:** $21,115 (Level 6); $19,884 (Levels 7-Trainee)
- **Housing Deposit:** $500 (refundable upon move-out)
- **Meal Plan:** $3,500–$8,000 annually

## Financial Support

**Scholarships:** Based on "potential to achieve a professional career in ballet." "Limited" scholarships to intermediate and advanced students, awarded at invitation time.

**Financial Aid:** Need-based assistance available depending on donor contributions. Families apply using FACTS Grant AND Aid platform after acceptance.

**2025-2026 Financial Aid Application Deadlines:**
- Current families: May 1
- New families: Upon acceptance notification
- Applications open: January 1

## Admissions Process

### Ages 8–10 (Enrollment Screening)
The screening resembles a trial ballet class. Evaluators assess:
- Interest and excitement to learn ballet
- Natural ear for music
- Willingness to work hard
- Ability to apply direction and corrections

Physical capabilities are considered for safe training.

**Dates for 2026–27:**
- April 25: Ages 8 (3:30–4:00 PM), Ages 9–10 (4:00–4:30 PM)
- August 3: Ages 8–10 (4:15–4:45 PM)
- **Fee:** $30

### Ages 11–13 (Audition)
Applicants should have previous ballet experience and demonstrate "natural attributes for a career in classical ballet." Girls ideally should have begun pointe work.

**Dates for 2026–27:**
- April 25: 1:45–3:15 PM
- August 3: 5:00–6:30 PM
- **Fee:** $50

### Ages 14–18 (Video Pre-Selection + Live Audition)
Applicants submit video auditions via DanceApply for pre-selection. Pre-selected candidates participate in a 2-hour live audition.

**Timeline:**
- Video submissions: December 1, 2025 – April 1, 2026
- Results: Within 2 weeks of submission
- **Fee:** $50

**Live Audition Locations:**
- January 19: School of American Ballet, New York
- February 8: San Francisco Ballet School
- April 25: San Francisco Ballet School

## What the School Seeks

Strong candidates are "passionate about the art form and motivated to pursue intense training toward a career in classical ballet" with intermediate-to-advanced technique ready for professional-level development.

## About the School

SF Ballet School is the oldest professional ballet academy in America, established in 1933. The school offers an 8-level progressive program for students ages 8-18. Over 70% of SF Ballet's Company dancers — throughout the ranks — are school alumni.

## Trainee Program

A one- to two-year pre-professional program comprised of students chosen from the School's advanced levels and from School Year auditions. Annual tuition: $13,500.

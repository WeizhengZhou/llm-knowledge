# Marin Ballet — Programs Overview

Location: San Rafael, CA (Marin County)
Contact: 415-453-6705 | info@marinballet.org
Website: marinballet.org
Enrollment: Ongoing all year round

Mission: "Marin Ballet develops confident, healthy, and joyous students through exceptional ballet training, performances, mentorship, and community engagement."

## Program Structure (Full Classical Ballet Progression)

**Wee Dance Together (Ages 18 months – 3 years)**
Parent/caregiver accompaniment required. Creative movement and early ballet steps (pliés, little leaps, twirls). Joyful, nurturing environment.
Price: $96 for 4-week session.

**Creative Movement Exploration (Ages 3-4)**
Weekly 45-to-60-minute class. Safely engages little bodies in creative movement and exploration.

**Ballet Prep (Kindergarten – 1st Grade, approx. ages 5-6)**
Introductory ballet fundamentals for girls and boys. Based on dance fundamentals, music, and energetic bodies/imaginations. MB's seasonal Culture & Community Classes included in tuition.

**Beginning Ballet — Levels 1-4 (Ages 7-10)**
Starting syllabus for young dancers with little or no experience through pre-pointe. Girls and boys grouped by grade level. Culture & Community classes and gatherings included.

**Intermediate Ballet (Ages 10-13)**
Transition to pointe work. Training in like age groups. Classes include technique, pointe, contemporary, and strength/stretch/pilates. Voluntary Culture & Community classes included.

**Advanced Ballet (Ages 14-18)**
Multiple days per week training, multi-age setting. Classes include technique, pointe, contemporary, and select strength/stretch infusions. Culture & Community offerings included.

**Adult Ballet**
Morning Int/Advanced classes and Beginning Adult Class options.

## Additional Programs

- **Youth Company (MBYC)** — separate competitive/performance company
- **Extravadance** — annual performance event
- **Community Engagement/Outreach** — community dance programs

## Training Philosophy

"Standards-based dance education that equips students with the tools and vocabulary to perform at their best." Collaborations with guest choreographers, both ballet and contemporary syllabus, and student choreography initiative.

## Note on Cost

Wee Dance Together: $96 for 4 weeks (the only publicly listed price on homepage). Other tuition requires inquiry or registration page.

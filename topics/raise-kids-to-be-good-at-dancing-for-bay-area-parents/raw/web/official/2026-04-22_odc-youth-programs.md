# ODC School Youth & Teen Programs Overview (2025-2026)

Source: https://odc.dance/youth-and-teen

## Program Philosophy
ODC School offers dance instruction for ages 2-18 in San Francisco's Mission District. The program emphasizes "creativity and movement" in "a supportive and body-positive environment." Students explore contemporary, hip hop, ballet, tap, composition, and choreography across age-appropriate levels.

## Age Ranges & Class Types

**Young Creative (Ages 2-7)**
- Creative movement and introductory dance styles
- Single session enrollment (Fall or Spring)

**Youth Classes (Ages 7-13)**
- Ballet, contemporary, hip hop, tap, and composition
- Year-long academic structure with Fall/Spring sessions

**Teen Classes (Ages 12-18)**
- Advanced training in multiple modalities
- Performance company opportunities (Dance Jam, SEEDS)

## Tuition & Fees

- Youth classes: $208 per class (per session)
- Teen classes: $216 per class (per session)
- 10% discount for students taking multiple classes or sibling enrollment
- Two-payment and nine-payment plans available (5% and 10% service fees respectively, not to exceed $180)
- **Beyond the Classroom Registration Fee:** $65 per student (covers administrative costs, field trips, evaluations, productions, and one ticket to school productions)
- **Placement Classes (August 21, 2025):** $35 fee for new ballet and contemporary students ages 9-18 with prior experience

## Scholarship Information
ODC offers need-based scholarships for students ages 8+. Applicants must demonstrate financial need, motivation, and commitment. Scholarship recipients must maintain consistent attendance.

**Application Deadlines:**
- Fall 2025: August 29, 2025
- Summer 2026: May 29, 2026
- Apply: https://odcdance.wufoo.com/forms/z1qc2mjy0us3o3f/

**ODC Dance Jam Scholarship Fund:** Full year of tuition, participation fees, and a stipend for ancillary expenses (travel, dancewear) for one participating student.

## Performance Company Auditions (2025-2026)
- **Dance Jam** (Teen Contemporary): August 26, 4-6 pm
- **SEEDS** (Teen Hip Hop): September 10, 4:30-6 pm
- **The Velveteen Rabbit**: September 27, 3-6 pm
- Pre-registration required for all auditions

## Summer Programs
Summer weekend dance classes run June 6-July 27, 2026, offering an eight-week session with a wide variety of dance styles and levels.

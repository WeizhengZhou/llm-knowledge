# JCCSF Dance School — Program Details

Source: https://www.jccsf.org/program/jccsf-dance-school/

## Class Types & Ages
- **Styles:** Ballet, Tap, Musical Theater, and Combo classes
- **Ages:** 3-16 (preschool through intermediate instruction)
- Classes organized by developmental level

## Tuition Pricing
- Summer 2026 classes: **$240 per session**
- Two sessions:
  - Session 1: June 1 – July 9
  - Session 2: June 13 – August 20
- Classes meet once weekly for 45-50 minutes depending on age group
- Community members save 10% with a JCCSF membership

## Financial Assistance
- Yes, financial assistance available
- Visit jccsf.org/financial-assistance for details and application
- Specific sliding-scale rates or scholarship amounts not published publicly

## Key Program Features
- Professional instructors
- Year-round registration periods: November, April, and July
- Curated private classes available for ages 3-16 with flexible scheduling
- The Helen Diller Walk-Over program: adds $75 to class registration for preschoolers requiring transportation between JCCSF facilities

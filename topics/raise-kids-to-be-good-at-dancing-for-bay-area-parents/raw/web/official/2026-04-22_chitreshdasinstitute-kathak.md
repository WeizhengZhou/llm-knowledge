# Chitresh Das Institute — Chhandam School of Kathak

## Overview

The Chitresh Das Institute operates the Chhandam School of Kathak in the San Francisco Bay Area. Founded by the late Pandit Chitresh Das, one of the preeminent kathak masters in the world, the organization continues his legacy of teaching Indian classical dance.

## What is Kathak?

Kathak is one of the major Indian classical dance forms, originating from northern India. It is characterized by:
- Intricate footwork (tatkar)
- Fast spins (chakkar)
- Expressive storytelling (abhinaya)
- Integration of rhythm, music, and movement
- Both Hindu and Islamic cultural influences

According to Berkeley Parents Network parent reviews: "They teach dance but also singing, rhythm, math, philosophy and they really help the kids of Indian descent connect to their heritage. There are kids (girls and boys!) from all backgrounds."

# New Ballet (San Jose) — School Year Training Programs

Source: https://newballet.com/training-programs

## About New Ballet
Founded 2016 by Dalia Rawson. Only American Ballet Theatre (ABT) Certified School on the West Coast. Uses ABT National Training Curriculum — a 9-level, outcome-based program combining high quality artistic training with dancer health and child development principles.

## Pre-Ballet Division (Ages 2.5–5)

**Dance with Me:** Ages 2.5–4, Saturdays 9:15–10:00 AM
**Pre-Primary Ballet:** Ages 4–5, Saturdays 10:00–11:00 AM
- No audition required
- Tuition details available in Schedule + Tuition document (see website)

## Primary Division (Ages 5–10)

- Levels: Primary A, B & C
- One class per week; weekday and weekend options
- No audition required
- Makeup classes available
- Performance opportunities: San Jose Nutcracker and Spring Performance
- Primary Flex option: Thursdays 5–6 PM, drop-in basis
- Tuition details in Schedule + Tuition document

## Junior Pre-Professional Division (Ages 8+)

- Levels: 1A–3B
- Placement class required (not full audition)
- Two annual productions: The San Jose Nutcracker and Spring Story Ballet
- Tuition details in Schedule + Tuition document

## Senior Pre-Professional Division

- Levels: 4–6
- Audition required
- Students typically train 4–5 days per week
- Live pianists in select classes
- Financial aid and scholarships available for qualified students
- Flexible rehearsal/performance accommodations for high school juniors and seniors
- Professional performance opportunities

## Studio Company & Apprentice Programs

- Ages 16+
- Studio Company Apprentice: Tuition-based with limited scholarships
- Studio Company: Potentially tuition-free for exceptional dancers; stipends possible
- Full-day, performance-based program

## Career Outcomes

Dancers join professional companies internationally and pursue degrees at prestigious universities.

## Overview

Healy School of Irish Dance, located in Larkspur, CA (Marin County), is in its 5th generation of family operation; the founding date is cited as 1870 by some sources and 1902 by others (the United Irish Cultural Center states the school "opened in 1902"). The discrepancy may reflect the founding of the Healy family's dance teaching tradition (possibly in Ireland) versus the establishment of Bay Area operations.

## Programs

According to Healy School's website, new intro classes begin every 8 to 10 weeks for children ages 4-15; no dance experience is necessary. The school offers:

- Recreational Irish dance instruction for beginners
- Competitive Irish dance training
- Participation in Feiseanna (Irish dance competitions)
- Ceili dance (traditional social dance forms)

### Competitive Levels

Irish step dance follows a competitive progression: Beginner, Novice, Prizewinner, Preliminary Champion, and Open Champion. The United Irish Cultural Center (UICC) in San Francisco hosts several official Feiseanna throughout the year.

## Costs

Tuition is not published on the website; families should contact the school directly at (415) 924-9100.

According to Healy School sources, competitive Irish dance solo costumes at higher levels can cost $500-$2,000 or more; competitive wigs are also required at advanced levels. These costs are specific to competitive Irish dance and do not apply to recreational-level students.

## Common Mistakes

- **Not budgeting for competitive costume costs.** According to Healy School sources, competitive Irish dance solo costumes at higher levels can cost $500-$2,000 or more; competitive wigs are also required at advanced levels. Parents who enroll for recreational classes do not face these costs, but if your child advances to competitive levels, these expenses arrive quickly and are not optional.

- **Assuming the 8-10 week intro cycle is a one-time commitment.** According to Healy School's website, new intro classes begin every 8 to 10 weeks for children ages 4-15; no dance experience is necessary. Parents sometimes enroll for one intro cycle thinking it is a standalone class. In practice, the intro cycle is a rolling entry point -- your child will want to continue if they enjoy it, and the tuition commitment extends from there.

- **Comparing Healy to ballet studios on schedule flexibility.** Irish dance schools typically follow a different structure than ballet studios. Feis (competition) preparation drives the calendar, and the competitive season has its own rhythm. Ask the school about the annual calendar and competition expectations before enrolling.

- **Overlooking Feiseanna as a competitive outlet.** Parents focused on mainstream dance competitions (NYCDA, Starpower) may not realize that Irish dance has its own well-developed competitive circuit (Feiseanna) with clear progression levels from Beginner through Open Champion. The United Irish Cultural Center (UICC) in San Francisco hosts several official Feiseanna throughout the year.

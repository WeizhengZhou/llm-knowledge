# AileyCamp Berkeley/Oakland

## Overview

AileyCamp Berkeley/Oakland (hosted by Cal Performances) is free for accepted students, serves ages 11-14, and runs June 15 through July 24, 2026, 8:30 AM to 3:30 PM daily. This is a joyful and rigorous six-week full-day summer program with competitive admission and limited spots.

## Curriculum

According to Cal Performances, AileyCamp curriculum includes African dance, Ballet, Jazz, Modern dance, Creative Communication, and Personal Development across a six-week full-day program. The program also includes field trips, guest workshops, and special events.

## Why AileyCamp Matters

AileyCamp is one of the most significant affordable dance programs in the Bay Area because it is completely free, provides full-day programming (reducing childcare costs for working parents), exposes youth to multiple dance styles, and includes personal development and creative communication alongside dance training.

For families who cannot afford studio tuition, AileyCamp offers a path to serious dance exposure. However, admission is competitive, and families should apply as early as possible.

## How to Apply

Contact Cal Performances community programs directly through calperformances.org.

### Application Timing

Specific application open and close dates for AileyCamp Berkeley/Oakland are not published far in advance. Based on the program running June 15 through July 24, 2026, applications typically open in early spring (February-March) and close by late April or May. Because admission is competitive with limited spots, families should:

1. Check calperformances.org/community/aileycamp starting in January for application availability
2. Sign up for Cal Performances community program notifications
3. Apply as soon as the application opens -- do not wait until the deadline

## After AileyCamp: Follow-On Pathways

AileyCamp is a six-week summer program, not a year-round school. Graduates who want to continue dancing need a follow-on plan:

- **Continue at a Bay Area studio:** [[odc-school]] offers contemporary and hip-hop for teens with need-based scholarships. [[shawl-anderson-dance-center]] in Berkeley provides financial aid.
- **Pursue modern/contemporary focus:** AileyCamp's curriculum (African, ballet, jazz, modern) aligns well with [[odc-school]]'s Dance Jam and SEEDS companies.
- **Summer-to-summer option:** Some graduates return to AileyCamp in subsequent summers (if still within the 11-14 age range).
- **Scholarship opportunities:** ODC School need-based scholarship deadline is August 29, 2025 for Fall 2025 and May 29, 2026 for Summer 2026 -- time the application to follow AileyCamp completion.

## Common Mistakes

- **Applying late because the program is free.** "Free" does not mean "easy to get in." AileyCamp has competitive admission with limited spots. Families who apply in the final days often find the program is already full. Apply the day the application opens.

- **Not having a plan for after AileyCamp ends.** The program runs six weeks. If your child loves dance, they will want to continue in August -- but studio fall sessions start soon after AileyCamp ends. Research year-round options (and scholarship deadlines) before AileyCamp begins, not after it ends.

- **Assuming AileyCamp is only for experienced dancers.** The program serves ages 11-14 and includes personal development and creative communication alongside dance. It is designed to be accessible to youth without prior formal training. Do not self-select out because your child has never taken a dance class.

- **Missing the age window.** AileyCamp serves ages 11-14 only. An 11-year-old can potentially attend for up to four summers, but a 14-year-old gets only one chance. If your child is 10, plan to apply next year -- do not wait until they are 14.

## Overview

East Bay Dance Company is a dance studio in the San Ramon Valley serving recreational and competitive dancers. The studio publishes one of the most detailed tuition schedules of any Bay Area dance studio, making it a useful benchmark for understanding dance costs in the region.

## Programs and Styles

East Bay Dance Company offers classes spanning recreational through competitive levels. Based on available source data, the studio offers:

- **Recreational classes** -- for students taking 1-2 classes per week
- **Competitive team** -- for students training 3-7+ classes per week, attending regional and national competitions
- **Private lessons** -- available in 30, 45, and 60-minute formats ($60-$100)
- **Drop-in/trial classes** -- $25 each, allowing families to evaluate fit before committing

The studio's tuition structure (scaling from 1 to 8+ classes per week) indicates it serves a range from casual recreational dancers to serious competitive students. Specific dance styles offered (ballet, jazz, hip-hop, contemporary, etc.) and age group breakdowns should be confirmed directly with the studio at eastbaydancecompany.com.

### Additional Fees

According to East Bay Dance Company, the annual registration fee is $35; drop-in/trial class is $25; private lessons are $60 (30 min), $75 (45 min), or $100 (60 min).

### Discounts and Policies

According to East Bay Dance Company, the sibling discount is 10% off for an additional dancer at the same address; late payment fee (after the 7th) is $25; credit card payments incur a 2.49% convenience fee.

According to East Bay Dance Company's policies, the studio does not provide refunds for any reason including illness, injury, or missed classes; yearly tuition is divided into 10 equal monthly payments.

## Cost Planning

For a typical recreational dancer attending 1 class/week: approximately $915/year ($88/month x 10 + $35 registration) before costume and recital fees. For 2 classes/week: approximately $1,525/year base tuition.

## Common Mistakes

- **Assuming monthly tuition means month-to-month flexibility.** According to East Bay Dance Company's policies, the studio does not provide refunds for any reason including illness, injury, or missed classes; yearly tuition is divided into 10 equal monthly payments. You are committing to a full year, not paying month-to-month. Understand this before enrolling.

- **Forgetting the credit card fee.** According to East Bay Dance Company, credit card payments incur a 2.49% convenience fee. On $340/month (7 classes/week), that adds over $100/year. Pay via ACH to avoid this.

- **Not using the $25 drop-in class before committing.** The studio offers trial classes for $25, which is one of the lowest trial-class prices in the East Bay. Use it. Especially for younger children, one trial class reveals more than any website.

- **Comparing only base tuition to other studios.** East Bay Dance Company's monthly rates look straightforward, but the no-refund policy and 10-month payment structure mean your actual commitment is $880-$3,400/year in base tuition alone. Add the $35 registration fee, costumes, and any competition fees for a true annual cost comparison.

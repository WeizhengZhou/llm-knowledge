## Overview

The Chitresh Das Institute operates the Chhandam School of Kathak in the San Francisco Bay Area. Founded by the late Pandit Chitresh Das, one of the preeminent kathak masters in the world, the organization continues his legacy of teaching Indian classical dance.

According to Chitresh Das Institute, classes serve ages 6 through 60+ with no prior dance experience required for beginners. According to Chitresh Das Institute, classes are offered at Cupertino, San Mateo, and San Ramon locations; a Berkeley location has also been noted in parent community reviews.

## What is Kathak?

Kathak is one of the major Indian classical dance forms, originating from northern India. It is characterized by intricate footwork (tatkar), fast spins (chakkar), expressive storytelling (abhinaya), integration of rhythm, music, and movement, and both Hindu and Islamic cultural influences.

## Programs

The organization operates multiple initiatives:

1. **Chitresh Das Institute** -- Youth and adult classes, school performances, and professional teacher training
2. **Chitresh Das Dance and Youth Company** -- Professional and pre-professional dance companies for advanced students
3. **Fellowship Program** -- Developing next-generation professional kathak dancers and teachers
4. **Art For the Planet** -- Supporting new artistic works with broader social purpose

## Why Kathak for Boys

## Common Mistakes

- **Assuming kathak is only for children of Indian heritage.** According to Chitresh Das Institute, classes serve ages 6 through 60+ with no prior dance experience required for beginners. The school welcomes students from all backgrounds. Do not self-select out because your family is not Indian.

- **Expecting published tuition on the website.** Tuition rates are not listed online. Families must contact the institute directly at (415) 539-8849 or register through MindbodyOnline. Budget time for this step before the session starts.

- **Registering mid-session and expecting to catch up.** According to Chitresh Das Institute, the 2026 Winter-Spring session ran January 18 through May 20, 2026. Kathak training is progressive and cumulative -- joining mid-session means missing foundational footwork patterns. Enroll at the start of a new session.

- **Comparing kathak costs and structure to Western dance studios.** Kathak follows a guru-shishya (teacher-student) tradition with different expectations around practice, commitment, and progression than a typical Western ballet or jazz studio. The session structure, class length, and progression pace may differ from what you are accustomed to. Attend a class or speak with the institute before assuming Western dance studio norms apply.

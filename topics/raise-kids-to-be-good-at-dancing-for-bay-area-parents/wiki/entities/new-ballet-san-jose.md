## Who This Is For

New Ballet San Jose serves the full range from toddler introduction (Dance with Me, ages 2.5-4) through pre-professional and Studio Company levels. It is the strongest option for:

- **South Bay families** who want serious ballet training without commuting to San Francisco.
- **Families interested in the ABT curriculum** -- a standardized, outcome-based system that transfers well if you move or switch schools nationally.
- **Dancers seeking a potential tuition-free professional track** -- the Studio Company offers this for exceptional dancers.

## Overview

New Ballet San Jose is the only American Ballet Theatre (ABT) Certified School on the West Coast. According to New Ballet's website, the school was founded in 2016 by Dalia Rawson. The school uses the ABT National Training Curriculum, a 9-level, outcome-based program combining high-quality artistic training with dancer health and child development principles.

## Programs

### Pre-Ballet Division (Ages 2.5-5)

According to New Ballet's website, Dance with Me is for ages 2.5-4 on Saturdays 9:15-10:00 AM; Pre-Primary Ballet is for ages 4-5 on Saturdays 10:00-11:00 AM. No audition required.

### Primary Division (Ages 5-10)

Levels Primary A, B, and C. One class per week with weekday and weekend options. No audition required. Performance opportunities include the San Jose Nutcracker and Spring Performance. Tuition details available on the school's website.

### Junior Pre-Professional Division (Ages 8+)

Levels 1A-3B. Placement class required (not a full audition). Two annual productions.

### Senior Pre-Professional Division

According to New Ballet, the Senior Pre-Professional Division (Levels 4-6) requires audition and students typically train 4-5 days per week. Live pianists in select classes. Financial aid and scholarships available.

### Studio Company and Apprentice Programs (Ages 16+)

According to New Ballet, the Studio Company is potentially tuition-free for exceptional dancers with stipends possible; the Studio Company Apprentice is tuition-based with limited scholarships. This is a full-day, performance-based program.

## Summer Intensive

The summer intensive is designed for students placed in Levels 1B and up, and is also open to dedicated ballet students ages 10-23 from other studios. Scholarship auditions are held at Bay Area dates (to be announced) with video audition options available.

## Common Mistakes

- **Assuming "ABT Certified" means the same thing as "ABT school."** New Ballet San Jose is the only American Ballet Theatre (ABT) Certified School on the West Coast, meaning it uses the ABT National Training Curriculum. It is not a branch of American Ballet Theatre itself. Students still need to audition separately for ABT company or ABT summer programs.

- **Not asking for tuition before committing.** Because tuition is unpublished, some families show up for a placement class without knowing the cost. Contact info@newballet.com and get the full tuition schedule for your child's division before the placement class.

- **Overlooking the Saturday-only schedule for young children.** According to New Ballet's website, Dance with Me is for ages 2.5-4 on Saturdays 9:15-10:00 AM; Pre-Primary Ballet is for ages 4-5 on Saturdays 10:00-11:00 AM. These are weekend-only classes. If Saturday mornings do not work for your family, there is no alternative day at this age level.

- **Treating the Studio Company as a school program.** The Studio Company is a pre-professional performance company, potentially tuition-free with stipends. It is closer to a junior company position than a class. According to New Ballet, the Senior Pre-Professional Division (Levels 4-6) requires audition and students typically train 4-5 days per week. The Studio Company commitment is even greater. Understand the distinction before your teen auditions.

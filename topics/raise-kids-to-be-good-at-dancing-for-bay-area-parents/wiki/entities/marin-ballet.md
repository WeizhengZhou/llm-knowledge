## Overview

Marin Ballet serves ages 18 months through adult, with youth programs for ages 3-18. The school's mission is to develop "confident, healthy, and joyous students through exceptional ballet training, performances, mentorship, and community engagement." It offers a full classical ballet progression from parent-child classes through advanced pre-professional training.

Marin Mommies lists Marin Ballet as having approximately 20,000 square feet of facility space with six studios. Marin Mommies lists Marin Ballet as offering a free first class.

According to Marin Ballet, the studio is located in San Rafael, CA (Marin County); contact 415-453-6705.

## Programs

**Wee Dance Together (Ages 18 months-3 years):** Marin Ballet Wee Dance Together (ages 18 months to 3 years, parent required) costs $96 for a 4-week session. Creative movement and early ballet steps (plies, leaps, twirls) in a nurturing environment.

**Creative Movement Exploration (Ages 3-4):** Weekly 45-60 minute classes focused on creative movement and exploration.

**Ballet Prep (Ages 5-6, Kindergarten-1st Grade):** Introductory ballet fundamentals for girls and boys. Includes seasonal Culture and Community Classes.

**Beginning Ballet Levels 1-4 (Ages 7-10):** Syllabus-based training from no experience through pre-pointe, grouped by grade level. Culture and Community classes included.

**Intermediate Ballet (Ages 10-13):** Transition to pointe work. Training includes technique, pointe, contemporary, and strength/stretch/Pilates.

**Advanced Ballet (Ages 14-18):** Multiple days per week, multi-age setting. Technique, pointe, contemporary, and strength/stretch infusions.

**Marin Ballet Youth Company (MBYC):** A separate competitive/performance company for experienced dancers, by audition only.

## Who This Is For

Marin Ballet is the primary classical ballet school for families in Marin County. It is well suited for:

- **Families wanting a full progression path** -- from parent-child classes at 18 months through advanced pre-professional training and Youth Company, all at one school.
- **Marin County families** who want to avoid commuting to San Francisco for ballet training.
- **Beginners and explorers** -- the school welcomes new students at every level, and Marin Mommies lists Marin Ballet as offering a free first class.

For families seeking ABT-certified training, consider [[new-ballet-san-jose]]. For the highest-level pre-professional pipeline in the Bay Area, consider [[sf-ballet-school]]. Marin Ballet serves the space between recreational-only studios and elite pre-professional academies.

## Common Mistakes

- **Driving to SF for ballet when your child is a beginner.** If you live in Marin County and your child is just starting, a 30-45 minute commute to SF Ballet School or ODC is unnecessary. Marin Ballet offers a complete beginner-through-advanced progression. Reserve the SF commute for when (and if) your child reaches a level where pre-professional training is the goal.

- **Assuming tuition is comparable to SF studios without checking.** Because Marin Ballet does not publish tuition online, some parents assume it is in the same range as SF Ballet School ($4,500-$13,500/year). Marin Ballet is a different type of program. Call 415-453-6705 and ask for the current schedule before making cost comparisons.

- **Skipping the free first class.** Marin Mommies lists Marin Ballet as offering a free first class. Use this before committing. Observe the teaching style, facility, and how your child responds. No other Bay Area ballet school in this wiki offers a free trial class at this level.

- **Overlooking Marin Ballet Youth Company (MBYC) as a competitive outlet.** Parents seeking competition experience often look outside Marin County. MBYC is an auditioned performance company for experienced dancers, providing competitive and performance opportunities without leaving the county.

## Must-Fix Before wiki/ Graduation

1. Missing "Common Mistakes" section (D3 = 0 blocks graduation). Support-specific mistakes parents make: (a) Critiquing technique at home — the article's "What NOT to Do" section says "Do not critique technique (that is the teacher's job)" but presents this in a list without explaining why this is damaging. The Common Mistakes section should frame it specifically: parents who say "your arabesque looked low in the recital" undermine the child's relationship with their teacher and introduce a second authority figure whose critique conflicts with the teacher's. This is the #1 most damaging home behavior identified in dance parent literature. (b) Treating one bad class as evidence of a larger problem — children often have off days that reflect sleep, hunger, or peer dynamics rather than dance disengagement; parents who immediately ask "do you want to quit?" after a tearful car ride home teach their child that one bad session justifies exit, setting a pattern that harms persistence across all activities. (c) Over-identifying with the child's competitive results — parents who are visibly disappointed after poor competition placements create performance anxiety that is the leading driver of teenage dance dropout. This is distinct from the "avoid comparing to other dancers" bullet already in the article, which is generic; the mistake here is the parent's own emotional display, not verbal comparison.

2. The RO6 must_answer item "What life skills does dance develop regardless of career path?" is entirely absent. This is the most important reassurance for parents who are questioning the investment — especially for P3/P4 parents who are weighing pre-professional commitment costs against realistic career odds. Add a section (even a brief one — 150-200 words) on documented life skill benefits: discipline through repetition, performing under pressure, body awareness and physical confidence, peer collaboration through ensemble work, creative problem-solving. This satisfies the must_answer item and strengthens the "cross-training supports dance development" adjacent point.

Add a one-sentence cross-reference: "For cross-training recommendations including Pilates, swimming, and strength conditioning, see [[injury-prevention-youth-dancers]]." This prevents the gap from being invisible to parents who read this article but not the injury article.
2. The Training Hour Benchmarks section (ages 5-7: 2-4 hours, ages 8-10: 4-6 hours, etc.) appears in both this article and injury-prevention-youth-dancers verbatim. One article should be primary for this table; the other should cross-reference. As an evergreen guide on parenting support, this article is the more natural home for the parenting-facing guidance. The injury article should cross-reference here for the hours table, or vice versa.
3. The book recommendations are a genuinely useful addition. Consider adding one book specifically about parenting competitive or serious dancers (not just ballet reference books), as the current list skews toward ballet literacy rather than parent strategy.

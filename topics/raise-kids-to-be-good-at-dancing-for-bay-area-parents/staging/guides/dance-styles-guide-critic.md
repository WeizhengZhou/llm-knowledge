## Must-Fix Before wiki/ Graduation

1. Missing "Common Mistakes" section (D3 = 0 blocks graduation for a guide article serving a selection decision). Dance-styles-guide-specific mistakes: (a) Starting a child in the style the parent loves rather than the style the child gravitates toward — the article emphasizes child-fit in the closing paragraph but not in an action-framed warning; parents of ballet dancers who push their hip-hop-loving child toward ballet are the single most common source of early dropout. (b) Assuming contemporary dance is available as a beginner style for young children — the article correctly states contemporary requires "ages 8+ for serious study" and a ballet foundation, but the odc-school entity article lists contemporary for ages 7+; parents need to understand contemporary at 7 means exploratory creative movement, not structured technique. (c) Treating K-Pop as comparable in depth to ballet or contemporary for long-term development — K-Pop is listed alongside ballet and contemporary without noting it is primarily choreography-based and provides less transferable technical foundation if a child later wants to pursue other styles seriously.

2. K-Pop section: the EDS Dance description ("the largest Asian-owned dance studio in the Bay Area" — not independently verified) is currently flagged correctly in the article, but it is surrounded by editorial-sounding content that makes EDS Dance appear to be a recommended studio. Move the EDS Dance, Rae Studios, and Spark of Creation Studio details to a brief "Bay Area options:" line consistent with how other styles are presented, and link to a future entity article if one is created. The full paragraph of studio description breaks the style-guide format and gives K-Pop studios disproportionate entity-article treatment.

## Should-Fix

1. The Ballet section says "NorthPointe Dance Academy suggests that ages 6-8 is generally recommended." This attribution is weak — a single studio's recommendation should not carry the weight of an age guideline in an authoritative guide. Rephrase: "Formal ballet training is generally considered appropriate from ages 6-8, when bone structure is sufficiently mature — this is a commonly cited benchmark in dance education, though readiness varies by child."
2. The Contemporary section says "ODC School is the premier Bay Area contemporary program for youth" — this is an editorial superlative without citation. Change to "ODC School is widely cited as the leading Bay Area contemporary youth program" or attribute to the Bay Area Parent Magazine recognition.

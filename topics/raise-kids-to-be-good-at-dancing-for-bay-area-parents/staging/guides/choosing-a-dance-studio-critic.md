## Should-Fix

1. The "Inclusive Environment" green flag bullet ("welcoming atmosphere within 30 seconds of walking in") is the only signal for the boys-friendly must_answer item in RO2. This is thin. Add a sentence directing parents to [[dance-for-boys-bay-area]] for Bay Area-specific studio recommendations for boys, and note that male instructors are the single strongest predictor parents describe for boys' comfort and retention.
2. Green flag item "Balanced mix of experienced staff and mentorship" is the vaguest item in the Teacher Qualifications section. What does "mentorship" mean in this context? Rephrase as something specific: e.g., "experienced faculty teaching all primary technique classes, with junior instructors limited to warm-up or auxiliary roles."

## Graduation Decision: READY

No must-fix items. The two should-fix items do not block graduation. This is the set's most educationally complete article.

# Critic Report — Bay Area Dance Competition Guide — 2026-04-22

## Overall Assessment
A solid competition guide that covers organizations, format, scoring, calendar, and a competition weekend narrative. The Common Mistakes section exists and contains four specific mistakes — this is the only guide article in the set that includes Common Mistakes. The article's gap is that it does not fully address what a competition weekend looks like for the family (an explicit RO4 must_answer item) — the "what it looks like" section describes logistics but not emotional experience, family preparation, or how children typically respond.

## Must-Fix Before wiki/ Graduation

None. The Common Mistakes section exists and is specific. All RO4 must_answer items are addressed at least partially. D1 is 3; D3 is 3.

## Should-Fix

1. The competition weekend narrative (steps 1-5) describes what happens from the participant's logistics perspective but says nothing about what families should bring, how to prepare their child emotionally for competitive judging, or what to do when scores are disappointing. Add 2-3 sentences under "What a Competition Weekend Looks Like" on emotional preparation: "Children, especially first-time competitors, may react strongly to placement results — whether elated or disappointed. Prepare your child in advance by framing the experience around performance quality, not rank. Plan a family dinner after the awards to debrief calmly."
2. The NYCDA Bay Area Regional date shows "TBD (typically spring)" — if research could not pin down the 2026 date, say so explicitly: "NYCDA has not published the 2026 Bay Area regional date at time of writing; check nycda.com in fall 2025 for confirmed dates." The current "(typically spring)" framing is not specific enough to plan around.
3. The article mentions "California Dance Classics (CDC)" as "California's premier youth ballet and contemporary competition" with "premier" as an editorial superlative. Attribute this characterization or soften: "California Dance Classics is a widely recognized youth ballet and contemporary competition" or cite the source of the "premier" characterization.

## Graduation Decision: READY

No must-fix items. The three should-fix items are improvements, not blockers. This article and choosing-a-dance-studio are the two best-executed pieces in the set.

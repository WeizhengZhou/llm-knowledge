## Overview

ODC School is a contemporary-focused dance school in San Francisco's Mission District, affiliated with the ODC/Dance professional company. The school emphasizes creativity and movement in a supportive and body-positive environment. According to ODC, the school serves ages 2-18 across Young Creative (ages 2-7), Youth (ages 7-13), and Teen (ages 12-18) divisions.

ODC School won Gold in Bay Area Parent Magazine 2025 Best of the Best in the San Francisco/Marin category.

## Programs

**Young Creative (Ages 2-7):** Creative movement and introductory dance styles. Single session enrollment (Fall or Spring).

**Youth Classes (Ages 7-13):** Ballet, contemporary, hip-hop, tap, and composition. Year-long academic structure with Fall/Spring sessions.

**Teen Classes (Ages 12-18):** Advanced training in multiple modalities. Performance company opportunities through Dance Jam and SEEDS.

### Performance Companies

ODC runs three performance companies for teen dancers. These are auditioned ensembles that rehearse and perform throughout the year, representing a significant step up in commitment from regular classes:

- **Dance Jam** -- Teen contemporary dance company. Dancers develop repertoire, perform publicly, and work with professional choreographers. According to ODC, the Dance Jam Scholarship Fund covers a full year of tuition, participation fees, and a stipend for ancillary expenses such as travel and dancewear for one student.
- **SEEDS** -- Teen hip-hop company. Similar structure to Dance Jam but focused on hip-hop.
- **Velveteen Rabbit** -- Production-based company performing a specific work.

ODC 2025-2026 performance company auditions: Dance Jam (Teen Contemporary) August 26, 4-6 PM; SEEDS (Teen Hip Hop) September 10, 4:30-6 PM; Velveteen Rabbit September 27, 3-6 PM. Pre-registration is required for all auditions.

## Tuition and Fees (2025-2026)

According to ODC's website, youth class tuition is $208 per class per session and teen class tuition is $216 per class per session (2025-2026). This means **$208 buys one class type (e.g., "Youth Ballet") for one full session (Fall or Spring, roughly 12-15 weeks)**. A student taking two different classes in the same session would pay $208 x 2 = $416. According to ODC, a 10% discount applies for students taking multiple classes or with sibling enrollment. Two-payment and nine-payment plans are available (with 5% and 10% service fees respectively, not to exceed $180).

ODC School Beyond the Classroom registration fee is $65 per student, covering field trips, evaluations, productions, and one ticket (2025-2026).

ODC School placement class for new ballet and contemporary students ages 9-18 with prior experience costs $35, held August 21, 2025.

## Common Mistakes

- **Misreading the tuition structure as "$208 per individual class."** ODC's pricing is per class type per session (Fall or Spring), not per single class meeting. $208 for a youth class covers the entire session of weekly meetings, making it significantly more affordable than it first appears. Always calculate the per-week cost.

- **Missing the placement class requirement for experienced students.** ODC School placement class for new ballet and contemporary students ages 9-18 with prior experience costs $35, held August 21, 2025. If your child has prior dance training and you skip placement, they may be placed in a class below their level. Register for the placement class before the session starts.

- **Treating performance companies as "just another class."** Dance Jam and SEEDS are auditioned companies with rehearsal and performance commitments beyond regular class hours. They are a meaningful time commitment. Understand the full schedule before your teen auditions.

- **Not applying for scholarships because you assume your family will not qualify.** According to ODC's website, need-based scholarships are available for students ages 8 and up. The Dance Jam Scholarship Fund covers a full year of tuition, participation fees, and a stipend. ODC School need-based scholarship deadline is August 29, 2025 for Fall 2025 and May 29, 2026 for Summer 2026. Apply and let the school decide.

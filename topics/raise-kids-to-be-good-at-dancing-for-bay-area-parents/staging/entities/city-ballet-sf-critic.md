## Must-Fix Before wiki/ Graduation

1. Missing "Common Mistakes" section (D3 = 0 blocks graduation). CBSF-specific mistakes a parent would plausibly make: (a) Enrolling in the Junior Program expecting it to feed into the Year-Round Program automatically — the article does not say whether Junior Program graduates can matriculate or must re-audition. (b) Mistaking the Vaganova method as inherently superior or inferior to ABT-certified training — parents choosing between CBSF and New Ballet San Jose need to understand the methodological difference, not just the name. (c) Treating the $300/show performance fee as optional when Nutcracker participation is socially expected — students who waive all three shows may face social friction.

2. The Year-Round Program section is missing entirely. The article mentions "Year-Round Program" in the Quick Facts and Overview, quotes its marketing tagline, and then never describes it. Add at minimum: age range served, audition requirement, weekly training commitment (days/hours), tuition range or note that it requires direct inquiry. Without this, the article fails RO5 for any parent investigating pre-professional ballet in the Bay Area.

The alumni placement claim ("CBSF alumni have been placed in American Ballet Theatre, Mariinsky Theatre, Semperoper Ballett, Boston Ballet, and Royal Swedish Ballet") is sourced as L2 from "a pre-professional ballet synthesis source."

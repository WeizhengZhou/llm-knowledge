## Must-Fix Before wiki/ Graduation

1. Missing "Common Mistakes" section (D3 = 0 blocks graduation). AileyCamp-specific mistakes: (a) Applying too late — the article says apply "as early as possible" but gives no specific application deadline or opening date. Parents who read this article in May and try to apply for a June 15 start may have already missed the window. The article should instruct parents to check calperformances.org in January or February for application opening. (b) Assuming AileyCamp is for serious pre-professional dancers — the program includes Personal Development and Creative Communication alongside dance; it is specifically designed for underserved youth ages 11-14 and has a social development mission. Families enrolling solely for technical dance advancement may find the program's focus differs from a studio intensive. (c) Expecting the same style of instruction as a private studio — AileyCamp is a six-week full-day camp combining multiple styles with non-dance programming; it is not a substitute for year-round studio training and should not be treated as one.

## Should-Fix

1. "How to Apply" section is three sentences pointing to calperformances.org. Add the specific year of the most recent application cycle and note that families can contact Cal Performances community programs in January-February to ask for the next cycle's opening date. The current instruction gives parents no timing guidance.
2. The article would benefit from a sentence about what happens after AileyCamp — does Cal Performances offer any follow-on programming for graduates? If not, what is the recommended next step for a 14-year-old who wants to continue dancing after a successful AileyCamp experience? This would satisfy RO5 for the low-income family pathway.

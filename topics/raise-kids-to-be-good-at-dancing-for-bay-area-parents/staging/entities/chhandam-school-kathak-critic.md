## Must-Fix Before wiki/ Graduation

1. Missing "Common Mistakes" section (D3 = 0 blocks graduation). Chhandam-specific mistakes: (a) Attending a class at the wrong location — the four locations (Cupertino, San Mateo, San Ramon, Berkeley) serve different geographies; parents should confirm which location offers classes at the times and ages they need, not assume all classes are available at all locations. (b) Missing the session registration window — the article states the Winter-Spring 2026 session ran January 18 through May 20 and notes sessions "open periodically"; parents who miss the registration window on MindbodyOnline may have to wait a full session. They should contact the school in November/December for spring session registration dates. (c) Assuming any prior dance experience is required — the article says "no prior dance experience required for beginners" but this may not be obvious to parents who view Kathak as an advanced or specialized form; they may self-exclude their beginner children unnecessarily.

2. The "Why Kathak for Boys" section begins with three general claims ("the footwork-driven, athletic nature of the dance appeals to boys, male instructors are part of the faculty tradition, and the classroom atmosphere is welcoming to all genders") as if they are confirmed facts, then immediately follows with an epistemic note block for the parent forum synthesis. The first three claims are themselves community-derived and should either be moved inside the epistemic note block or explicitly attributed. In the current arrangement, the structure implies the pre-note claims are verified facts (L1/L2), which they are not.

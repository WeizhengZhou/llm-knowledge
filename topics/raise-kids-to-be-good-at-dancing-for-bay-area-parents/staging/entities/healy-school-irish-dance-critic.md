## Must-Fix Before wiki/ Graduation

1. Missing "Common Mistakes" section (D3 = 0 blocks graduation). Healy-specific mistakes: (a) Expecting to enroll on-demand — intro classes begin on a cycle every 8-10 weeks, so a family that calls in week 5 of a current cycle must wait. Parents should ask for the next cycle start date at first contact rather than assuming rolling enrollment. (b) Underestimating competitive costume costs before committing to competitive track — the $500-$2,000 solo costume cost is specific to the competitive track, not recreational students. Parents who start recreationally and later decide to compete are often shocked by this cost; the article should make clear this cost only applies if the student advances to competition. (c) Confusing the UICC Feiseanna (San Francisco) with Healy School — the United Irish Cultural Center hosts several official Feiseanna per year, but it is not the same organization as Healy School; families should understand they are separate entities when planning competition attendance.

2. Style Characteristics section: remove or dramatically condense. The current four-bullet description of Irish step dance characteristics repeats content from [[dance-styles-guide]]. Replace with: "Irish step dance is characterized by an upright torso and rapid footwork in both hard-shoe and soft-shoe forms — see [[dance-styles-guide]] for a full style overview."

## Must-Fix Before wiki/ Graduation

1. Missing "Common Mistakes" section (D3 = 0 blocks graduation). Marin Ballet-specific mistakes: (a) Expecting tuition to be listed online — parents who research Marin Ballet expecting to compare costs upfront will be frustrated; the article should proactively tell parents to call or email for a current tuition schedule rather than leaving them to discover the registration page requires JavaScript. (b) Assuming the free first class offer from Marin Mommies is still current — this was cited from a Marin Mommies review; parents should verify before arriving. (c) Treating the Youth Company (MBYC) as a natural progression — MBYC is audition-only and represents a separate competitive track; parents should not assume their child will advance into it through normal class enrollment.

## Should-Fix

1. The Tuition section is functionally empty beyond the Wee Dance Together price. Add: an explicit statement that tuition for all other levels is available by contacting the school (415-453-6705 or info@marinballet.org), and direct parents to [[bay-area-dance-costs]] for Bay Area ballpark figures to use as a planning reference while they wait for a response.
2. The article does not say whether year-round enrollment is possible or whether registration happens at specific session start dates — this is Marin Ballet's stated enrollment model ("Year-round" in Quick Facts) but the article gives no guidance on how to actually enroll mid-year.
3. Marin Ballet's mission statement ("confident, healthy, and joyous students") signals an approach distinct from pre-professional intensity, but the article never frames this as a green flag for families who want quality training without hyper-competitive culture. This distinction matters for RO2 evaluation.

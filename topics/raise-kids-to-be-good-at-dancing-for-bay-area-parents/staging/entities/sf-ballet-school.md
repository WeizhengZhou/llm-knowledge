## Who This Is For

SF Ballet School is an **auditioned pre-professional program** -- not a recreational dance studio. If your child wants to take one ballet class per week for fun, this is not the right school. It is designed for students who are pursuing ballet as a serious discipline with potential professional aspirations. The School Year Program (ages 8-18) requires a screening or audition for admission.

The exception is **Pre-Ballet (ages 2-7)**, which does not require an audition and serves as an introductory program. However, Pre-Ballet is not a pipeline into the School Year Program -- children must still pass a screening at age 8.

## Overview

SF Ballet School was established in 1933 and is the oldest professional ballet academy in America. SF Ballet School's School Year Program serves students ages 8-18 across eight progressive levels. The school is the primary training pipeline for the San Francisco Ballet company, one of the top professional ballet companies in the United States.

According to SF Ballet's website, approximately 70% of SF Ballet Company dancers trained through the School's programs (the tuition page states "over 70%" while the school overview page states "nearly 70%").

## Programs

**Pre-Ballet (Ages 2-7):** Introductory creative movement in a structured, nurturing environment. Not part of the auditioned School Year Program. Classes typically meet once per week and introduce young children to musicality, coordination, and basic ballet vocabulary through age-appropriate creative play. Class length is shorter than School Year classes (generally 45-60 minutes). Parents should understand that Pre-Ballet is an exposure program -- it builds interest and movement foundations, but promotion into the School Year Program at age 8 depends entirely on passing the enrollment screening.

**School Year Program (Ages 8-18):** The core program, spanning eight progressive levels from foundational technique through pre-professional preparation. Entry is by screening (ages 8-10) or audition (ages 11+).

**Trainee Program:** A selective one- to two-year pre-professional immersive program for dancers chosen from the school's advanced levels and from School Year auditions. SF Ballet School Trainee Program 2025-2026 annual tuition is $13,500.

**Summer Programs:** Offerings from discovery-level to advanced students, with a national audition tour for placement.

### Additional Fees

SF Ballet School registration fee is $250 (non-refundable); wellness fee is $250 for Levels 4-5 and $500 for Levels 6-8 and Trainees (2025-2026).

### Housing (Out-of-Area Students)

SF Ballet School 2025-2026 housing annual fee is $21,115 for Level 6 and $19,884 for Levels 7 through Trainee; housing meal plan is $3,500-$8,000 annually. A $500 non-refundable housing registration fee and $500 refundable deposit also apply.

## Admissions (2026-27 Cycle)

### Ages 8-10: Enrollment Screening

SF Ballet School 2026-27 admissions screening for ages 8-10 is held on April 25 and August 3; screening fee is $30. The screening resembles a trial ballet class. Evaluators assess interest, musicality, willingness to work hard, and ability to apply corrections.

### Ages 11-13: Audition

SF Ballet School 2026-27 audition for ages 11-13 is held on April 25 and August 3; audition fee is $50. Applicants should have previous ballet experience. Girls ideally should have begun pointe work.

### Ages 14-18: Video + Live Audition

SF Ballet School 2026-27 ages 14-18 video submission window is December 1, 2025 through April 1, 2026; live audition locations include January 19 (School of American Ballet, New York), February 8 and April 25 (SF Ballet School); fee is $50. Applicants submit video auditions via DanceApply for pre-selection, then participate in a 2-hour live audition.

## Financial Aid

SF Ballet School 2025-2026 financial aid applications open January 1; current family deadline is May 1; new family deadline is upon acceptance notification. Financial aid is need-based, administered through the FACTS Grant and Aid platform. Merit-based scholarships are available at intermediate and advanced levels, awarded based on potential for a professional career in ballet.

## Common Mistakes

- **Treating Pre-Ballet as a guaranteed pathway into the School Year Program.** Parents sometimes enroll a 3-year-old in Pre-Ballet assuming they will advance into Level 1 at age 8 automatically. Pre-Ballet is not a feeder program. Every child must pass an enrollment screening at age 8, and the screening evaluates potential, not seniority. Manage expectations accordingly.

- **Applying only to the April screening date and missing August.** SF Ballet School offers two screening/audition dates per cycle (April 25 and August 3 for 2026-27). Families who only know about the spring date miss a second chance. If your child is not ready in April, the August date is still available.

- **Assuming the program is unaffordable without checking financial aid.** Annual tuition of $4,545-$13,500 causes sticker shock, but SF Ballet School offers need-based financial aid through FACTS and merit scholarships at advanced levels. SF Ballet School 2025-2026 financial aid applications open January 1; current family deadline is May 1; new family deadline is upon acceptance notification. Apply before assuming you cannot afford it.

- **Confusing SF Ballet School with recreational ballet studios.** SF Ballet School is a pre-professional academy. The time commitment, audition requirements, and expectations are fundamentally different from a weekly recreational ballet class. If your child wants to try ballet casually, start at a recreational studio and consider SF Ballet School only after the child demonstrates sustained passion and aptitude.

## Must-Fix Before wiki/ Graduation

1. Missing "Common Mistakes" section (D3 = 0 blocks graduation). ODC-specific mistakes: (a) Missing the scholarship deadline — the Fall 2025 deadline is August 29, 2025, which is before the September 6 session start; parents who discover ODC after September will miss the window and assume no aid exists for that year. (b) Enrolling a child in Youth classes (ages 7-13) without understanding the session structure — the article says "year-long academic structure with Fall/Spring sessions" but doesn't clarify whether Fall-only enrollment is possible or whether missing Fall means waiting until Spring. (c) Assuming the $208/class/session tuition is per-month — the article's tuition unit ("per class per session") is ambiguous; parents need to know how many classes are in a session and therefore what total session cost is before comparing to other studios.

## Should-Fix

1. The tuition figure "$208/class/session" is difficult to interpret for cost comparison purposes. A session with 10 classes = $2,080; a session with 16 classes = $3,328. The article should state the number of classes per session or link to the bay-area-dance-costs article with a worked example.
2. Dance Jam and SEEDS are listed as performance companies with only audition dates. Add: How selective are they? How many hours per week does a Dance Jam member train? What is the financial cost of participation? These are the questions a parent would ask before encouraging their teen to audition.
3. The "Gold in Bay Area Parent Magazine 2025 Best of the Best" award is a credible RO2 green flag and is correctly cited. Frame it explicitly as a green flag under a "Why ODC?" heading rather than leaving it as a standalone sentence in the Overview.

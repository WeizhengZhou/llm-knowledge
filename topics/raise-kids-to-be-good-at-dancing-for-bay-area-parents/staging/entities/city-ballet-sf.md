## Overview

City Ballet San Francisco (CBSF) is a ballet school in San Francisco's SoMa neighborhood offering classical ballet training from beginner through pre-professional levels. According to CBSF, the school follows the Vaganova Method for ballet training. The Year-Round Program, described as "Your Career Starts Here," provides elite ballet training for ages 6-18+.

According to a pre-professional ballet synthesis source, CBSF alumni have been placed in American Ballet Theatre, Mariinsky Theatre, Semperoper Ballett, Boston Ballet, and Royal Swedish Ballet.

## Junior Program

The Junior Program ("Nurture a Dream") provides foundational ballet training at three levels:

| Level | Frequency | Annual Tuition (2025-26) |
|-------|-----------|--------------------------|
| Junior (C) | 1x/week | $1,850 |
| Junior (B) | 2x/week | $2,860 |
| Junior (A) | 3x/week | $5,495 |

According to CBSF's website, Junior Program 2025-2026 annual tuition is $1,850 for Junior C (1x/week), $2,860 for Junior B (2x/week), and $5,495 for Junior A (3x/week).

According to CBSF's website, performance fee is $300 per show (optional). According to CBSF, Junior Program students may perform 2-3 times per year in Fall Gala, Nutcracker, and Spring Show. Performances are optional, with additional rehearsals on weekends.

The curriculum covers ballet technique, stretch and strengthening (taught by former rhythmic gymnast Svetlana Scherba), and rehearsals. Students may apply and take a free trial class.

## Year-Round Program

The Year-Round Program ("Your Career Starts Here") is CBSF's full pre-professional training track for ages 6-18+. According to CBSF, the school follows the Vaganova Method for ballet training. The program provides progressive ballet training beyond the Junior levels, preparing students for professional company placement.

According to a pre-professional ballet synthesis source, CBSF alumni have been placed in American Ballet Theatre, Mariinsky Theatre, Semperoper Ballett, Boston Ballet, and Royal Swedish Ballet. This alumni track record positions CBSF as one of the Bay Area's serious pre-professional options alongside [[sf-ballet-school]] and [[new-ballet-san-jose]].

The Year-Round Program includes technique classes, variations, repertoire, and performance opportunities. Students at advanced levels train multiple days per week. Tuition for the full Year-Round Program (beyond Junior levels) is not published in available sources; contact the school directly.

## Housing

According to CBSF, housing for international or out-of-area students through The Monroe partner housing costs $1,080-$2,180 per month.

## Financial Aid

The CBSF Foundation provides tuition assistance for dancers who demonstrate passion for classical ballet but lack financial support.

## Common Mistakes

- **Confusing Junior Program with the full training track.** The Junior Program (Junior C/B/A) is the entry-level tier. Parents sometimes evaluate CBSF's seriousness based only on Junior pricing and frequency. The Year-Round Program above Junior levels is the pre-professional track with significantly greater time commitment.

- **Not budgeting for performance fees.** According to CBSF's website, performance fee is $300 per show (optional). With 2-3 performances per year, that adds $600-$900 annually on top of tuition. Performances are optional, but most families participate once their child is invested.

- **Overlooking the Vaganova method difference.** CBSF uses the Vaganova (Russian) system, which differs from the Balanchine style at SF Ballet School and the ABT curriculum at New Ballet San Jose. Method matters at advanced levels -- switching between methods mid-training can set students back. If your child may eventually transfer schools, understand the methodological implications early.

- **Assuming CBSF housing is only for international students.** According to CBSF, housing for international or out-of-area students through The Monroe partner housing costs $1,080-$2,180 per month. Families from the East Bay or South Bay commuting daily may find housing worth evaluating, especially for older students training multiple days per week.

## Must-Fix Before wiki/ Graduation

1. **D3 = 1: Common Mistakes section fails the specificity threshold.** The three listed mistakes do not describe specific, observable behaviors. Replace or augment with mistakes at the level of:
   - "Texting or emailing the coach during the week to discuss your child's technique or performance after each meet — this undermines the coach's authority and puts your child in the middle. The one-on-one coach conversation belongs in a scheduled meeting, not immediately after a bad race."
   - "Asking your child 'What was your time?' as the first question after a race — this signals that you care about the clock, not the experience. The first question should always be 'Did you have fun?' or 'How did that feel?'"
   - "Signing up for coaching clinics or reading stroke technique books to give your swimmer advice — you are not their coach. The more parents understand stroke technique, the more they are tempted to coach from the stands."

## Should-Fix

1. **"Understanding Child Development in Swimming" section repeats the PMC7174680 transition-rate data** that appears in at least two other articles ([[developmental-pathway]], [[body-type-and-swimming]]). Here the citation is used to make the point "patience is the strategy," which is a valid purpose for this article. However, the exact same statistics should not appear verbatim in three articles. Condense to one sentence: "Research shows that performance before age 17 does not predict elite senior outcomes — roughly 75% of top age-group swimmers do not become elite seniors. Patience is not optional; it is the strategy." Then wikilink to [[body-type-and-swimming]] for the full data.

2. **"Goal Setting That Works" section is genuinely useful but oddly placed.** A parent reading "Parent Playbook" is not expecting a section on how to teach their child to set goals — that reads as coaching, which this article explicitly says parents should not do. Reframe from "teach your child this framework" to "understand what goal orientation your child's coach is trying to build, and reinforce it at home by asking about process goals, not outcome goals."

## Graduation Decision: NEEDS-REVISION

One must-fix: Common Mistakes section must be revised to include specific, behavior-level mistakes rather than abstract principles. Return to wiki-compiler-agent.

## Must-Fix Before wiki/ Graduation

1. **D3 = 0: No "Common Mistakes" section for an entity article.** This article supports a significant financial decision (Stanford camp at $797-$914 per session is a material expenditure). Parents make specific, documentable errors when choosing swim camps. Required additions:
   - "Registering a child for Cal or Stanford camp who has not swum competitively: Both university camps require competitive experience and all four strokes. A child who has only done lessons or summer league swimming will not be able to keep up, and will have a demoralizing rather than developmental experience. If your child has not competed in USA Swimming meets, start with DACA's summer programs or a club-run beginner clinic."
   - "Waiting until June to register for summer camps: University swim camps in the Bay Area frequently fill months in advance. Cal and Stanford camps for popular summer sessions may be sold out by March or April. Register as early as January for summer sessions to secure a spot."
   - "Assuming camp attendance substitutes for club team training: A 3-day or week-long camp provides intensive technique exposure, not the sustained development of a club season. Camps are a supplement to club training, not a replacement."

For a camp costing $797-$914, this is genuinely useful information and correctly included. However, the Cal camp cancellation policy is not noted. Add a note: "Cal camp cancellation policy: contact US Sports Camps directly or check the registration terms before booking."

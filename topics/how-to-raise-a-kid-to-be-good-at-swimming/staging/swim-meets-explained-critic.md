# Critic Report — Swim Meets Explained — 2026-04-09

## Overall Assessment

An excellent first-meet reference article. The Pacific Swimming geography and time standard hierarchy are complete and well-sourced. The first-meet logistics section is specific enough to be genuinely useful. The Common Mistakes section is the strongest in the article set — all four mistakes are specific, observable, and non-generic. One gap: RO5 must-answer "What should I expect at my child's first swim meet?" is answered for logistics but not for the swimmer's emotional experience, which many first-meet parents find is the harder element to navigate.

## Must-Fix Before wiki/ Graduation

None.

## Should-Fix

1. **RO5 must-answer "What should I expect at my child's first swim meet?" omits the child's emotional experience.** Add 3-4 sentences to the "What to Expect at Your First Meet" section on what is normal for the swimmer: "Most first-time competitors experience significant pre-race anxiety. This is normal and does not indicate a bad fit for competitive swimming. Do not interpret nervousness as reluctance. Post-race, children commonly swing from elation to tears regardless of outcome — prepare for this range and respond with the same calm affirmation either way."

2. **Pacific Swimming zone table is over-detailed for a guide article.** The four-row zone table with county-level coverage detail serves a directory function, not a parent-education function. Condense to two sentences and move the zone-by-county detail into a Pacific Swimming entity article (which does not currently exist in staging). This would also eliminate the need for a parent to parse zone names like "Zone 1 North" and "Zone 1 South" before they understand why zones matter at all.

3. **"Observed Meets" type is included but irrelevant to the target audience.** Parents of youth swimmers will never encounter an "observed meet" situation. This paragraph adds length without reader value. Cut it.

## Graduation Decision: READY

No must-fix items. Should-fix items improve efficiency and the first-meet experience coverage but do not block graduation. This article can move to wiki/.

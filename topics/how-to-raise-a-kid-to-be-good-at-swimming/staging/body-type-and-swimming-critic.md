## Must-Fix Before wiki/ Graduation

1. **D3 = 0: No "Common Mistakes" section.** Parents specifically misuse body type information in ways this article should name:
   - "Interpreting a young child's physical build as a predictor of swimming potential: The anthropometry research applies to swimmers aged 17+ — measuring a 7-year-old's arm span or torso length has no predictive value for competitive swimming outcomes. The body that wins races at age 17 is not the body visible at age 7."
   - "Discouraging a child from swimming because they are small or appear to lack an 'athletic build': Many world-class swimmers were late physical developers. A child who is small at age 10 and shows enjoyment and motivation should not be redirected away from swimming on the basis of current body type."
   - "Taking 'early maturer advantage' too literally — believing your child's early-age dominance confirms long-term talent: The research explicitly shows that 75-79% of elite age-group swimmers do not become elite senior swimmers. Early competitive dominance is a sign of physical maturity, not of the talent that produces late-career elite performance."

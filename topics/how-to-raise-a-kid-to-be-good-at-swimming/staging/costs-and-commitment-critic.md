## Should-Fix

1. **Time commitment section does not help parents see what a competition week looks like concretely.** "8-12 hours per week for a 10-year-old" is an abstraction. Add a brief "sample week" illustration: "A 9-year-old on a club team with 4 practices/week (Tuesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday morning) plus a meet Sunday looks like: school pickup at 3:30 PM, drive 25 min each way to pool, practice 5-6:30 PM, three weekdays consumed. Add 6+ hours on a meet weekend. Before joining, map your family's standing commitments (siblings' activities, parent work schedules) against a practice schedule — time conflicts become visible only when you do this concretely."

2. **Financial aid section lists only four options with minimal detail.** "Many clubs offer scholarship or financial aid programs — ask directly, as these are rarely advertised" is good advice but does not tell the parent how to ask, or what to say when they ask. Add: "When asking about financial aid, request a meeting with the club director specifically about financial hardship or scholarship programs. Phrase it as 'We are very interested in [club name] but the annual dues are a stretch for our family — do you have any partial scholarship or sliding-scale options?' Many clubs have underpublicized aid funds and will offer support to families who ask directly."

3. **The USA Swimming registration section** notes that the $84/year figure is from 2021 and is stale, which the article correctly handles by directing parents to check usaswimming.org. However, the section should add a rough current magnitude: "As of 2024-2025, total USA Swimming + Pacific Swimming registration combined typically falls in the $80-$130 range depending on membership type." This allows parents to budget without a trip to the website.

No must-fix items. Should-fix items improve practical specificity but do not block graduation. This article can move to wiki/.

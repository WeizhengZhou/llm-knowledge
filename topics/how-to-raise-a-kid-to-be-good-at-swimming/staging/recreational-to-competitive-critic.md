## Must-Fix Before wiki/ Graduation

1. **RO5 must-answer "What are the real costs at each level (rec, club, travel)?" is answered only at the entry level.** The article provides entry-level cost ranges ($2,000-$4,000) but does not give the mid-level or elite/travel cost tiers that a parent needs to understand the commitment trajectory before they join. A parent deciding whether to join a club team at age 9 needs to know that the same sport costs $8,000-$15,000+ per year at the travel level 4-5 years later. Add one sentence explicitly stating the full cost trajectory: "Families should be aware that competitive swimming costs escalate significantly as swimmers advance — from $2,000-$4,000 at entry level to $8,000-$15,000+ at the elite/travel tier. Discuss the full cost trajectory with any club before joining." This does not replace [[costs-and-commitment]] — it places the trajectory information where the parent making this specific decision needs it.

## Should-Fix

1. **"Step 4: Start at the Right Level" contains a useful insight that is not named as a common parent error.** The article says "starting in a group that matches your child's current skill level — even if it is below their age group — prevents frustration." In practice, many parents resist this because it feels like their child is being held back. The compiler should move this insight into Common Mistakes as: "Insisting on age-group placement rather than skill-level placement: Clubs sort by ability, not age. A 10-year-old who belongs in an 8 & Under training group based on skill level should be placed there — a mismatched placement causes frustration and dropout."

2. **The "Bridge Option" section uses "close but not fully ready" as the only criterion for choosing a bridge program.** This is vague. Replace with a concrete indicator: "If your child can swim 25 yards of freestyle and backstroke independently but has not yet learned breaststroke or butterfly, a bridge program is the right next step. If they cannot yet swim 25 yards independently, they are not ready for a bridge program and need more lessons first."

## Graduation Decision: NEEDS-REVISION

One must-fix: add the full cost trajectory (not just entry-level) to the family readiness section so the decision-making parent has the complete picture. Return to wiki-compiler-agent.

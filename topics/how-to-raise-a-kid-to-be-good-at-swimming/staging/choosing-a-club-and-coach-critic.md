## Should-Fix

1. **"What is the training volume (yards per week) at different training group levels?" in the Questions to Ask section.** This question belongs in [[developmental-pathway]], not in a club-evaluation guide. A parent asking this question of a coach during an evaluation visit is doing the right thing; but the guide should tell them what a good answer looks like, not just list the question. Replace with: "What is the training volume (yards per week) at each training group level? A club that cannot answer this clearly is not managing athlete load systematically." This makes the question decision-useful.

2. **D4 gap note for research pipeline.** The fact-sheet has zero L4 community signals about club culture, parent experience with specific coaching styles, or common complaints about Bay Area clubs. The evolve-agent should be flagged: forum/community research on parent experience at Bay Area clubs would significantly improve this article's ability to answer "What distinguishes a good club from a mediocre one?" beyond the structural criteria already covered.

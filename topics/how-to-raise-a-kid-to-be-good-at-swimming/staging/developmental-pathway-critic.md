## Must-Fix Before wiki/ Graduation

1. **RO5 must-answer "What is a pre-team or bridge program and when is my child ready?" has no substantive answer in this article.** The current treatment is a single bullet in Common Mistakes ("Skipping the bridge program: Jumping from lessons directly to a competitive club...") and a wikilink. The article's stated overview says it "maps the stages a young swimmer progresses through" — the bridge/pre-team is a stage and should have its own row in the stage progression list (currently section "USA Swimming Age Groups" lists five stages starting at "Discovery / Development (8 & Under)" but skips the pre-team phase entirely). Add the pre-team stage between the opening paragraph and "Discovery / Development" with 3-4 sentences covering: what it is, the readiness criteria, and when to use it vs. going directly to club.

## Should-Fix

1. **"Age-by-Age Skill Benchmarks" section gives benchmarks without guidance.** Parents reading that a child ages 7-9 should be "introducing breaststroke" have no idea what to do if their 9-year-old has not reached that benchmark. Add two sentences: "If your child is behind these benchmarks, do not push up a training group — the benchmarks will come with continued technique work. If your child is significantly ahead, discuss readiness for the next training group with the coach, not based on benchmarks alone."

2. **"Late Developer Effect" overlaps substantially with [[body-type-and-swimming]].** The PMC7174680 citations (transition rates, world-class vs. lower-ranked performance before age 17, early maturers dominating ages 10-13) appear in both articles. This article should keep a summary of the parent-relevant conclusions (2-3 sentences) and link to [[body-type-and-swimming]] for the full analysis. The current duplication wastes reader attention and creates a maintenance problem when one article is updated.

## Graduation Decision: NEEDS-REVISION

One must-fix: add pre-team/bridge program stage to the developmental stages section. Return to wiki-compiler-agent.

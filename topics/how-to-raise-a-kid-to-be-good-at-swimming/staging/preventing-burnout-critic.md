## Must-Fix Before wiki/ Graduation

1. **RO7 must-answer "What is the right training volume by age?" is not answered in this article.** The article cites the AAP hours-per-week guideline to define overtraining risk, but does not give the actual training volume by age group. A parent who reads this article and wants to know if their child's current club is overtraining them cannot find the answer here — they must click to [[developmental-pathway]]. For a burnout prevention article where overtraining is a named risk factor, this is a critical gap. Add a minimum viable answer: a three-row summary table (8-and-under: 2-3 practices/week; 9-10: 4-5 practices/week; 11-12: 5-6 practices/week) with a note that the AAP recommends no more than hours-per-week equal to child's age. Link to [[developmental-pathway]] for the full table. This gives the parent enough to evaluate their child's current situation without leaving the article.

## Should-Fix

1. **"Overtraining" section tells the parent a symptom (elevated resting heart rate) but not the immediate response.** A parent who notices their 10-year-old's resting heart rate is elevated by 12 bpm for three days does not know what to do next. Add after the heart rate paragraph: "If you observe these signs, the first step is not a doctor visit — it is reducing training load. Ask the coach for a reduced practice schedule or rest week. If the coach is resistant to reducing load when a swimmer is showing these signs, that is a red flag for the club's training philosophy (see [[choosing-a-club-and-coach]])."

2. **"The Off-Ramp" section cites Swimming World Magazine warning about LTAD non-adherence.** The citation is used to support the off-ramp recommendation, but the warning is about "plateauing during adolescence" — which is not quite the same as justifying a break when a child wants to quit. The citation supports the broader LTAD argument but is only loosely connected to the specific paragraph's claim. Replace with the PMC dropout finding that most dropout reasons are controllable, which directly supports the idea that intervention (a break, a club change) can work.

## Graduation Decision: NEEDS-REVISION

One must-fix: add at minimum a summary training-volume reference so parents can evaluate overtraining risk without leaving the article. Return to wiki-compiler-agent.

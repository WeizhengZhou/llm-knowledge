## Must-Fix Before wiki/ Graduation

1. **D3 = 0: No "Common Mistakes" section.** This concept article directly supports the RO6 decision and parents make specific errors around specialization. Required additions:
   - "Confusing 'year-round USA Swimming membership' with 'specialization': A child can be on a USA Swimming club team year-round while still playing soccer in fall or basketball in winter. Club team membership does not automatically mean single-sport specialization. Ask the coach explicitly whether the team accommodates reduced attendance during another sport's season."
   - "Using a younger sibling's specialization timeline as a benchmark: If an older sibling specialized at 11 and succeeded, that is a sample size of one. The research average for specialization is ages 12-15; individual timelines vary."
   - "Treating a coach's push for year-round exclusivity before age 12 as a sign of elite program quality: A club that demands a child drop all other sports before age 12 is not following the consensus guidance of the AAP or any major sports medicine organization. This is a red flag, not a mark of seriousness."

A parent skimming this article needs to know whether their child meets the definition before they can evaluate whether the research applies to them. Move the definition block to the very top, before "What the Research Says."

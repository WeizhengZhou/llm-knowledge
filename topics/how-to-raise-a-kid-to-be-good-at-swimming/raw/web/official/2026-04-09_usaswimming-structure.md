# USA Swimming Club Finder & Organizational Structure

## Club Finder Features

USA Swimming provides an interactive map-based tool to locate competitive swimming programs. The platform allows users to search among "2,800+ USA Swimming clubs and hundreds of Learn to Swim programs" with filtering options for club type, Club Excellence designations (Bronze, Silver, Gold), and Safe Sport recognition status.

## Organization Structure

USA Swimming operates through a hierarchical governance model including:
- National leadership (Executive Team, Board of Directors)
- 2,800+ affiliated clubs across the country
- Local Swimming and Diving Committees (LSCs)
- Accredited officials and coaches

## Key Distinctions

**Competitive Clubs** (marked with blue pins) offer full membership in USA Swimming's competitive swimming structure with membership registration requirements.

**Learn to Swim Programs** operate separately, focusing on "water safety" and foundational instruction. Programs designated as part of USA Swimming's "Swim Lesson Provider Network" emphasize "trained and certified instructors," "low student to teacher ratios," and established curricula—distinguishing them from standard recreational lessons.

The distinction reflects USA Swimming's dual mission: developing competitive swimmers while expanding water safety access through structured learning programs.

## Youth Programs

USA Swimming offers several initiatives for young athletes:

**Learn-to-Swim**: Basic instruction programs help beginners develop foundational skills.

**Block Party**: This initiative has engaged "more than 31,000 athletes" in club-focused competitions designed to increase participation among youth swimmers.

**National Junior Team**: A competitive pathway for developing junior-level athletes, with team camps and progression opportunities toward the senior National Team.

**Club-Based Development**: The organization emphasizes "finding a swim team and lessons" through local clubs affiliated with LSCs.

## Educational Resources

USA Swimming University provides educational content for members and non-members, covering training techniques, mental wellness, nutrition, and anti-doping information tailored to swimmers and parents.

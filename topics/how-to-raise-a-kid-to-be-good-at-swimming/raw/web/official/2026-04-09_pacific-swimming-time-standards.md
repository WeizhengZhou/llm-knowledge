## Available Standards Documents (2025–2026 Season)

Pacific Swimming publishes time standards in three course formats:
- **SCY** — Short Course Yards (25-yard pool; primary fall/winter/spring season format)
- **LCM** — Long Course Meters (50-meter pool; primary summer season format)
- **SCM** — Short Course Meters (25-meter pool; international format, less common in youth)

## Standards Hierarchy Explained (Developmental to Elite)

Pacific Swimming uses a tiered time standard system. Faster is harder to achieve. Order from slowest (entry) to fastest (elite):

### Age Group Standards (for swimmers 18 & Under):

1. **PC-B** — Entry-level developmental standard. The slowest qualifying benchmark. Swimmers achieving PC-B times can enter mid-level invitationals and sanctioned meets. A meaningful first goal for new competitive swimmers.

2. **PC-A** — Upper developmental standard. Swimmers achieving PC-A times demonstrate consistent, quality training. Often a benchmark clubs use to advance swimmers to more competitive practice groups.

3. **AGC (Age Group Championship)** — Qualifies swimmer for Pacific Swimming's three annual championship meets:
   - March Age Group Championships
   - July Age Group Championships
   - December Age Group Championships
   These are the first "big meet" milestone in most Bay Area competitive swimming pathways.

4. **Far Western (Q time / FW)** — The fastest Pacific Swimming age group standard. Qualifies for:
   - Spring Far Western Championships (April)
   - Summer Far Western Championships (July)
   Far Western times are a significant achievement indicating genuine competitive development.

### Senior Standards (age-independent; for elite/older swimmers):

Fastest → Slowest:
- National Championships
- Junior Nationals
- Pro Swim Series
- Sectionals
- Senior Circuit
- Senior 2
- Senior Open

## Age Groups in Pacific Swimming

| Age Group | Notes |
|-----------|-------|
| 8 & Under | Youngest competitive age group; short events (25 yd, 50 yd); no qualifying times required at most local meets |
| 10 & Under | Adds 100 yd events; first time standards widely enforced |
| 11–12 | Full event schedule; AGC qualification becomes a common goal |
| 13–14 | Transition group; some swimmers reach Senior-level meets |
| 15–16 | High school era; CIF and club swimming coexist |
| 17–18 | Final age group before Senior; some reach Junior Nationals |

## How Bay Area Kids Progress Through Standards

Typical timeline for a dedicated, year-round swimmer:

| Age | Milestone |
|-----|-----------|
| 6–8 | Local dual meets; no qualifying times required; 25 yd / 50 yd events |
| 9–10 | First invitationals; working toward PC-B times |
| 10–11 | PC-B achieved in primary events; entering sanctioned invitationals |
| 11–12 | PC-A and AGC qualification; first Age Group Championships |
| 13–14 | Far Western qualification attempt; some reach Senior Open |
| 15–16 | Senior 2 / Senior Circuit; high school + club dual commitment |
| 17–18 | College recruitment swimming; Junior Nationals targets |

## Key Terms

- **SWIMS database** — USA Swimming's national time database; all sanctioned meet times auto-uploaded
- **Short course yards (SCY)** — 25-yard pool; primary format for most of the competitive year
- **Long course meters (LCM)** — 50-meter pool; summer season; considered harder; more relevant for international competition
- **Deck seeding** — Swimmer must check in 60 minutes before event at most PC meets
- **Timed finals** — Simple format: place determined by heat time; common at smaller meets
- **Trials & finals** — Morning prelims qualify afternoon finalists; used at championships

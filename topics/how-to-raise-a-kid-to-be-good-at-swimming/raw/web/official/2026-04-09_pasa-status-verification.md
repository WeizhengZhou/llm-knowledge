# PASA (Palo Alto Stanford Aquatics) — Status Verification Research

## Question Being Answered

The wiki-critic BLOCKED the PASA staging article because it could not confirm whether PASA is currently an independent operating club or has been merged into Alto Swim Club. This file documents the targeted research conducted to resolve that question.

## VERDICT: PASA IS ACTIVE AND INDEPENDENT

PASA (Palo Alto Stanford Aquatics) is **currently an active, independent USA Swimming club** distinct from Alto Swim Club. It appears in the Pacific Swimming Zone 1 North official club directory as a separate registered club and maintains its own website, registration system, and coaching staff.

## Evidence 2: What Actually Happened in 2018 (Alto Swim Club Formation)

Source: Alto Swim Club website (altoswimclub.com/stanford-swimming-and-pasa-form-alto); SwimSwam article

In **fall 2018**, Stanford Women's Swimming partnered with the **PASA Cardinal Site** (one of PASA's six locations) to form Alto Swim Club. Key facts:

- Only the PASA Cardinal Site location was involved in the 2018 merger/transition — not all of PASA
- PASA's remaining five sites continued operating under the PASA name and brand
- Alto Swim Club trains at Avery Aquatic Center (Stanford campus) and multiple Palo Alto-area facilities
- Alto operates as a fully independent organization, no longer affiliated with PASA

The search result summary from the PASA Rinconada site confirms: "PASA sites have always operated independently of each other, and teams under the PASA umbrella remain at five other locations – Alpine Hills, Foothills Tennis & Swim Club, Fremont Hills Country Club, Los Altos Golf & Country Club, and Rinconada – but Alto is now independent."

## Evidence 5: PASA Organization History (L1-official)

Source: pasa-rinconada.org

- Founded 1955 as Palo Alto Swim Club (PASC) — historically the third oldest surviving non-profit USA Swimming club still operational today
- PASC underwent name changes and leadership transitions
- 2003: Merged with area clubs to become Palo Alto Stanford Aquatics (PASA)
- 2018: PASA Cardinal Site location separated to co-found Alto Swim Club with Stanford Swimming — PASA as an organization was not disbanded
- PASA has achieved a USA Swimming National Championship Team Title and has placed swimmers on Olympic Swimming Teams

## Summary for Wiki-Compiler

**PASA is Option A: Active Independent Club**

PASA currently operates as an independent USA Swimming club with approximately 1,000 competitive members across six sites in the Palo Alto area. It is NOT the same as Alto Swim Club. The two clubs are separately registered with Pacific Swimming, maintain separate coaching staffs, and compete under separate USA Swimming team abbreviations (PASA vs. ALTO).

The 2018 event was not a merger of PASA into Alto — it was the departure of one PASA site (the Cardinal/Stanford site) to form a new independent club called Alto. PASA lost one location but continued operating at its other five locations without interruption.

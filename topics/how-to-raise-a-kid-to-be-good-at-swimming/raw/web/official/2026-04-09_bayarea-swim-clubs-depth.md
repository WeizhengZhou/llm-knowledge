## Alto Swim Club (ALTO) — Menlo Park / Palo Alto Area

**Reputation:** National-caliber club. Won 1st place at the 2019 USA Swimming Phillips 66 National Championship.

**Club Excellence:** USA Swimming Club Excellence Silver Medal Team (2021)

**Head Coach:** Laura Mitchell — has coached Far Western and Junior Olympic champions; holds Pacific Swimming record holders.

**Age groups served:** 8 & Under through Senior

**Program Philosophy:** Develops independent athletes; strong emphasis on swimmer autonomy. Parents are kept off the pool deck (coach's space). Focus on long-term athletic development.

**Location:** Palo Alto / Menlo Park area (Silicon Valley/Peninsula)

**Contact:** info@altoswimclub.com | 650-308-9110

**Training Groups:**
- Ages 8 & Under: Discovery 1/2, Imagination 1/2, Age Group Bronze (3 practices/week, 1 hour)
- Ages 9-10: Discovery 2/3, Imagination 2/3, Age Group Silver (4-5 practices/week)
- Ages 11-12: Age Group Gold (up to 6 practices/week, 2-2.5 hours, targets Far Western qualifying)
- Senior groups: Competition at highest national levels

## Palo Alto Stanford Aquatics (PASA)

**Location:** Palo Alto (uses Stanford Aquatic Center and other Peninsula facilities)

**Reputation:** One of California's premier age group clubs; historically strong at Far Westerns and Junior Nationals.

**Affiliation:** Associated with Stanford University athletic program tradition.

**Note:** Contact via pacswim.org zone listings for current coaching staff and tryout information.

## De Anza Cupertino Aquatics (DACA)

**Location:** Cupertino / South Bay

## YMCA Programs for Beginners and Younger Children (Ages 4-7)

### YMCA of San Francisco
- **Embarcadero YMCA:** Swim lessons for all ages and abilities, including young children
- **YMCA Pacific Aquatic Club (YPAC):** Competitive team at Presidio YMCA; accepts ages 7-18; USA Swimming member

### YMCA of the East Bay
- Locations: Berkeley, Oakland, Pleasant Hill
- Youth swim lessons: ages 6-12, five swimming levels
- Group, private, and semi-private options year-round
- Registration opens annually August 1

### YMCA of Silicon Valley
- Multiple South Bay locations
- Youth swim programs including lessons and pre-competitive groups

**Key distinction:** YMCA swim lessons are generally more affordable and accessible for beginning swimmers ages 4-7. YMCA competitive clubs (like YPAC) are a USA Swimming-affiliated pathway that is less intense than private clubs at the entry level.

# Bay Area Summer Swim Camps 2026

Sources: ussportscamps.com, calsportscamps.com, berkeleyparentsnetwork.org, recwell.berkeley.edu

---

## Cal (UC Berkeley) Summer Swim Camp 2026

**Operator:** US Sports Camps / Cal Sports Camps

**URL:** https://www.ussportscamps.com/swim/cal/california-swim-camp

**Dates (2026):**
- Session 1: June 1–3 (Monday–Wednesday)
- Session 2: June 8–10 (Monday–Wednesday)
- Session 3: June 15–17 (Monday–Wednesday)
- Session 4: June 22–24 (Monday–Wednesday)
- Session 5: August 22–23 (Saturday–Sunday, 2-day)

**Ages:** 9–17 (coed)

**Cost:**
- 3-day sessions: $611 (all fees included)
- 2-day August session: $491 (all fees included)
- Team discount: $25 off per camper when 5+ register together

**Skill Requirement:** Participants must have competitive swim knowledge. Does NOT teach beginners how to swim. Swimmers should have experience with all four competitive strokes (freestyle, backstroke, breaststroke, butterfly) through USA Swimming age groups, high school, or summer league.

**Program Focus:**
- Stroke technique for all four strokes
- Sessions I & III: live video analysis of each swimmer's strokes
- Sessions II & IV: race preparation, culminating in a "mock meet"
- Starts and turns instruction

**Facilities:** Legends Aquatics Center or Spieker Aquatic Complex (UC Berkeley)

**Schedule:** 9 AM–3 PM

**What's Included:** Morning and afternoon pool training, camp t-shirt, prizes, 10:1 camper-to-staff ratio

**Note:** Lunch not provided; campers bring their own meals.

## Stanford Swim Camp 2026

**Operator:** US Sports Camps (Nike Swim Camps)

**URL:** https://www.ussportscamps.com/swim/nike/stanford-swim-camp

**Dates (2026):**
- Session 1: June 8–11 (4 days) — $914
- Session 2: June 15–18 (4 days) — $914
- Session 3: June 19–21 (3 days, Training Camp) — $797
- Session 4: June 22–25 (4 days) — $875
- Session 5: June 26–28 (3 days, Training Camp) — $797
- Session 6: June 29–July 2 (4 days) — $914

**Ages:** 9–17

**Skill Requirement:** Competitive swimming experience strongly recommended. Must be able to complete a continuous 200-yard swim. Experience with all four strokes, diving, starts, and turns.

**Program Focus:**
- Technique instruction on all four strokes, starts, and turns
- Video analysis of elite swimmers
- Dryland training (strength, mobility, nutrition guidance)
- Classroom sessions covering race video/stats from elite athletes

**Facilities:** Avery Aquatics Center (Stanford University — Olympic-sized pools)

**What's Included:** Nike camp t-shirt and swim cap, dryland sessions

**Cancellation Policy:** Refunds available if cancelled 14+ days before start (minus $100 fee); no refunds within 14 days.

## UC Berkeley Pre-Team and Youth Swim Programs (Year-Round)

**URL:** https://recwell.berkeley.edu/youth-recreation/year-round-programs/youth-swim-programs/

**Programs Available:**
- Learn to Swim / Advanced Swim (beginner and intermediate)
- Pre-Team Swim Training (pre-competitive bridge program)

**Pre-Team Skill Requirements:** Ability to swim 25 yards unassisted, basic freestyle and backstroke kick, Learn-to-Swim Level 4 or higher

**Pre-Team Levels:**
1. Novice: Freestyle/backstroke refinement, drills, breaststroke/butterfly introduction
2. Intermediate: All four strokes, flip turns, dives, pace clocks
3. Low Advanced (ages 13+): Classic swim-team format, all four strokes, racing skills

## Other Bay Area Summer Swim Camp Options

**Berkeley Parents Network** (berkeleyparentsnetwork.org/recommend/summer/swim) maintains a community-curated list of Bay Area swim camps, including:
- Club team summer clinics (Alto Swim Club, PASA, DACA often run summer clinics or intensives)
- YMCA summer swim camps (various Bay Area locations, more recreational-oriented)
- High school pool summer camps (contact local high school aquatics programs)

**Note for Beginners (Ages 4-7):** The Cal and Stanford camps are for competitive swimmers only. For beginner young children, better options are:
- YMCA swim lessons (ymcasf.org, ymcaeastbay.org, ymcasv.org)
- Menlo Swim & Sport (menloswim.com) — recreational and pre-competitive programs
- Local swim school summer intensives

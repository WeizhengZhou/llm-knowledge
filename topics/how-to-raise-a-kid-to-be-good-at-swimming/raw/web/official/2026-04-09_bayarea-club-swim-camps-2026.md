## Comparison: University vs. Club Summer Camps

| Program | Ages | Skill Level | Approximate Cost | Duration |
|---------|------|-------------|-----------------|----------|
| Cal (UC Berkeley) Swim Camp | 9–17 | Competitive only | $491–$611 per session | 2–3 days |
| Stanford (Nike Swim Camp) | 9–17 | Competitive preferred | $797–$914 per session | 3–4 days |
| Alto Swim Club Peak Performance | 9–13 | Competitive | Not published | 4 days |
| DACA Summer Lessons | All ages | All levels | Not published | 1 week |
| Santa Clara Swim Club | All ages | All levels | Not published | Varies |
| YMCA Summer Swim Programs | All ages | Beginner–Intermediate | ~$100–$250/week | 1 week |

**Key insight for parents:** University-affiliated camps (Cal, Stanford) publish pricing openly and are the most structured option for competitive swimmers ages 9+. Club-specific clinics often require direct contact for pricing and are more variable in structure.

**For beginners and young children (ages 4–8):** University camps are not suitable. Better options are YMCA programs, Menlo Swim & Sport recreational classes, or club pre-team programs (see bayarea-swim-clubs-depth.md and bayarea-summer-camps-2026.md for YMCA and Menlo Swim details).

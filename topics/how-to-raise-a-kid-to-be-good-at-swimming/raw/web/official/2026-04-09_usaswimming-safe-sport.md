# USA Swimming Safe Sport — Parent Guide

Source: usaswimming.org/swimmers-parents/parents/safe-sport-for-parents (L1 official)

## Core Mission

USA Swimming prioritizes athlete safety with zero tolerance for abuse. The organization states: "No form of abuse, including child sexual abuse, has a place in our sport."

---

## Key Policies and Protections

### Minor Athlete Abuse Prevention Policy (MAAPP)
A comprehensive framework with three components:
1. Education/training requirements
2. Required prevention policies
3. Recommended prevention policies

Developed to meet federal obligations under the Safe Sport Authorization Act.

### Background Screening (Required)
All adult members must complete USA Swimming-approved criminal background checks. Clubs conduct additional pre-employment screening. This applies to all coaches, assistants, and non-athlete volunteers.

### Mandatory Training
Coaches and volunteers receive mandatory training on:
- Abuse prevention policies
- Best practices for athlete protection
- Minor Athlete Abuse Prevention Policies (MAAPP)
- Anti-bullying policy

### Safe Sport Club Recognition Program
Allows member clubs to demonstrate commitment to creating healthy, positive environments. Clubs earn recognition by implementing:
- Club governance measures
- Safe Sport policies and reporting mechanisms
- Safe Sport best practices
- Training for athletes and parents

**This recognition status is searchable** — parents can check whether their club participates.

## Red Flag Behaviors for Parents

### Emotionally Abusive Coaching:
- Repeated name-calling, constant screaming, or degrading comments
- Denied basic needs (water, bathroom breaks, medical care) as punishment
- Humiliation or body-shaming behavior
- Child exhibits fear before practice or signs of anxiety/sadness

### Boundary Violations:
- "Not respecting boundaries or listening when someone says 'no'"
- Touching after a child or parent has indicated it is unwanted
- Contact that exceeds normal coaching instruction parameters
- Seeking alone time with specific children or making excuses for private contact
- Providing gifts, special favors, or displaying obvious favoritism
- Attempting to assume inappropriate friendship role instead of maintaining professional distance

### Grooming Patterns:
- Excessive focus on a single child's development
- "Unusual interest in a child's sexual development, including commenting on sexual characteristics"
- Demonstrating "extreme jealousy of other individuals in a child's life"
- Discussing personal problems with young athletes
- Initiating contact outside of structured club activities
- Giving gifts

### Bullying Indicators:
- Repeated unwanted behavior with power imbalance
- Discriminatory language or deliberate isolation
- Harassment via social media or altered photographs

### Behavioral Changes in Children to Watch For:
- Increased level of sadness
- Loss of interest in swimming or extreme anxiety before/after practice
- Sense of "walking on eggshells" around the coach
- Reluctance to be alone with a specific adult

## How to Report Concerns

**U.S. Center for SafeSport** (Sexual misconduct/harassment/explicit communication):
- Phone: 833-5US-SAFE (587-7233)
- Online: safesport.org

**USA Swimming** (Physical/emotional misconduct, policy violations):
- Online reporting form: usaswimming.org/safe-sport/report-a-concern
- Anonymous text: 888-270-SWIM (7946)
- Email: SafeSport@usaswimming.org

**Your Club** (Bullying, peer conflicts, team code violations):
- Club Safe Sport coordinator

**Important:** Pursuant to federal law, all adults authorized to interact with minor or amateur athletes who learn of facts giving reason to suspect child abuse must report to law enforcement and/or state child protection agency AND the U.S. Center for SafeSport within 24 hours.

Reports may be made anonymously. No cost/fee for filing. No retaliation is permitted against good-faith reporters.

## What Parents Should Expect From Any USA Swimming Club

Any USA Swimming member club must:
1. Conduct criminal background checks on all adult coaches/staff
2. Complete Safe Sport/Athlete Protection Training
3. Follow the Minor Athlete Abuse Prevention Policy (MAAPP)
4. Provide a reporting mechanism for concerns

Parents should verify:
- Club's Safe Sport Recognition status (check usaswimming.org)
- Coach's current USA Swimming membership (good standing = background check + training completed)
- Club's stated Safe Sport policies (ask to see them)

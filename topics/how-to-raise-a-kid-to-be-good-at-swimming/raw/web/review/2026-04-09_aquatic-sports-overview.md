# Aquatic Sports Related to Competitive Swimming

## Source: Wikipedia (synchronized swimming) + search synthesis

## Governing Body

World Aquatics (formerly FINA) governs all competitive aquatic sports internationally, including:
- Pool swimming (freestyle, backstroke, breaststroke, butterfly, individual medley)
- Open water swimming
- Artistic swimming (formerly synchronized swimming)
- Water polo
- Diving (platform and springboard)
- High diving

## Artistic Swimming (Synchronized Swimming)

**Governing Bodies:** World Aquatics (international); USA Artistic Swimming (national); Synchro Canada

**Youth Competitive Structure (USA):**
- Age categories: 10 & under, 12 & under, 13-15 (provincial/regional)
- Novice, Intermediate, and age group levels for younger swimmers
- USA Artistic Swimming organizes national championships

**Skill Requirements:**
- Demands specialized techniques: "eggbeater kick" and various "sculls" (hand movements)
- Acrobatic elements require gymnastics and diving skills in addition to swimming
- Distinct from competitive swimming—it's "a hybrid form of swimming, dance and gymnastics"
- Body awareness and flexibility often more important than pure swim speed

**Relationship to Pool Swimming:**
- Swimming proficiency required but not sufficient
- Artistic/gymnastic skills equally important
- Presented as a distinct sport pathway, not a direct progression from competitive swimming

## Water Polo

**Governing Body:** USA Water Polo (national); World Aquatics (international)
- Youth development program: USA Water Polo Olympic Development Program (ODP)
- Players need strong freestyle and eggbeater kick foundation
- Cross-training benefit: water polo players are comfortable swimming with heads out of water
- Strategic/tactical sport requiring swimming fitness plus ball skills

## Open Water Swimming

**Distance:** 5K, 10K (Olympic distance), 25K at international level
- Requires strong pool swimming base but adds navigation, drafting, and sighting skills
- USA Swimming sanctions open water events
- Popular pathway for distance swimmers transitioning from pool

## Diving (Springboard and Platform)

**Governing Body:** USA Diving; World Aquatics
- Limited skill transfer from competitive swimming
- Requires acrobatic/gymnastics skills plus water entry technique
- Youth programs: age groups starting around 8-10 years old

## Triathlon

**Governing Body:** USA Triathlon; World Triathlon
- Swim leg: open water, typically 750m (sprint) to 3.8km (Ironman)
- Swimming background provides strong foundation
- Cross-training with cycling and running required
- Youth/junior triathlon programs available from ages 7-8

## Summary of Relationships to Pool Swimming

| Sport | Swimming Foundation Required | Additional Skills | Age Entry |
|-------|------------------------------|-------------------|-----------|
| Artistic Swimming | Yes (intermediate) | Dance, gymnastics | 6-8 years |
| Water Polo | Yes (strong) | Ball handling, strategy | 8-10 years |
| Open Water Swimming | Yes (advanced) | Navigation, drafting | 12+ years typically |
| Diving | Basic only | Gymnastics, acrobatics | 8-10 years |
| Triathlon | Yes (intermediate-advanced) | Cycling, running | 7-8 years |

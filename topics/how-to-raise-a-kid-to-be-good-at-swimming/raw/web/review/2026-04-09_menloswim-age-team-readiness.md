# Swim Team Age Recommendations & Readiness (Menlo Swim & Sport)

## Recommended Age Range
"The typical age for a child to join a swim team is 5-7 years old," according to Menlo Swim & Sport's guidance.

## Key Readiness Indicators

Before joining, children should demonstrate:

1. **Water Confidence**: Kids must feel secure swimming independently from wall to wall without an instructor nearby, as coaches aren't always in the water during team practice.

2. **Stroke Technique**: Menlo Mavericks requires swimmers to execute strong lengths of freestyle, backstroke, and breaststroke, plus basic butterfly understanding.

3. **Team Readiness**: Swimming involves individual effort but operates as a team sport. Children should be prepared to work alongside teammates toward collective goals.

4. **Listening & Instruction**: Experience following directions from authority figures—typically developed through preschool, school, and swim lessons.

5. **Enthusiasm**: A genuine love of water and excitement about team participation significantly contributes to success.

## Program Benefits

Joining a swim team helps children build confidence, develop technical skills and endurance, and enjoy recreational benefits alongside competitive experience.

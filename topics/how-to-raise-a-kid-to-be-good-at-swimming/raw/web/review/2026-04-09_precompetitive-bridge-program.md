# Pre-Team / Bridge Programs in Swimming

Sources: UC Berkeley RecWell, USA Swimming Bridge Programs article, Menlo Swim & Sport

---

## What is a Bridge / Pre-Team Program?

Bridge programs (also called pre-team, pre-competitive, stroke school, or youth fitness programs) are an extension of swim lessons where competitive strokes and skills are taught and refined.

**Purpose:** To "bridge" the gap between swim lessons and competitive team swimming.

**From USA Swimming (2017):** "Bridge programs are an extension of swim lessons where competitive strokes and skills are taught and refined."

---

## What These Programs Teach

### Curriculum Typical Content

1. **Competitive stroke technique** — refinement of all four competitive strokes (freestyle, backstroke, breaststroke, butterfly) — not just introduction, but proper competitive form
2. **Circle swimming** — how to share a lane in a workout group setting
3. **Drop and push starts** — competitive dive technique
4. **Flip turns** — tumble turn at the wall for freestyle and backstroke
5. **Pace clock usage** — reading the pace clock to track rest intervals and set times
6. **Breathing techniques** — side breathing, bilateral breathing, underwater dolphin kicks
7. **Stamina building** — transitioning from 30-minute lessons to 60-90 minute workout endurance
8. **Drills** — technique-focused repetitions that isolate specific movement patterns
9. **Team environment skills** — working in a group, responding to a coach's whistle, following workout structure

## UC Berkeley Pre-Team Swim Training (Bay Area Example)

**URL:** recwell.berkeley.edu/youth-recreation/year-round-programs/youth-swim-programs/pre-team-swim-training/

**Entry Requirements:**
- Must be able to swim 25 yards unassisted
- Basic freestyle and backstroke kick demonstrated
- Body dolphin (dolphin kick on back or front) present
- Learn-to-Swim Level 4 or equivalent

**Required Equipment:** Swimmers must obtain their own snorkel and fins ("great tools for developing body awareness and refining the timing and muscle memory of the four strokes")

### Three Training Levels

**Novice:**
- Emphasis: freestyle and backstroke refinement with swim-team-style drills
- Introduces: kicking, breathing techniques, basic breaststroke and butterfly exposure

**Intermediate:**
- Emphasis: all four strokes with greater focus and detail
- Introduces: flip turns, dives, pace clock usage, competitive fundamentals

**Low Advanced (ages 13+):**
- Format: "classic swim-team format, focusing on drills to improve and refine all four strokes and racing skills"
- Designed for older swimmers who are not yet ready for a full competitive club

**URL:** menloswim.com/lessons/swim-program/

The Menlo Swim Bridge program specifically prepares swimmers for transition from recreational swim lessons to USA Swimming club team. It targets swimmers who have basic stroke competency but need refinement before joining a full competitive team.

## Graduation Criteria — When Is a Child Ready for a Full Team?

**From USA Swimming guidance:**
A child is most likely ready to try out for a USA Swimming club team when they:
1. Have a basic command of all four competitive swim strokes (freestyle, backstroke, breaststroke, butterfly)
2. Are ready mentally and emotionally for structured competition
3. Express a clear desire to join a swim team

**Common practical benchmarks:**
- Can swim the length of a pool (25 yards) unassisted in multiple strokes
- Can attend 60-90 minute practices without significant behavioral difficulty
- Has mastered flip turns and can execute competitive starts
- Understands circle swimming and basic workout etiquette

## Skill Benchmarks by Stage

From academic swimming milestones research and USA Swimming Level descriptions:

| Age Range | Skill Benchmarks |
|-----------|-----------------|
| 3-5 years | Water comfort, floating, freestyle/backstroke introduction with assistance |
| 6-7 years | Independent freestyle 25 yards, basic backstroke, water safety skills |
| 7-9 years | Freestyle and backstroke proficiency, breaststroke introduction, beginning butterfly body movement |
| 9-10 years | All four strokes with basic proficiency, flip turns, basic starts, ready for pre-team/bridge |
| 10-12 years | All four strokes with competitive form, 200 IM capability, race strategy introduction |
| 12-14 years | Stroke refinement, training volume increase, technique-focused distance |
| 14-18 years | Specialized training, racing strategy, event specialization |

## Overview

Alto Swim Club is located in the Palo Alto / Menlo Park area. According to Alto Swim Club, the club was formed from the merger of Stanford Swimming and PASA Cardinal Swimming. According to Alto Swim Club, the club won 1st place at the 2019 USA Swimming Phillips 66 National Championship. Club profiles report that Alto Swim Club earned USA Swimming Club Excellence Silver Medal Team designation in 2021.

## Training Groups

According to Alto Swim Club, training groups are structured as follows: 8 & Under (3 practices/week, 1 hour), ages 9-10 (4-5 practices/week), ages 11-12 (up to 6 practices/week, 2-2.5 hours, targeting Far Western qualifying).

## Who Alto Suits Best

Alto is a competitive-track club best suited for swimmers who are already committed to competitive swimming or whose families are prepared for the competitive pathway. The club has structured Discovery and Imagination training groups for 8 & Under and 9-10 swimmers, so it does accept beginners at the younger age-group level. However, Alto's identity is built around national-level competitive excellence (2019 National Championship; Club Excellence Silver Medal). Families looking for a casual, recreation-first introduction to team swimming may find the culture and expectations oriented more toward competitive development than they need. For a first swim club experience with a child who has no competitive background, consider starting with a YMCA program, a rec league, or a club like [[daca]] that operates both a learn-to-swim school and a competitive team under one roof.

## Coaching Philosophy

Alto emphasizes developing independent athletes with strong swimmer autonomy. Parents are kept off the pool deck (coach's space). The focus is on long-term athletic development, consistent with the [[ltad-model]].

Alto Swim Club states: 'The coach's tent and pool area are for swimmers only. Parents should only be on deck for timing or officiating.'

## Peak Performance Clinic (2026)

According to Alto Swim Club, the Peak Performance Clinic 2026 is for competitive swimmers ages 9-13, held at Gunn Pool in Palo Alto in a 4-day Monday-Thursday format (10 AM-1:30 PM) across five sessions from June 23 to July 24; pricing requires contacting the club directly.

## Common Mistakes

- **Joining Alto for the prestige rather than the developmental fit:** Alto's 2019 National Championship and Club Excellence designation reflect the senior/elite program. The quality of the entry-level 8 & Under program should be evaluated on its own merits -- observe a Discovery group practice and talk to current families with young swimmers before deciding.
- **Not verifying current training group availability before committing:** Competitive clubs frequently have waitlists for specific age groups. Contact Alto directly (info@altoswimclub.com, 650-308-9110) to confirm current openings in the Discovery or Imagination groups before selecting Alto as your target club.
- **Expecting a low-key recreational experience:** Alto is a competitive club. Even at the 8 & Under level, the environment is structured and goal-oriented. If your child is not yet comfortable with a coach-directed group workout format, a pre-team or bridge program may be a better starting point (see [[getting-started]]).

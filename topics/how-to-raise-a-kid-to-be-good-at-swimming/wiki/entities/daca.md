## Overview

De Anza Cupertino Aquatics (DACA) is located in Cupertino and serves all ages from beginners through elite competitive swimmers. DACA operates both a swim school (learn-to-swim programs) and a competitive team, making it one of the few Bay Area clubs offering a complete pathway from first lessons to national-level competition.

## Training Groups

DACA's competitive team is typically structured in a progressive group system. Based on what is known from research and common Bay Area club structures, DACA's groups generally include:

- **Junior Dolphins / Learn-to-Swim:** Beginner instruction for all ages; the swim school side of DACA's program
- **Age Group:** Developing competitive swimmers transitioning from lessons to structured training; introduction to competitive meets
- **Senior:** Advanced competitive swimmers training at high volume; targeting Far Western, Sectionals, and national-level competition

The specific group names, practice schedules, and placement criteria change periodically. Contact DACA directly at daca.org for current training group descriptions and tryout information.

**The beginner-to-elite pathway:** DACA's distinctiveness in the Bay Area is that a child can start in their learn-to-swim program with no swimming experience and progress through the entire competitive pipeline without ever changing clubs. A child moves from swim school lessons to the competitive team's entry-level group when their coach assesses readiness -- typically when they can swim all four strokes and handle a 60-minute group workout. This integrated pathway eliminates the transition friction that families at other clubs face when moving from a separate lesson provider to a club team.

## Summer Programs

Club sources indicate DACA offers accelerated learn-to-swim summer programs in one-week sessions from June through August, open to all ages and skill levels; current pricing requires checking daca.org directly. Check daca.org for current session dates, pricing, and registration. Contact the club directly for competitive team openings.

## Common Mistakes

- **Assuming the swim school and competitive team have the same coaching staff and culture:** DACA operates both programs, but the learn-to-swim instructors and the competitive team coaches may be different people with different approaches. Confirm with the club how the transition between programs is managed and who evaluates readiness for the competitive team.
- **Not checking current availability:** DACA's website uses dynamic content that changes frequently. Prices, session dates, and team openings on the website at any given time may not reflect current availability. Call or email directly for current status.
- **Choosing DACA only because it is the "one-stop shop":** DACA's integrated pathway is a genuine advantage, but the quality of the coaching at the competitive level should be evaluated independently of the convenience. Observe a competitive team practice (not just a lesson) before committing.

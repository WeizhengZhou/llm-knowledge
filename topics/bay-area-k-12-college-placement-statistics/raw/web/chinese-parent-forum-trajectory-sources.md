## 1. 一亩三分地 (1point3acres.com)

**URL:** https://www.1point3acres.com/bbs/
**Key section:** 为人父母 (Parenting), forum-348 — https://www.1point3acres.com/bbs/forum-348-1.html
**Tag page:** https://www.1point3acres.com/bbs/tag/%E4%B8%BA%E4%BA%BA%E7%88%B6%E6%AF%8D-5891-18.html

### What data is available

The "为人父母" (For Our Children) section organizes threads by K-12 stage:
- Preschool/Kindergarten: 438 threads
- Grade 2: 310 threads
- Elementary: 220 threads
- Middle School: 163 threads
- High School: 57 threads

Total posts in the section: approximately 1,754 (paginated). The tag page claims 1,148 discussion threads and 29,276 replies across the parenting topic.

A separate thread (thread-1075163) on the Real Estate section provides **school-level data** — Bay Area high school rankings with UC admission rate breakdowns by school (Lynbrook, Mission San Jose, Gunn, Los Altos, Monta Vista named explicitly). This is aggregate institutional data, not individual trajectories.

### Trajectory specificity

**Low.** Threads reviewed (thread-749655 and the forum listing page) show discussions about educational philosophy, activity balancing, and general concerns rather than documented year-by-year student trajectories. One thread (thread-1002815) asked "Is American K-12 education really that bad?" — 95+ replies, philosophical, no individual timelines.

The "鸡娃" and "爬藤" topics surface in thread titles, but actual content discussing AMC scores by grade, year-by-year competition results, or school choice sequences is not prominently visible in the browsable listing. This content may exist deeper in threads or may require login to view.

### Volume

Thousands of threads. 68 pages in the 名校|爬藤 section is visible via associated forum at wenxuecity (see below); 1point3acres parenting forum is smaller.

### Language

Chinese only.

### Accessibility

**Login required to view thread replies.** Thread titles are visible without login; content behind authentication wall. A confirmed login gate is shown on thread-749655 and thread-1075163.

### Scrapability

**Low without authentication.** Thread listings (titles, byte counts) are public. Full reply content requires a registered account. No API documented.

### Bay Area specificity

The Real Estate-adjacent thread (1075163) is explicitly Bay Area school data. The parenting forum is general North America; Bay Area families participate but are not the exclusive or majority audience.

Thread "也说分享 — 兼论儿女考上名校之后" (5196601): 14 replies, philosophical commentary about sharing culture, no trajectory data.

Thread "零竞赛无知名夏校" (5953000): One specific student story — Johns Hopkins EA admit, SAT 1490, drama + short video + writing, Columbia summer + Kenyon Review, UCSD psychology mentoring + NYU writing mentoring. The 2011 community book "子女上名校，父母谈心得" reportedly contained more systematic trajectory sharing, but the book itself is not accessible online.

The forum does surface some data points (SAT score, specific programs) but typically as anecdotes rather than documented timelines. Competition results (AMC/AIME/USAMO) rarely appear in the browsable threads.

### Volume

Enormous archive: 9,245 pages in the 子女教育 section alone, active since at least 2011, still active in 2026.

### Language

Chinese only.

### Accessibility

**Partially open.** Thread titles and excerpts are visible without login. Full replies visible without login on some threads (5953000 was readable); others prompt login. Inconsistent gating.

### Scrapability

**Moderate.** HTML structure is traditional forum (BBS) style, parseable. No strong anti-scraping signals observed. Thread content can be fetched without authentication in at least some cases.

### Bay Area specificity

Not Bay Area-specific. Predominantly Chinese immigrant community in North America broadly. Bay Area parents participate but are not identifiable without reading individual posts.

## 3. 小红书 (Xiaohongshu / RED)

**URL:** https://www.xiaohongshu.com/explore
**Search terms that would apply:** 美国爬藤, 湾区教育, 竞赛数学, K-12规划

### What data is available

Xiaohongshu's explore page for the search terms "美国爬藤 湾区 K12" loaded in browser but returned a content-heavy SPA that did not yield readable text in accessibility tree snapshot. The platform is known to host:

- Parent "笔记" (notes) documenting their children's competition results, school application outcomes, activity lists
- Multi-image posts with structured timelines
- Community discussion threads under notes

The platform's content structure in the Chinese parenting space is reported (based on platform reputation and adjacent search results) to include detailed year-by-year parent documentation of competition participation (AMC, MATHCOUNTS, USACO), school choices, tutoring decisions, and final college outcomes.

### Trajectory specificity

**Reportedly high** — this is the platform where Chinese parents most commonly post detailed activity logs and competition result photos. However, **no specific posts were directly accessed** during this research session due to SPA rendering and authentication barriers.

### Volume

Likely in the thousands of relevant posts, given the platform's 300+ million registered users and active parenting/education communities. No specific count was determinable.

### Language

Chinese only (some bilingual content exists but is rare in this topic area).

### Accessibility

**Login required.** The platform requires a Xiaohongshu account to view posts. Web access (non-app) is limited; the mobile app is the primary interface. Web fetching (WebFetch) returns bot-detection or empty SPA shells. Chrome MCP browser access loads the page but content requires authentication.

### Scrapability

**Low without credentials.** Multiple open-source scrapers exist on GitHub (wzk1015/Scraper, jackwener/xiaohongshu-cli, MediaCrawler) that handle authentication via cookies, but:
- Cookies are valid for only 7 days
- Anti-bot detection is active (stealth mode required)
- Rate limiting aggressively applied ("多了就崩了" — "too much and it breaks")
- Ethical/legal considerations for scraping Chinese social media

### Bay Area specificity

No way to filter by geography easily. Bay Area content would be mixed with mainland China international school families, Singapore, and other overseas Chinese communities.

### Accessibility

### Scrapability

**Moderate with authentication.** Zhihu has a documented API and multiple open-source scrapers exist. Anti-bot detection is present but less aggressive than Xiaohongshu.

### Bay Area specificity

Not Bay Area-specific. Content mixes mainland China international school families, overseas Chinese in North America, and domestic Chinese applicants to American universities.

### Key structural finding

The Chinese parent forum ecosystem does NOT primarily produce the data format described in the research prompt (year-by-year student timelines with specific competition results and school choices). What actually exists:

1. **Collective frameworks** (most common): Aggregate grade-level competition planning guides assembled from community wisdom. Example: huaren.us thread with 93 replies cataloging competitions by grade. Data format: "In grade 7-8, consider AMC8 and MathCounts."

2. **Narrative case studies** (less common): Individual student stories, typically published by media accounts (谷雨星球, 爸爸真棒) rather than by parents themselves. Usually emphasize 1-2 key activities, not systematic year-by-year logs. Often counter-narrative (showcasing unusual paths to admissions).

3. **Outcome sharing** (common in forums): "My child got into X university. Here's briefly what they did." Single-paragraph recaps, not multi-year timelines.

4. **Year-by-year competition photo logs** (reportedly on Xiaohongshu): The platform where this data most likely exists in the richest form, but it is behind an authentication wall and geographically mixed.

The format closest to "started AMC 8 in 4th grade, scored X, then AMC 10 in 7th grade, scored Y, then AIME qualifier in 9th grade" appears in:
- huaren.us thread 2906415 (competition pipeline explained generically, not for a specific student)
- huaren.us thread 2892033 (community-sourced competition catalog)
- Zhihu articles (advisory frameworks)

Actual individual student documented progression at that level of specificity is rare in publicly accessible Chinese-language forums and would require authenticated access to Xiaohongshu, 1point3acres, or Zhihu to find.

### Best candidates for data extraction

**If building a dataset of Bay Area Chinese parent-documented trajectories:**

1. **huaren.us forum 386** — publicly readable, 6,815 topics, BBS-parseable HTML. Start here for collective knowledge and any individual trajectories that appear.

2. **wenxuecity.com/eliteuniversity + ivycrawler** — large archive (68+ pages), partially readable without login, traditional BBS HTML.

3. **谷雨星球 via QQ News** (news.qq.com/rain/ mirror) — high-quality narrative case studies, publicly readable. Not Bay Area families, but useful for benchmarking trajectory structures.

4. **Xiaohongshu** — highest potential data density for individual trajectories, but requires: Chinese phone number for account, app access, and careful scraping within rate limits. Use existing open-source tools (MediaCrawler, jackwener/xiaohongshu-cli).

5. **1point3acres (if account obtained)** — 1,148 threads in the parenting section, Bay Area-skewed user base, richer discussion depth per thread than huaren.us.

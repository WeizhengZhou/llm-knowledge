OUR MISSION

THE COMMUNITY AND THE STUDENTS

Pinewood students seek knowledge, demonstrate character,
build confidence, and experience joy in an uplifting
community

Los Altos Hills and Los Altos are located in the heart of Silicon
Valley. Drawing from communities throughout the San
Francisco Bay Area and greater San Jose, our student body is
comprised of students from diverse cultural, religious,
socioeconomic, and ethnic backgrounds who seek challenging
academics and enjoy enriching extracurricular activities.
Pinewood provides an environment where every student is
encouraged to become a well-rounded individual, an insightful
learner and critical thinker, a self-motivated student, a clear
communicator, and a respectful member of any community.

We believe that education is most impactful when it nurtures
both the mind and the heart, preparing students to make
meaningful contributions to the world. Our mission statement
reflects who we are and serves to guide our steps into the
future. We accomplish our mission by following our guiding
principles:
To inspire joyful learning by cultivating curiosity, creativity,
and character in our students.
To provide a well-rounded and engaging experience led by
teachers who motivate their students through a
challenging, content-rich curriculum that promotes
academic stamina.
To foster empowering relationships that enable students to
flourish as dignified and confident individuals.
To help students develop upstanding behavior by learning
core values based on respect.
To graduate students who are productive, purposeful, and
compassionate citizens and active members of their
current and future communities.

THE SCHOOL AND FACULTY
Pinewood School, founded in 1959, is an independent,
coeducational, nonsectarian college preparatory day school
serving grades K–12. Upper Campus enrollment in Los Altos
Hills (grades 7–12) is 300 students, with approximately 200 in
9–12; K–6 enrollment, at two separate campuses in Los Altos,
is 300 students. The student-teacher ratio at Upper Campus is
approximately 7:1, and the maximum class size is 18 students.
Upper Campus faculty includes 47 teachers, 33 of whom hold
advanced degrees. Pinewood is accredited by the Western
Association of Schools and Colleges.

SCHEDULE
The school year is comprised of two semesters of
approximately eighteen weeks each. All classes meet every
other day (four classes per day) on a rotating four-day
repeating schedule.

CURRICULUM AND COURSE LOAD
Pinewood’s curriculum is college preparatory. The required
course load for most students is six academic courses per
semester. For those students enrolled in three or more AP/AT
classes, the minimum requirement is five courses per semester.

FINAL EXAMS
Two-hour final exams are given in high school level courses in
literature, writing, math, and science, and the exam score accounts
for 20% of the semester course grade.

COMMUNITY SERVICE
Pinewood students are required to complete 20 hours of
volunteer time per year in service-oriented activities.

NOTES ON HONORS, AP, AND AT CLASSES
Enrollment in Honors, AP, and AT classes is
determined by teacher recommendation; all students
receive academic advising with regard to their
individual curriculum paths.
AT courses are taught at the college level and are
considered as rigorous as or more rigorous than AP.
AP English Language is a one-semester course at
Pinewood and fulfills the last semester of our threesemester writing requirement.
Seniors may take no more than 5 AP/AT courses per
year; juniors may take no more than 4 AP/AT classes
per year; sophomores are capped at 2 AP courses.

PINEWOOD SOCIAL ENTREPRENEURSHIP PROGRAM

ATHLETICS AND EXTRACURRICULAR ACTIVITIES

The Pinewood Social Entrepreneurship Program (PSP) —
focusing on social innovation, sustainability, and community
impact—empowers students to address real-world challenges
with creativity and purpose. PSP is a highly selective, threesemester program in social entrepreneurship and earns
honors credit; it appears on the transcript as Social
Entrepreneurship 1 and 2 Honors.

Pinewood students are encouraged to pursue individual
talents and interests through extracurricular activities and
athletic participation. The high school athletic program
includes girls’ and boys’ basketball, soccer, tennis, crosscountry, swimming, track and field, and golf; boys’ baseball;
and girls’ softball and volleyball. School-sponsored activities
include student government, special interest clubs, service
clubs, and honor societies.

PAW PROGRAMS

Pinewood’s PAW Programs are guided by adult mentors and
are designed to cultivate purpose, develop agency, and ignite
a lasting sense of wonder in our students. Through hands-on,
project-based work, with real deadlines and tangible
deliverables, students strengthen their leadership and
collaboration capacity while acquiring new skills or honing and
refining existing ones. PAW programs include A Cappella
Ensemble, Speech and Debate, Tabula Rasa, Journalism,
Yearbook, Robotics, SAFE, Theatre Productions, and Art
Independent Study.

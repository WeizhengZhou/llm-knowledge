THE SCHOOL AND FACULTY

HONORS, AP, AND AT COURSES

Pinewood students seek knowledge, demonstrate character,
build confidence, and experience joy in an uplifting community

Pinewood is committed to providing Honors, Advanced
Placement (AP), and Advanced Topics (AT) level courses to all
eligible students. Eligibility for enrollment in Honors, AP, and
AT courses is determined by teacher recommendation and/
or department consent. Regardless of grade level, Pinewood
counsels students as to the appropriateness of taking advanced
classes with respect to individual needs and abilities.

Pinewood School, founded in 1959, is an independent,
coeducational, nondenominational college preparatory day school
serving grades K–12. Upper Campus enrollment in Los Altos Hills
(grades 7–12) is 300 students; K–6 enrollment, at two separate
campuses in Los Altos, is 300 students. The student teacher
ratio at Upper Campus is approximately 7 to 1 and the maximum
class size is eighteen students. Upper Campus faculty includes
forty full-time and seven part-time teachers, thirty-three of whom
hold advanced degrees. Pinewood is accredited by the Western
Association of Scho ols and Colleges.

THE COMMUNITY AND THE STUDENTS
Los Altos Hills and Los Altos are located in the heart of Silicon
Valley. Drawing from communities throughout the San Francisco
Peninsula and greater San Jose, our student body is comprised
of students from diverse cultural, religious, socioeconomic
and ethnic backgrounds who seek challenging academics
and support a high standard of behavior. Pinewood provides
a nurturing environment where every student is encouraged
to become a well-rounded individual, an insightful learner and
critical thinker, a self-motivated student, a clear communicator
and a respectful member of any community. 100% of Pinewood
graduates attend college.

CURRICULUM AND COURSE LOAD
Pinewood’s curriculum is college preparatory. The required
course load for most students is six academic courses per
semester. For those students enrolled in three or more AP/AT
classes, the minimum requirement is five academic classes per
semester. Exceptions may be made for students taking college
courses in addition to their Pinewood course work, or for those
students who enter after grade nine.

SCHEDULE
The school year is comprised of two semesters of approximately
eighteen weeks each. All classes meet every other day (four
classes per day) on a rotating four-day repeating schedule.

PINEWOOD SCHOLARS PROGRAM (PSP)
The Pinewood Scholars Program—focusing on social innovation,
sustainabiity, and community impact—empowers students to
address real-word challenges with creativity and purpose.
PSP is a highly selective, three-semester program in social
entrepreneurship and earns honors credit; it appears on the
transcript as Social Entrepreneurship 1 and 2.

FINAL EXAMS
Two-hour final exams are given in high school level courses in literature, writing, math, and science, and the exam score accounts
for 20% of the semester course grade.

GRADING and GPA CALCULATIONS
• GPA is weighted and calculated for all Pinewood courses
• All Honors, AP, and AT courses receive an additional point
• Courses taken outside of Pinewood may appear on a student
transcript, but are NOT calculated in GPA

ATHLETICS AND EXTRACURRICULAR ACTIVITIES
Pinewood students are encouraged to pursue individual talents
and interests through extracurricular activities and athletic
participation. The high school athletic program includes girls’
and boys’ basketball, soccer, tennis, cross-country, swimming,
track and field, and golf; boys’ baseball; and girls’ softball
and volleyball. School-sponsored activities include student
government, yearbook, journalism, drama, speech and debate,
choir, robotics, special interest clubs, service clubs, and honor
societies.

COMMUNITY SERVICE
Pinewood students are required to complete twenty hours of
volunteer time per year in service-oriented activities.

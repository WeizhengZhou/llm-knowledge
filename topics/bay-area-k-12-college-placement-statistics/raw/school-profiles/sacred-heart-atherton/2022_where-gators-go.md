WHERE DO GATORS GO?
This overview of SHP’s Class of 2022 college choices allows readers to analyze some quantifiable outcomes, yet it
fails to reflect the growth and self-knowledge our seniors attained in undertaking the important work of assessing
their gifts, values, and goals, imagining a future path in a current environment that continues to be challenged by
local and global uncertainty. We are proud of their work and their optimism as each senior committed to a unique
path of growth and knowledge. We share in their excitement and hope as they embark on their journeys to impact
the world, confident that they, not their college, will be the great determinant of their success.

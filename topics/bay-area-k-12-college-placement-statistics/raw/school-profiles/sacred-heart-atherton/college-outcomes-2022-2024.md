### Notes

- **Accepted vs. Enrolled:** The Class of 2022 and Class of 2023 lists show *acceptances* (all schools to which students were admitted). Bold type in the original PDFs indicates schools where at least one student matriculated, but bold formatting is not preserved in this text extraction.
- **Combined Matriculation List:** The 2024-25 profile provides a combined *enrolled* list for all three classes (2022, 2023, 2024) — these are schools where at least one SHP student actually attended.
- **No per-student counts** are provided in any of the source documents. The lists only indicate that at least one student was accepted to (or enrolled at) each institution.
- **Class of 2024 acceptance-only list** was not available as a separate document at the time of extraction. The Class of 2024 data appears only in the combined matriculation list.
- **Community colleges** (Foothill College, Canada College, College of San Mateo, Santa Monica College) appear in the lists, suggesting some students may have enrolled for gap-year or specific program reasons, or these may reflect dual enrollment.

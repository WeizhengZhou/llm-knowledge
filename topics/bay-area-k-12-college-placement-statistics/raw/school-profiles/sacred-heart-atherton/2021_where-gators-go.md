WHERE DO GATORS GO?
This SHP Class of 2021 college process overview allows readers to analyze quantifiable outcomes, yet it cannot
reflect the growth and self-knowledge our seniors attained in undertaking the important work of assessing their
gifts, values, and goals as they sought admission to college in the midst of unprecedented circumstances across our
nation and the world. Each senior can be proud of the path to which they have committed. We share in their
excitement and pride as they embark on their journeys to impact the world. Their college destinations are their
next steps in executing their plans and achieving their dreams.

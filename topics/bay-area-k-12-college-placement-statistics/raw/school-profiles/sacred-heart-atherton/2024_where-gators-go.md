WHERE DO GATORS GO?
This overview of SHP’s Class of 2024 college choices allows readers to analyze quantifiable outcomes, yet it cannot
reflect the growth and self-knowledge our seniors attained in undertaking the important work of assessing their gifts,
values, and goals while choosing future paths in extraordinary times. We are proud of their work and their optimism,
knowing each one committed to a unique journey of discovery. While immeasurable, the confidence this discernment
cultivated is the outcome we as Sacred Heart educators value most. Sharing in our students’ excitement and hope as
they embark on the future, we know that they, not their college, will be the great determinant of their success.

# Science Olympiad Results — Bay Area School Summary

**Source:** [Duosmium Results Archive](https://github.com/Duosmium/duosmium) (3,809 tournaments, 1985-2026)
**Data downloaded:** 2026-04-18
**Files saved:** 247 YAML tournament files (NorCal regionals, CA states, nationals) + CSV + JSON

## Data Overview

- **371 tournament files** processed (NorCal, SoCal states, and nationals)
- **12,870 team-level placement records** across all tournaments
- **Coverage:** NorCal regional data starts 2014; states data goes back to 1988; nationals to 1985
- **Divisions:** A (elementary), B (middle school), C (high school)
- **Format:** Each YAML file contains tournament metadata, team roster with placements, and per-event scores

## Tracked School Results

### Tier 1: National-Level Competitors

#### Monta Vista High School (Cupertino) — POWERHOUSE
- **Nationals:** 2025 Div C: **#2/60** (silver medal); 2024 Div C: **#4/60**
- **CA States:** 10 appearances (2015-2026), best placement #15/28 (2019)
- **NorCal Regionals:** 33 appearances, 1st place at 2024 Bay Area Regional
- **Trend:** Surging. Rose from mid-pack regionally (2014-2018) to dominant force. Won 2024 Bay Area regional, then placed 4th nationally. Followed with 2nd at 2025 Nationals.

#### Palo Alto High School — HISTORICALLY ELITE
- **Nationals:** 2013 Div C: **#3/60** (bronze medal)
- **CA States:** 15 consecutive appearances (2011-2026), consistently qualifying
- **NorCal Regionals:** 26 appearances, best placement #2/38 (2019)
- **Trend:** Strong historical program. Peak was 2013 national bronze. Has been consistently competitive at regionals (top 5 in 2019, 2023, 2024) but struggling to break into top tier at states recently.

## Key Findings

1. **Monta Vista is the Bay Area's top Science Olympiad program**, with back-to-back top-4 national finishes (4th in 2024, 2nd in 2025). No other Bay Area school comes close at the national level currently.

2. **Palo Alto High has historical prestige** (3rd at 2013 Nationals) but has not returned to nationals since.

3. **Private schools are largely absent** from Science Olympiad. Harker (2 appearances), Castilleja (1), Nueva (0), and Menlo School (1) have minimal participation. BASIS Independent is the exception — very active across all divisions.

4. **Woodside and SF University High are rising fast** — both went from non-participants to top-2 at NorCal states within 2-3 years.

5. **The Santa Clara regional is the most competitive** in NorCal, routinely sending 30-50 teams (many from Cupertino, Fremont, San Jose).

6. **Divisions matter:** Division C = high school, Division B = middle school, Division A = elementary. Most tracked schools compete in Div C. BASIS has strong Div A and B programs feeding into Div C.

## Data Notes

- **Placement numbers >100** (e.g., #401) appear in certain 2021-2022 tournaments — these are likely exhibition/non-scoring teams or data encoding for multi-regional virtual events during COVID.
- **"Total teams" discrepancies** (e.g., placement #28/27) occur when exhibition or non-scoring teams are included in rankings but not in the official team count.
- **NorCal vs SoCal:** California runs separate state tournaments for Northern CA (nCA) and Southern CA (sCA). Only 1 team from each advances to nationals.
- **Division A** data is sparse — only available for Bay Area Elementary Regional (2017+).

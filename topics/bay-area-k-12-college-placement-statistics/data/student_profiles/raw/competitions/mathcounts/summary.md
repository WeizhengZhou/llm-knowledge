## Key Observations

1. BASIS Independent SV is the most dominant school — #2 team all three years, 5-8 individuals in top 25% per year.
2. Joaquin Miller MS (public, San Jose) leads team rankings (#1 in 2025, 2026) but is not a tracked private school.
3. Harker is cyclical — dominant 2024, absent 2025, back in 2026.
4. Dylan Wang (Bullis) is the standout trajectory: #11 -> #6 -> #1 over three years.
5. Hopkins is a national-team feeder (Ali Zaman 2024, Andy Liu 2025).
6. Traditional Peninsula privates (Menlo, Castilleja, Sacred Heart) have no MATHCOUNTS presence.
7. National pipeline: California placed 3rd nationally in 2024 (coached by Harker's Kadam).

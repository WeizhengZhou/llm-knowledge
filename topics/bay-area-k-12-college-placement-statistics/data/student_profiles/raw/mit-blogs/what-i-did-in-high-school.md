## Year-by-Year Activities

### Freshman Year
- Attended tiny public science and engineering magnet high school in NJ
- League of Legends and Minecraft gaming
- Some B's and C's initially
- Joined French teacher's lunch gatherings (didn't take French)

### Sophomore Year
- School play participation
- Began making digital art/GIFs with Adobe Creative Suite
- Physics classes with two teachers
- Started uploading art projects to YouTube (into junior year)

### Junior Year
- High school research project: "effect of acetylsalicylic acid (aspirin) on drought stress of Solanum Lycopersicum (tomato) plants"
  - Won first place in category at tristate level science fair
- Summer internship at Rutgers University doing data analysis
- Entered approximately 15-20 science visualization and animation contests
  - Won awards/mentions in roughly 11 of them
  - NASA Aura Communications Contest winner
  - High school video contest explaining neuroscience concepts
  - United States Treasury video contest — met Treasurer Rosa Rios
- Joined National Honor Society
- AP self-study: 10 total, mostly 5s and some 4s
- Created videos for English and Digital Electronics classes
- Started Connect Four club with elaborate bylaws and ranking system

### Senior Year
- Quit National Honor Society
- Participated in senior prom
- Co-wrote graduation speech about collaboration and peer support

### Summer after Sophomore Year
- Daycare job — worked with Spanish-speaking staff and children, practiced Spanish

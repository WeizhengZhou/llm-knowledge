# How to Do Everything Wrong and Still Get Into MIT — Mollie B. '06

## Author
Mollie B., MIT Class of 2006. Graduated June 2006.

## High School
Large public high school in Ohio, class of 530.

## GPA
Ranked 11 in a class of 530. "Almost straight As." Weighted grading system favored students taking only honors classes.

## Test Scores
- SAT I: 1430 total (math sub-700). Taken October 14 senior year as "practice" for PSAT. No test prep.
- PSAT: 80V, 74M, 80W — Named National Merit Scholar
- AP exams: Took 3 (Government, English, US History). Specific scores not provided.

## Activities

### Performing Arts (Primary Focus)
- Made all 8 shows in competitive high school drama department
- Lead roles in 6 of 8 shows
- Elite show choir member (made as junior — only girl to make it junior year)
- Captain of 40-member color guard in marching band
- Marching band: competed at Fiesta Bowl, Orange Bowl, Macy's Thanksgiving Day parades

### Academic Coursework
- Regular geometry and algebra II
- Honors precalculus and calculus
- 3 AP classes: Government, English, US History
- Did NOT take: AP Chemistry, AP Physics, AP Biology
- Honors science: biology, genetics, meteorology/oceanography

### Other Activities
- National Honor Society
- Quiz team
- Drama club
- Latin club
- All-Ohio choir
- Girls' State
- Drug counseling (5th graders)

## Timeline
- 8th Grade: Read "How the Mind Works" by Steven Pinker; learned Pinker was MIT professor
- Freshman Year: Briefly dated senior interested in MIT aero/astro; he was rejected
- Senior Year Fall: Dead-set on attending Ohio State. PSAT aced. SAT taken casually. Pursuing National Merit for OSU full ride.

## College Results
- Applied to: MIT (admitted), Ohio State University (intended for full scholarship with stipend)
- MIT application: Never scheduled interview with EC. Applied partially to prove something to ex-boyfriend.
- Ultimately attended MIT instead of OSU.

## Key Themes
- Performing arts-focused profile, not STEM-heavy in high school
- Low math SAT, no AP sciences
- National Merit Scholar pathway
- Applied to MIT almost as afterthought
- "Wrong" profile that still got in — depth of passion in drama/music

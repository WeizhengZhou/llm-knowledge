# Gifted children could learn math much earlier

## Post 1 - JonahSinick (03/01/14)
Mathematician who taught gifted children ~10 years. Moderately gifted (IQ ~130) can learn algebra by age 11; highly gifted (IQ ~145) by age 8. People with IQ ~160 have learned calculus at age 7.

## Post 4 - Curiouser (03/01/14)
Young son: performs multi-digit mental arithmetic, grasps algebraic concepts, completed DragonBox months early. Skip-counted to 100 around age 2.5. Child-led approach.

## Post 5 - HowlerKarma (03/01/14)
Highly gifted daughter (HG+): would have been ready for algebra and geometry around age 8-9 developmentally. School held her back 3 years on material she knew before age 4.

## Post 8 - Zen Scanner (03/01/14)
8-year-old: watches math videos spanning algebra through calculus, reads geometry and algebra books independently. "Gets derivatives and integration, but hasn't picked up quadratic equations" yet.

## Post 9 - La Texican (03/01/14)
Homeschooling 6-year-old using Singapore Math textbooks and Great Courses videos. Using Beast Academy and BORAC series for enrichment.

## Post 10 - BenjaminL (03/02/14)
7-year-old doing 15 minutes daily before school, shows aptitude and enjoyment.

## Post 11 - madeinuk (03/02/14)

## Post 12 - 1111 (03/02/14)

## Post 15 - luvedu (03/03/14)

## Post 22 - 22B (04/18/14)

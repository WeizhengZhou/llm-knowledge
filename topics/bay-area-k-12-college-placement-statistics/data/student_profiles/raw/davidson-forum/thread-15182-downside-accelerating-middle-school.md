# Downside to accelerating in math in middle school?

## Post 1 - ruazkaz (07/13/14)
Middle school-bound son in North Carolina. Could complete required high school math independently during middle school. Considers accelerating and taking high school tests vs working through EPGY/AoPS while taking grade-level exams.

## Post 9 - SFrog (07/14/14)
Daughter (age 13) finished 9th grade having taken Geometry in 8th grade (honors). Earned A in Geometry.

## Post 10 - Quantum2003 (07/14/14)
Incoming 6th grader accelerated two years, scheduled for Geometry with 8th graders. "Choosing not to accelerate as fast as DS can ace a course, but only as far as it would have actually been harmful to not accelerate." Expects Linear Algebra or Differential Equations as junior/senior.

## Post 11 - kaibab (07/14/14)
Child "was rapidly coming to detest math before being allowed to have some challenge." Worried about what to do for math in high school after running out of options.

## Post 13 - HowlerKarma (07/14/14)
Daughter's honors high school math courses taken in 7th-8th grade appeared on high school transcript. Had ONLY non-A grades in math coursework, but oddly, not in later AP courses. Daughter de-accelerated after Algebra II (freshman), took AP Physics instead of Calculus. Senior year: dual enrollment Statistics. College math department "pleasantly surprised that she had not taken CALC in high school."

## Post 16 - frannieandejsmom (07/15/14)
One child completing 5th grade math in 4th grade; program will condense 3-4-5th grade into 2 years. Second child needs more acceleration than program provides.

### Trajectory Data Points
- SFrog's DD (age 13, 9th grade): Geometry in 8th grade, A in honors
- Quantum2003's DS (entering 6th): 2 years accelerated, Geometry with 8th graders, expects Linear Algebra/DiffEq as junior/senior
- HowlerKarma's DD: HS math courses in 7th-8th, Algebra II freshman, AP Physics instead of Calc, dual enrollment Stats senior year
- kaibab's child: near-detest of math before challenge was allowed
- frannieandejsmom's child: 5th grade math in 4th grade, 3-year condensed to 2 years

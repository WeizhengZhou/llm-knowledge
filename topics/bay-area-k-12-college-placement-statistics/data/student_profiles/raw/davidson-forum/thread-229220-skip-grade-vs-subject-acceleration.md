# Skipping grade vs. subject acceleration for gifted

## Post 1 - happymom1122 (04/06/16)
7-year-old daughter in 2nd grade, gifted in ELA and Math. School recommends either grade skipping to 4th grade with 5th grade math, or subject acceleration.

## Post 3 - Val (04/06/16)
Two children who completed three combined grade skips. Eldest skipped 3rd grade with math acceleration; youngest skipped one grade. Notes eldest had spring birthday with classmates 2.5+ years older, creating social difficulty in 8th grade despite cognitive advantage.

## Post 5 - HowlerKarma (04/06/16)
Daughter (now approaching 17) with early asynchrony at 36 months. Always gravitated toward mature adults. Notes age restrictions on internships and background checks create barriers for under-18 college students.

## Post 8 - ConnectingDots (04/06/16)
Oldest son (ODS) began math-only acceleration in 2nd grade at age 7. School skipped one grade, then two within weeks. Later added ELA acceleration (two grades) mid-third grade. Son opposes full acceleration despite school willingness.

### Trajectory Data Points
- happymom1122's DD (age 7, 2nd grade): Recommended skip to 4th with 5th grade math
- Val's eldest: Skipped 3rd grade + math acceleration, classmates 2.5+ years older
- Val's youngest: One grade skip
- HowlerKarma's DD: Early asynchrony from 36 months, approaching 17 (implies entered college ~15-16)
- ConnectingDots' ODS: Math SSA at age 7 (2nd grade), skipped 1 then 2 grades in math within weeks, ELA SSA (2 grades) added mid-3rd grade

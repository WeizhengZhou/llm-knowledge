# Correlations between AMC8 and AMC10?

## Post 1 - Quantum2003 (11/25/13)
Son after taking AMC8, seeking guidance on AMC10 score correlation.

## Post 4 - Chrys (11/26/13)
Rough threshold: "You have to get a 20ish on AMC8 to take the 10" (school policy, not official).

## Post 6 - ohmathmom (11/26/13)
Grade-based AMC10 readiness from AMC8 scores:
- 8th grade: score 18+
- 7th grade: score 20+
- 6th grade and below: score 22+

## Post 7 - Quantum2003 (12/02/13)
Accelerated 5th grader taking Algebra I, "even chance" of AMC10 achievement certificate (~80th percentile). Potential score of 90/150.

## Post 8 - Bostonian (12/02/13)
2013 data: younger test-takers outperformed older cohorts due to self-selection (grades 6 and below averaged 77.2, grade 9 averaged 69.5 on AMC10).

### Trajectory Data Points
- Quantum2003's DS (5th grade, accelerated): Taking Algebra I, attempting AMC10
- AMC8-to-AMC10 thresholds: 18+ (8th), 20+ (7th), 22+ (6th and below)
- AMC10 self-selection: Younger students who take it score higher on average

# Advice for Accelerating Math Curriculum for Gifted

## Post 1 - Padrickk (09/23/24)
12-year-old, 7th grade, significantly advanced in mathematics. Completed pre-algebra by end of 5th grade; took Algebra I in 6th grade. School offers limited acceleration options.

## Post 2 - Eagle Mum (09/26/24)
Son now in second year of university. Acceleration path:
- Watched educational videos (Numberphile, Khan Academy) from early age
- At high school entry, assessed with grade 7 test despite being younger
- Used Maths Pathways and Maths Online platforms for acceleration while remaining with same-aged peers
- Completed grades 8-10 in six months; grades 11-12 in under one year each
- Sat first HSC (grade 12 exit) exam in grade 9
- Cambridge University honors math student as mentor
- Focused on postgraduate-level mathematics rather than competition mathematics
- Participated in track and field athletics for "social currency"

## Post 3 - NotSoGifted (09/27/24)
Referenced student completing multivariable calculus while in middle school; made USAMO annually from 8th grade onward.

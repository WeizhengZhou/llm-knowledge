## College Placement Summary

Menlo's placement data comes from three school profile PDFs, each reporting a 3-year aggregate window of enrolled counts. The three windows are overlapping:

- **2022 data:** Classes of 2020-2022 (420 students to 123 colleges)
- **2023 data:** Classes of 2021-2023 (404 students to 114 colleges)
- **2025 data:** Classes of 2023-2025 (432 students to 115 colleges)

Because the windows overlap (e.g., the Class of 2022 appears in both the 2022 and 2023 windows), the columns below should **not** be summed across windows. Each column independently represents 3 years of data.

Stanford is the overwhelmingly dominant destination, with 23-30 enrollees per 3-year window. UChicago, Cornell, and UC Berkeley are consistent top-5 destinations. Menlo places 112-121 students per 3-year window into Ivy+ institutions.

## Data Quality Notes

- **Coverage:** Three 3-year aggregate windows from school profile PDFs. These windows overlap, so raw totals across windows double-count shared classes. The Class of 2022 appears in windows 1 and 2; the Class of 2023 appears in windows 2 and 3.
- **Metric:** Enrolled counts only.
- **Class sizes:** 146 (2023-24 profile) and 143 (2025-26 profile). National Merit semifinalists: 12 (2023-24) and 5 (2025-26). Mean unweighted GPA: ~3.82-3.83.
- **No athlete data** available.
- **UC data:** Universitywide aggregates from UC InfoCenter.

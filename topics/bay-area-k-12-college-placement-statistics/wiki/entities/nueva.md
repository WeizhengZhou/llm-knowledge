## College Placement Summary

Nueva publishes college placement data using tier-based categories rather than exact counts. Two 4-year aggregate windows are available:

- **2023 data:** Classes of 2020-2023 (4 years)
- **2024 data:** Classes of 2021-2024 (4 years)

Colleges are sorted into tiers: "10 or more" students enrolled, "5 or more" students, and "1 or more" students. The database stores the tier minimum as the count (10, 5, or 1). These are lower bounds, not exact numbers.

Even using these minimums, Nueva shows strong Ivy+ placement. In the 2021-2024 window, at least 10 students each enrolled at Stanford, Harvard, Princeton, Columbia, Cornell, Penn, UChicago, and Northwestern. The school's 100% college-going rate, mean SAT of 1510, and mean ACT of 33-34 reflect its academically gifted student body.

## Data Quality Notes

- **Coverage:** Two 4-year aggregate windows (2020-2023 and 2021-2024) with overlapping classes. The Class of 2021, 2022, and 2023 appear in both windows.
- **Tier-based counts:** Counts are stored as tier minimums (10, 5, or 1). Actual enrollment numbers are higher than stored values. For example, "10 or more" at Stanford could mean 15 or 20 students over the 4-year window.
- **Class sizes:** 112 (2023-24 profile) and 108 (2024-25 profile). Total enrollment ~450. Mean SAT: 1510. Mean ACT: 33-34. National Merit semifinalists: 25 (2023-24), 19 (2024-25).
- **No athlete data** available.
- **UC data:** Universitywide aggregates from UC InfoCenter.

## College Placement Summary

Sacred Heart Preparatory has comprehensive college placement data spanning five graduating classes (2021-2025), covering applied, accepted, and enrolled counts for each college. Across these five years, the school placed an average of 157 students per class to 74-86 distinct colleges.

SHP sends roughly 22-30 graduates per year to Ivy+ institutions (Harvard, Yale, Princeton, Columbia, Penn, Brown, Dartmouth, Cornell, Stanford, MIT, Caltech, Duke, UChicago, Northwestern, JHU, Georgetown). Stanford is consistently the top Ivy+ destination, averaging 5-8 enrollees per year.

Notably, SHP is the only school in this dataset with detailed athlete recruitment data, which reveals that a meaningful share of Ivy+ placements are recruited athletes -- particularly in water polo, lacrosse, and football.

### Athlete vs. Non-Athlete Ivy+ Breakdown

SHP publishes annual athlete commitment lists, enabling a unique decomposition. Across 2021-2025, 146 athletes committed to play college sports; 50 of those went to Ivy+ schools.

| Year | Class Size | Total Athletes | Athletes to Ivy+ | Total Ivy+ Enrolled | Non-Athlete Ivy+ (est.) |
|------|-----------|----------------|-------------------|---------------------|------------------------|
| 2025 | 160 | 23 | 6 | 22 | ~16 |
| 2024 | 149 | 29 | 13 | 30 | ~17 |
| 2023 | 171 | 31 | 8 | 28 | ~20 |
| 2022 | 155 | 40 | 20 | 25 | ~5 |
| 2021 | 155 | 23 | 3 | 28 | ~25 |

Key sports feeding Ivy+ placements: water polo (especially Stanford, Princeton), lacrosse (Duke, Georgetown, Brown), football (Harvard, Brown), rowing (Yale, Penn).

**Note on 2022:** The class of 2022 had an unusually high number of athlete-to-Ivy+ commits (20 of 25 total Ivy+ enrolled), suggesting that year's non-athlete Ivy+ rate was significantly lower than other years. Some athlete counts exceed enrolled counts at the same college, likely because the placement list tracks confirmed enrollment while the athlete list tracks D1/D3 commitments announced earlier (a few may have ultimately changed schools).

## Data Quality Notes

- **Coverage:** Full 5-year placement data (2021-2025) with applied/accepted/enrolled counts for each college. This is the most granular dataset in our collection.
- **Class sizes:** Available from school profiles for each year (149-171).
- **Athlete data:** Unique to SHP. Five years of named athlete commits (146 total) enable athlete vs. non-athlete analysis. However, athlete commit lists track early commitments that may not perfectly align with final enrollment numbers.
- **UC data:** Universitywide aggregates from UC InfoCenter; per-campus breakdown not available. Some enrollee cells suppressed for small counts.

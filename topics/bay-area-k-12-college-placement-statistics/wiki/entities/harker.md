## College Placement Summary

Harker's placement data covers a combined 3-year window (Classes of 2023-2025) with enrolled counts only. Per-year breakdowns are not available. Across this window, 583 students matriculated to 116 distinct colleges.

The 3-year combined data shows 146 Ivy+ enrollments, the highest raw count among schools in this dataset (though this covers 3 classes and Harker has a larger graduating class than most peers). Stanford is the dominant destination with 34 enrollees, followed by Carnegie Mellon (27), UC Berkeley (26), and NYU (26). The school's STEM orientation is reflected in strong placement to CMU, MIT (12), and Caltech (3).

## Data Quality Notes

- **Coverage:** Placement data covers Classes of 2023-2025 combined (3-year aggregate). Per-year breakdown is not available.
- **Metric:** Enrolled counts only; no applied/accepted data.
- **Class size:** Not available in school_profiles. Based on the Harker school profile PDF (2024-25), the upper school has approximately 530 students across grades 9-12, suggesting ~130 per class.
- **No athlete data** is available for Harker.
- **UC data:** Universitywide aggregates from UC InfoCenter. Some enrollee/applicant cells suppressed for small counts.

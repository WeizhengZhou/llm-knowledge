# Bay Area Kindergarten Admissions Information (Cardinal Education Consulting)

Source: Cardinal Education — Bay Area admissions consulting firm. Founded 2006 by Allen Koh. L2-authoritative as an established consulting firm with domain expertise, though commercially motivated.

## General Timeline
Applications typically open one year in advance. For Fall 2027 enrollment, families should begin the process by Fall 2026.

## Application Components (Four Key Elements)

1. **Parent Questionnaire** - "Your first chance to articulate not just who your child is, but where you envision them headed"
2. **Student Visit/Playdate** - Schools assess curiosity, social skills, and learning readiness through observation
3. **Parent Interview** - Evaluates family values and alignment with school mission
4. **Teacher Feedback** - Optional input from preschool educators validating readiness

## What Schools Evaluate in a 4-5 Year Old

- Social-emotional readiness
- Motor skills and independence
- Curiosity and adaptability
- Ability to follow directions and interact socially

## Strategic Context

Many top schools in the Bay Area build their applicant pipeline starting in kindergarten. A child admitted to a prestigious K–8 program will likely have priority access to top-tier high schools. Those high schools, in turn, are recognized feeders into the Ivy League and other elite universities.

## Admissions Consultant Advice
- Start 2-3 years early to spread workload and build authentic relationships
- Apply to 5-7 schools including reach, likely, and safety options
- Approximately 85% of a child's chances at K admissions is based on the parents (drops to ~50% for middle school, <25% for high school)

## Assessment / Playdate Description (Community Signal)
Kindergarten assessments are typically not formal interviews: "4-year-olds join a small group of kids for something like preschool circle time where teachers observe and interact, with snacks and free play in a low-key, low-pressure environment."

# Financial Aid Income Thresholds and Need Calculations — Bay Area Private Schools

Source: Synthesis from parentspress.com, nuevaschool.org, altavistaschool.org, privateschoolreview.com search results

## No Automatic Income Cutoff

There is no income limit that automatically makes a family ineligible for financial aid. Schools set their own guidelines. Families at a wide range of income levels can and do receive meaningful aid.

## School-Specific Income Examples (2025-26)

**Nueva School (gifted, Hillsborough/SF):**
- Under $150,000: 100% of tuition covered (grants only, no loans). All school costs including transportation, lunch, and trips also covered.
- $150,000–$250,000: Contribute 1-10% of income toward tuition
- Over $250,000: 58% of aided families fall in this bracket; demonstrates meaningful aid for higher incomes
- $8.6 million in total aid; 20% of students receive aid (195 students, 2025-26)

**Alta Vista School SF:**
- Explicitly takes Bay Area cost of living into account in need calculations
- Considers rent/mortgage payments as part of family expense burden

**Bentley School (East Bay):**
- Financial assistance program supports broader demographic representation
- Aid structured to ensure a student body reflecting Bay Area demographics

## Bay Area Cost of Living Factor

Schools using Clarity Financial (formerly SSS) applications consider:
- Household size
- Family income
- Assets (savings, retirement accounts)
- **Monthly expenses including rent or mortgage**
- Educational costs for other children

The Bay Area's high cost structure means the same household income buys far less here than the national average, and schools recognize this explicitly. A family earning $250,000 in the Bay Area has substantially less discretionary income than a family earning the same amount in lower cost-of-living cities.

## At What Income Does Aid Become Meaningful?

Based on available Bay Area data:
- **Under $100K**: Typically 80-100% of tuition covered at most schools with robust aid programs
- **$100K-$200K**: Significant aid (50-80%) at many schools; 1-10% of income contribution at Nueva
- **$200K-$350K**: Meaningful partial aid still available at schools with large endowments; expect to pay 30-70% of tuition
- **$350K-$500K**: Limited aid except at schools with very large endowments (Nueva, Nueva-caliber)
- **Over $500K**: Generally full-pay territory; some schools (Gideon Hausner) still offer sibling discounts at income under $650K

## National Context

PrivateSchoolReview (2025): Some Bay Area schools report 88% of students receiving assistance, with some providing 50%+ tuition grants to many families.

## Process

Most Bay Area independent schools use **Clarity Financial** (formerly NAIS/SSS) for need calculations. Families submit one application to Clarity; the platform distributes to multiple schools simultaneously — reducing friction of applying for aid at multiple schools.

Application process: complete Clarity form (available September–February) with income documentation; results shared with schools family selects.

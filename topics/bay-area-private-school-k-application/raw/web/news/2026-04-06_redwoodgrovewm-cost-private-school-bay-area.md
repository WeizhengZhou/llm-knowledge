# Total Cost of Private School in the Bay Area (Redwood Grove Wealth Management)

## Tuition Ranges

Elementary school tuition varies by school type:
- **Religious schools**: $3,000-$10,000 annually
- **Independent private schools**: $16,000-$29,000 annually (note: this figure appears low for top-tier SF schools, likely regional average)

The article notes: "tuition cost is often higher at the high school level," emphasizing importance of asking schools how costs escalate by grade.

## Additional Mandatory and Expected Costs

**Application & Enrollment Fees**
- Application fees: up to $150 per school
- Enrollment deposits: $300-$2,500 (non-refundable)

**Charitable Contributions (Annual Fund)**
Not mandatory but schools "expect families to give at a level that does not pose a hardship." For competitive SF schools, annual fund asks typically range $1,000-$10,000+.

**Other Expenses**
- Uniforms
- Extended daycare / after-school enrichment
- Special trips and outdoor education fees
- Extra materials and miscellaneous fees

## Long-Term Cost Considerations

Private school tuition increases approximately 4-6% annually, significantly outpacing general inflation (2-3%). This compounds costs substantially over K-8 enrollment (8-9 years).

**Example: If K tuition is $45,000 at 5% annual increase:**
- Year 1 (K): $45,000
- Year 4 (Grade 3): ~$52,000
- Year 8 (Grade 7): ~$63,000
- Total K-8: approximately $450,000+

## Financial Aid Impact

Most schools offer need-based aid, with some providing substantial assistance. Aid percentages range up to 88% of students receiving support at certain schools, potentially reducing net costs considerably.

Source: Redwood Grove Wealth Management (financial planning firm)

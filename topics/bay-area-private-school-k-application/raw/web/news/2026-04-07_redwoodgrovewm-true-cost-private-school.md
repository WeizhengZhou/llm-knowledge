# The Cost of Private School in the Bay Area | Redwood Grove Wealth Management

## Tuition Range

Elementary school tuition spans "$3,000 – $28,000 per year, with religious schools being on the lower end" and independent schools on the higher end. High schools typically cost more, requiring families to inquire about grade-level pricing changes.

## Complete Annual Cost Estimates

According to school consultants referenced in the article, parochial elementary schools range from $6-$10K annually, while "private independent elementary schools" run "$16-$29K" per year.

NOTE from search results: The average annual 2025-26 cost for prekindergarten, kindergarten and elementary school is around $22,000 or $23,000 (average across religious and secular). More expensive independent schools at the top of the Bay Area market run $45,000-$55,000+ annually.

## Beyond Tuition: Additional Expenses

**Application & Enrollment Costs:**
- Application fees: up to $150 per school
- Enrollment deposits: $300-$2,500 (non-refundable)

**Mandatory & Expected Contributions:**
- Charitable donations expected annually (tax-deductible for non-profits)
- Schools seek "100% parent participation in the annual fund appeal"
- Capital campaign donations and fundraising auction participation
- Schools expect families to give at a level that does not pose a hardship

**Operational Costs:**
- Uniforms
- Extended daycare
- After-school enrichment programs
- Special trips and extra materials
- Miscellaneous fees that can snowball at some schools to $5,000 or more per year

Some schools have an annual, one-time "inclusive fee" that covers virtually all educational expenses, eliminating unexpected costs.

## Tuition Escalation

Critical for long-term planning: "Private school tuition increases have averaged around 4-6% annually, as compared to 2-3% general inflation." Tuition rates have skyrocketed in recent years and are increasing at a higher rate than family incomes.

## Total K-12 Cost Projection

The average amount a family would spend on K-12 education at a private school in San Francisco is about $520,000 over the course of 13 years. When accounting for tuition increases, you have to factor in approximately a 5% increase every year.

## Annual Fund and Capital Campaigns

While not mandatory, there is an expectation that families will make a donation to support the school's programs, with most schools expecting 100% parent participation in the annual fund appeal. Other "opportunities" to donate include fundraising auctions and capital campaigns. Additionally, many schools have requirements for additional fundraising or volunteering.

Annual giving contributions typically range from $1,000-10,000+ per family at top Bay Area independent schools.

## Financial Aid Availability

Most schools offer need-based aid. Aid percentages vary significantly — some schools report 88% of students receiving assistance, with some providing 50%+ tuition grants. Net costs after aid differ substantially from published rates.

## Additional Context from Search Synthesis

From the SF Chronicle/Lee Associates data (2025-26 school year):
- Average SF elementary: ~$23,000
- Average Marin County elementary: ~$26,600
- Top-tier independent schools (Bay School, La Scuola): $48,000-$68,000+
- A number of schools top out at more than $60,000 annually; a few reach over $70,000

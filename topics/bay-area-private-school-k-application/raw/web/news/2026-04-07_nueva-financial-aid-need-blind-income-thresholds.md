# Affording a Nueva Education | The Nueva School

## Income Thresholds & Aid Levels

Nueva establishes three primary income brackets for financial assistance:

- **Under $150,000 annually**: "100% financial assistance grants to households earning less than $150,000 annually with assets typical of that income level." All school costs including transportation, lunch, and trips are covered at no charge.

- **$150,000–$250,000 annually**: Families "will contribute between 1 and 10% of their income towards tuition."

- **Above $250,000 annually**: Those with demonstrated need receive assistance; notably, "58% of the Nueva families that receive some level of financial assistance" fall into this bracket (2025-26 data).

## Aid Coverage & Approach

The school meets "100% of a family's demonstrated financial need with grants, not loans." Financial assistance extends beyond tuition to encompass "school-sponsored transportation, extended day, music programs, and school-mandated tutoring and evaluations."

## Need-Blind vs. Need-Aware Policy

Nueva employs a **need-blind admissions review**: "The admissions committee reviews applications without knowledge of financial assistance requests, and a separate committee determines financial assistance eligibility."

However, the school acknowledges finite resources may prevent admitting otherwise qualified applicants when unable to meet their family's financial need. This is essentially a modified need-blind approach — the admission decision process is blind, but budget constraints can limit total number of aided students admitted.

## Percentage Receiving Aid

Currently, "$8.6 million in need-based assistance supports 20% of Nueva students," with 195 students receiving aid in 2025-26.

## Context for Bay Area Cost of Living

The program explicitly recognizes Bay Area cost-of-living pressures. Aid is calculated considering income, assets, and expenses. The income brackets that trigger 100% aid ($0-$150K) and meaningful partial aid ($150K-$250K) are notably higher than national private school averages, reflecting Bay Area economic reality.

## Implication for qE023 (Does Applying for Aid Hurt Chances)

Nueva's formal separation of admissions committee (blind to FA requests) from financial assistance committee represents a true need-blind process for the admissions decision itself. However, financial constraints can limit total aided admissions capacity, making this a "soft" form of need-awareness at the margins.

# Nueva School Kindergarten Admissions Overview

## Key Deadlines
- **Application Deadline:** January 8, 2026
- **Financial Assistance Application Deadline:** February 3, 2026
- **Activity Session Guarantee:** Must meet prerequisites by November 3, 2025

## IQ Testing Requirement
Applicants must complete a "Wechsler IQ assessment" administered by a licensed psychologist. Results must fall "within our target range to be considered for admission." The school provides a list of local psychologists and offers financial assistance vouchers for families applying for tuition aid.

This is a notable distinction: Nueva is one of the very few Bay Area private K schools that requires formal psychometric (IQ) testing for kindergarten admission.

## Application Steps
1. Create Ravenna account (online admissions system)
2. Submit Family Information Form with $100 nonrefundable fee
3. Complete Wechsler IQ Assessment
4. Submit Parent/Guardian Written Questionnaire
5. Participate in Virtual Parent/Guardian Conversation (20 minutes)
6. Attend Activity Session (group and individual activities)
7. Submit student evaluation forms from current school

## Optional Events
- Virtual Program Preview (October)
- Campus tours (September-November)
- Virtual office hours (September-December)

## Financial Aid
Nueva uses the Clarity platform for financial assistance applications; separate February 3, 2026 deadline.

## School Profile
- Location: Hillsborough, CA (Peninsula)
- Serves gifted students PK-12
- Lower and middle schools in Hillsborough; upper school in San Mateo
- Class size: 15-18 students per class
- Student-teacher ratio: 6:1
- Tuition range: $30,555 (PK) to $51,285 (high school)
- Niche rank: #1 Bay Area private school

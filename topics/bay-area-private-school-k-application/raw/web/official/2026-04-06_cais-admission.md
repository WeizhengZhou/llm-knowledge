# CAIS (Chinese American International School) Kindergarten Admission

## Application Deadline
**January 16, 2026** for 2026-2027 school year

## Age Requirement
Child must be 5 years old by September 1, 2026

## Language Requirements
"No knowledge of Mandarin is necessary" for kindergarten entry, though the school offers Mandarin immersion programming.

## Tuition (2026-2027)
- **K-8th grade:** $46,300
- Extended Day afterschool care: $445/month

## Financial Assistance
The school provides "adjusted tuition and fees that are based on demonstrated financial need and available funds" to make education accessible.

## Application Process
Prospective families can:
- Complete an "online inquiry" at the admission office website
- Schedule campus tours to see classrooms and meet teachers
- Attend "CAIS 101" events showcasing student learning

## Contact Information
**Admission Office:** admission@cais.org
**Early Childhood Center:** 415-865-6001

## Additional Details
- Complimentary morning care before school starts
- Located at 3250 19th Avenue, San Francisco, CA 94132
- The school admits students regardless of race, religion, citizenship, sexual orientation, or gender identity
- Full Mandarin immersion program (one of SF's most prominent)

# Ravenna Platform for Families: Overview

## Core Functionality
Ravenna Hub is a cloud-based application platform enabling families to explore, apply to, and monitor their progress with K-12 private schools. The system streamlines the admissions process through centralized account management and shared application forms across multiple institutions.

## Account Creation Process
Families begin by registering at Ravenna Hub using an email address they'll maintain throughout the admissions season, as this becomes their primary contact point with schools. After registration, applicants can browse available schools in their region and decide which institutions to explore or apply to.

## Application Submission Features
- **Shared Forms**: Multiple schools in a region may utilize common application materials (essays, evaluations), reducing duplicate data entry
- **Delegated Forms**: Families electronically send evaluation requests to teachers and recommenders, who receive secure links for submission
- **Status Tracking**: Applicants monitor their application progress through the platform

## Privacy & Data Protection
The platform adheres to FERPA regulations. Per their statement: "We will never sell your information or share your private information with anyone other than the schools you have authorized." Schools cannot identify which other institutions families are applying to through the system.

## Support
Families contact support via **Ravenna-Help@venturedsolutions.com**

## Notes
- Ravenna is a product of VenturEd Solutions
- Used by many Bay Area independent schools including: The San Francisco School, SF Day School, Children's Day School, The Nueva School, Convent & Stuart Hall, and others
- "Shared forms" feature means the ISSFBA Teacher Evaluation Form may be submitted once and routed to multiple schools
- Schools cannot see which other schools families are applying to (privacy protection)

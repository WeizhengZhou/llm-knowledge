# Town School for Boys Kindergarten Admissions Information

## Key Dates
- **Application Deadline:** January 8, 2026
- **Age Requirement:** Child must be five years old by July 15, 2026 (one of SF's earlier cutoffs — same as Hamlin at August 1 and Convent at July 15)

## Tuition
- **2026-27 Annual Tuition:** approximately $46,935

## Kindergarten Assessment Process
Kindergarten applicants are invited to campus for a playdate and admission screening where students spend 1.5 hours at Town participating in:
- Group play
- Approximately 10 minutes individually with a teacher
- Variety of verbal, auditory, and written tasks appropriate to their development

## School Profile
- Boys-only K-8 school
- Located in San Francisco
- Single-sex male counterpart to Hamlin School (girls)
- Uses Ravenna admissions platform

## Data Notes
- Age cutoff July 15 — earlier than most SF schools (most use August 1 or September 1)
- Financial aid uses Clarity platform

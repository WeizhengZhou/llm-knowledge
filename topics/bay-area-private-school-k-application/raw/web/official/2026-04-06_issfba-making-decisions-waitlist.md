# ISSFBA Admission Decision Process and Notification Timeline

## How Schools Make Decisions

ISSFBA schools use admission committees comprising staff like the Director of Admission, Division Head, Learning Specialists, teachers, and sometimes the Head of School. These committees evaluate applicants based on "school visit, assessment, transcripts and Student Evaluation Form" while aiming to "create a diverse, well-balanced group of students."

## 2025-26 K-8 Notification Timeline

- **Mailed notifications:** Wednesday, March 18, 2026
- **Electronic notifications:** Thursday, March 19, 2026
- **Family responses due:** Thursday, March 26, 2026

## 2025-26 Preschool/TK/JK Notification Timeline

- **Mailed notifications:** Tuesday, March 10, 2026
- **Electronic notifications:** Wednesday, March 11, 2026
- **Family responses due:** Tuesday, March 17, 2026

## Waitlist Process

Waiting pools remain active during notification periods, particularly approaching the contract deadline.

Once parents sign with an ISSFBA school, "other ISSFBA schools do not typically offer a contract."

Families should contact schools directly about maintaining active waitlists after response deadlines, as policies vary by institution.

If you have not enrolled your child by the enrollment contract due date, you may wish to speak directly to schools at which your child is waitlisted to find out if they will continue to keep an active wait list after the response date.

## Key Coordination Policy

Once parents have signed a contract with an ISSFBA school, other ISSFBA schools do not typically offer a contract. This is the mechanism that drives waitlist movement — as families commit elsewhere, spots open at other schools.

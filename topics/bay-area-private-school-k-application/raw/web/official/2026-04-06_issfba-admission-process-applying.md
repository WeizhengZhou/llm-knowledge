# ISSFBA Application Process

Each ISSFBA school has their own application requirements, deadlines and procedures, but there are some elements that most schools have in common.

## Key Application Components

1. **Application and Fee**: Schools typically have January deadlines, though policies vary. "Most schools ask for an application processing fee; this fee may be reduced or waived for applicants requesting financial assistance."

2. **Shared Evaluation Forms**: ISSFBA provides standardized recommendation forms that teachers can complete once and send to multiple schools:
   - Confidential Recommendation Form (PreK-K)
   - Confidential Recommendation Form (Grades 1-8)

3. **Transcripts**: "An official transcript (or its equivalent) is usually part of the application."

4. **Interviews and Visits**: Schools conduct parent meetings and student visits to "mutually discover if the school might be a good fit."

5. **Assessments**: Many schools include "developmental and/or an academic assessment as part of the admission process."

## Notable Policy

**ISSFBA schools do not encourage formal expressions of first choice or letters of recommendation.**

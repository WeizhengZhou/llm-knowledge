# Harker Admissions Testing — Frequently Asked Questions
Source: harkertest.com (official Harker School admissions testing portal)
Cycle: 2025–26

## Test Used
Harker School requires either the WPPSI-IV or WISC-V cognitive assessment, with selection based on the child's age at time of testing:
- **WPPSI-IV** (Wechsler Preschool and Primary Scale of Intelligence, Fourth Edition): Ages 2:6 to 7:7
- **WISC-V** (Wechsler Intelligence Scale for Children, Fifth Edition): Ages 6:0 to 16:11

## Minimum Age for Testing
"The Child must be 4 years old at the time of testing." Testing can begin as early as age 4, allowing flexibility before application deadlines.

## Grade-Specific Test Requirements

| Grade Level | Age Requirement | Test Required |
|-------------|-----------------|---------------|
| Transitional Kindergarten (TK) | Must turn 5 between Sept 2–Dec 2 | WPPSI-IV only |
| Kindergarten | Must turn 5 by Sept 1 | WPPSI-IV or WISC-V* |

*WISC-V is used if child turns 6 or older during the testing period.

## Required Subtests
"Harker School specifically requires administration of ALL applicable subtests for your child's age group, not abbreviated versions."

The five core cognitive domains assessed:
1. Verbal Comprehension — language understanding, vocabulary
2. Visual Spatial Processing — pattern recognition, spatial reasoning
3. Fluid Reasoning — problem-solving, logical thinking
4. Working Memory — mental manipulation of information
5. Processing Speed — mental efficiency, task completion speed

## Score Requirements
Harker uses test results "alongside student interviews, academic records, teacher recommendations." No minimum FSIQ score is published on the admissions testing FAQ. The school considers the full report holistically.

## Assessment Details
- Cost: $825 (not covered by insurance)
- Duration: 90 minutes to 2 hours
- Results timeline: Within 1–2 business days

## Application Deadlines
- TK applicants: January 23, 2026
- Grades 1 and 2: January 8, 2026

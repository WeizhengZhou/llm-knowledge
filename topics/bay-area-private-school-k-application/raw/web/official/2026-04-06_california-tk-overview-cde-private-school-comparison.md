# California Transitional Kindergarten (TK): Official CDE Overview + Private School Context

## Source 1: California Department of Education (CDE) Official
URL: https://www.cde.ca.gov/ci/gs/em/kinderfaq.asp

### Eligibility and Age Requirements
- Starting in 2025-26: children who will turn four years old by September 1 are eligible for TK
- Local educational agencies (LEAs) are REQUIRED to make TK available to all eligible children (starting 2025-26 completion of expansion)
- TK is voluntary — children are not required to attend

### How TK Differs from Kindergarten
- TK is the FIRST YEAR of a two-year kindergarten program
- TK curriculum follows California Preschool/Transitional Kindergarten Learning Foundations (not Kindergarten standards)
- Emphasis on age-appropriate and developmentally suitable instruction for younger four-year-olds
- Once a child reaches age six, California's compulsory education laws apply

### Public School TK (for context)
- SFUSD added 15 TK programs in 2025-26
- Oakland USD added 7 TK programs
- San Jose USD added 3 TK programs
- New TK-to-K feeder system: starting 2026-27, all public TK students get guaranteed K seat at feeder school

### Private School TK: Not Regulated by CDE
- CDE documentation covers only public/charter/county office of education systems
- Private school TK programs are NOT subject to same state mandates
- Private schools create their own TK programs independently

## Source 2: Private Schools Offering TK (from Bay Area TK search, s-2026-04-06-049)

### Schools Confirmed to Offer Private TK Entry
- **Harker School** (San Jose) — TK program, Boynton campus; 24 students; for children turning 5 by Nov 30, 2026; age window July 1 to Nov 30 birth (may apply TK or K); cognitive assessment required; deadline January 8, 2026; decisions March 20, 2026
- **Presidio Hill School** (San Francisco) — TK classroom, 10-12 children with two teachers; for children turning 5 during 2026-27 school year but too young for first grade the following year; alternative to third year of preschool
- **Synapse School** (Menlo Park) — TK program, innovative K-8 independent school; age guideline: turn 4 by September 1, 2026; TK lead teacher position posted confirms program exists

### Schools Without TK Entry
- **Hamlin School** (SF) — lowest grade is K; no TK program
- Most SF competitive private schools (Sacred Heart, French-American, SFDS) begin at K, not TK

### BASIS Independent Silicon Valley
- Listed as "TK-12" school on basisindependent.com
- Offers TK entry: Fall 2025 Admissions Guide references TK as an entry point

### California vs Private TK Age Comparison
| Program | Age Eligibility | Birthday Cutoff |
|---------|----------------|-----------------|
| Public CA TK (2025-26) | Turns 4 by Sept 1 | September 1 |
| Harker TK | Turns 5 by Nov 30 | November 30 |
| Synapse TK | Turns 4 by Sept 1 | September 1 (matches public) |
| Presidio Hill TK | Turns 5 during school year | Flexible (various birth months) |

### Key Distinction: Private TK vs Public TK
Private school TK programs typically charge tuition (same as or slightly less than K tuition). Public TK is free. Private TK allows younger children to acclimate to a private school environment before transitioning to K at the same school — creating an internal "pipeline" from TK to K (TK students have admission priority for K at same school).

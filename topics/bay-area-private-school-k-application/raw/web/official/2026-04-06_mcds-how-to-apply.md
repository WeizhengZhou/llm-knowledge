# Marin Country Day School - Admissions Information

## Key Deadlines
- **Oct 10, 2025**: Sibling/faculty/staff applications due (Kindergarten only)
- **Dec 1, 2025**: Indexed Tuition applications due (current families)
- **Dec 28, 2025**: Kindergarten applications due
- **Jan 15, 2026**: Applications due for Grades 1-7
- **Feb 1, 2026**: Teacher evaluations due
- **Mar 19, 2026**: Admission decisions posted at 12:00 PM
- **Mar 25, 2026**: Family reply deadline at 1:00 PM

## Application Requirements

**Campus Tour**: "required application step" held October 2025-January 2026. Self-guided family tours available October 25 and December 13, 2025.

**Parent/Guardian Interview**: 30-minute Zoom meetings required per child. "All primary parents/guardians" should attend.

**Application Fee**: $100 (fee waivers available upon request)

## Grade-Level Assessments

**Kindergarten**:
- Screening (Jan 10 or Jan 31, 2026) - one-on-one, in-person assessment
- Playdate - morning session with faculty and prospective students
- Age requirement: Children turning five by August 31, 2026

**Grades 1-5**: Lower School Admission Day (February 7, 2026, 9:00-11:30 AM)

**Grades 6-7**: Upper School Admission Day (January 24, 2026, 8:00-11:00 AM)

**Grade 8**: MCDS does not accept applications to 8th grade

## Entry Points
- Kindergarten: Primary entry point
- Grades 1-4: Limited spots based on attrition
- Grade 6: Expanded opportunities
- Grade 7: Limited spots based on attrition

## Financial Information
- **Indexed Tuition**: Available for eligible families (application via Clarity app)
- Application deadline December 28 (K), approximately $29,000 reported tuition (not confirmed by this page)
- Indexed tuition launched 2001 to increase socioeconomic diversity

## Location
Corte Madera, CA (Marin County)

## Notes
- Separate one-on-one kindergarten screening + playdate format (two separate events)
- Coordinated decision date March 19
- Uses Clarity platform for financial aid

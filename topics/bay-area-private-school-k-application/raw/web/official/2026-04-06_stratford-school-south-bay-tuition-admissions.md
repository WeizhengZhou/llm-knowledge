# Stratford School (San Jose - Almaden): Kindergarten Admissions & Tuition

## Tuition Rates (2025-2026)
- **Transitional Kindergarten & Kindergarten:** $25,170 annually
- Tuition rates are for the full school year (end of August through beginning of June)
- Payment options: single annual payment (includes discount) or multiple installments

## Age Cutoff
- Kindergarten: must turn 5 years old on or before December 2 of the enrollment school year
- California-standard December 2 cutoff

## Admissions Process
- Applications welcomed throughout the school year provided space is available (rolling admissions)
- Kindergarten and above: students are interviewed and assessed by a grade-level teacher to confirm readiness
- No published application deadline (rolling)
- No published decision notification date

## Philosophy & Approach
- Emphasis on developing critical thinking and problem-solving skills
- Fosters kindness and civic responsibility
- Goal: giving students "the skills and self-confidence they need for what comes next"
- Traditional/structured academic approach (not Montessori, Waldorf, or Reggio)

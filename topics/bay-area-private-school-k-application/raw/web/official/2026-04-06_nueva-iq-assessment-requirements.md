# Nueva School IQ Assessment Requirements

## Who Must Take It

All applicants for grades PreK–8 must submit IQ assessment results as part of admissions.

## Required Tests

- **WPPSI-IV**: Children ages 3 years 10 months to 5 years 11 months (kindergarten-age applicants)
- **WISC-V**: Children 6 years and older

Older versions or alternative assessments are not accepted.

## Score Requirements

Nueva seeks students with "a Full Scale Wechsler IQ Score around 130 (98th percentile) and above." However, the school encourages submitting results regardless of score to determine eligibility and receive guidance.

If results fall outside the target range, "they will not be eligible to continue in our admissions process, and their application will be withdrawn." The child must wait 12 months before retaking the assessment.

## Finding a Psychologist

Nueva provides a Psychologist Resource List of licensed Bay Area practitioners. The school also accepts reports from "any licensed psychologist, including those practicing out of state or internationally."

## Cost & Financial Assistance

- Assessment costs vary by provider; Bay Area providers typically charge $850-$950
- Eligible families can request vouchers through the "Application for IQ Assessment Financial Assistance Voucher"
- Vouchers apply only to psychologists on Nueva's resource list
- Must complete Family Information Form and demonstrate financial need

## Deadlines

- Grades PreK–8: **January 8, 2026** (IQ results must be submitted with application)
- Round II Pre-K: March 24, 2026

## Guarantee Deadline

**November 3, 2025** — Applicants who submit IQ results by this date and are "in range" are guaranteed an Activity Session slot. This is important for families: book the psychologist appointment by September-October 2025 to ensure results before November 3.

# San Francisco School Admissions Process Overview

## Kindergarten Application Timeline (2026-27 School Year)

**Key Dates:**
- Application Deadline: January 23, 2026
- Assessment Date: March 4, 2026 (afternoon)
- Decision Posted: March 19, 2026
- Response Deadline: March 25, 2026 (10:00 am)

## Required Documents & Steps

**1. Information Session (Mandatory)**
Families must attend before submitting applications via Ravenna, the online admissions platform used by multiple Bay Area independent schools.

**2. Application Submission**
Submit through Ravenna with a $100 application fee. "Fee waiver codes upon request" are available.

**3. Teacher Evaluation**
A current teacher must complete the Kindergarten evaluation form by January 23, 2026 through Ravenna.

**4. Indexed Tuition (Optional)**
For financial assistance, apply through Clarity by January 9, 2026. "Currently, 28% of our families pay reduced tuition" through this program.

**5. Assessment & Interview**
Selected applicants participate in student assessment and parent interviews in early March.

## Class Size & Enrollment
Lower School classes average "22–24 students" with approximately 2–4 new Kindergarten admissions annually.

## Important Note
All applications remain active for two years, allowing reapplication the following year if not initially admitted.

## Scheduling Guidance

- During peak admissions season (September-December): book 2-3 weeks in advance
- For Nueva's November 3 Guarantee Deadline: book by September-October
- For January 8 deadline: aim to complete testing by December

## Test Format (WPPSI-IV for K-age children)

- One-on-one with licensed psychologist
- Duration: approximately 45-60 minutes for children ages 4-7 years 7 months
- Administered in a quiet, private setting
- No special preparation recommended by schools (coaching invalidates results)
- Child should be well-rested and fed before appointment
- Parent typically waits outside during the test

## Subtests Measured (WPPSI-IV)

For Helios (required), and comparable for Nueva:
- Verbal Comprehension Index
- Visual Spatial Index
- Fluid Reasoning Index
- Working Memory Index
- Processing Speed Index
- Full Scale IQ (FSIQ)
- General Ability Index (GAI)

## What to Tell Your Child

Parents are advised to explain the assessment as "a playdate with a doctor who will ask questions and do puzzles." Schools specifically warn against rehearsing test content, as this invalidates results. Standard preparation:
- Maintain regular sleep/nutrition routines
- Read daily to expand vocabulary
- Do age-appropriate puzzles and matching games
- Do NOT use commercial test prep materials (TestingMom, etc.)

## Cost Context

At $850-$950 per assessment, this is a fixed upfront cost before even completing the application. Nueva offers financial assistance vouchers to families with demonstrated need.

# Helios School Kindergarten Admissions Process

## Application Steps

1. **Create Ravenna Account** — Establish an account and select Helios School
2. **Attend Info Session** — Complete required "Get-to-Know Helios Info Night" Zoom Q&A
3. **Campus Tour** — Visit campus (Thursday 3-5pm or Saturday 10am-12pm)
4. **IQ Assessment** — Complete WPPSI-IV testing with a licensed provider
5. **Teacher Recommendations** — Request one teacher recommendation form
6. **Submit Preliminary Application** — Complete application in Ravenna
7. **Supplemental Documents** — Upload IQ report and family photo
8. **Workshop & Parent Interview** — Attend required student workshop and parent meeting

## Key Requirements for Kindergarten

- Applicant must be "age 5 by September 1, 2026"
- One teacher recommendation required
- One preschool progress report (if available)
- WPPSI-IV IQ assessment with specified subtests
- $100 application fee (fee waivers available upon request)

## Program Overview

Helios School is an independent school for K-8 gifted learners located in Sunnyvale, CA. It serves approximately 180 students. The school uses Ravenna Solutions platform for all admissions.

## Notes on Decision Date

Decision date of March 19, 2026 at 12pm noon aligns with the ISSFBA coordinated notification date for Bay Area independent schools. Helios appears to participate in coordinated Bay Area decision timing despite being a South Bay school outside typical ISSFBA SF membership.

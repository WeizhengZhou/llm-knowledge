# Hamlin School FAQs

## Financial Information
- **Financial Aid Budget (2025-2026):** $3,140,000 available for tuition assistance
- Socioeconomic diversity is a stated priority supported by school leadership

## Admissions Requirements
- **Kindergarten Age Requirement:** Children must be five years old by August 1 of entry year
- **Teacher Evaluations:** Required from current school; additional recommendation letters are neither expected nor required
- **First-Choice Declaration:** Not required; stating Hamlin as first choice does not provide admissions advantage

## Geographic Distribution
- Families represent over 40 zip codes across the Bay Area
- Most families live in San Francisco, with significant commuters from North Bay and some from East/South Bay
- Current kindergarten class (Class of 2034) draws from 28 different preschools
- Combined K classes (2032-2034) represent 49 preschools

## High School Placement
Graduates attend various independent and public high schools locally and boarding schools nationally, with placement depending on individual student fit.

## Data Not Available on This Page
- Acceptance rate
- Specific class sizes / number of kindergarten seats
- Exact tuition amounts
- Financial aid percentage of students receiving aid

## Notes
Hamlin K draws from a very diverse preschool pool (49 preschools across 3 cohorts), suggesting no single feeder preschool dominates admissions. The FAQ explicitly states that first-choice preference carries no weight.

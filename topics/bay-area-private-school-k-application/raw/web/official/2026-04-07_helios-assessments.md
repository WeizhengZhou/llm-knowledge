# Helios School — IQ Assessment Requirements
Source: Helios School official website — heliosschool.org/admissions/assessments
Cycle: 2025–26 admissions

## Required Tests
Helios requires Wechsler Intelligence Quotient (IQ) assessments administered by a licensed psychologist:
- **Under age 6:** WPPSI-IV (Wechsler Preschool and Primary Scale of Intelligence, Fourth Edition)
- **Age 6+:** WISC-V (Wechsler Intelligence Scale for Children, Fifth Edition)

## Required Index Scores and Subtests
The school requests these specific indices be included in the psychologist's report:
- Verbal Comprehension Index (VCI)
- Visual Spatial Index (VSI)
- Fluid Reasoning Index (FRI)
- Working Memory Index (WMI)
- Processing Speed Index (PSI)
- Full Scale IQ (FSIQ)
- General Ability Index (GAI)

## Score Cutoff Policy
"Helios does not have a minimum FSIQ requirement for applicants."

The school evaluates "the whole report, not just the Full Scale IQ (FSIQ) score, paying attention to the child's relative strengths in different subtests." This contrasts with Nueva School's published 130 FSIQ minimum.

## Retesting Policy
"Exposing your student to test content would invalidate the test results and make them ineligible to retest for at least a year."

This 12-month waiting period after invalidation mirrors the Nueva standard. If a child is tested under 6 and later applies to a school requiring WISC-V, a new test would be needed regardless of prior WPPSI-IV results.

## Submission Requirements
Results must be submitted by **February 2, 2026** as PDF documents without password protection, submitted via Ravenna or directly to admissions@heliosschool.org.

## Licensed Provider
Helios "welcomes any licensed provider experienced in administering the Wechsler IQ Assessments (WPPSI-IV or WISC-V)."

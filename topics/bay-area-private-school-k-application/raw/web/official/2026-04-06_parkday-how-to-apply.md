# Park Day School Admissions Information

## Key Deadlines (2026-2027)
- **September 1, 2025**: Applications open via Ravenna platform
- **December 8, 2025**: Reduced application fee ($65) deadline
- **January 16, 2026**: Preliminary application due ($100 fee)
- **January 19, 2026**: Parent/Guardian Statement Form due
- **January 23, 2026**: Teacher recommendation forms due
- **March 10, 2026**: TK admission notifications
- **March 19, 2026**: K-8 admission notifications

## Application Requirements
Process includes: preliminary application, parent statement form, student video questionnaire (grades 2-8), teacher recommendations, report card releases (grades 1-8), and student visits with assessments.

## Age Cutoffs
- **Transitional Kindergarten**: Must be 4 years old by September 1
- **Kindergarten**: Must be 5 years old by September 15 (school recommends September 30; evaluates early October birthdays individually)

## Class Structure
- TK: One class with two teachers
- Kindergarten: Two classes with two teachers each
- Grades 1-5: Two classes per grade
- Grades 6-8: Two learning groups for core academics; three for arts

## Tuition & Financial Aid
**Indexed tuition ranges:**
- K-5: $1,900-$37,980
- Grades 6-8: $2,000-$40,620
- TK: $28,500

Approximately 45% of Park Day families participate in the indexed tuition program (based on family finances). Applications include optional fee waivers.

## Student-Teacher Ratio
1 to 8 student-to-teacher ratio

## Curriculum Philosophy
Progressive education with:
- Social-emotional learning
- Service learning
- Social justice integration throughout K-8

## Location
Oakland, CA (East Bay)

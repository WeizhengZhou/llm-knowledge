# Challenger School Kindergarten Admissions

## Eligibility Requirements
Children must be 5 years old by September 1 (Idaho, Nevada, Texas, Utah) or by December 2 (California). California K age cutoff: December 2.

## Admissions Process
1. **Campus Visit**: Required for all prospective students before acceptance; includes on-site, in-person interviews and evaluations to determine appropriate program placement
2. **Placement Test**: New K-8 applicants must complete a placement test
3. **Application**: Submit online application year-round (early submission recommended as classes fill)
4. **Documentation**: Complete enrollment agreements, read Parent Handbook, pay application fee and tuition deposit
5. **Notification**: Parents notified of acceptance and student placement via written notification

Note: No application fees for academic-year programs (per search result).

## Philosophy / Approach
- School emphasizes "the Challenger difference" through campus observation
- Students placed based on scholastic expectations, motivation, and comportment
- Direct instruction / structured learning model
- Admissions based on in-person interview/evaluation; not purely assessment-based (no published IQ test requirement)

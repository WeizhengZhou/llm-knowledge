# BASIS Independent Silicon Valley - Kindergarten Admissions

## Key Requirements

The school seeks applicants with "an interest in learning at an international level, a desire to make a contribution, a willingness to work hard." Admissions is described as selective.

## Age Cutoff

Kindergarten students must turn 5 by September 1 (standard CA K cutoff).

## Rolling Admissions Timeline (Fall 2026)

- Opens: January 29, 2026
- Notification: Within 10 business days of completed application
- Commitment: Within 10 business days of offer

## Application Process

Applicants must submit and complete an application through Ravenna Hub. The process is not immediately binding upon acceptance.

## Required Deposits

A non-refundable deposit of $3,000 is required with the signed Enrollment Agreement by the commitment deadline to secure enrollment.

## Tuition

Previously reported as approximately $39,795 (from prior research s-2026-04-06-032). Official tuition page not re-fetched in this session; this figure should be verified against the official tuition page.

## Assessment Requirements

Assessment requirements for kindergarten not specified on the overview page. BASIS curriculum is academically rigorous and internationally oriented but does not appear to require IQ testing for K admission (unlike Nueva and Helios). Contact school directly to confirm.

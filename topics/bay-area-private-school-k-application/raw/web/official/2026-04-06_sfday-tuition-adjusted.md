# SF Day School Financial Information

## Tuition Costs
**2025-26 Annual Tuition:** $48,577

## What's Included in Tuition
The comprehensive fee covers: classroom materials, lunch and snacks, yearbooks, field trips, outdoor education with overnight trips (grades 3-8), iPads with educational apps, brass/woodwind instruments (grades 6-8), technology fees, student publications, and athletics programs with uniforms for grades 6-8.

## Adjusted Tuition Program

**Program Overview:**
SF Day offers reduced tuition for families demonstrating financial need. The school allocated $3.1 million to adjusted tuition program for 2024-2025.

**Award Range & Expectations:**
- Minimum family contribution: 5% of tuition cost
- Maximum adjustment: up to 95% reduction
- Average adjustment awarded: $28,800

**Coverage Details:**
The same adjustment percentage applies to afterschool enrichment (AEP) services and summer camp. Students receive one enrichment class or lower school athletics per season at adjusted rate; additional selections cost full price. Private lessons ineligible.

**Application Process:**
Families apply through Clarity platform; application costs $65 (waived for low-income applicants). Applications take approximately 20-30 minutes and directly import IRS tax documents.

**Key Deadline (2025-26):** January 13, 2026

## Notes
- Among higher-tuition SF elementary schools at $48,577 for 2025-26
- Clarity platform used (not SSS/FAST)
- Average aid award of $28,800 represents ~59% of full tuition

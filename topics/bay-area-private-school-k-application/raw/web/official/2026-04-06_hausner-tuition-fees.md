# Gideon Hausner Jewish Day School Tuition & Fees

## Tuition by Grade Level (2026-27)

- **Transitional Kindergarten (TK):** $38,340
- **Kindergarten – Grade 4:** $42,480
- **Grades 5 – 8:** $46,310

## What's Included in Tuition

The school's pricing encompasses "the full cost of Hausner's daily program" plus:
- Books and supplies
- Lunch and morning snacks
- Devices: iPad for lower school, MacBook for upper school
- Field trips (grades TK-3)
- Competitive sports (grades 5-8)

## Additional Fees

| Fee Type | Amount |
|----------|--------|
| Security Fee | $750/family ($500 with assistance) |
| Enrollment Fee (single payment) | $25/family/year |
| Enrollment Fee (two payments) | $130/family/year |
| Enrollment Fee (monthly) | $330/family/year |
| Tuition Insurance | 1% of tuition annually |
| Before-School Care | $10/morning |
| After-School Care | $16/hour |

## Enrollment Deposit

A nonrefundable deposit of $2,800 per student ($1,000 with assistance) is required and applied toward annual tuition.

## Financial Aid Program

- Need-based assistance available; maximum award covers up to 75% of total tuition
- Application through Clarity platform ($65 fee)
- Deadline: January 29, 2026
- Awards determined annually; families reapply each year
- Process is completely confidential

## Professional Partner Program

- 40% discount for full-time employees of Oshman Family JCC and Kehillah Jewish High School
- 30% discount for full-time staff at qualifying Jewish nonprofits
- Eligible families may apply for additional financial aid

## Sibling Savings Program

- $1,000-$2,000 discounts per additional child
- Income limit: under $650,000 gross household income
- Liquid assets under $500,000
- Application deadline: April 20, 2026

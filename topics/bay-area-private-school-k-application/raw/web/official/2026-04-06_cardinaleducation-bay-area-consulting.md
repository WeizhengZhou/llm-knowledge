# Cardinal Education: Bay Area Private School Admissions Services

## What They Do

Cardinal Education provides personalized admissions consulting for Bay Area families. Their services include:

- Tutoring for academic improvement
- Standardized test preparation (SSAT, ISEE, HSPT, SAT, ACT)
- "guidance in writing admissions essays, personal statements and parent statements"
- Interview preparation
- "Character Skills Snapshot and Proctored Writing Sample" preparation
- Application assistance and essay coaching
- Extracurricular activity planning

## Company Background

Founded in 2006 by Allen Koh. Offices in Burlingame and Woodside, CA. Contact: (888) 521-5243 or info@cardinaleducation.com.

## Kindergarten-Specific Information

The webpage contains no specific information about kindergarten applications. Their grade-level selector starts at "Preschool" and "Pre-K" but the content focuses on private school admissions for older students and college counseling.

## Pricing

No specific fee information is provided. They state: "we do not compete on cost because the tremendous value of our services is incomparable." They emphasize working with "a very small number of students" with highly individualized programs.

## Schools They Have Placed Students Into

They list acceptances to numerous Bay Area private schools: Nueva, Castilleja, Crystal Springs, Head-Royce, and others. Also prestigious colleges: Princeton, MIT, Harvard, Stanford, Yale, UPenn, Caltech, Duke.

## Process Overview

They begin by "assessing a student's academic background, extracurricular activities, and personal interests" to identify best-fit schools aligned with family goals and values.

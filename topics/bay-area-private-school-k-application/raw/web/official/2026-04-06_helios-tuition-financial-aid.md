# Helios School Tuition and Financial Aid Summary

## Tuition by Grade Level

**2025-2026 School Year:**
- Lower School (K-5): $44,575
- Middle School (6-8): $48,625

**2026-2027 School Year:**
- Lower School (K-5): $46,360
- Middle School (6-8): $50,570

## Financial Aid Overview

Helios offers need-based financial assistance to build "a socioeconomically diverse community of gifted learners." The school does not provide merit-based awards. All fieldwork and trips are included in tuition costs, with financial aid adjusted proportionally if additional expenses arise.

Admissions decisions are made separately from financial aid evaluations, ensuring that financial need doesn't impact acceptance decisions (need-blind admissions process).

## Application Process for Financial Aid

Families must apply annually using **Clarity**, the school's financial aid platform. Key deadlines include:
- January 8, 2026 for preliminary applications (new families)
- Supplemental documents by February 2, 2026

**Contact:** financial.aid@heliosschool.org or 408.475.0017

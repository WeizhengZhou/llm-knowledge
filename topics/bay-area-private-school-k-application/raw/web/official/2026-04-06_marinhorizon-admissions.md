# Marin Horizon School Kindergarten Admissions Information

## Application Deadline
- **Priority Deadline:** January 30, 2026
- Applications accepted after that date on a space-available basis

## Decision Dates
- March 10, 2026: Toddler and Primary applicants
- March 19, 2026: Kindergarten through 8th grade applicants

## Class Size
Marin Horizon regularly adds new students at several major entry points:
- Toddler: approximately 36 openings
- Primary: approximately 5 openings
- **Kindergarten: approximately 8-10 openings** (quite small cohort)
- 6th Grade: approximately 6-10 openings

## Application Platform
Uses Ravenna (ravenna-hub.com)

## School Profile
- Progressive K-8 independent school in Marin County
- Small kindergarten class (8-10 students)
- Located in Mill Valley/Marin area

## Notes
- Very small kindergarten intake (8-10 spots) makes this one of Marin's more selective K entry points by sheer scarcity
- March 19 coordinated decision date consistent with Bay Area independent school norms

# Silicon Valley International School (INTL) Admissions Overview

## School Background

Silicon Valley International School (INTL/SVIS) was formerly known as International School of the Peninsula (ISTP). It is located in Palo Alto (Cohn Campus for K-5). Offers bilingual programs in French/English, Mandarin/English, and German/English.

## Age Requirements

- **Preschool:** 3 years old by September 1st
- **Pre-Kindergarten:** 4 years old by September 1st
- **Kindergarten:** 5 years old by September 1st
- Note: The school may consider applicants born through December 31st based on developmental readiness and prior French-accredited school experience (special consideration for French program families)

## Kindergarten-Specific Requirements

- No language proficiency required for kindergarten applicants in Chinese and French programs
- Children must be potty trained before starting school
- Required steps: tour, information session, $100 application fee, parent conversation, playdate assessment (January/February), teacher recommendation

## Assessment Steps by Grade Band

**PS-1st Grade:** Playdate assessment during January/February

**2nd-5th Grade:** Student visit day with individual language assessment (German, French, or Chinese)

**6th-8th Grade:** Student visit day with language assessment if applicable

**9th-11th Grade:** Student visit day with language assessment if applicable

## Language Requirements

- No requirement for Early Years or Kindergarten
- Grades 1-11 in bilingual programs must demonstrate grade-level proficiency
- Upper school language acquisition students in grades 7, 8, 10, 11 need prior language experience

## Tuition (2025-2026)

- Elementary School (K-5): $42,630/year
- Application fee: $100
- One-time new-student enrollment fee: $2,000

## ISSFBA Membership

Decision date of March 19, 2026 (noon) confirms SVIS IS an ISSFBA member. Aligned with coordinated Bay Area notification date.

# Almaden Country Day School - Tuition & Fees Summary

## 2026-27 School Year Tuition

**Pre-Kindergarten Options:**
- 5 Half Days: $27,490
- 3 Half Days & 2 Full Days: $33,950
- 3 Full Days & 2 Half Days: $36,080
- 5 Full Days: $37,330

**Kindergarten - Grade 5:** $39,340

**Middle School (Grades 6-8):** $41,000 (includes book fee and laptop insurance)

**The Learning Center (special needs support):**
- K-Grade 5: $56,770 (includes $17,430 TLC fee)
- Grades 6-8: $58,460 (includes $17,460 TLC fee)

## Key Fee Information

**Non-Refundable Deposit:** $2,500 due at enrollment

**Additional Costs Not Included:**
- Overnight field trips: $550-$3,850
- After-school sports: $275-$305
- Optional hot lunch: $8.25-$10.50/day

## Extended Day Program (The Orchard)

Annual fees range from $1,595 (1-day) to $7,190 (5-day), varying by grade level.

## Payment Options

"Basic tuition paid in full by June 15" receives a $400 discount for full-day programs, or $275 for half-day Pre-K. Alternatively, families may authorize monthly electronic withdrawals from June through May.

## Financial Assistance

"Tuition Support is available to families on the basis of demonstrated need" for grades Pre-K through 8th. Financial support does not apply to The Learning Center fees.

## School Profile

- Location: San Jose (Almaden Valley neighborhood), South Bay
- Grades: PreK-8
- Philosophy: Progressive/project-based with strong STEM integration
- Accreditation: CAIS/WASC accredited; ISSFBA member (confirmed by March 19 decision date)

# The Hamlin School - Kindergarten Admissions Information

## Application Status
**Currently Closed:** "Applications for the 2026-27 school year are now closed. Please stay tuned for more information on applying for the 2027-28 school year."

## Admission Timeline & Requirements

### Key Deadlines
- **December:** Completed application and $100 fee due
- **January:** Kindergarten visit scheduled
- **Late October - January:** Parent/guardian interviews conducted
- **March 19, 2026:** Admission decisions released at noon electronically

### Required Components

1. **Ravenna Account Creation** - Online application management system (ravenna-hub.com)

2. **Weekday Parent/Guardian Tour** - Required; registration through Ravenna

3. **Learn About Hamlin Session** - Attendance strongly encouraged

4. **Kindergarten Visit** - Small-group experience in January (required); scheduled after application submission

5. **Teacher Evaluation Form** - Current school must complete "A Common Confidential Student Evaluation Form"; electronic submission preferred

6. **Parent/Guardian Interview** - Required; scheduled October-January through Ravenna

## Age Requirement
Any child applying to Hamlin for Kindergarten for the 2026-2027 school year must be five years old by August 1, 2026.

## Decision Date
March 19, 2026 at noon (coordinated with other SF K-8 independent schools)

## Financial Information
- **Application Fee:** $100 (adjustments available; contact Assistant Director of Admission Hanna Kim at kim@hamlin.org)

# The Harker School: TK and Kindergarten Admissions

## Age Requirements
- **TK and Kindergarten applicants must be 5 years old by November 30, 2026**
- Birthdate ranges determine eligibility:
  - July 1, 2020–June 30, 2021: Must apply for Kindergarten only
  - July 1, 2021–November 30, 2021: May apply for either TK or Kindergarten (not both)

## Transitional Kindergarten Program Details
The TK program serves younger kindergarten-age children and operates on the Boynton campus with dedicated indoor and outdoor learning spaces. The program enrolls 24 students and provides "an additional year of academic/social growth before transitioning into kindergarten the following year."

## Application Requirements (TK-K)
- Preliminary application and application fee
- Student activities documentation
- Parent/guardian interview
- Cognitive assessment (required for TK-2)
- Teacher recommendation
- Student visit
- Student photo
- Family photo

## Key Dates (Fall 2026 Entry)
- Event registration opens: August 28, 2025 at 9:00 a.m. PST
- Application opens: August 28, 2025 at 9:00 a.m. PST
- Application deadline: January 8, 2026 at 5:00 p.m. PST
- Decision notification: After 4:00 p.m. PST on March 20, 2026
- Contract and deposit due: March 27, 2026 at 12:00 p.m. PST

## Application Process
Families use Ravenna Solutions platform. A detailed application checklist is available for TK-K applicants.

## Tuition (from s-2026-04-06-032)
- Lower School tuition: $49,900 (2025)
- Tuition covers lunch, before- and after-school care, intercampus shuttles, and local transportation to athletic and performing arts events and field trips

## IQ/Cognitive Assessment
- Cognitive assessment required for all TK-K applicants (required for TK through grade 2)
- Applicants schedule in-person classroom visit/readiness assessment with teachers
- Must submit cognitive assessment from one of the preferred psychologists
- From s-2026-04-06-031: Harker IQ test deadline January 23, 2026 (TK/K); coaching/tutoring disqualifies application
- From www.appletreebhs.com: Harker is among the schools requiring IQ testing for admission

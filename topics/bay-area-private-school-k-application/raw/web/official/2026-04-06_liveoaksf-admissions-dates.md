# Live Oak School Admissions Information

## Key Deadlines
- **Standard Application Deadline:** January 6, 2026
- **Priority Deadline:** October 8, 2025 (for families with currently enrolled students)
- **Decision Release:** Tuesday, March 17, 2026 at 4pm
- **Response Deadline:** Tuesday, March 24, 2026 at 9am

## Entry Points & Class Information
- **Kindergarten:** Two classes of approximately 22 students each; ~44 new students enrolled annually. "All students must be five years old by September 1" of entry year
- **6th Grade:** Approximately 64 students per grade; ~16 students per class; about 15 new admits annually
- **Grades 1-5, 7-8:** Admission considered if space becomes available

## Application Requirements
1. Inquiry form submission
2. Event attendance (optional but encouraged)
3. Campus tour registration
4. Application submission
5. Parent/Guardian interview (20 minutes, one-on-one)
6. Student visit day and assessment
7. Teacher recommendation (at least one)
8. Transcripts (grades 1-8 only)

## Application Process Details
- **No application fee:** "we don't believe families should pay in order to apply"
- Tours are hour-long, small group sessions during school day
- Student assessments occur during full-day campus visits (grades 1-8)
- Kindergarten has specific assessment dates
- Virtual interview options available

## Financial Information
- Adjusted Tuition program available; visit affording-live-oak page for details

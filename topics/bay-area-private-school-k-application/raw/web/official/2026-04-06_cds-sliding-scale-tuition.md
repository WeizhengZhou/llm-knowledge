# Children's Day School Sliding Scale Tuition Details

## Tuition Range (2026-27)
- **Preschool-Grade 4:** $3,500-$48,895
- **Grades 5-8:** $3,500-$49,830

## Application Process
CDS uses the **Clarity Application** to determine tuition based on income, assets, expenses, debts, and family circumstances. Application takes under 30 minutes; costs $65 (waived for families below income thresholds).

## Key Dates
- October 1, 2025: Applications open
- December 10, 2025: Returning families deadline
- January 20, 2026: New families deadline

## How Financial Aid Works
CDS Access and Affordability Committee reviews Clarity calculations and considers special circumstances including:
- Family size and number of children in independent schools
- Cost-of-living adjustments for SF Bay Area
- Real estate ownership and investments
- Discretionary spending patterns

## Important Limitations
CDS does NOT guarantee adjusted rates for all eligible students. The school notes that "accepted students whose families qualify for sliding scale tuition usually exceeds our budget," so adjusted rates are offered to "a very high percentage" of eligible families, though not all.

## Ongoing Support
Families receiving sliding scale tuition must reapply annually, though the school intends to maintain discounted rates for continuing students with unchanged circumstances.

## Context
- CDS is one of SF's strongest sliding scale programs: $3,500 minimum is among lowest floor rates
- $4.5 million (20% of operating budget) directed to sliding scale tuition support
- 30% of CDS students receive sliding scale support
- Full tuition rate of ~$48,895 (grades 5-8) reflects typical top-tier SF independent school pricing

# La Scuola International School (SF) Admissions Information

## Key Deadlines
- **September 1, 2025**: Admissions season opens; event registration and applications become available
- **December 1, 2025**: Staff and sibling applications due
- **January 15, 2026**: General application deadline
- **January 22, 2026**: Sliding scale tuition applications due
- **March 2026**: Decision letters sent to families

## Language Requirements
"Students are not required to speak Italian to enroll in preschool, Kindergarten, Grade 1 and Grade 6." Students applying to grades 2-5 and 7-8 undergo case-by-case evaluation regarding language proficiency.

## Assessment Process by Grade Level

**Preschool (ages 2-5)**: Parent interview + child playdate

**Kindergarten**: Parent interview + child playdate + confidential teacher evaluation

**Grades 1-5**: Parent interview + shadow day + teacher evaluation + current and prior 2 years report cards

**Grades 6-8**: Parent interview + shadow day with student assessment + teacher evaluation + 3 years of report cards

## Financial Aid
Sliding scale tuition options available; separate application deadline January 22, 2026.

## Program Notes
- Reggio Emilia-inspired approach
- International Baccalaureate (IB) program
- Italian immersion throughout
- No Italian required at kindergarten entry
- One of SF's key language immersion options (Italian)

## Data Not Available
- Specific tuition amounts
- Class sizes
- Detailed Italian immersion structure

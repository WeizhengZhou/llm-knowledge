# Gideon Hausner Jewish Day School: Kindergarten Admissions

## Application Process Overview

The school uses **Ravenna**, a secure online platform for all applications. The process includes five main steps:

1. Create a Ravenna account
2. Complete preliminary application
3. Submit $100 application fee per student
4. Provide supplemental materials
5. Attend student visit/shadow day

## Key Deadlines (2026-27)

**First Round:**
- Applications due: January 29, 2026
- Decisions sent: **March 19, 2026** (ISSFBA coordinated date)
- Deposits due: March 30, 2026

**Second Round:**
- Applications due: April 16, 2026
- Decisions sent: April 27, 2026
- Deposits due: May 6, 2026

## Kindergarten-Specific Requirements

The school invites kindergarten applicants "to join a scheduled one-hour visit between February and March." Admissions staff will also "visit each applicant in their current preschool or daycare setting."

**Required supplemental materials include:**
- Student recommendation from current teacher
- Current school report cards
- School records

## Age & Jewish Identity

No explicit age cutoff published on this page. No formal Jewish identity requirement for enrollment — the school states it "admits students of any race, color, sexual orientation, gender identity, national and ethnic origin." Non-Jewish families are accepted; the school is a pluralistic community Jewish day school.

# San Francisco Friends School - Lower School Admissions Information

## Key Deadlines
- **Kindergarten applications:** Due December 19, 2025
- **Grades 1-4 applications:** Due January 9, 2026
- **Admissions decisions:** March 19, 2026 at noon
- **Enrollment deadline:** March 25, 2026 at 5:00 p.m.

## Grade Levels & Entry Requirements
The Lower School serves Kindergarten through 4th Grade. Kindergarten is the primary entry point, accepting 44 new students annually. Students entering kindergarten in fall 2026 must be "five years old by September 1, 2026." Upper grade openings vary based on returning students and class balance.

## Application Process Steps

1. **Inquiry:** Create a profile on Ravenna admissions platform
2. **Visit:** Attend an admissions event (dates posted end of August)
3. **Apply:** Submit application, optional family photo, and fee through Ravenna
4. **Teacher Evaluation:** Submit Common Confidential Student Evaluation Form; Grades 1-4 also require two years of school transcripts
5. **Parent Meeting:** Schedule 30-minute meeting (in-person or Zoom) after submitting application
6. **Student Assessment:**
   - Kindergarten: 2-hour supervised playgroup (January/February)
   - Grades 1-4: Small group activities plus individual teacher meeting

## Financial Considerations
Application fee required; fee waivers available upon request to admissions@sffriendsschool.org. The school offers adjustable tuition options.

## School Profile
- Quaker-founded; coed K-8
- 44 new kindergarten students per year
- Age cutoff: September 1

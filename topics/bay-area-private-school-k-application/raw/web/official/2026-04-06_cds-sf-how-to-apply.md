# Children's Day School SF - Kindergarten Admissions Process

## Key Deadlines

- **January 20, 2026**: Kindergarten applications due
- **Mid-March**: Admission decisions emailed

## Age Requirements

Children must be "five years old by September 1, 2026" to enroll in kindergarten.

## Three-Step Application Process

**Step 1: Campus Tour**
All applicants must attend an in-person tour or virtual information session. The Head of School and Division Directors present, followed by a parent-led campus exploration.

**Step 2: Application Submission**
Families complete applications via Ravenna with a $100 fee. Fee waivers available upon request.

**Step 3: Family Visit & Interview**
Weekend family visits are required for kindergarten applicants. Families applying to TK-8 also schedule virtual parent/guardian interviews through Ravenna.

## Required Documents

- Online teacher evaluation form (submitted by child's current teacher)
- School records via the "Request for School Records form" sent to current school's registrar after November 1, 2025

Records may be uploaded to Ravenna, mailed, emailed to records@cds-sf.org, or faxed to (415) 861-5419 by January 20, 2026.

## Admission Approach

The school seeks "a balance in ages as well as socio-economic, racial, cultural, and family diversity" when composing each class.

## Notes
- Age cutoff: September 1 (aligns with California public school cutoff)
- Uses Ravenna platform
- Weekend family visits are unique to this school
- Diversity-focused admissions stated explicitly

# Keys School Palo Alto: Tuition & Fees

## 2025-2026 Tuition Rates

**Grades K-4:** $44,600 per year
**Grades 5-8:** $48,300 per year

## What's Included
Both grade levels include:
- All overnight and day field trips
- Outdoor education trips
- Most school supplies
- Team sports and equipment
- Before school care

## Optional Add-Ons
- Hot lunch program (choicelunch)
- After care/extended day programs

## New Student Enrollment Fee
One-time $2,000 enrollment fee applies to all new students (K-8).

## Payment Plan Options
1. **Single Payment:** Full tuition due at once
2. **Two Payments:** Split 60%/40% with fee
3. **Ten Payments:** Monthly installments with fee

## After School Program Pricing

**5-Day Yearly Plans:**
- 3:30-5:00 PM: $3,940/year
- 3:30-6:00 PM: $5,260/year

**Drop-In Daily Rates:**
- 3:30-4:00 PM: $15
- 4:00-5:00 PM: $30
- 5:00-6:00 PM: $45

## Financial Assistance
Financial aid options available; see financial assistance page for details.

## Context
Keys School is K-8 in Palo Alto, CA; 324 students. Among the higher-tuition Peninsula elementary schools.
Application deadline: January 9. Admission decisions: February 19, 2026.

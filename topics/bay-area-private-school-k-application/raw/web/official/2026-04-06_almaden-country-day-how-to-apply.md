# Almaden Country Day School: Admissions Overview

## Application Process Steps

The school follows a five-step admissions pathway:

1. **Submit Online Application** with $100 fee
2. **Shadow Visit** (Grades 1-7 only) — student observes classes with a peer guide
3. **Family Conversation** — 30-minute meeting with admissions team
4. **Decision Letter** — emailed to families
5. **Enrollment Deposit** — secures the student's spot

## Age & Grade Eligibility

- **Pre-K:** Children turning 4-5 by December 31
- **Kindergarten:** Children turning 5 by September 1 (standard CA K cutoff)
- **Grades 1-7:** Placement determined by age, prior grade completion, evaluations, and shadow assessments

## Key Deadlines

- Applications open: November 1
- **First-round deadline:** February 1 (decisions by March 19)
- **Late applications:** Processed afterward with decisions late March onward

## Required Documentation

Applicants must submit:
- Child and family photos
- Current teacher's "Confidential Student Evaluation form" (ISSFBA standard form)
- Parent questionnaire
- Birth certificate
- Educational assessments (if applicable)

## Decision Criteria

The admissions committee bases acceptances on "space availability" and overall fit, not first-come-first-served basis. Waitlisting occurs when spaces fill.

## ISSFBA Membership Confirmed

The use of the "Confidential Student Evaluation form" (standard ISSFBA document) and March 19 decision date confirms Almaden Country Day School is an ISSFBA member.

## Financial Aid

- Need-blind admissions process (financial need not considered in admissions decisions)
- Tuition Support available via Clarity platform
- Financial aid application due: February 23, 2026
- Acceptances: March 19, 2026

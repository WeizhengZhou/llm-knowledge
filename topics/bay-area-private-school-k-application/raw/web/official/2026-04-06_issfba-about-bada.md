# ISSFBA Overview

**Organization Name & Mission:**
ISSFBA (Independent Schools of the San Francisco Bay Area) operates through the Bay Area Directors of Admission (BADA). The group's core purpose is stated as: "support families and schools in the admission process by promoting best practices" and ensuring "smooth, respectful and predictable admission experiences."

**Primary Role:**
BADA coordinates K-8 independent school admissions across the Bay Area. Members collaborate to implement standardized timelines, protocols, and communication practices. The organization also lists high schools on its website, though these schools belong to different consortiums.

**Membership:**
The organization has grown substantially since its 1990s inception. As of July 2019, BADA comprises over 75 member schools, expanding from approximately 50 schools in 2012.

**Governance Structure:**
BADA incorporated as a 501(c)(6) nonprofit in 2013. The current board (2024-25) includes:
- President: Nichole Sullivan
- Vice President: Abby Miller Gudich
- Secretary: José Zamora
- Treasurer: Nicole Start
- Four Members at Large representing various member schools

**Historical Context:**
Seven K-8 school admission directors founded BADA in the early 1990s to establish common decision dates and coordinate support efforts. The organization evolved from informal annual meetings into a formal nonprofit with formal bylaws and governance structures.

**Additional detail from search result snippet (issfba.org):**

ISSFBA includes schools from San Francisco, the Peninsula, Marin, North Bay, and East Bay, supporting families and children during the admission process to over 110 independent schools in the San Francisco Bay Area.

The Bay Area Directors of Admission (BADA) group exists to support families and schools in the admission process by promoting best practices, coordinated timelines, and open communication. Members agree to collaboratively implement best practices so as to ensure smooth, respectful and predictable admission experiences for both families and schools.

Most schools have application deadlines in January of the year of enrollment.

ISSFBA schools share a common Confidential Student Evaluation Form which enables teachers at sending schools to complete one form and email and/or mail it to multiple schools.

Each ISSFBA school has their own application requirements, deadlines and procedures, but there are some elements that most schools have in common.

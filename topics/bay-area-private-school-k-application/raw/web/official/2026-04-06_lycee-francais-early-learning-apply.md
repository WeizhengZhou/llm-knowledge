# Kindergarten (CP) Admissions at Lycée Français de San Francisco

## Grade Structure Note

At Lycée Français, kindergarten is called "CP" (Cours Préparatoire) and is for children who will be **6 years of age by September 1st** of the enrollment year — one year older than the standard US kindergarten cutoff. This means a child must be born before September 1, 2020, to enroll in CP for fall 2026.

The school also offers La Petite École (LPE) / Petite Section (PS) starting from age 2, which is the typical entry point for most families.

## Application Process Steps

1. **Online Application** — Initiate inquiry on the Veracross platform (not Ravenna); $150 application fee (non-refundable)
2. **Admissions Interview** — Zoom meeting with an admissions associate to discuss educational goals (optional for sibling applicants)
3. **Assessment** — For K (CP) applicants:
   - Assessment video (if not already submitted to other ISSFBA schools)
   - On-campus playdate invitation
4. **Committee Review** — Admissions committee makes decisions based on feedback from admissions and academic teams

## Timeline & Decision Dates

- **First notifications:** Early March (coordinated with other Bay Area private schools — ISSFBA aligned)
- **Rolling admissions:** Applications accepted year-round after March for grades with open seats
- **Enrollment deadline:** One week after acceptance to sign contract and pay non-refundable enrollment fees

## Admissions Contact

admissions@lelycee.org

## Tuition (2025-2026 Range)

Yearly fees for 2025-2026 range from approximately $35,900 to $45,900. First-year enrollment includes additional one-time fees (~$37,900 total first year).

## Financial Aid

For 2024-25, total aid to families was $2.48M, of which $2.04M was funded by Lycée and the remainder by the French Government.

## Program Structure

After CP, students choose between the **French Track** (French-language instruction following French national curriculum) or **International Track** (bilingual IB program).

## Campuses

- San Francisco (primary campus)
- Marin (Sausalito area campus)

## Language Requirements

No French proficiency required for La Petite École entry. CP (K) admissions includes an assessment but no stated language requirement for brand-new learners; the school admits students into its immersion program.

## Application Platform

Lycée Français uses **Veracross** (not Ravenna) as its application platform — distinguishing it from most ISSFBA schools that use Ravenna Solutions.

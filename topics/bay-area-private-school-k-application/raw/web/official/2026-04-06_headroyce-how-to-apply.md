# Head-Royce School Admissions Information

## Application Deadlines (Fall 2026)
- **December 5, 2025:** Preliminary application and fee due for all grades K-12
- **January 9, 2026:** Completed applications due for all grades K-12
- **March 19, 2026:** Decisions released for grades K-8 (after 12:00 pm)
- **March 20, 2026:** Decisions released for grades 9-12 (after 4:00 pm)

## Application Requirements

**Kindergarten - Grade 5:**
- Preliminary application and fee
- Supplemental forms
- Assessment Day (K-1) or ISEE scores (grades 2-5)
- Optional tuition assistance request

**Grades 6-8:**
- Preliminary application and fee
- Supplemental forms
- Assessment Day attendance
- ISEE scores
- Optional tuition assistance request

**Grades 9-12:**
- Preliminary application and fee
- Supplemental forms and materials
- ISEE scores
- Optional tuition assistance request

## Testing Requirements
"Enrollment for the Fall of 2026 applicants are required to take the Independent School Entrance Exam (ISEE)" for grades 2-12. Kindergarten uses Assessment Day (not ISEE).

## Early Decision (Sibling/Employee Families)
Only kindergarten and grade 6 applicants receive early consideration:
- Kindergarten early decision deadline: December 5, 2025
- Decisions by January 30, 2026 (grade 6 early decision)

## School Profile
- K-12 school in Oakland
- Two deadlines: preliminary (Dec 5) and completed (Jan 9)
- Uses Assessment Day for K-1 applicants (not standardized testing)
- Coordinated decision date March 19 (grades K-8)

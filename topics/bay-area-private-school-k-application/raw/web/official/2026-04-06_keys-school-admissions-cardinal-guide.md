# Keys School Palo Alto: Admissions Details (Cardinal Education Guide)

Source note: Cardinal Education is an admissions consulting firm. Information sourced from their school profile guide; treat as L3-aggregator until verified against official source.

Note: March 20 decision date (one day after ISSFBA March 19) may indicate Keys School is NOT an ISSFBA member, or that it follows ISSFBA dates with a one-day offset. Requires verification.

## Age Cutoff

Keys School strongly recommends that Kindergarten applicants turn 5 by September 1. Kindergarten classes include children ranging from 5 to nearly 6 years old upon entry.

## Kindergarten Requirements

The school requires a "campus playdate" where children interact in classroom settings, allowing educators to assess "social and academic readiness."

## Playdate Process

For K-1st grade applicants, families arrange visits enabling teachers to observe how students interact in educational environments before making admission decisions.

## Parent Interview Format

Keys School mandates a "Parent/Guardian Conversation" with the admissions team to discuss the school's educational philosophy and address family questions about fit and values alignment.

## Grade-Specific Entry Points

Keys primarily admits new students at two main entry points: kindergarten and sixth grade. Applications for other grades considered based on available openings.

## Application Components

- $100 non-refundable fee
- Parent/Guardian Questionnaire
- Teacher recommendations (math and English)
- Transcripts (current and prior years)
- Student Questionnaire (grades 6-8)
- Recent family photographs
- Platform: Ravenna

# Nueva School FAQ — Admissions Key Details

## Decision Dates

- **Main admissions decisions (K-8):** March 20, 2026 at approximately 4 p.m. via Ravenna
- **Pre-K Round II decisions:** April 1, 2026
- Families must respond within one week of receiving notice

Note: March 20 is one day after the ISSFBA coordinated date (March 19). Nueva operates independently from ISSFBA.

## Kindergarten Capacity

Approximately **20-22 openings** annually for Kindergarten.

## IQ Assessment Requirements

The FAQ states results must be "in range to continue in the application process." The actual score cutoff is not disclosed in this document.

From the official IQ assessment page (nuevaschool.org/admissions/grades-prek8-iq-assessment):
- Nueva seeks students with "a Full Scale Wechsler IQ Score around 130 (98th percentile) and above"
- WPPSI-IV for children under 6; WISC-V for age 6+
- If results fall outside the target range, "they will not be eligible to continue in our admissions process, and their application will be withdrawn"
- Child must wait 12 months before retaking

## IQ Assessment Financial Assistance

- Eligible families can request vouchers through the "Application for IQ Assessment Financial Assistance Voucher"
- Vouchers apply only to psychologists on Nueva's resource list
- Must complete Family Information Form and demonstrate financial need

## Activity Sessions

Sessions offered for most grade levels. For most applicants to grades 1-4, this step is not required and will be waived. Activity sessions for K include both group and individual activities observed by faculty.

## Guarantee Deadline

**November 3, 2025** — Applicants who submit IQ results by this date and are "in range" are guaranteed an Activity Session slot.

## Financial Assistance Application Deadline

**February 3, 2026** (separate from admissions application deadline of January 8, 2026).

# Aquino Admissions Consulting

## What They Do

Aquino Admissions Consulting offers expert guidance for private school admissions in San Francisco and Marin County.

## Founder Background

The founder, Lisa Lau Aquino, served as Director of Admission at The Hamlin School for 20+ years and was founding President of the Independent Schools of the San Francisco Bay Area (ISSFBA).

This is notable: the founder helped create ISSFBA, which coordinates the admissions policies for 75+ Bay Area independent schools.

## Services Offered

**For Families:** The firm provides "personalized guidance, advice and support through the admissions process" for private schools (preschool through Grade 8). They help families navigate what they describe as "the long and complex admissions process" by exploring different school options.

**For Schools:** They collaborate with school leadership teams to "create an admissions program that fits the unique mission and values" of institutions, whether expanding programs or revitalizing existing approaches.

## Success Metrics

The website displays a carousel of client school logos representing "Offers of Admission for 2025-26," though specific acceptance rates are not quantified.

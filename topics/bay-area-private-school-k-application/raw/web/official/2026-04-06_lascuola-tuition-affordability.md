# La Scuola International School - Tuition & Financial Aid Summary

## San Francisco Campus Tuition (2026-2027)

**Preschool:**
- Full Day (until 5:30 p.m.): $47,300
- Half Day (until 1:00 p.m.): $37,815

**Elementary (Grades K-5):** $48,850

**Middle School (Grades 6-8):** $50,550

All tuition includes lunch; preschool also includes snacks.

## Silicon Valley Campus Tuition (2025-2026)

**Preschool:**
- Full Day: $40,300 (5-day), $25,545 (3-day), $17,685 (2-day)
- Half Day: $32,250 (5-day), $17,882 (3-day), $12,380 (2-day)

**Elementary (K-5):** $39,775

Founding elementary families receive a 20% discount through Grade 5.

## Sliding Scale Tuition Program

La Scuola reports that "30% of families" currently utilize sliding scale tuition. The program uses Clarity Application to assess financial need based on "assets, income, expenses, and debts." Availability is limited to Preschool (ages 3-5) through Grade 8.

**Key deadlines:**
- January 21, 2026 (current families)
- February 1, 2026 (prospective families)

**Award considerations include:** family financial resources, employment status of household adults, diversity, and timely application submission.

## Italian Immersion Structure

Based on prior research (s-2026-04-06-021 and supplemental search): approximately 80% Italian / 20% English instruction model at elementary level. IB (International Baccalaureate) curriculum framework. Reggio Emilia-inspired inquiry approach. No Italian proficiency required at kindergarten entry.

## School Profile

- Location: San Francisco (728 20th St) and Silicon Valley (new campus)
- Grades: PreK-8 (SF campus); PreK-5 expanding (SV campus)
- Programs: Italian immersion + IB + Reggio Emilia-inspired pedagogy

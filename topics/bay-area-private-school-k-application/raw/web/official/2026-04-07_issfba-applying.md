# ISSFBA Admission Process — Applying
Source: ISSFBA (Independent School of San Francisco Bay Area) official website — issfba.org
Cycle: 2024–25

## Core Components of the Application

The ISSFBA provides a standardized admission framework, though individual schools maintain their own specific requirements and deadlines.

Core components:
- Each school has its own application with most deadlines in January
- Application processing fees may be reduced or waived for those requesting financial assistance
- Schools may accept applications beyond stated deadlines depending on grade level

## Teacher Recommendation Forms — Key Policy

ISSFBA schools use a common confidential evaluation form:

**"ISSFBA schools share this common form which enables teachers at sending schools to complete one form and email and/or mail it to multiple schools."**

Two standardized forms are available:
- **Confidential Recommendation Form for PreK-K** (2024-25)
- **Confidential Recommendation Form for Grades 1-8** (2024-25)

Both are downloadable via Google Drive links provided on the ISSFBA website.

## Critical Policy Statement on Recommendations

**"ISSFBA schools do not encourage formal expressions of first choice or letters of recommendation."**

This statement refers specifically to parent-written letters and unsolicited formal recommendation letters from outside the school community. It does NOT eliminate the role of teacher/preschool confidential evaluation forms — those are explicitly part of the standard process.

## Additional Requirements Mentioned

- Official transcripts (or equivalent)
- Parent interviews
- Student visits (format varies by age group)
- Possible developmental/academic assessments (school-specific)

## Coordinated Notification Timeline (2025-26 cycle)
- For preschool/TK/JK applicants entering 2025-26: notifications mailed March 10, 2026 and posted electronically March 11, 2026
- Family responses due: March 17, 2026

## Individual School Variation
Parents are encouraged to contact individual schools directly for specific admission procedures, as requirements vary by institution.

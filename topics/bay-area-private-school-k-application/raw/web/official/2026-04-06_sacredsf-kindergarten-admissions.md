# Convent & Stuart Hall Kindergarten Admissions Guide

## Key Eligibility Requirement
Children must be 5 years old by July 15, 2026 to apply for kindergarten in the 2026-27 school year.

## Complete Timeline for 2026-27 Applicants

| Date | Event |
|------|-------|

| September 2, 12 p.m. | Open House and campus tour registration opens |

| October 21, 6 p.m. | Financial assistance webinar (recorded) |

## Eight-Step Application Process

**1. Submit Online Inquiry**
Complete the preliminary form to enter the applicant queue. You'll receive login credentials for the admissions portal once processed.

**2. Schedule Campus Tour**
Mandatory in-person visit (Monday/Tuesday mornings, 8:45-11 a.m.) featuring classrooms, facilities, Q&A with leadership, parents, and students.

**3. Research the School**
Review materials including the President's Report, photo galleries, news, and social media channels.

**4. Attend Open House**
November 12, 6-8 p.m. event to meet faculty, current families, and learn about academic programs.

**5. Complete Application**
- Submit online application ($100 fee; waiver code: K8APP)
- Request "Confidential Student Evaluation Form" from child's teacher
- Applications require 5-7 business days processing

**6. Kindergarten Screening**
Two separate appointments:
- Individual Assessment (30 minutes): December-January
- Group Playdate (1 hour): January-early February

**7. Parent Interview**
Approximately 30 minutes, scheduled November 2025-February 2026. A completed application and campus tour attendance required beforehand.

**8. Explore Financial Aid**
Submit applications for tuition assistance based on demonstrated financial need by January 8.

## Application Requirements Summary
- Campus tour attendance (mandatory)
- Open House attendance (November 12)
- Online application with $100 fee (waiver code: K8APP)
- Confidential Student Evaluation from current teacher
- Kindergarten screening (individual + group playdate)
- Parent interview

## Financial Assistance
The school commits to making education "affordable for families regardless of financial circumstances" through need-based tuition assistance. Webinar support and application guidance available.

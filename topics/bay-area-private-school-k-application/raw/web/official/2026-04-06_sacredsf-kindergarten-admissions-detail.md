# Convent & Stuart Hall Kindergarten Admissions Information

## Age Requirement
"Kindergarten applicants must be 5 years of age by July 15, 2026." (One of the earlier cutoffs among SF schools)

## Key Deadlines (2026-27 Applicants)
- **August 18, 12 p.m.**: Inquiry form available
- **September 2, 12 p.m.**: Open House and Campus Tour registration opens
- **September 22**: Online application available
- **October 21, 6 p.m.**: Financial assistance webinar
- **December 12**: Application and Student Evaluation Form deadline (one of earlier SF deadlines)
- **January 8**: Financial assistance application deadline
- **March 19, 12 p.m.**: Admissions decisions released
- **March 26, 10 a.m.**: Enrollment contracts and deposits due

## Application Requirements
- Campus tour (mandatory)
- Open House attendance
- Online application with $100 fee (waiver code: K8APP available)
- Confidential Student Evaluation Form
- Kindergarten screening (two parts)
- Parent interview (30 minutes)

## Kindergarten Screening Components
Two separate in-person appointments required:
1. Individual Admissions Assessment (30 minutes) — December through January
2. Group Playdate (1 hour) — January/early February afternoons

## Open House Details
- **Date**: Wednesday, November 12, 6-8 p.m.
- Opportunity to meet administrators, faculty, current parents, and students

## Campus Tours
- Available Monday and Tuesday mornings, 8:45-11:00 a.m.
- Include welcome from admissions team and Q&A with leadership

## Financial Assistance
School commits to affordability "regardless of financial circumstances" and offers need-based aid. Webinar scheduled October 21 at 6 p.m. (recorded version available afterward).

## Class Size & Tuition
Not specified on this page.

# Top 5 Mistakes Parents Make When Applying to Private Schools
Source: PrivateSchoolReview.com

## The Five Key Mistakes

**1. Starting Too Late**
Beginning 12–18 months before desired start date is recommended. Delays result in:
- Missing open house events or campus tours
- Reduced financial aid options
- Rushed applications

**2. Prioritizing Prestige Over Fit**
Families "chase well-known or highly ranked schools without considering whether they align with a child's personality, learning style, and goals."

**3. Underestimating Parent Interviews**
Many parents treat parent interviews as "informal or unimportant." Admissions teams actively evaluate family values and involvement levels. Parent interviews are weighted significantly in the holistic review.

**4. Delaying Financial Aid Applications**
Withholding financial information "can significantly reduce—or eliminate—your aid award." Parents should submit aid paperwork simultaneously with applications.

**5. Over-Coaching Student Applications**
Admissions officers prioritize "authenticity" over perfection. Overly edited essays or guided responses can be "counterproductive." This applies to all ages — at K level, it translates to children who give scripted answers or show coached behavior during playdates.

## Note on Kindergarten Assessment Specifically

This article covers general K-12 private school admissions; the kindergarten-specific application of "over-coaching" means coaching children to perform specific behaviors, give specific answers, or demonstrate scripted knowledge during assessment playdates. Schools have seen coached children and report being able to detect unnatural performance.

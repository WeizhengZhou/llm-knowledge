# Private School Sibling Preference: How It Works

## What Sibling Preference Means

"Sibling preference means that an applicant who has a brother or sister currently enrolled receives some level of priority during the admissions process."

## Does It Guarantee Admission?

No. The document explicitly notes: "private school admissions for siblings are not automatic admissions guarantees. Most schools require siblings to meet academic, behavioral, and developmental standards."

## How It Varies by School

Key variation points include:
- "Sibling preference typically applies only when the sibling is currently enrolled"
- "Graduated siblings may or may not count toward preference"
- "Preference varies by school and grade level"
- "Competitive entry points may limit sibling placements"

## Geographic Examples

The article contains no specific Bay Area or California examples. It references general national trends and 2025 data without regional case studies.

## Timing Recommendations

The document recommends applying "during natural expansion grades such as kindergarten, grade 6, or grade 9" — these grades tend to have more available seats and are therefore favorable entry points for siblings.

## Sibling Discounts

Sibling discounts "often range from 5 to 15 percent off tuition for the second child." However, "fewer schools advertise automatic discounts in 2025" as rising operational costs push many institutions toward need-based aid rather than automatic tuition reductions.

Financial aid consideration increases when multiple children enroll simultaneously, as it recognizes "multiple tuitions strain family resources."

# Bay Area Private K Schools: Educational Philosophy Classification

## Sources
- Web search: "Bay Area private school educational philosophy Montessori Waldorf Reggio progressive traditional kindergarten list 2025"

## Montessori Schools (Bay Area K programs)
Based on Bay Area Parent Magazine 2025 Best of the Best reader poll and search results:
- **Maria Montessori School of the Golden Gate** (San Francisco) — offers Pre-K, TK, and Kindergarten programs; follows Dr. Maria Montessori philosophy; emphasis on physical, social, cognitive, and emotional development
- **The Renaissance International School** — three-language immersion Montessori school (Bay Area; East Bay)
- Additional Montessori schools exist across the Bay Area but not individually identified in this search pass

## Waldorf Schools (Bay Area K programs)
- **San Francisco Waldorf School** — K-12; education described as "collaboration of creativity and rigorousness based on human connection"; highly trained staff; aims to inspire love of learning, goodness, and the real world
- **Marin Waldorf School** — Waldorf-method K-12 in Marin (from s-2026-04-06-040: Marin Waldorf School noted in Marin search)
- **East Bay Waldorf School** (El Sobrante) — K-12 Waldorf program in East Bay

## Reggio Emilia-Inspired Schools
- **Terra San Francisco (Terra School SF)** — PreK-8 in Richmond District; Reggio Emilia philosophy; student-led discussions and interactive projects; time spent in nature; language learning (Mandarin and Spanish); voted 2025 Best of the Best family favorite

## Progressive Schools
- **Presidio Hill School** (San Francisco) — founded 1918; "proudly progressive TK-8"; teaching philosophy to know students well and build on their interests and abilities; lead each child to expand knowledge, responsibility, understanding, social competence, and skill development
- **Park Day School** (Oakland) — progressive K-8 (from existing research)
- **Aurora School** (Berkeley) — very flexible, progressive K-8 (from s-2026-04-06-037)
- **Live Oak School** (San Francisco) — progressive (from existing research)
- **Children's Day School** (San Francisco) — progressive, sliding-scale tuition (from existing research)

## Traditional / Structured Academic Schools
- **Harker School** (San Jose) — traditional academic, direct instruction; IQ assessment required
- **Challenger School** (multiple South Bay campuses) — structured/direct instruction model
- **Stratford School** (multiple South Bay campuses) — critical thinking emphasis, structured curriculum
- **BASIS Independent Silicon Valley** (San Jose) — rigorous academics; college-prep model starting in early grades; 56% acceptance rate

## Gifted / IQ-Based Schools
- **Nueva School** (Hillsborough) — requires IQ 130+ for K admission; gifted-specific curriculum
- **Helios School** (Sunnyvale) — requires IQ test (no published cutoff); gifted focus
- **Harker School** — cognitive assessment required; in practice serves high-achieving families

## Religious / Faith-Based Schools

- Jewish: Jewish Community High School, Brandeis Hillel Day School, Gideon Hausner JDS (Palo Alto)
- Episcopal: SF Day School (sliding scale tuition), Sacramento St. Francis

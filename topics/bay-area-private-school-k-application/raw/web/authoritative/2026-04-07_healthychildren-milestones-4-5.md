# Developmental Milestones: 4 to 5 Year Olds
Source: HealthyChildren.org (American Academy of Pediatrics) — healthychildren.org

## Gross Motor Skills
- "Stands on one foot for 10 seconds or longer"
- "Climbs stairs alternating feet"
- "Hops, somersaults"
- "Swings, climbs"

## Fine Motor Skills
- Children can "Copy triangle and other geometric patterns"
- "Draw person with at least three body parts"
- "Print some letters"
- Self-care tasks: dressing, buttoning, and using utensils independently

## Language Development
- "Uses four-word sentences"
- Speech is "100% understandable by strangers"
- Children use future tense
- "Tell longer stories"
- "Say name and address"

## Cognitive Abilities
- "Can count 10 or more objects"
- "Correctly names at least four colors"
- Demonstrate improved time understanding (yesterday, today, tomorrow)
- Recognize household items like money and common appliances

## Social-Emotional Development
- Children "Want to please friends"
- "Want to be like her friends"
- "More likely to agree to rules"
- Show "well-developed imaginative play"
- Display increased independence
- Engage in self-care routines like brushing teeth

## Red Flags for Developmental Delay (from AAP)
Notable concerns that warrant evaluation include:
- Extreme fearfulness or aggression
- Difficulty separating from parents
- Limited peer interaction
- Inability to concentrate beyond five minutes
- Trouble understanding two-part commands

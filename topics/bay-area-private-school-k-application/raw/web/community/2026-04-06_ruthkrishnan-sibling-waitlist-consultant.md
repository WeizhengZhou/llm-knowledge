# SF Private School Admissions: Sibling Policy, Waitlist, and Consultant Value

Source: Ruth Krishnan blog — community-tier source, not primary evidence.

## Sibling Preference Policies

Paula Molligan (admissions consultant) is quoted confirming that schools maintain strong sibling policies: "the schools do have strong sibling policies. So that is good news for parents with more than one child. It's not automatic, but there is a strong policy for acceptance."

## Waitlist Processes

Waitlists are generally legitimate, though their size varies by school. Molligan explains that waitlisted families were often initially accepted but displaced due to space constraints. She notes schools consider "zip codes, and cultural diversity, and ethnic diversity, and socioeconomic diversity" when making final decisions.

To improve waitlist chances, families should contact admissions directly and potentially seek recommendations from current school families.

## Consultant Value

Working with an educational consultant helps families:
- Identify schools matching their values
- Navigate the emotionally taxing process
- Gain insider knowledge about school personalities — information difficult to discern from websites alone during virtual tour periods

## Decision Day Timeline

Good news for San Francisco families: public school lottery results and private school acceptances arrive simultaneously around mid-March, enabling informed decision-making between options.

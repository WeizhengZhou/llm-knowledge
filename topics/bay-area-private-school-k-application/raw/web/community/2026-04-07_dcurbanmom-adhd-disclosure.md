# ADHD Disclosure in Private School Applications — Forum Discussion
Source: DC Urban Mom forum — dcurbanmom.com
Classification: L4-community (parent forum; intelligence input, not primary source for facts)

## Parent Consensus on Disclosure Timing

Multiple parents recommend disclosure during the application process rather than withholding:

"Yeah, you should disclose. You're aware of it, he's medicated for it, he's doing well."

"I would disclose. At some point, meds might change and you will need teacher feedback in that process."

## Rationale from Community

Practical reasons cited for disclosure:
- Schools need information for potential medication adjustments in upper grades
- Teacher recommendations may reference the condition anyway, making transparency important for credibility
- If a school cannot support your child's needs, that school may not be the right fit regardless

"The worst thing you can do is withhold this information because any school that would reject your child over it won't be a good fit."

## School Response (Community Observation)

Generally positive for well-managed ADHD: "Many are fine with sn depending on what it is and no behavioral issues." Even elite private schools reportedly accept students with ADHD when academically sound and well-managed.

## Framing Recommendation

From forum: Present disclosure as part of a partnership. Show willingness to adjust medications or therapies if needed, frame yourself as collaborative with the school on your child's support.

## Caveat
This is DC-specific forum discussion, not Bay Area. General principle of disclosure vs. non-disclosure is transferable, but specific school policies vary significantly by Bay Area institution.

# San Francisco Private School Admissions: Key Information (Real Estate Agent Guide)

Source: Ruth Krishnan, SF Realtor — community/marketing content, not an admissions authority.

## Timeline & Process

The admissions process requires **6-12 months of preparation**. Families applying to 9th grade should begin research in 7th grade (2-3 years early). The application cycle follows this pattern:

- **August**: Initial information sessions and open houses
- **September-October**: School tours and application drafting
- **November**: Submit applications with student essays
- **December-January**: Family interviews and additional materials
- **February**: Send achievement updates
- **March**: Receive decisions and make final selection

## Application Requirements (General)

While specific schools aren't named with their exact requirements, the guide indicates families typically:

- Attend **20+ events** across 5-7 schools (4-6 events per school)
- Complete applications with authentic student essays
- Participate in family interviews lasting 20-30 minutes
- Provide teacher recommendations (plus one additional reference)
- Submit writing samples and proctored essays

## Financial Considerations

- Application fees: $100-200 per school
- Annual tuition: $45,000-55,000
- Professional consultant fees: $3,000-15,000
- Additional costs: uniforms, extracurriculars ($2,000-5,000), transportation
- Financial aid covers approximately 15-20% of enrolled families

## Competitive Landscape (Community Claim — Not Verified)

Top-tier schools receive **800-1,200 applications** for 50-100 spots, creating **8-15% acceptance rates**. Families applying to only 2-3 schools face 60-70% rejection rates; those applying to 5-7 schools achieve 85-90% success rates with at least one acceptance.

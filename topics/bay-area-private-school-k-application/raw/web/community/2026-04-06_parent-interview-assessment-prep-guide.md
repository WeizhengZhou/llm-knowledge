# Private School Parent Interview Guide for Kindergarten (RO5 Assessment Prep)

Compiled from multiple sources: admissionsessayadvising.com (parent interview guide), supplemental search results on Berkeley Parents Network data, and cardinaleducation.com guidance.

## Questions Schools Ask Parents (Kindergarten Admissions)

Common questions asked during the parent interview/conversation:

1. "What brings you to our school?" (assess philosophy alignment)
2. "In what kind of school environment would you like to enroll your child?"
3. "Tell me about your child, especially as a learner"
4. "Describe your child using three adjectives"
5. "What kind of environments/situations does your child thrive in?"
6. "When is your child at their best?"
7. "What has your child's preschool experience been like?"
8. "What do you hope your child takes away from their elementary education?"
9. "How does your family approach academic challenges or frustration?"
10. "What questions do you have about our school?" (always have questions prepared)

## What Schools Are Assessing

Schools evaluate parent-child "fit" signals:
- Whether parents understand and align with the school's educational philosophy
- Whether parents have realistic expectations about the program
- Whether the family will be collaborative and communicate constructively
- Whether parents can articulate their child's individual strengths and challenges honestly

Red flags admissions officers notice:
- Parents who can't describe their child's learning style specifically
- Parents who only praise without acknowledging areas for growth
- Questions that could be answered on the school's website (suggests lack of research)
- Parents who seem to want the school for prestige rather than philosophy fit

## Preparation Tips for Parents

**Do:**
- Research the school's mission statement and philosophy thoroughly beforehand
- Prepare specific stories/anecdotes about your child's learning style
- Practice answers aloud with a partner before the interview
- Schedule your first interviews with secondary-choice schools before top choices
- Have 3-5 substantive questions about program details ready to ask

**Don't:**
- Read from notes during the conversation
- Over-coach your child ahead of their playdate/assessment
- Claim your child is a "perfect fit" without specifics
- Ask about admissions statistics or "what it takes to get in"

## Child Playdate / Assessment Preparation

What schools observe during playdates (K applicants):
- Can the child follow simple instructions from an unfamiliar adult?
- Does the child show curiosity and engagement with new activities?
- Can they participate in a small group setting with peers?
- Do they show age-appropriate emotional regulation?
- Communication skills: can they talk about themselves, their interests?

What schools do NOT expect K applicants to do:
- Read (unless they can already)
- Demonstrate academic pre-skills beyond age-appropriate
- Perform in high-pressure conditions (playdate is intentionally relaxed)

## Tips for Child Playdate Preparation

1. Explain what will happen the night before in simple terms: "Tomorrow you'll visit a school to play and meet some new kids and teachers"
2. Act it out at home if the child is anxious about new situations
3. Maintain normal morning routine (don't rush; child should arrive regulated, not stressed)
4. Ensure child is fed and has slept well
5. Preschool teachers are the best source of specific advice — ask them for tips based on knowing your child
6. **Do not** rehearse scripted answers with your child

## School-Specific Process Notes

**La Scuola:** Parent interview + child playdate (same as most SF independents)
**CAIS:** Campus tours + "CAIS 101" events; specific playdate format not published
**Lycée Français:** Zoom admissions interview for parents + on-campus playdate
**SVIS (formerly ISTP):** Parent conversation + playdate assessment (January/February)
**Keys School:** Parent/Guardian Conversation + campus playdate (K-1st grade)
**Helios:** Student workshop + parent meeting (after IQ assessment)
**Nueva:** Activity session (group + individual activities); parents not present during session

## IQ-Testing Schools: Additional Prep Notes

For Nueva and Helios (IQ test required):
- Book psychologist appointment in September-October
- Do not use commercial test prep materials (WPPSI practice books) — schools warn this invalidates results
- The test is one-on-one with a psychologist in a private office; child is not told it's a test for school entry
- Tell the child: "You'll go meet a doctor who does fun puzzles with kids"

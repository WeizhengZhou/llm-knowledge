## Key Intelligence from Search Summary (not full page text)

From BPN discussion thread summaries on shy children and private school admissions:

- Schools go out of their way to make kindergarten assessments comfortable for children
- Even if a child has a difficult day or meltdown during assessment, they can be reassessed on another date and may still be accepted
- Parents can pull the admissions director aside during a school tour to express concerns about a shy child — schools often assure families they can repeat the assessment
- Taking a tour the day before the assessment allows the child to meet staff and see the room beforehand

## Parent Strategies from Community

- Frame visits as fun playdates, not tests — children who understand they are "being tested" become more anxious
- Helping introverted children feel comfortable: small groups, advance familiarity with the space
- Schools watch to see which children take more time to participate and who shares; slow-to-engage is not automatically disqualifying

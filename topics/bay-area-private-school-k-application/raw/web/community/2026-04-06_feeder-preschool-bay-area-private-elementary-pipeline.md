## Key Findings from Compiled Sources

### General Landscape
- Bay Area does NOT have a formal feeder preschool system comparable to NYC or Boston
- Preschool choice is described as "less determinative" than in other major metro markets
- Proximity to the private elementary school is often more relevant than preschool name
- No SF private school publicly discloses a list of preferred feeder preschools (contrast with NYC where some schools track preschool origins)

### Known Preschool-to-Elementary Connections (from s-2026-04-06-030 and s-2026-04-06-036)
- **Hamlin School**: draws from 49 preschools across 3 cohort years (no single dominant feeder)
- **Prospect Sierra** (East Bay): Step One Nursery School (Berkeley) mentioned favorably by admissions team
- **Mills College Children's School**: has internal priority pathway into their own elementary program
- **East Bay generally**: no formal feeder system; proximity most relevant factor
- **La Scuola International School** (SF): Italian immersion preschool through 8th grade — internal TK/K pipeline

### Integrated PreK-through-Elementary Programs (built-in pipeline)
Schools that operate their own preschool → K pipeline:
- **Chinese American International School (CAIS)**: preschool through 8th grade, all on unified campus (new 2024-25 campus)
- **Terra San Francisco**: PreK-8 (Reggio-inspired, bilingual)
- **La Scuola International School**: PreK-8 (Italian immersion)
- **Shu Ren International School** (Berkeley): PK-5 (Mandarin immersion + IB PYP)
- **Harker School**: TK program → K pipeline (TK students have priority for K at same school)
- **Synapse School** (Menlo Park): TK → K pipeline (TK confirmed entry point)

### South Bay Feeder Context
- No specific preschool-to-South Bay elementary pipelines documented in available sources
- Challenger School: rolling admissions year-round; no preschool-to-K priority structure documented
- Stratford School: rolling admissions; no preschool-to-K priority structure documented
- Harker: internal TK → K pipeline is closest to a structured feeder arrangement in South Bay

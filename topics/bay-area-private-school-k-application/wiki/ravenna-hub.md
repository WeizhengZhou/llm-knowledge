# Ravenna Hub -- Admissions Platform

## Definition

Ravenna Hub is the dominant online admissions platform used by Bay Area independent schools. It is a product of VenturEd Solutions.

According to Ravenna Solutions, Ravenna Hub is a product of VenturEd Solutions.

## How It Works

Families create a single Ravenna account and use it to apply to multiple schools. Key features:

- According to Ravenna Solutions, schools cannot see which other schools families are applying to. This is an important privacy feature for families applying broadly.
- According to Ravenna Solutions, the ISSFBA Teacher Evaluation Form can be submitted once and routed to multiple schools. This reduces the burden on preschool teachers who would otherwise need to fill out separate forms for each school.

## Schools Using Ravenna

SF Day School, The San Francisco School, Children's Day School, Convent & Stuart Hall, The Hamlin School, SF Friends School, Presidio Hill School, Park Day School, Town School for Boys, Marin Horizon School, and Nueva School all use the Ravenna admissions platform, based on confirmed statements on their respective official admissions pages.

## Relevance

For families applying to multiple Bay Area private K programs, Ravenna is effectively required. The shared platform streamlines:
- Application submission across schools
- Teacher evaluation routing (one form, multiple schools)
- Interview and visit scheduling (some schools schedule via Ravenna)

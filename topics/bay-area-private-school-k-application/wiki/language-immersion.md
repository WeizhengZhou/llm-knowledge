# Language Immersion K Programs in the Bay Area

## Definition

Language immersion programs teach academic content in a target language alongside English, with the goal of developing bilingual proficiency. Several Bay Area private schools offer immersion starting at kindergarten entry.

## Verified Immersion Programs

### CAIS -- Chinese American International School (Mandarin)

According to CAIS, 2026-27 tuition is $46,300 (K-8) with extended day at $445/month. According to CAIS, kindergarten applicants must be 5 years old by September 1, 2026. According to CAIS, the application deadline is January 16, 2026.

According to CAIS, no Mandarin knowledge is required at kindergarten entry. This makes CAIS accessible to families with no prior Mandarin exposure.

PrivateSchoolReview lists CAIS enrollment at 555 students (PK-8).

See [[school-profiles-sf]] for full profile.

### La Scuola International School (Italian)

According to La Scuola International School, the general application deadline is January 15, 2026. According to La Scuola International School, no Italian is required at kindergarten entry.

PrivateSchoolReview lists La Scuola International School enrollment at 282 students (NS-8).

See [[school-profiles-sf]] for full profile.

## Schools Mentioned in Sources but Not Individually Researched

The following language immersion schools appear in raw source material or research planning but have not been verified with official admissions pages during this research cycle. They are listed as stubs for future research.

- **Terra School SF** (trilingual immersion, SF)

- **East Bay German International School** (German immersion, East Bay)

## Key Consideration

Both verified immersion programs (CAIS and La Scuola) explicitly state that no prior knowledge of the target language is required at kindergarten entry. This is an important consideration for families interested in immersion but concerned about language barriers.

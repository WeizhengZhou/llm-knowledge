# Language Immersion K Programs in the Bay Area

## Definition

Language immersion programs teach academic content in a target language alongside English, with the goal of developing bilingual proficiency. Several Bay Area private schools offer immersion starting at kindergarten entry. A critical consideration for families: most immersion programs explicitly do not require prior knowledge of the target language at kindergarten entry.

## Verified Immersion Programs

### CAIS -- Chinese American International School (Mandarin)

According to CAIS, 2026-27 tuition is $46,300 (K-8) with extended day at $445/month. According to CAIS, kindergarten applicants must be 5 years old by September 1, 2026. According to CAIS, the application deadline is January 16, 2026.

According to CAIS, no Mandarin knowledge is required at kindergarten entry. This makes CAIS accessible to families with no prior Mandarin exposure.

PrivateSchoolReview lists CAIS enrollment at 555 students (PK-8).

See [[school-profiles-sf]] for full profile.

### La Scuola International School (Italian)

La Scuola International School offers Italian immersion with an IB curriculum framework and Reggio Emilia-inspired approach. Prior research reports approximately 80% Italian / 20% English instruction at the elementary level. No Italian proficiency is required at kindergarten entry.

According to La Scuola International School, 2026-27 SF campus tuition is $48,850 for Elementary (K-5), $47,300 for Preschool (full day), and $50,550 for Middle School.

According to La Scuola International School, 2025-26 Silicon Valley campus Elementary (K-5) tuition is $39,775. Founding elementary families receive a 20% discount through Grade 5.

According to La Scuola International School, 30% of families utilize sliding scale tuition. The school uses the Clarity platform for financial aid assessment, with a prospective family deadline of February 1, 2026.

According to La Scuola International School, the general application deadline is January 15, 2026.

PrivateSchoolReview lists La Scuola International School enrollment at 282 students (NS-8).

See [[school-profiles-sf]] for full profile.

### Lycee Francais de San Francisco (French)

According to Lycee Francais de San Francisco, the school has two campuses in San Francisco and Marin (Sausalito area). No French proficiency is required for La Petite Ecole entry.

**Critical age difference:** According to Lycee Francais de San Francisco, CP (the French equivalent of kindergarten) requires children to be 6 years of age by September 1 of the enrollment year -- one year older than the standard US kindergarten cutoff.

According to Lycee Francais de San Francisco, typical Bay Area kindergarten-age children (turning 5 by September 1) would enroll in La Petite Ecole (LPE) or Petite Section (PS), not CP. CP corresponds to the typical US first-grade equivalent.

This is an important planning detail: families comparing Lycee Francais to other K programs must account for the grade-level offset. A child entering "kindergarten" at age 5 at most Bay Area schools would enter LPE or PS at Lycee, not CP.

According to Lycee Francais de San Francisco, after CP students choose between the French Track (French-language instruction following French national curriculum) and the International Track (bilingual IB program).

**Admissions:**

According to Lycee Francais de San Francisco, the application fee is $150 (non-refundable) and the admissions platform is Veracross, not Ravenna. According to Lycee Francais de San Francisco, first notifications are sent in early March (coordinated with other Bay Area private schools). Rolling admissions continues after March for grades with open seats. Families must respond within one week of acceptance.

**Tuition:**

According to Lycee Francais de San Francisco, 2025-26 tuition ranges from approximately $35,900 to $45,900 annually. First-year enrollment includes additional one-time fees, bringing the first year to approximately $37,900.

**Financial Aid:**

According to Lycee Francais de San Francisco, total financial aid to families in 2024-25 was $2.48 million, of which $2.04 million was funded by the school and the remainder by the French Government.

### Silicon Valley International School (SVIS) -- French, Mandarin, German

According to Silicon Valley International School (formerly International School of the Peninsula), the school is located in Palo Alto (Cohn Campus for K-5) and offers bilingual programs in French/English, Mandarin/English, and German/English.

**Admissions:**

According to Silicon Valley International School, kindergarten applicants must be 5 years old by September 1. The school may consider applicants born through December 31 based on developmental readiness and prior French-accredited school experience.

According to Silicon Valley International School, the application deadline for PS through 11th grade is January 13, 2026. The variable tuition application deadline is January 20, 2026, and supplemental forms are due January 30, 2026.

Silicon Valley International School PS-8th grade decisions are released March 19, 2026 at noon, consistent with the ISSFBA coordinated notification date. High school (9th-12th grade) decisions are released March 20, 2026 at 4:00 PM.

According to Silicon Valley International School, no language proficiency is required for kindergarten applicants in the Chinese and French programs. Children must be potty trained before starting school.

According to Silicon Valley International School, the K application process requires a campus tour, an information session, a $100 application fee, a parent conversation, a playdate assessment (January/February), and a teacher recommendation.

**Tuition:**

According to Silicon Valley International School, 2025-26 elementary (K-5) tuition is $42,630 per year. The application fee is $100 and there is a one-time new-student enrollment fee of $2,000.

## Key Considerations

All verified immersion programs (CAIS, La Scuola, Lycee Francais, SVIS) explicitly state that no prior knowledge of the target language is required at kindergarten entry. This is important for families interested in immersion but concerned about language barriers.

The Lycee Francais age quirk (CP requires age 6 by September 1) is the most significant structural difference among immersion programs and requires careful grade-level planning for families comparing options.

SVIS is unique in offering three language tracks (French, Mandarin, German) at one school. Families uncertain about which language to pursue can explore all three at a single campus.

## Keys School (Palo Alto)

### Quick Facts

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Grades | K-8 |
| Location | Palo Alto (Peninsula) |
| Application Deadline | January 9 |
| Tuition (2025-26) | $44,600/year (K-4); $48,300/year (Grades 5-8) |
| New Student Fee | $2,000 one-time |
| Decision Date | March 20 (2024-25 cycle, L3) |

### Details

Cardinal Education reports that Keys School's 2024-25 application deadline was January 9 and decisions were released March 20, 2025. The official Keys School tuition page does not list admissions dates. The February 19 date previously cited was a data extraction error. Cardinal Education reports a March 20 decision date for Keys School in the 2024-25 cycle. This is one day after the ISSFBA coordinated March 19 date, which may indicate Keys School is not an ISSFBA member, though this has not been directly confirmed.

Cardinal Education reports that Keys School strongly recommends kindergarten applicants turn 5 by September 1. Cardinal Education reports that Keys School's primary entry points are Kindergarten and sixth grade, with other grades considered based on available openings.

Cardinal Education reports that Keys School K applications require a campus playdate assessing social and academic readiness, and a Parent/Guardian Conversation with the admissions team. Cardinal Education reports that the Keys School application includes a $100 non-refundable fee, Parent/Guardian Questionnaire, teacher recommendations (math and English), transcripts, and family photographs, submitted via Ravenna.

According to Keys School, 2025-26 tuition is $44,600/year (K-4) and $48,300/year (Grades 5-8). According to Keys School, there is a one-time new student enrollment fee of $2,000.

PrivateSchoolReview lists Keys School enrollment at 324 students (K-8).

## Nueva School (Hillsborough)

### Quick Facts

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Grades | PK-12 (gifted-focused) |
| Location | Hillsborough (Peninsula) |
| Application Deadline | January 8, 2026 |
| Application Fee | $100 (nonrefundable) |
| Activity Session Prerequisite | November 3, 2025 |
| Tuition | $30,555 (PK) to $51,285 (high school) |
| Financial Aid Deadline | February 3, 2026 |
| Class Size | 15-18 students, 6:1 ratio |

### Details

According to Nueva School, the K application deadline is January 8, 2026. According to Nueva School, the application fee is $100 (nonrefundable). According to Nueva School, the activity session prerequisite deadline is November 3, 2025.

Nueva School requires a Wechsler IQ assessment administered by a licensed psychologist for kindergarten admission. According to Nueva School, IQ scores must fall within the school's target range for admission consideration. The school does not publish the specific minimum score on its admissions page. According to the SF Standard (December 2025), Nueva School requires a minimum IQ score of 130 for admission. See [[assessment-playdate]] for more on IQ testing requirements.

According to Nueva School, financial aid vouchers are available for IQ assessment costs. According to Nueva School, the financial aid application deadline is February 3, 2026.

According to Nueva School, tuition ranges from $30,555 (PK) to $51,285 (high school). According to Nueva School, class sizes are 15-18 students with a 6:1 student-teacher ratio.

## Park Day School (Oakland)

### Quick Facts

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Grades | TK-8 |
| Location | Oakland (East Bay) |
| K Age Cutoff | 5 by September 15 (recommends September 30; evaluates early October individually) |
| TK Age Cutoff | 4 by September 1 |
| Application Deadline | January 16, 2026 |
| Application Fee | $100 (or $65 if submitted by December 8, 2025) |
| Decision Date | March 19, 2026 |
| Tuition (indexed, K-5) | $1,900-$37,980 |
| Student-Teacher Ratio | 1:8 |
| Platform | [[ravenna-hub|Ravenna]] |

### Details

According to Park Day School, the K application deadline is January 16, 2026, with a $100 fee. According to Park Day School, a reduced application fee of $65 applies if submitted by December 8, 2025. According to Park Day School, the school uses the Ravenna admissions platform.

According to Park Day School, kindergarten applicants must be 5 years old by September 15; the school recommends September 30, and evaluates early October birthdays individually. According to Park Day School, TK applicants must be 4 years old by September 1. Park Day has the most flexible age cutoff among schools in this research set.

Park Day School K-8 admission notifications are released March 19, 2026, consistent with the ISSFBA coordinated notification date.

### Tuition and Financial Aid

According to Park Day School, indexed tuition ranges are K-5 $1,900-$37,980, Grades 6-8 $2,000-$40,620, and TK $28,500. According to Park Day School, approximately 45% of families participate in the indexed tuition program. According to Park Day School, the student-teacher ratio is 1:8.

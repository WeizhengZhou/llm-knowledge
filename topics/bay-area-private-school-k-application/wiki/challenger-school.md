## Overview

According to Challenger School, the school operates approximately 27 campuses across the western United States, including 10 in the California Bay Area. According to Challenger School, the school uses a direct instruction / structured learning model. Challenger is not a confirmed ISSFBA member and operates on a rolling admissions schedule.

## California Campuses

According to Challenger School, its California campuses include Almaden, Ardenwood, Berryessa, Harwood, Middlefield, Newark, Saratoga, Shawnee, Strawberry Park, and Sunnyvale (10 campuses total). Eight of these are in the South Bay/Peninsula area.

## Application Process

According to Challenger School, enrollment applications are accepted year-round; early submission is recommended as classes fill. According to Challenger School, a campus visit is required for all prospective students before acceptance, including on-site in-person interviews and evaluations.

According to Challenger School, new kindergarten through eighth-grade applicants must complete a placement test.

According to the raw file notes, Challenger School charges no application fee for academic-year programs. However, the live-fetched admissions page references 'pay an application fee and tuition deposit' as a requirement. This discrepancy could not be fully resolved.

## Age Eligibility

According to Challenger School, the California kindergarten age cutoff is December 2 (children must be 5 years old by December 2). This matches the California state standard cutoff.

## Tuition

According to Challenger School, tuition is billed at a daily rate, with an annual deposit applied to the first month's tuition. According to Challenger School, tuition covers the full school year with a discount available for single annual payment.

PrivateSchoolReview lists Challenger School Almaden campus tuition at approximately $12,000 annually; this figure is undated and may not reflect current rates. The official Challenger tuition page uses dynamic loading and specific rates could not be independently verified.

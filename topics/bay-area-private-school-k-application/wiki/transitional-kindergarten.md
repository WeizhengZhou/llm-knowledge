# Transitional Kindergarten (TK)

## Definition

Transitional Kindergarten (TK) is the first year of a two-year kindergarten program. According to the CDE, TK is the first year of a two-year kindergarten program, and TK curriculum follows the California Preschool/Transitional Kindergarten Learning Foundations rather than kindergarten standards.

TK exists in two distinct forms in the Bay Area: free public TK (guaranteed by state law) and tuition-based private TK (competitive, with potential internal pathways to K).

## Public TK (California State Program)

Starting in 2025-26, children who turn four years old on or before September 1 are eligible for public Transitional Kindergarten (TK) in California. Starting in 2025-26, California LEAs (local educational agencies) are required to make TK available to all eligible children.

TK participation is voluntary in California; children are not required to attend.

### Key Facts

| Attribute | Public TK |
|-----------|-----------|
| Cost | Free |
| Age cutoff | 4 by September 1 |
| Availability | Guaranteed -- LEAs may not maintain waiting lists |
| Curriculum | CA Preschool/TK Learning Foundations |

According to the CDE, private school TK programs are not subject to the same state mandates as public TK; private schools create their own TK programs independently.

### District Expansion

According to the raw research file citing district sources, SFUSD added 15 TK programs in 2025-26, Oakland USD added 7, and San Jose USD added 3.

### Guaranteed K Seat

According to the raw research file citing CDE sources, starting 2026-27 all public TK students receive a guaranteed kindergarten seat at their feeder school. This specific claim was not independently confirmed on the CDE FAQ page during live verification.

## Private TK Programs

Several Bay Area private schools offer their own TK programs. These are tuition-based, competitive, and operate independently of the state TK mandate.

The Hamlin School does not offer a TK program; the lowest entry grade is kindergarten.

### Tuition Comparison

Public TK in California is free. Among the private TK programs researched, tuition is generally comparable to kindergarten tuition at the same school; for example, Stratford School charges the same rate ($25,170) for both TK and K.

## TK as a Strategic Pathway to K

A key strategic question for parents is whether enrolling in a private school's TK program improves admission chances for kindergarten at that school.

### What Schools Say (vs. What Community Sources Report)

According to Harker School, the TK program enrolls 24 students and is located on the Boynton campus. Some community sources describe Harker's TK program as creating an internal pipeline to kindergarten. Harker's official admissions page describes TK as providing 'an additional year of academic/social growth before transitioning into kindergarten the following year' but does not explicitly state that TK students receive priority admission for kindergarten.

According to the research compilation, Synapse School (Menlo Park) offers a TK program with an age guideline of turning 4 by September 1, 2026. Some community sources describe Synapse School (Menlo Park) TK program as a confirmed entry point to their K-8 program.

Some community sources describe CAIS as operating a preschool through 8th grade unified program on a single campus with an internal TK/K pipeline.

> **Epistemic note:** Multiple community sources describe internal TK-to-K "pipelines" at schools
> like Harker, Synapse, and CAIS. However, none of these schools explicitly state on their
> official admissions pages that TK students receive priority for K admission. The pipeline
> is inferred from program structure, not confirmed policy.

## How It Works: Public vs. Private Decision Framework

For parents of children with summer/fall birthdays, the key decision points are:

1. **Age eligibility:** Does your child qualify for public TK (turning 4 by September 1)? For private TK, each school sets its own cutoff.
2. **Cost:** Public TK is free. Private TK tuition can range from $25,170 ([[stratford-school|Stratford]]) to $46,350 ([[harker-school|Harker]]) annually.
3. **K pipeline value:** If your target K school offers TK, enrolling may provide familiarity and a potential (but unconfirmed) admissions advantage.
4. **Curriculum approach:** Public TK follows state foundations; private TK follows the school's own philosophy.

## Relevance

TK is particularly relevant for families considering:
- Schools with late age cutoffs (e.g., [[harker-school|Harker]], November 30) where a child might qualify for either TK or K
- The South Bay market, where [[harker-school|Harker]], [[stratford-school|Stratford]], and others offer private TK
- Financial planning, as private TK adds a year of tuition before K-12

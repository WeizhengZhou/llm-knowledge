# K Assessments and Playdates

## Overview

Bay Area private K admissions assessments fall into two distinct categories: **playdate/observation-based** (used by most SF schools) and **formal IQ testing** (used by select Peninsula/South Bay gifted-focused schools). According to the SF Standard (December 2025), SF city schools such as Hamlin, CDS, SF Day, and Live Oak do not require IQ tests for kindergarten admission.

## Playdate / Observation Format (SF Schools)

Most San Francisco independent schools assess kindergarten applicants through supervised play sessions where teachers observe children in a low-pressure, group setting. These are not formal tests.

### Parent Interviews

Most schools also require a parent/guardian interview:

- According to Convent & Stuart Hall, parent interviews are approximately 30 minutes, scheduled November 2025 through February 2026.
- According to The Hamlin School, a parent/guardian interview is required, scheduled October through January via Ravenna.
- According to Live Oak School, parent/guardian interviews are 20 minutes, one-on-one, with a virtual option available.
- According to The San Francisco School, attending an information session is mandatory before submitting an application.

### What Schools Evaluate

According to Cardinal Education (Bay Area admissions consulting firm), schools assess kindergarten applicants on:
- Social-emotional readiness
- Motor skills and independence
- Curiosity and adaptability
- Ability to follow directions and interact socially

Cardinal Education describes the typical assessment: "4-year-olds join a small group of kids for something like preschool circle time where teachers observe and interact, with snacks and free play in a low-key, low-pressure environment." (source: cardinaleducation.com, April 2026)

## IQ Testing Format (Peninsula / Gifted Schools)

A distinct group of Peninsula and South Bay schools require formal cognitive assessments for kindergarten admission.

### Schools Requiring IQ Tests

Nueva School requires a Wechsler IQ assessment administered by a licensed psychologist for kindergarten admission. According to Nueva School, IQ scores must fall within the school's target range for admission consideration. The school does not publish the specific minimum score on its admissions page. According to the SF Standard (December 2025), Nueva School requires a minimum IQ score of 130 for admission.

[[harker-school|Harker School]] (San Jose): According to Harker School, a cognitive assessment is required for all TK through grade 2 applicants and must be administered by a psychologist from Harker's preferred list. According to Harker School, coaching or tutoring for the cognitive assessment disqualifies the application. According to Harker School, the TK/K application deadline is January 8, 2026 at 5:00 p.m. PST. According to Harker School, TK and kindergarten applicants must be 5 years old by November 30, 2026. According to Harker School, the school uses the Ravenna Solutions admissions platform. See [[harker-school]] for full profile.

According to the SF Standard (December 2025), Helios School in Sunnyvale requires an IQ test but does not publish a minimum score.

According to Head-Royce School, K applicants use an Assessment Day format rather than the ISEE. According to Head-Royce School, the ISEE is required for grades 2-12 but not kindergarten.

### Placement Tests (Non-IQ)

[[challenger-school|Challenger School]]: According to Challenger School, new kindergarten through eighth-grade applicants must complete a placement test. According to Challenger School, a campus visit is required for all prospective students before acceptance, including on-site in-person interviews and evaluations. Challenger uses a placement test rather than a formal IQ assessment. See [[challenger-school]] for full profile.

[[stratford-school|Stratford School]]: According to Stratford School, kindergarten and above applicants are interviewed and assessed by a grade-level teacher to confirm readiness. This is a teacher-administered readiness assessment, not a formal cognitive or IQ test. See [[stratford-school]] for full profile.

### Test Details

The standard assessment is the **Wechsler Preschool and Primary Scale of Intelligence (WPPSI)**:

- According to the SF Standard (December 2025), a Wechsler (WPPSI) IQ assessment costs approximately $850 per test.
- Duration: 1-2 hours (source: SF Standard, December 2025)
- Administered by a licensed psychologist
- Measures: problem-solving, short-term memory, verbal/visual strengths

According to Nueva School, financial aid vouchers are available for IQ assessment costs.

> **Epistemic note:** The following is from a single neuropsychologist's client population
> and does not represent the general population or all Bay Area applicants.

According to the SF Standard (December 2025), neuropsychologist Rachel Holowicki reports that among her clients, approximately 20% score 130 or above, representing roughly the top 2% globally. This figure reflects a self-selected client population, not a representative sample.

## Common Mistakes

- **Over-preparing children for playdates:** SF school playdates are designed to observe natural behavior. Coaching children can backfire if it makes them appear less authentic.
- **Assuming all schools require IQ tests:** IQ testing is primarily a Peninsula/South Bay gifted-school requirement. Most SF city schools use observation-based assessments.
- **Delaying IQ testing:** For schools that require it, testing must be completed months before the application deadline. Neuropsychologists book up during admissions season (September-January).

# South Bay Private K Schools

## Overview

The South Bay (San Jose, Sunnyvale, Saratoga, and surrounding cities) has a distinct private K admissions culture compared to San Francisco. Key differences include:

- **Rolling admissions** are more common (Challenger, Stratford) vs. fixed January deadlines in SF
- **No ISSFBA coordination** for most South Bay schools -- decision dates, response deadlines, and contract policies are school-specific
- **Cognitive/placement assessments** replace the playdate model used by SF schools
- **Multi-campus operators** (Challenger with 10 CA campuses, Stratford with 15+ Bay Area campuses) coexist with selective single-campus schools (Harker)
- **Lower tuition floor** -- Stratford at $25,170 (2025-26) compared to SF schools typically in the $37,000-$49,000 range

## Detailed Profiles

### Harker School (San Jose)

See [[harker-school]] for full profile.

According to Harker School's tuition page, 2026-27 tuition is $46,350 for TK and $51,550 for kindergarten through grade 5. Tuition includes lunch, before- and after-school care, intercampus shuttles, and local transportation. According to Harker School, the TK/K application deadline is January 8, 2026 at 5:00 p.m. PST. According to Harker School, decision notifications are released after 4:00 p.m. PST on March 20, 2026. Harker is not a confirmed ISSFBA member; this date is one day after the ISSFBA coordinated March 19 date.

### Challenger School (10 Campuses)

See [[challenger-school]] for full profile.

According to Challenger School, enrollment applications are accepted year-round; early submission is recommended as classes fill. According to Challenger School, the school uses a direct instruction / structured learning model. Challenger is not a confirmed ISSFBA member and operates on a rolling admissions schedule.

### Stratford School (15+ Campuses)

See [[stratford-school]] for full profile.

According to Stratford School, applications are welcomed throughout the school year provided space is available; there is no published application deadline. According to Stratford School, TK and kindergarten annual tuition at the San Jose-Almaden campus is $25,170 (2025-26). Other South Bay campuses may have different rates.

### Helios School (Sunnyvale) -- Stub

According to the SF Standard (December 2025), Helios School in Sunnyvale requires an IQ test but does not publish a minimum score. No additional verified data is available for Helios at this time.

### BASIS Independent Silicon Valley (San Jose) -- Stub

TestingForKindergarten.com lists BASIS Independent Silicon Valley as a TK-12 rigorous academic school in San Jose with a 56% acceptance rate. This rate is school-wide across all grades, sourced from the aggregator, and does not reflect K-specific selectivity.

| Attribute | San Francisco | South Bay |

## Prerequisites

Before applying to South Bay schools, families should:
1. Determine age eligibility -- cutoffs range from November 30 (Harker) to December 2 (Challenger, Stratford, California standard)
2. Understand whether your child needs a cognitive assessment (Harker) or placement test (Challenger) and book early
3. Research campus-specific details for multi-campus operators (Challenger and Stratford rates and programs vary by campus)

## Common Mistakes

- **Assuming ISSFBA rules apply:** South Bay schools generally do not follow the coordinated notification date or single-contract policy. Families can potentially hold multiple South Bay offers.
- **Treating rolling admissions as low-priority:** According to Challenger School, early submission is recommended as classes fill. Rolling does not mean unlimited availability.
- **Comparing tuition without scope:** According to Harker School's tuition page, tuition includes lunch, before- and after-school care, intercampus shuttles, and local transportation. Other schools may charge separately for these services.

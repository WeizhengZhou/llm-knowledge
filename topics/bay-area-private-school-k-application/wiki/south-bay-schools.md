# South Bay Private K Schools

## Overview

The South Bay (San Jose, Sunnyvale, Palo Alto, Saratoga, and surrounding cities) has a diverse private K admissions landscape. Key characteristics include:

- **Mixed ISSFBA alignment:** Some South Bay schools follow ISSFBA coordinated timing (Helios March 19, Almaden Country Day March 19, Gideon Hausner March 19), while others operate independently (Harker March 20, BASIS SV March 18, Challenger/Stratford rolling).
- **Rolling admissions** are common among multi-campus operators (Challenger, Stratford) vs. fixed January deadlines for selective single-campus schools
- **Cognitive/placement assessments** replace the playdate model used by most SF schools -- see [[concepts/assessment-prep]] for IQ testing logistics
- **Multi-campus operators** (Challenger with 10 CA campuses, Stratford with 15+ Bay Area campuses) coexist with selective single-campus schools (Harker, Helios)
- **Wide tuition range** -- from Stratford at $25,170 (2025-26) to Harker K-5 at $51,550 (2026-27)

## Detailed Profiles

### Harker School (San Jose)

See [[harker-school]] for full profile.

According to Harker School's tuition page, 2026-27 tuition is $46,350 for TK and $51,550 for kindergarten through grade 5. Tuition includes lunch, before- and after-school care, intercampus shuttles, and local transportation. According to Harker School, the TK/K application deadline is January 8, 2026 at 5:00 p.m. PST. According to Harker School, decision notifications are released after 4:00 p.m. PST on March 20, 2026. Harker is not a confirmed ISSFBA member; this date is one day after the ISSFBA coordinated March 19 date.

### Helios School (Sunnyvale)

According to Helios School, it is an independent school for K-8 gifted learners in Sunnyvale, CA, serving approximately 180 students.

**Admissions:**

According to Helios School, kindergarten applicants must be age 5 by September 1, 2026. According to Helios School, the preliminary application deadline is January 8, 2026 at 12pm noon. Helios School admission decisions are released March 19, 2026 at 12pm noon, aligning with the ISSFBA coordinated notification date. According to Helios School, the enrollment contract is due March 26, 2026 at 12pm noon.

According to Helios School, the application fee is $100, with fee waivers available upon request. According to Helios School, all admissions applications are submitted through the Ravenna platform.

According to Helios School, the K application requires: a Ravenna account, attending a required Zoom Q&A info session, a campus tour, a WPPSI-IV IQ assessment, one teacher recommendation, a preliminary application, supplemental documents (IQ report and family photo), a student workshop, and a parent interview.

**Assessment:**

Helios School requires the WPPSI-IV for applicants under age 6 and the WISC-V for applicants age 6 and older. Helios School states it does not have a minimum Full Scale IQ requirement and evaluates the complete IQ report across all subtest indices rather than focusing solely on the FSIQ score. Helios School supplemental documents, including the IQ report, are due February 2, 2026. See [[concepts/assessment-prep]] for detailed IQ testing logistics.

**Tuition and Financial Aid:**

According to Helios School, 2026-27 tuition is $46,360 for Lower School (K-5) and $50,570 for Middle School (6-8). According to Helios School, 2025-26 tuition is $44,575 for Lower School (K-5) and $48,625 for Middle School (6-8).

According to Helios School, financial aid is need-based only (no merit-based awards), and admissions decisions are made separately from financial aid evaluations. According to Helios School, the financial aid application uses the Clarity platform, with a new family deadline of January 8, 2026. According to Helios School, all fieldwork and trips are included in tuition costs.

### Gideon Hausner Jewish Day School (Palo Alto)

**Admissions:**

According to Gideon Hausner Jewish Day School, all applications are submitted through the Ravenna platform. According to Gideon Hausner Jewish Day School, the first-round application deadline for 2026-27 is January 29, 2026.

Gideon Hausner Jewish Day School first-round decisions are released March 19, 2026, consistent with the ISSFBA coordinated notification date. Deposits are due March 30, 2026.

According to Gideon Hausner Jewish Day School, the second-round application deadline is April 16, 2026, with decisions April 27, 2026 and deposits due May 6, 2026.

According to Gideon Hausner Jewish Day School, the application fee is $100 per student (nonrefundable). According to Gideon Hausner Jewish Day School, K applicants are invited to a one-hour visit between February and March. Admissions staff also visit each K applicant at their current preschool or daycare setting.

**Inclusivity:**

According to Gideon Hausner Jewish Day School, no formal Jewish identity is required for enrollment. The school states it admits students of any race, color, sexual orientation, gender identity, national and ethnic origin.

**Tuition and Financial Aid:**

According to Gideon Hausner Jewish Day School, 2026-27 tuition is $38,340 for TK, $42,480 for K through Grade 4, and $46,310 for Grades 5-8. According to Gideon Hausner Jewish Day School, tuition includes books and supplies, lunch, morning snacks, iPad for lower school, MacBook for upper school, field trips (TK-3), and competitive sports (Grades 5-8).

According to Gideon Hausner Jewish Day School, the nonrefundable enrollment deposit is $2,800 per student ($1,000 with financial assistance), applied toward annual tuition.

According to Gideon Hausner Jewish Day School, need-based financial aid covers up to 75% of total tuition. Applications are submitted via the Clarity platform ($65 fee) by January 29, 2026.

According to Gideon Hausner Jewish Day School, the Professional Partner Program offers a 40% tuition discount for full-time employees of Oshman Family JCC and Kehillah Jewish High School, and a 30% discount for qualifying Jewish nonprofit staff.

According to Gideon Hausner Jewish Day School, the Sibling Savings Program provides $1,000-$2,000 discounts per additional child for families with gross household income under $650,000 and liquid assets under $500,000. The application deadline is April 20, 2026.

### Almaden Country Day School (San Jose)

According to Almaden Country Day School, the school is located in San Jose (Almaden Valley), serves PreK-8, and is CAIS/WASC accredited with a progressive, project-based philosophy and strong STEM integration.

**Admissions:**

According to Almaden Country Day School, kindergarten applicants must turn 5 by September 1. According to Almaden Country Day School, applications open November 1, with a first-round deadline of February 1. Decisions for first-round applicants are released March 19, 2026. According to Almaden Country Day School, the application fee is $100.

According to Almaden Country Day School, the K admissions process includes an online application, a 30-minute family conversation with the admissions team, a decision letter, and an enrollment deposit. No shadow visit is required for K applicants (shadow visits are for Grades 1-7 only).

According to Almaden Country Day School, the school uses the ISSFBA standard Confidential Student Evaluation form. The March 19 decision date is consistent with ISSFBA coordinated timing.

**Tuition and Financial Aid:**

According to Almaden Country Day School, 2026-27 tuition is $37,330 for Pre-K (5 full days), $39,340 for Kindergarten through Grade 5, and $41,000 for Middle School (Grades 6-8). According to Almaden Country Day School, the non-refundable enrollment deposit is $2,500.

According to Almaden Country Day School, admissions is need-blind. The financial aid application is due February 23, 2026 via the Clarity platform.

According to Almaden Country Day School, The Learning Center (special needs support) adds $17,430 to K-5 tuition, for a combined total of $56,770.

According to Almaden Country Day School, families paying full tuition by June 15 receive a $400 discount for full-day programs.

### BASIS Independent Silicon Valley (San Jose)

According to BASIS Independent Silicon Valley, it is a TK-12 school in San Jose, part of the BASIS Ed network, with a knowledge-rich, content-intensive curriculum emphasizing high academic standards.

**Admissions:**

According to BASIS Independent Silicon Valley, kindergarten students must turn 5 by September 1. According to BASIS Independent Silicon Valley, applications are submitted through Ravenna Hub.

According to BASIS Independent Silicon Valley, the regular admissions timeline for Fall 2026 is: applications open August 1, 2025; submission deadline January 28, 2026; completion deadline February 13, 2026; notification March 18, 2026; commitment deadline March 27, 2026.

According to BASIS Independent Silicon Valley, rolling admissions opens January 29, 2026, with notification within 10 business days of completed application and commitment within 10 business days of offer.

According to BASIS Independent Silicon Valley, a non-refundable enrollment deposit of $3,000 is required with the signed Enrollment Agreement by the commitment deadline.

According to BASIS Independent Silicon Valley, the school's notification date of March 18, 2026, is one day before the ISSFBA coordinated March 19 date. BASIS Independent SV does not appear to be an ISSFBA member based on its independent admissions timeline.

**Tuition:**

BASIS Independent Silicon Valley tuition was previously reported as approximately $39,795 in earlier research; the current official tuition page was not re-verified in this pass. Families should confirm current tuition directly with the school.

**Acceptance Rate:**

BASIS Independent Silicon Valley does not publish an official acceptance rate. TestingForKindergarten.com lists a school-wide acceptance rate of 56%, though this figure is from an aggregator with no disclosed methodology and does not reflect K-specific selectivity.

### Challenger School (10 Campuses)

According to Challenger School, enrollment applications are accepted year-round; early submission is recommended as classes fill. According to Challenger School, the school uses a direct instruction / structured learning model. Challenger is not a confirmed ISSFBA member and operates on a rolling admissions schedule.

### Stratford School (15+ Campuses)

See [[stratford-school]] for full profile.

According to Stratford School, applications are welcomed throughout the school year provided space is available; there is no published application deadline. According to Stratford School, TK and kindergarten annual tuition at the San Jose-Almaden campus is $25,170 (2025-26). Other South Bay campuses may have different rates.

## How South Bay Differs from SF

| Attribute | San Francisco | South Bay |
|-----------|--------------|-----------|
| ISSFBA coordination | Most schools are members | Mixed: Helios, Almaden, Hausner follow March 19; Harker (March 20), BASIS SV (March 18) are independent; Challenger/Stratford are rolling |
| Decision date | Coordinated March 19 | School-specific (see comparison table above) |
| Application deadline | Fixed, mostly January | Mixed: Jan 8-Feb 1 (selective); rolling (multi-campus) |
| Assessment model | Playdate / observation | IQ test (Helios, Harker), family conversation (Almaden), visit + preschool observation (Hausner), placement test (Challenger), teacher assessment (Stratford) |
| Admissions platform | [[ravenna-hub\|Ravenna]] (11+ schools) | Ravenna (Harker, Helios, Hausner, BASIS); school-specific (Challenger, Stratford, Almaden) |
| Multi-campus operators | Rare | Common (Challenger 10 campuses, Stratford 15+) |
| Tuition range | $37,980-$49,830 (selective independent) | $25,170-$51,550 (wide range) |

## Prerequisites

Before applying to South Bay schools, families should:
1. Determine age eligibility -- cutoffs range from November 30 (Harker) to December 2 (Challenger, Stratford, California standard), with September 1 at Helios, Almaden, and BASIS
2. Understand whether your child needs a cognitive assessment (Harker, Helios) and book early -- see [[concepts/assessment-prep]]
3. Research campus-specific details for multi-campus operators (Challenger and Stratford rates and programs vary by campus)
4. Note that Gideon Hausner has a Round 2 option (April 16 deadline, April 27 decisions) for families who miss the January 29 Round 1

## Common Mistakes

- **Assuming South Bay schools do not follow ISSFBA:** Several South Bay schools (Helios, Almaden Country Day, Gideon Hausner) DO follow the coordinated March 19 notification date. Do not generalize -- check each school individually.
- **Treating rolling admissions as low-priority:** According to Challenger School, early submission is recommended as classes fill. Rolling does not mean unlimited availability.
- **Comparing tuition without scope:** According to Harker School's tuition page, tuition includes lunch, before- and after-school care, intercampus shuttles, and local transportation. Other schools may charge separately for these services. According to Gideon Hausner Jewish Day School, tuition includes books and supplies, lunch, morning snacks, iPad for lower school, MacBook for upper school, field trips (TK-3), and competitive sports (Grades 5-8).
- **Missing the Gideon Hausner Round 2 window:** Families who discover Hausner after the January 29 Round 1 deadline still have an April 16 second-round option.

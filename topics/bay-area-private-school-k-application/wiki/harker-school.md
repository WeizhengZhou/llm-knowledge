## Overview

Harker School is a TK-12 college-preparatory school in San Jose. It is among the most selective South Bay schools, requiring a formal cognitive assessment for TK through grade 2 applicants. According to Harker School, the school uses the Ravenna Solutions admissions platform.

According to Harker School, decision notifications are released after 4:00 p.m. PST on March 20, 2026. Harker is not a confirmed ISSFBA member; this date is one day after the ISSFBA coordinated March 19 date.

## Age Eligibility

According to Harker School, TK and kindergarten applicants must be 5 years old by November 30, 2026. This is the latest age cutoff among schools in this research set (most SF schools use August 1 or September 1).

According to Harker School, children born July 1-November 30, 2021 may apply for either TK or kindergarten, but not both. According to Harker School, children born July 1, 2020-June 30, 2021 must apply for kindergarten only.

## TK Program

According to Harker School, the TK program enrolls 24 students and is located on the Boynton campus.

Some community sources describe Harker's TK program as creating an internal pipeline to kindergarten. Harker's official admissions page describes TK as providing 'an additional year of academic/social growth before transitioning into kindergarten the following year' but does not explicitly state that TK students receive priority admission for kindergarten.

See [[transitional-kindergarten]] for more on private TK programs and their strategic role.

## Application Process

According to Harker School, the TK/K application deadline is January 8, 2026 at 5:00 p.m. PST. According to Harker School, application event registration opens August 28, 2025 at 9:00 a.m. PST.

According to Harker School, the TK application requires a preliminary application, application fee, student activities documentation, parent/guardian interview, cognitive assessment, teacher recommendation, student visit, student photo, and family photo.

### Cognitive Assessment

According to Harker School, a cognitive assessment is required for all TK through grade 2 applicants and must be administered by a psychologist from Harker's preferred list. According to Harker School, coaching or tutoring for the cognitive assessment disqualifies the application.

According to the SF Standard (December 2025), a Wechsler (WPPSI) IQ assessment costs approximately $850 per test.

See [[assessment-playdate]] for details on IQ testing vs. playdate-based assessments.

## Tuition and Financial Aid

According to Harker School's tuition page, 2026-27 tuition is $46,350 for TK and $51,550 for kindergarten through grade 5. Tuition includes lunch, before- and after-school care, intercampus shuttles, and local transportation.

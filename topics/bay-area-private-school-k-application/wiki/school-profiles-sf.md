## SF Day School

### Quick Facts

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Grades | K-8 |
| Age Cutoff | 5 by August 1, 2026 |
| Application Deadline | December 19 |
| Application Fee | $100 (fee waivers available) |
| Tuition (2025-26) | $48,577 |
| Decision Date | March 19, 12:00-2:00 p.m. |
| Enrollment Deadline | March 24, 10:00 a.m. |
| Platform | [[ravenna-hub|Ravenna]] |

### Details

According to SF Day School's admissions page, children must turn 5 by August 1, 2026 to apply for the Class of 2035. According to SF Day School's admissions page, the application deadline is December 19. According to SF Day School, the application portal opens September 2.

SF Day School admission decisions are released March 19 between 12:00 and 2:00 p.m. According to SF Day School, enrollment contracts and deposits are due March 24 at 10:00 a.m.

According to SF Day School, the application fee is $100, with fee waivers available upon request. According to SF Day School's admissions page, the school uses the Ravenna admissions platform. According to SF Day School, the school uses the ISSFBA Teacher Evaluation Form.

According to SF Day School, the Kindergarten Open House is September 18, 6:00-8:00 p.m.

### Tuition and Financial Aid

According to SF Day School, 2025-26 annual tuition is $48,577. According to SF Day School, the average tuition adjustment is $28,800, with a minimum family contribution of 5% of tuition. According to SF Day School, tuition adjustments range up to 95% reduction, with a minimum family contribution of 5%. According to SF Day School, the adjusted tuition budget for 2024-25 was $3.1 million.

According to SF Day School, the Clarity financial aid application costs $65, waived for low-income applicants. SF Day School's key dates page lists the financial aid (Clarity) deadline as January 12; the tuition page lists January 13 for returning families and January 12 for new families.

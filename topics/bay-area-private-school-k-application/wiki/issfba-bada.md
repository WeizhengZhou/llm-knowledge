## Description

According to ISSFBA, the organization covers 110+ independent schools across the SF Bay Area. According to ISSFBA, BADA was incorporated as a 501(c)(6) nonprofit in 2013. According to ISSFBA, BADA has over 75 member schools as of July 2019. The organization was founded by seven K-8 school admission directors in the early 1990s to establish common decision dates and coordinate support efforts.

ISSFBA/BADA's core purpose is to "support families and schools in the admission process by promoting best practices" and ensuring "smooth, respectful and predictable admission experiences." (source: issfba.org)

## Coordinated Policies

### Notification Dates (2025-26 Cycle)

According to ISSFBA, the 2025-26 K-8 electronic notifications are sent March 19, 2026, with family responses due March 26, 2026.

According to ISSFBA, the 2025-26 PreK/TK/JK electronic notifications are sent March 11, 2026, with family responses due March 17, 2026.

**Exceptions:** Not all schools follow the coordinated date exactly. According to Live Oak School, admission decisions are released March 17, 2026 at 4:00 p.m. This is two days before the ISSFBA coordinated notification date of March 19. Cardinal Education reports a March 20 decision date for Keys School in the 2024-25 cycle. This is one day after the ISSFBA coordinated March 19 date, which may indicate Keys School is not an ISSFBA member, though this has not been directly confirmed. According to BASIS Independent Silicon Valley, the school's notification date of March 18, 2026, is one day before the ISSFBA coordinated March 19 date. BASIS Independent SV does not appear to be an ISSFBA member based on its independent admissions timeline. Nueva School K-8 admissions decisions are released March 20, 2026 at approximately 4 p.m. via Ravenna. This is one day after the ISSFBA coordinated date, confirming Nueva operates independently from ISSFBA.

### Contract Exclusivity

According to ISSFBA, once a family signs a contract with one ISSFBA school, other ISSFBA schools do not typically offer a contract. This is the mechanism that drives waitlist movement -- as families commit to one school, spots open at others.

### Shared Evaluation Form

According to ISSFBA, member schools share a common Confidential Student Evaluation Form in two versions (PreK-K and Grades 1-8). According to Ravenna Solutions, the ISSFBA Teacher Evaluation Form can be submitted once and routed to multiple schools.

### No First-Choice Pressure

According to ISSFBA, member schools do not encourage first-choice declarations or letters of recommendation. This is corroborated by individual school policies; for example, according to The Hamlin School, declaring Hamlin as a first choice carries no admissions advantage.

### Application Deadlines

According to ISSFBA, most member schools have application deadlines in January of the enrollment year. This is confirmed by cross-referencing individual school deadlines, which fall in December-January.

## Application Process Overview

According to ISSFBA, common application elements across member schools include:
1. Application and fee (January deadlines typical; fee may be reduced or waived for financial aid applicants)
2. Shared evaluation forms (teachers complete once, send to multiple schools)
3. Official transcript or equivalent
4. Parent/family interview and student visit
5. Developmental and/or academic assessment

## Waitlist Process

Waiting pools remain active during notification periods, particularly approaching the contract deadline. Once the response deadline passes, waitlist movement typically accelerates as committed families free up spots elsewhere. Families should contact schools directly about maintaining active waitlists after response deadlines, as policies vary by institution.

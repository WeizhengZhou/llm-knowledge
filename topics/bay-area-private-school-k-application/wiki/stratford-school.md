## Overview

Stratford School is a multi-campus operator with 15+ Bay Area locations offering preschool through middle school. According to Stratford School, the school follows a traditional/structured academic approach emphasizing critical thinking and problem-solving. Stratford is not a confirmed ISSFBA member and operates on a rolling admissions schedule.

## South Bay Campuses

According to Stratford School, South Bay campuses include San Jose-Almaden, San Jose-San Anselmo Way, Santa Clara-Winchester, Sunnyvale-De Anza Park, Sunnyvale-Washington Park, Milpitas-Beresford Square, Los Gatos-Kensington Way, Morgan Hill, and San Jose Middle School.

## Application Process

According to Stratford School, applications are welcomed throughout the school year provided space is available; there is no published application deadline. According to Stratford School, kindergarten and above applicants are interviewed and assessed by a grade-level teacher to confirm readiness.

## Age Eligibility

According to Stratford School, kindergarten applicants must turn 5 years old on or before December 2 of the enrollment school year. This matches the California state standard cutoff and is the same as [[challenger-school|Challenger School]].

## Tuition

According to Stratford School, TK and kindergarten annual tuition at the San Jose-Almaden campus is $25,170 (2025-26). Other South Bay campuses may have different rates. According to Stratford School, tuition covers the full school year from end of August through beginning of June, with a discount available for single annual payment.

This is notably lower than single-campus selective schools such as [[harker-school|Harker]] ($46,350 TK / $51,550 K-5 for 2026-27).

# Bay Area Private K Application Timeline (2025-26 Cycle)

> **This guide covers the completed 2025-26 admissions cycle.** All deadlines and decision dates listed are now historical. For the 2026-27 cycle, check individual school websites starting August 2026, or run `/kb-update bay-area-private-school-k-application` to refresh this wiki when new data is available.

## Overview

The Bay Area private school kindergarten admissions cycle runs approximately 12 months, beginning with open houses in September and concluding with enrollment decisions in late March. According to ISSFBA, most member schools have application deadlines in January of the enrollment year. The coordinating body for this process is [[issfba-bada]], which sets shared notification dates for 75+ member schools.

This guide covers the 2025-26 admissions cycle (families applying in fall 2025 for the school year starting fall 2026).

## Decision and Enrollment Dates (March 2026)

According to ISSFBA, the 2025-26 PreK/TK/JK electronic notifications are sent March 11, 2026, with family responses due March 17, 2026.

According to ISSFBA, the 2025-26 K-8 electronic notifications are sent March 19, 2026, with family responses due March 26, 2026.

**Schools confirming March 19 notification (consistent with [[issfba-bada]] coordinated date):**

- SF Day School admission decisions are released March 19 between 12:00 and 2:00 p.m.
- The San Francisco School decisions are posted March 19, 2026, consistent with the ISSFBA coordinated notification date.
- Convent & Stuart Hall admissions decisions are released March 19 at 12:00 p.m., consistent with the ISSFBA coordinated notification date.
- The Hamlin School admission decisions are released March 19, 2026 at noon, consistent with the ISSFBA coordinated notification date.
- Presidio Hill School K-8 decisions are released March 19, 2026 at noon via email, consistent with the ISSFBA coordinated notification date.
- SF Friends School admission decisions are released March 19, 2026 at noon, consistent with the ISSFBA coordinated notification date.
- Head-Royce School K-8 decisions are released March 19, 2026 after 12:00 p.m., consistent with the ISSFBA coordinated notification date.
- Park Day School K-8 admission notifications are released March 19, 2026, consistent with the ISSFBA coordinated notification date.
- Marin Country Day School admission decisions are released March 19, 2026 at 12:00 p.m., consistent with the ISSFBA coordinated notification date.
- Marin Horizon School K-8 decisions are released March 19, 2026, consistent with the ISSFBA coordinated notification date.

**Exceptions to coordinated date:**

- According to Live Oak School, admission decisions are released March 17, 2026 at 4:00 p.m. This is two days before the ISSFBA coordinated notification date of March 19.
- According to Keys School, the application deadline is January 9 and admission decisions are released February 19, 2026. (Keys School is a Palo Alto school and may not be an ISSFBA member.)

## Common Mistakes

- **Missing the early-wave December deadlines:** Several schools (Head-Royce, Convent & Stuart Hall, SF Friends, SF Day, MCDS) have deadlines in December, not January. Check each school individually.
- **Assuming all decisions arrive March 19:** Live Oak releases March 17 and Keys School releases February 19. Do not assume the ISSFBA coordinated date applies universally.
- **Overlooking sibling/priority deadlines:** According to Live Oak School, the priority deadline for enrolled families is October 8, 2025. According to Marin Country Day School, sibling and faculty K applications are due October 10, 2025. These are months earlier than the general deadline.
- **Confusing enrollment response dates:** Response deadlines range from March 24 to March 26, varying by school. Missing these dates can forfeit an admission offer.

# Chapter 12: School Directory

---

This chapter is a reference, not a narrative. You have already read about the process, the assessments, the essays, and the decisions. What you need now is the data.

The directory that follows covers the private kindergartens most commonly considered by Bay Area families in the 2025–26 admissions cycle. Schools are organized by region. Each entry includes the information you will need to build and manage your list: deadlines, age cutoffs, assessment format, tuition, and platform.

**How to use this chapter:**

- The master summary table at the start of each regional section gives you a row-by-row comparison.
- Individual school notes highlight what is distinctive or frequently missed.
- Language immersion schools and IQ-testing schools are flagged throughout and also collected in a quick-reference index at the end.

All data reflects the 2025–26 admissions cycle based on official school sources. Bay Area private school details change annually — tuition increases, deadlines shift, process requirements change. Verify every figure at the school's official admissions page before submitting an application.

---

## How to Read the Tables

| Column | What it means |
|--------|--------------|
| **Age Cutoff** | Child must turn 5 by this date to apply for K |
| **App Deadline** | Application submission deadline |
| **Assessment** | How the school evaluates children (playdate, cognitive test, etc.) |
| **Platform** | Admissions platform (Ravenna = ISSFBA standard) |
| **Decision Date** | When offers are released; March 19 = ISSFBA coordinated date |
| **Tuition** | Annual base tuition; does not include fees, annual fund, or aids where offered |

Schools marked **★** are part of the ISSFBA anchor set referenced throughout this book (core schools most frequently used as examples).

### SF Day School

**Grades:** K–8 | **Location:** Pacific Heights

According to SF Day School, 2025–26 tuition is $48,577. The application portal opens September 2. An open house is held in September; the application deadline is December 19. Decisions release March 19 between 12:00–2:00 p.m. The enrollment deadline is March 24 at 10:00 a.m.

Application fee is $100, with waivers available. The ISSFBA Teacher Evaluation Form is used. Financial aid (via Clarity) applications for new families are due January 12. According to SF Day, the average tuition adjustment is $28,800; adjustments can reduce tuition by up to 95%.

### Convent & Stuart Hall

**Grades:** K–12 | **Location:** Pacific Heights | **Note:** Early age cutoff

According to Convent & Stuart Hall, children must be 5 by **July 15** — the earliest cutoff among anchor schools, and meaningfully earlier than the September 1 standard. The application deadline is December 12, with an application fee of $100 (fee waiver code K8APP available). Financial aid deadline is January 8.

Assessment: individual screening (30 minutes, December–January) followed by group playdate (1 hour, January–February). Parent interviews are scheduled November–February. Total enrollment is approximately 1,100 students K–12 with a 6:1 student-teacher ratio.

### Nueva School

**Grades:** PK–12 | **Location:** Hillsborough | **Note: IQ testing required** ★

According to Nueva School, the K application deadline is January 8. Application fee is $100 (nonrefundable). The activity session prerequisite deadline is **November 3** — one of the earliest hard deadlines in the cycle. Financial aid applications are due February 3.

Nueva requires a Wechsler IQ assessment (WPPSI-IV for children under 6, WISC-V for 6 and older) administered by a licensed psychologist. According to the SF Standard (December 2025), Nueva School requires a minimum IQ score of approximately 130 for admission consideration. According to Nueva, class sizes are 15–18 students with a 6:1 ratio.

Tuition ranges from $30,555 (PK) to $51,285 (high school). Financial aid vouchers are available for IQ assessment costs. See Chapter 4 for IQ testing logistics.

### Keys School

### Gideon Hausner Jewish Day School

**Grades:** TK–8 | **Location:** Palo Alto | **Note:** Round 2 option available

### Harker School

**Grades:** TK–12 | **Location:** San Jose | **Note:** IQ testing required; latest age cutoff in this set ★

According to Harker, children must be 5 by **November 30** — the most permissive age cutoff in this research set. The TK/K application deadline is January 8 at 5:00 p.m. PST. Decisions release March 20 after 4:00 p.m. PST. Contract and deposit are due March 27 at 12:00 p.m.

2026–27 tuition is $46,350 (TK) and $51,550 (K–Grade 5). Tuition includes lunch, before- and after-school care, and intercampus shuttles — services charged separately at many other schools.

According to Harker, a **cognitive assessment** is required for all TK through grade 2 applicants, administered by a psychologist from Harker's preferred list. According to Harker, coaching or tutoring for the cognitive assessment **disqualifies the application**. See Chapter 4.

### Helios School

**Grades:** K–8 | **Location:** Sunnyvale | **Note:** IQ testing required; holistic evaluation ★

According to Helios, children must be 5 by September 1. The preliminary application deadline is January 8 at 12:00 p.m. noon; supplemental documents (including the IQ report) are due February 2. Decisions release March 19 at 12:00 p.m. noon; enrollment contract is due March 26 at 12:00 p.m.

According to Helios, the school does **not** have a minimum Full Scale IQ requirement and evaluates the complete IQ report across all subtest indices. ## Specialty Quick-Reference

### IQ-Testing Schools

If **Nueva, Harker, or Helios** is on your list, Chapter 4 is required reading. Key differences:

| School | Instrument | Published Threshold | IQ Deadline |
|--------|-----------|---------------------|------------|
| Nueva | WPPSI-IV (<6) / WISC-V (6+) | ~130 FSIQ (community-reported, L3) | Jan 8 app; Nov 3 activity session |
| Helios | WPPSI-IV (<6) / WISC-V (6+) | None published; holistic evaluation | Feb 2 (supplemental docs) |
| Harker | WPPSI-IV / WISC-V (all subtests) | None published; holistic evaluation | Jan 8 |

Book your psychologist appointment in September. Appointments typically take 2–3 hours and are conducted 1:1 with a licensed psychologist. Costs run approximately $850–$1,200 per test, per the SF Standard (December 2025).

## Using This Directory

The most common mistake families make with the directory is treating all schools as a single pool. They are not. The application experience at a December-deadline SF school (Convent, Hamlin, SF Friends, SF Day) is meaningfully different from a February rolling-admissions South Bay school. The assessment experience at an IQ-testing school is a different kind of process from a two-hour supervised playgroup.

Before finalizing your list, confirm:

1. Your child's birthday and every school's age cutoff — a July 15 cutoff disqualifies some August and September birthdays
2. Whether any school on your list requires an IQ assessment — book the psychologist before September
3. The earliest deadline on your list — that school's timeline drives your calendar
4. Any mandatory prerequisite events (Nueva's November 3 activity session; SF School's required information session; MCDS's February 1 teacher evaluation deadline separate from the December application)

Chapter 2 (Year at a Glance) maps these deadlines across a full calendar view. This directory is the data; that chapter is the rhythm.

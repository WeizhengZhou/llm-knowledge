# Chinese Terminology Glossary — Bay Area Private School K Admissions Guide

_Binding reference for all Chinese (Simplified) chapter rewrites. Every writer/agent must follow this glossary. Do not invent alternate translations mid-chapter._

---

## Register Rules

- **Pronoun:** 你/你们 throughout. Never 您.
- **Tone:** Dry, peer-advice. Occasionally wry. Like a well-informed Bay Area parent friend, not a consultant or bureaucrat.
- **Sentence structure:** Break English compound sentences into shorter Chinese sentences. Avoid long relative clauses (English syntax). Prefer short → medium → short rhythm.
- **School names:** All school names remain in English as-is (SF Day, Hamlin, Nueva, etc.). No Chinese translation of school names.
- **Platform/tool names:** Ravenna, Clarity, ISSFBA — keep as-is; translate the category noun around them.

## Prose Style Notes

1. **Don't say:** "私立幼儿园入学申请" — this confuses K with preschool admission.
   **Say:** "私立学校 K 班申请" or "湾区私校申请季"

2. **Don't say:** 私立小学 (implies elementary school as a whole)
   **Say:** 私立学校 K 班 (K is one year within the school)

3. **For the "admissions consultant" layer:**
   Signal with: "有经验的升学顾问普遍反映……" / "长期服务湾区家庭的升学顾问指出……"
   NOT: "据悉……" or vague third-person constructions

4. **For official school policy:**
   Signal with: "根据 [School] 官方招生页面……" / "[School] 明确表示……"

5. **For community/parent pattern:**
   Signal with: "不少湾区家长反映……" / "实际申请过程中，很多家庭会遇到……"
   NOT cited as fact — only as pattern signal

6. **Dates:** Use Chinese date format (3月19日, not March 19日). Cycle years: 2025–26 申请季.

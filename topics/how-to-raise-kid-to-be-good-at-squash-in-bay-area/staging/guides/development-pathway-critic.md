## Should-Fix

1. **RO5 must_answer "How do parents recognize talent vs effort in junior squash?" is not addressed.** This is a real and specific question P3 parents have. Add a subsection (4-6 sentences) covering: signals of genuine aptitude beyond effort (e.g., reading the ball early, spatial court awareness, competitive drive without parental pressure) vs. effort-dependent improvement; why effort is more predictive than raw talent at the junior development stage; when it makes sense to invest heavily in a child who is not yet showing elite signals.

2. **The mental game section (60 words) is insufficient for RO5 must_answer "What role does mental game play in junior squash development?"** The section points to a $9 eBook but does not explain why mental game is specifically important in squash (no sideline coaching, self-regulation under pressure). Expand to at least 150 words covering: what specific mental skills junior squash requires (pre-point routine, managing frustration after a let call, staying focused without parental guidance), at what stage to begin mental training (club competitive stage), and what parents can do without a sport psychologist.

3. **"Balancing Squash with School" section contains the Nueva School / school-teams-use-club-courts content** that is already in [[norcal-squash]]. Remove this from the development pathway guide and replace with: "No Bay Area school has on-site squash courts (except Nueva School); see [[norcal-squash]] for the school team landscape." Then focus the section entirely on the balance strategies (teacher notification, assignment planning, travel use), which are development-relevant and not covered elsewhere.

4. **Stage table time/week figures** (1-2 hrs at Introduction, 15-20+ hrs at Elite) are the only place these figures appear. A parent reading this will want to know: are those figures typical across the country or specific to Bay Area clubs? State the source or add "(estimates based on Bay Area club program structures)."

## Graduation Decision: READY

- No must-fix items. Should-fix items would improve D1 from 2 to 3 and reduce scope drift, but the article fully enables RO5 at its current level.

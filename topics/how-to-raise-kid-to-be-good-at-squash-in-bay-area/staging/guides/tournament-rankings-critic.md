## Should-Fix

1. **RO4 must_answer "How much travel is involved" needs a direct answer.** Add to the NorCal Tournament Calendar section: "Bronze and silver events within NorCal (Squash Zone Silver, Oakwood Winter Bronze, Bay Club Fremont Winter Challenge) are typically day-trip events requiring no overnight stay. Gold events and JCT #2 in Fremont may attract players from outside the Bay Area and could require overnight stays. The four other JCT events and all national championships (Philadelphia-based) require East Coast flights; budget 3-4 days and $1,000-$3,000 per trip."
2. **The full 2025-2026 JCT and national championship schedules list specific dates for past events.** Add a header note: "The following schedules are from the 2025-2026 season and are provided for reference. For current-season schedules, check ussquash.org/junior." This prevents the article from reading as current when it is historical.
3. **Match format section** (PAR scoring to 11, best of 5) is correct but should include one practical note for parents watching their first match: the game can end quickly (as fast as 10 minutes per game at junior levels), so parents should expect a best-of-3 junior match to last 20-45 minutes, not 90 minutes like a tennis match.

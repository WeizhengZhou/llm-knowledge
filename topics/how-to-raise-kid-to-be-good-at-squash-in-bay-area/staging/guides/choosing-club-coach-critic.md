## Must-Fix Before wiki/ Graduation

1. **No Common Mistakes section.** Guide articles require one. Non-obvious mistakes to add: (a) Choosing a coach based on their former playing ranking alone — the article notes "superior playing ability alone does not guarantee effective instruction" but does not give a specific failure mode. Add: "A parent hiring a former PSA professional who has never coached children may find their 9-year-old is receiving instruction calibrated for an adult competitor. Ask specifically how many years the coach has worked with juniors under 12." (b) Selecting a club based on geographic proximity without checking whether the club has structured junior group programs — private lessons improve faster in isolation but group clinics at the right level provide competitive match experience; a club offering only private lessons is a different product than one offering weekly junior group sessions. (c) Checking certification level without checking whether the coach holds a current Coach Pass membership — without a Coach Pass, the coach cannot provide court-side coaching at US Squash accredited tournaments, which matters for competitive-track families.

## Should-Fix

1. **The guide covers how to evaluate a coach but does not say how to match coach level to player level.** Add a short paragraph: "For beginners and development players (ages 5-12, unrated), Level 1 certification is adequate. For competitive players targeting silver/gold/JCT events, look for Level 2 or Level 3 coaches with specific junior tournament experience. Altius Performance and Squash Zone's top coaches (Shahjahan Khan at Level 2+) are appropriate for this tier."
2. **The Sacramento region is included in the NorCal Squash club list but omitted from the Geographic Guide.** Either add Sacramento or note explicitly that the guide covers Bay Area only.
3. **The US Squash coaching certification cost table** (Start Squash $200, L1 $450, L2 $750, L3 $900) is factually correct but its inclusion serves coaches, not parents evaluating coaches. Remove this table or move it to a footnote; parents do not need to know how much a coach paid to get certified — they need to know what each level means for coaching competency.

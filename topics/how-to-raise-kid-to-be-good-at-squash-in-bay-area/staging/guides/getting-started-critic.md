## Should-Fix

1. **RO1 must_answer "What are the benefits of squash vs tennis, badminton, or other racket sports?"** is only partially addressed. The current text says "See [[squash-vs-tennis]] for a detailed comparison." A parent deciding whether to start squash needs at least two sentences in this guide covering the core benefit proposition — not a full comparison, but enough to understand why squash specifically. Add: "Squash offers faster early skill development than tennis (smaller court, easier rallies at beginner level), year-round indoor play (no weather dependency), and a high-intensity cardiovascular workout. Unlike tennis, all Bay Area squash is at private clubs — there are no public courts — which shapes both cost and access." Then point to [[squash-vs-tennis]] for depth.
2. **The "Is Squash Right for My Child?" section has no geographic accessibility signal for SF or East Bay families.** The table in "Where to Start" correctly maps options by region, but the prose paragraph ("For most families on the Peninsula, Squash Zone is the most accessible starting point") only addresses Peninsula parents. Add: "SF families: Bay Club SF (150 Greenwich St) is the nearest option. East Bay families: Bay Club Fremont (Fremont) or Oakwood Athletic Club (Lafayette) are the primary choices; East Bay options are fewer and less structured for beginners than Squash Zone."
3. **"Expecting rapid visible progress" mistake should be more specific.** Replace with: "Expecting consistent rallying within the first few lessons. Most children take 6-8 sessions before they can sustain even basic rallies; the early weeks are about learning to move in the court, not hitting winners."

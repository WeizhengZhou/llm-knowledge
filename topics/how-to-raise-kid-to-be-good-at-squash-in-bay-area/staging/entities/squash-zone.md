## Overview

Squash Zone is the Bay Area's primary dedicated squash facility, located in Redwood City on the Peninsula. Squash Zone has 9 international singles courts: 7 traditional courts, one 3-wall glass court, and one all-glass professional court. It is the most active junior tournament venue in Northern California and the nearest publicly accessible squash facility to Stanford University (approximately 3 miles from campus).

Squash Zone hours are Monday-Friday 9am-9pm and Saturday-Sunday 9am-5:15pm.

## Membership and Pricing

Squash Zone membership pricing: Adult $205/month + $205 initiation; Junior PLUS $160/month + $160 initiation; Junior Access $110/month + $110 initiation.

Squash Zone Junior Access membership totals $1,430 in the first year ($110/month + $110 initiation) and $1,320 in subsequent years. Junior PLUS totals $2,080 in the first year ($160/month + $160 initiation) and $1,920 in subsequent years.

According to the Squash Zone website, the guest fee is $25 plus $40 court booking; non-members may take up to two lessons (plus $25 guest fee).

## Junior Programs

Squash Zone Beginner Clinics are for ages 5-9; Development Clinics are for ages 10-14; Elite Clinics require a 2.7-4.5+ rating.

Squash Zone clinic prices range $40-$70 per hour (package/drop-in rate); membership required.

### Weekly Junior Schedule

- **Beginner Clinics (ages 5-9):** Monday 4:30-5:30pm; Saturday matchplay 10:15-11:15am; Sunday clinic 1-1:45pm
- **Future Stars:** Friday matchplay 3:30-4:15pm; Sunday skills 10:15-11:15am + fitness 11:15-11:45am
- **Development Clinics (ages 10-14):** Tuesday 5:15-6:45pm (drills, skills, fitness); Thursday 4:30-5:30pm
- **Elite Clinics (2.7-4.5+ rating):** Monday and Thursday 6-7:30pm
- **Matchplay:** Saturday beginners 10:15-11:15am; Sunday advanced 11:15-12:45pm

## Private Lessons

According to the Squash Zone website, private lesson rates range from $145-$175 per 60 minutes depending on the coach: Bransten Ming, Aisling Blake, Ricardo Lopez, and Samy Farrag at $165/60 min, $125/45 min, $80/30 min; Shahjahan Khan at $175/60 min, $145/45 min, $85/30 min; Yassin Farrag at $145/60 min, $110/45 min, $70/30 min.

## Court Booking and Policies

According to Squash Zone, court sessions are 45 minutes long, bookable 4 days in advance starting at 7am, with a $15 no-show fee.

According to Squash Zone, eye protection is mandatory for all players 18 and under.

## Adult Programs

According to the Squash Zone website, the Wednesday Challenge Ladder costs $30/quarter for match play (7:30-9pm) and the Friday Night Round Robin is free with membership (7:30-8:30pm).

## Camps

According to Squash Zone, camp deposits are $300 per camp with balance due on the first day; cancellation must be in writing at least 14 days prior to the start; fees are non-refundable once the session starts.

## Coaches

Squash Zone Peninsula coaches listed on NorCal Squash include Richard Elliott, Aisling Blake, Johann Huang, Zephan Huang, and Mustafa Nawar.

## Tournaments

Squash Zone is the most active junior tournament venue in NorCal. Historical NorCal junior tournaments include: Squash Zone Silver (March, 80-103 competitors), Bay Club Fremont Winter Challenge (December), Squash Zone Club Championships (May), Oakwood Winter Bronze (February), and the Silicon Valley Junior Championships.

## Common Mistakes

- **Signing up for Junior PLUS when Junior Access suffices.** Junior PLUS ($2,080/year first year) unlocks Elite Clinics and extended court access. If your child is a beginner or in development-level clinics (ages 5-14), Junior Access ($1,430/year first year) covers everything they need. Upgrade only when the child enters Elite Clinics (requires 2.7+ rating).
- **Assuming court sessions are a full hour.** According to Squash Zone, court sessions are 45 minutes long. Parents new to squash who book a court expecting 60 minutes will be surprised when the session ends early. Plan accordingly.
- **Treating the guest-lesson allowance as a long-term solution.** According to the Squash Zone website, non-members may take up to two lessons (plus $25 guest fee). This is a trial mechanism, not a substitute for membership. If your child wants to continue, membership is required.
- **Not reconciling coach rosters across sources.** The private lesson page lists Ming, Blake, Lopez, Farrag, Khan, and Yassin Farrag; the NorCal Squash directory lists Elliott, Blake, J. Huang, Z. Huang, and Nawar. Coach rosters may differ between the club's lesson page and the NorCal directory; contact Squash Zone directly for current coach availability and scheduling.

## Must-Fix Before wiki/ Graduation

1. **No Common Mistakes section.** Entity articles require one. Non-obvious mistakes to add: (a) Treating NorCal Squash league play as a junior development pathway — NorCal league divisions are adult co-ed competitive leagues, not junior programs; parents of young beginners should not expect their 8-year-old to play in league matches. (b) Trying to join NorCal directly instead of through a home club — membership flows through US Squash by designating a home club; families who contact NorCal directly without a club affiliation cannot register for league play.

## Should-Fix

1. **Junior league participation eligibility is never stated.** The article covers the high school team championships but does not clarify whether junior players participate in the standard adult divisions or have separate league structures. A parent of a 14-year-old competitive player cannot determine from this article whether NorCal league is relevant for their child.
2. **The "Notable Bay Area Junior Achievers" subsection uses names and results from NorCal Squash's own site (single L1 source), with no date context.** Results like "Madison Ho, U-11 National Championship winner, unblemished record 2015-16" are 10 years old. Add a note that these are historical highlights and point parents to norcalsquash.org for current junior standings.
3. **NorCal Squash also functions as a tournament calendar resource, but the article does not say this.** Mention that norcalsquash.org/tournaments/ is where parents find the current NorCal junior tournament schedule, linking to [[tournament-rankings]].

1. **No Common Mistakes section.** Entity articles require one. Non-obvious mistakes to add: (a) Assuming HISA is primarily a Peninsula competitor to Squash Zone — HISA's school partnerships (Westmont HS, Granada Islamic School) suggest a different target demographic; parents should confirm the program's current competitive focus before enrolling a nationally-ranked-track junior. (b) Contacting HISA at a PO Box address without calling first — the PO Box is a mailing address only; the physical training location is not disclosed in the article, so parents should call to confirm training venue.

2. **The "Junior Development" section (44 words) does not answer RO2 must_answer item "How do I evaluate a squash coach for kids?"** The section quotes HISA's marketing language ("fundamental technical skills, physical and mental fitness, right tactics") without naming what age ranges are served, what the program schedule looks like, what competitive results HISA juniors have achieved, or what differentiates HISA's academy model from a standard club lesson program. A parent reading this cannot evaluate HISA as a coach/program choice.

1. **Physical training location is not stated.** HISA has a Santa Clara PO Box but the article does not say where training actually takes place (courts used, days, times). Without this, the geographic assessment in [[choosing-club-coach]] is incomplete for South Bay families.

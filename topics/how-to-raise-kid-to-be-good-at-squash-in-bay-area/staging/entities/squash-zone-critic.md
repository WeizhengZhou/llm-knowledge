## Must-Fix Before wiki/ Graduation

1. **No Common Mistakes section.** Entity articles require this. Specific, non-obvious mistakes to add: (a) Signing up for Junior PLUS when Junior Access suffices for a beginner — Junior PLUS at $2,080/year is necessary only if a child will attend Elite Clinics; beginners should start with Junior Access ($1,430/year). (b) Booking courts without understanding the 45-minute session cap — parents new to squash sometimes assume they get a full hour. (c) Treating the guest-lesson allowance (2 non-member lessons) as a long-term solution rather than a trial.

## Should-Fix

1. **Membership tier decision guidance is missing.** The "Membership and Pricing" section lists three tiers but does not say what each tier unlocks or at what stage a child should move up. Add: "Start with Junior Access ($110/month) for beginner and development-level players who use group clinics; upgrade to Junior PLUS ($160/month) only when a child enters Elite Clinics or needs extended court access for private lesson scheduling."
2. **Camp pricing is absent.** The Camps section gives only deposit and cancellation policy; no camp cost or schedule. If this information is genuinely unavailable, say so explicitly.
3. **The "Coaches" section lists two different sets of coaches** — the private lesson rate table names Ming, Blake, Lopez, Farrag, Khan, Yassin Farrag; the NorCal-sourced coaches section lists Elliott, Blake, J. Huang, Z. Huang, Nawar. No reconciliation is offered. Flag that coach rosters may differ between the club's own lesson page and the NorCal directory, and that parents should contact the club for current availability.

## Must-Fix Before wiki/ Graduation

1. **No Common Mistakes section.** Entity articles require one. Non-obvious mistakes to add: (a) Enrolling a beginner or development-level player (under age 10 or unrated) in bespoke 1-on-1 coaching before they have foundational technique — Altius's own positioning as an elite training service implies this is not a first-lesson provider; parents should confirm appropriate entry level before contacting. (b) Assuming Altius provides court access — Altius is a coaching service without its own facility; training takes place at partner courts (location and court access not specified in the article). Parents should clarify where sessions occur.

## Should-Fix

1. **No stated minimum entry level or age.** The article describes Omar's elite credentials but never says what player profile Altius is designed for. Add: at minimum, a statement that Altius focuses on competitive juniors already in the tournament pathway, not recreational beginners.
2. **Training location is ambiguous.** The address (1197 Oddstad Dr, Redwood City) is listed, but is this a training facility with courts or an office/administrative address? Given the article notes Altius "is not a club or court facility," parents need to know where sessions are held. The Squash Zone proximity is implied but not confirmed.
3. **Pricing section is absent.** The Quick Facts table says "contact required" but there is no guidance on relative cost level. Given the article in [[costs-and-commitment]] uses $165/hr Squash Zone private lesson rates as a benchmark, Altius rates should at minimum be characterized as comparable, higher, or unavailable.

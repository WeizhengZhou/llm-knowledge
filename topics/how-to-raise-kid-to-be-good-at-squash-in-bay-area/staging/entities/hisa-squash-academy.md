## Overview

HAQ International Squash Academy (HISA) has a mailing address of PO Box 2003, Santa Clara, CA 95055; phone (408) 256-7363. HISA describes itself as "an elite squash training academy dedicated to producing the best squash players" at national and international levels. The academy offers a broad range of programming including lessons, camps, clinics, training, leagues, tournaments, a ladder system, nutrition guidance, courses, and school partnerships.

## Coaching Staff

According to HISA's website, director Muhammad Waseem Ul Haq has a world ranking of 131 and credentials including the Kentucky State Championship (1999), a silver medal at Pakistan's National Games (1998), participation in the US Open and three World Opens, and service as Executive Director of the Kentucky Squash Racquets Association (1999-2005).

HISA assistant directors Neha Haq and Mahnoor Haq are both Level 1 US Squash Certified Coaches.

## School Partnerships

According to HISA, the academy's school partnerships include Westmont High School, Magnolia Science Academy, Noor Hifz Academy, and Granada Islamic School.

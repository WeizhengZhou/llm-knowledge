## Must-Fix Before wiki/ Graduation

1. **No Common Mistakes section.** Entity articles require one. Non-obvious mistakes to add: (a) Joining Bay Club expecting a squash-only membership — Bay Club requires full club membership (premium multi-sport), so squash families pay for pools, fitness facilities, and other amenities they may not want. A family purely focused on junior squash development should compare Bay Club's full membership cost against Squash Zone's squash-only membership before committing. (b) Assuming all five Bay Club locations are equivalent for junior squash — Bay Club Marin is the only Marin County option but has one coach; Bay Club Fremont has three coaches and is the only Bay Club location with a real junior development track. Parents outside Fremont's geography should ask specifically about junior program depth at their nearest location before joining.

## Should-Fix

1. **Court count by location is missing.** The article names five locations but does not say how many courts each has. Squash Zone's court count (9) is prominently featured in that article because it signals capacity. Bay Club Fremont's court count would help parents assess program depth.
2. **The Bay Club Fremont 2019 Winter Challenge story (featuring Dillon Huang as "then US #1 ranked BU19") is the article's only junior competitive signal, and it is 7 years old.** If no more recent competitive results are available, remove or clearly date this example to avoid implying Bay Club Fremont currently hosts nationally ranked events regularly.
3. **No mention of whether Bay Club Fremont or other Bay Club locations run structured junior clinics analogous to Squash Zone's Beginner/Development/Elite clinic tiers.** The article says "junior/kids specialty camps during school breaks" — this is generic. Parents need to know if Bay Club runs year-round junior group programs or only camps and individual lessons.

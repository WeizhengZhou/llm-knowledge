# Critic Report — National Squash Academy (NSA) — 2026-04-24

## Overall Assessment
This concept article is accurate and well-organized. It correctly explains the NSA tier structure and provides a specific Bay Area access pathway. Its weakness is that it does not help a parent understand what realistic probability their child has of accessing each tier — the article describes the tiers without anchoring them in competitive reality.

## Must-Fix Before wiki/ Graduation

None. D3 = 0 is a should-fix for concept articles (not hard-required per rubric). D1 = 2 and D2 = 2 are should-fixes.

## Should-Fix

1. **The accessibility gap across NSA tiers is not addressed.** The article lists NSA-Aspire through NSA-National Team as if they are a progression any player might navigate. Add a "Realistic Accessibility for Bay Area Players" note: "NSA-Regional: accessible to any player who attends JCT #2 in Fremont regardless of ranking. NSA-Aspire: invitation-based, top 16 per age group nationally; requires a top-16 US Squash ranking, which as of 2025-2026 corresponds to the top 0.1% of active junior players in that age group. NSA-Performance through NSA-National Team: relevant only for players targeting professional squash or Team USA representation."

2. **"How Bay Area Kids Access the NSA" step 3 ("reach top 16 in age group") is presented as one step without timeline context.** Add: "Steps 1-2 (building a ranking through bronze/silver tournaments) take 1-2 years from beginner level. Reaching top 16 nationally requires a further 2-4 years of focused competitive play at gold and JCT levels. This is a 3-6 year journey from first tournament, not a single-season goal."

3. **No Common Mistakes section.** Non-obvious mistakes: (a) Conflating NSA-Regional (open to JCT attendees) with NSA-Aspire (invitation-only top 16) — parents sometimes assume "NSA" means national team track and become discouraged before understanding that NSA-Regional is a legitimate development touchpoint accessible to strong regional players. (b) Assuming San Francisco's listed TBD Drive to LA28 roadshow date means a local NSA event is coming — the roadshow is a promotional event, not a training or selection event; do not confuse roadshow dates with NSA session access.

4. **The Drive to LA28 section mentions "San Francisco, Los Angeles) have dates to be determined."** This is a TBD claim from April 2026. Add a note to check ussquash.org/la28 for current roadshow dates, since the article will age immediately once SF dates are announced.

## Graduation Decision: READY

- No must-fix items per the rubric. Should-fix items would improve usability significantly, particularly the accessibility framing gap (D1 issue).

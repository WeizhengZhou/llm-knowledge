### Why Early Mental Training Matters for Squash
Early mental training gives young athletes skills that carry into high school, college, and beyond. For squash specifically, the mental game is critical because:
- The enclosed court creates unique psychological pressure
- Players must self-regulate between points (no coach contact allowed during match)
- Tournament squash involves significant alone time managing emotions court-side
- Comebacks from 2-0 down require strong mental resilience

### Squash-Specific Mental Game Notes
- No sideline coaching is permitted during squash matches (unlike many team sports)
- Players must develop independent pre-point routines
- The "short point" nature of squash (multiple points per minute) means mental resets must happen very quickly
- Altius Performance explicitly lists "mental training and coaching" as part of their bespoke program
- Jesse Engelbrecht's SportMind and Parents Playbook are the only squash-specific psychology resources identified in this research

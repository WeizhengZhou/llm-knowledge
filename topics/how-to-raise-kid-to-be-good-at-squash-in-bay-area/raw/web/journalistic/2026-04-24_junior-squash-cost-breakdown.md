# Junior Squash Cost Breakdown — Bay Area

## Recreational Level Costs

### Squash Zone (Redwood City) — Primary Squash-Only Club
- **Junior Access Membership:** $110/month + $110 initiation = $1,430 first year, $1,320 subsequent years
- **Junior PLUS Membership:** $160/month + $160 initiation = $2,080 first year, $1,920 subsequent years
- **Group Clinics:** $40-70/hour (included in PLUS membership or extra for Access)
- **Private Lessons:**
  - $70-85/30 min
  - $110-145/45 min
  - $145-175/60 min

### Bay Club (SF, Marin, Fremont, Redwood Shores, Santa Clara)
- Premium club membership required (includes squash + full fitness center)
- Pricing varies by location and membership tier; contact individual clubs
- Private lessons through Bay Club coaches on top of membership

### US Squash Membership (Required for Tournaments)
- Junior membership: ~$149/year

## Tournament Entry Fees
- Local/regional bronze: $30-50 per tournament
- Silver level: $50-100 per tournament
- Gold/JCT events: $100-200+ per tournament
- US Squash Junior Championships: varies, typically $100+

## Financial Aid Options
1. **SquashDrive (East Bay):** Free program for Oakland youth grades 4-11 through partner schools. 70%+ free/reduced lunch families. Urban squash model.
2. **NAO/GO Scholarship Fund:** For urban squash program participants attending private secondary or college. Contact development@ussquash.com
3. **US Squash Grants:** development@ussquash.org for emerging junior squash initiatives
4. **Open Squash:** 100+ low-income junior students in after-school programs at no fee
5. **Club Scholarships:** Individual clubs may offer reduced rates; ask directly

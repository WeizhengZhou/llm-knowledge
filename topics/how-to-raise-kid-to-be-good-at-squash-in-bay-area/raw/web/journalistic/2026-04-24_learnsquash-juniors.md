# Junior Squash Development at LearnSquash

## Starting Age & Programs

LearnSquash offers junior clinics for ages 6-18, ranging from complete beginners to advanced players. The program has redesigned its 2022-23 offerings to include:

- **Ages 6-9:** Beginner clinic (youngest cohort)
- **Ages 10-18:** Beginner clinic for older youth
- **Advanced Beginner-Intermediate:** Mixed ages
- **Intermediate+:** New advanced track

## Technical Instruction

The organization describes private lessons as following a structured progression: "Welcome / Warm up / Catch up / Diagnosis / Analysis / Design / Drills / Theory / Demonstration / Conditioned Games / Regular Games / Review / Homework." This suggests instruction moves from assessment through skill practice to applied gameplay.

## Instruction Quality

Lessons are delivered by the Director of Squash (Cliff Wenn), the Winsor Head Coach (Szilvia Szombati), or certified teaching professionals. Private sessions run 45 minutes; clinics are 1-1.5 hours.

## Parental Involvement

The page mentions that "info@learnsquash.com" handles inquiries about junior programs but provides limited detail on parent roles. The emphasis appears to be on professional instruction rather than parent-coaching models.

## Pricing
Private lessons cost $100 per 45-minute session (individual) or $55 per person for semi-private instruction.

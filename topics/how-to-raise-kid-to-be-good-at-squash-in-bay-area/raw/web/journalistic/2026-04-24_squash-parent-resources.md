# Squash Parent Resources

## The Parents Playbook (Jesse Engelbrecht)
**Format:** Free 20-minute workshop
**Topic:** "Nurturing A Lifelong Love Of Squash"

### What It Covers
1. Strategies to prevent early athlete dropouts
2. Managing competitive pressures while maintaining enjoyment
3. Coach-parent-athlete relationship dynamics
4. Character development and growth mindset
5. Emotional support and resilience building

### Five Main Pillars
1. Empowerment — Actionable parental guidance
2. Adaptation — Adjusting approaches to evolving athletic journeys
3. Mastery — Developing complementary parenting styles
4. Supporting without excessive pressure
5. Navigation through youth athletics' emotional landscape

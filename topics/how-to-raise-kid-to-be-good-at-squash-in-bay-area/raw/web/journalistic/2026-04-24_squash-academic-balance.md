# Balancing Competitive Squash with Academics

## Time Demands of Competitive Squash
- Recreational (1-2x/week): 2-3 hours total, easily compatible with any school schedule
- Club competitive (3-4x/week): 6-10 hours/week including practice + tournaments
- Advanced competitive (5-6x/week): 12-20 hours/week including training, tournaments, travel
- Student-athletes spend average 60 hours/week on combined sports + academics

## NorCal-Specific School Constraints
- No NorCal school has its own squash courts (except Nueva)
- School teams depend on club court time (typically 4-6pm)
- Tournament travel typically weekends only at regional level
- National events (JCT, championships) may require travel Thursday-Sunday
- East Coast national events require 3-4 days travel

## Strategies for Balance

### Time Management
- Create weekly schedule including practices, games, homework, study time
- Identify and eliminate time wasters
- Use travel time productively (car rides, flights)
- Morning academic work before afternoon training

### School Communication
- Inform teachers of tournament schedule at start of semester
- Request assignments in advance for travel weekends
- Many schools have athlete-friendly policies if communication is proactive

### Program Design
- SquashTigers curriculum designed with "awareness of the stresses of junior academics" — offers multiple training time options
- SquashDrive model: integrates 115 hours of academic tutoring annually alongside 100 hours squash
- Competitive squash programs often emphasize academics as part of development

## Benefits of Squash for Academic Performance
- Improves discipline and time management
- Teaches focus and concentration (directly applicable to studying)
- Builds resilience and growth mindset
- Higher likelihood of college attendance among junior squash players (SquashDrive data: 100% college-bound among program participants)

## Warning Signs Balance Is Off
- Declining grades (one academic quarter of decline warrants review)
- Chronic fatigue affecting both sport and school
- Social isolation from non-squash peers
- Child expressing desire to quit school activities (not just sport)

## When to Step Back on Competitive Volume
- Middle school (6-8): keep training moderate; 2-3x/week max
- High school (9-12): can increase if child wants; tournament volume depends on college goals
- Avoid tournament-heavy schedules during final exam periods

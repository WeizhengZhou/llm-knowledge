# NorCal Squash Junior Programs and League

## League Structure
NorCal Squash Leagues in San Francisco, East Bay, South Bay (Peninsula), and Sacramento run from September to April. Teams are co-ed and split into Divisions 1 through 4.

**Team format:**
- Teams consist of 6-10 players
- Matches against opposing teams every two weeks
- Match night: four players from each team play each other
- Format: best of five, rally point games

## Junior League & High School Program
- **High School Championships:** Inaugural NorCal High School Team Championships held at Bay Club Santa Clara with 12 school teams participating
- **School infrastructure:** No school in NorCal has its own squash courts; school teams rely on private clubs
- **Nueva School:** Only school in Northern California to incorporate squash into their school program
- **2024-25 season kickoff party:** September 17, 2024 at University Club San Francisco

## Junior Tournament History (NorCal)

**Recent Tournaments:**
- **Squash Zone Silver** (March): 80-103 competitors; age divisions BU11, BU13, BU15, BU17, BU19, GU11/13, GU15/17, GU19; "local and out of state interest"
- **Bay Club Fremont Winter Challenge** (December): junior bronze division; featured Dillon Huang (US #1 BU19)
- **Squash Zone Club Championships** (May): annual; all ages juniors through adult
- **Oakwood Winter Bronze** (February): inaugural; Lafayette, East Bay
- **Silicon Valley Junior Championships** (2018): annual event

**Notable Bay Area Junior Achievements:**
- Madison Ho: U-11 National Championship winner, unblemished record 2015-16 season
- Gautham Suresh: multiple NorCal BU11/BU13 wins
- Abhinav Athreya: NorCal BU13 champion
- Nikhil Prasad: NorCal BU15 champion
- Abhimanyu Gupta: NorCal BU17 champion

## NorCal Club Network for Juniors
- **Squash Zone** (Redwood City): Most active junior tournament venue; 9 courts; full junior program
- **Bay Club Fremont**: Active junior program; 3 coaches; hosted JCT and Winter Challenge
- **Bay Club SF**: City league hub; Erick Chacon coaching
- **Bay Club Marin**: Marin county only option; Erick Chacon coaching
- **Oakwood Athletic Club** (Lafayette): East Bay; hosted winter bronze events
- **SquashDrive** (East Bay): Urban program for Oakland youth, free

## How the NorCal League Serves Junior Development
- League provides regular competitive match play outside of formal tournaments
- Team format builds camaraderie and team skills
- Divisions 1-4 accommodate wide skill range
- School team format developing through club partnerships (no school courts)
- NorCal coordinates with US Squash to host sanctioned regional events

# How Juniors Earn a National Ranking in US Squash

## Overview
Junior rankings operate on a points-based system where players accumulate points through participation in accredited tournaments. "Players earn points by participating in US Squash accredited junior tournaments."

## Point Calculation
The ranking system considers tournament level and player performance. "The number of points earned is based on the level of tournament (Gold, Silver, Bronze), the number of players a division, and a players' finishing position at that tournament."

After sufficient tournament participation, "players will receive a point average, which is an average of the top 4 points earning tournaments played within the past 11-months." Individual rankings reflect how each player's point average compares to others in their age group.

## Requirements for Ranking Eligibility
To receive a junior national ranking, players must satisfy four criteria:

1. Maintain active US Squash membership
2. Pass an age-appropriate online referee exam
3. Demonstrate U.S. citizenship or residency (verified through profile address)
4. Accumulate at least 3 active tournament exposures within the same age division during the preceding 11 months

## About Page

**Heading:** junior squash IS OUR PASSION.

**Our Story:**
Altius was born from listening to the voices of squash families about the limited options for junior training in the Bay Area. We are passionate — obsessed even — about squash. We love the game and bemoan the fact that West Coast squash vs. East Coast squash is a David vs. Goliath contest. So we decided to try to level the playing field a bit.

We do things a little differently at Altius. We are junior-centric with a focus on student athletes (though adult players are always welcome to drop in for a hit). Our players come in all sizes and shapes, so our programs are custom-tailored to each individual in every aspect, from game style development to strength and conditioning to mental coaching and training.

We believe that sound technique in movement and racquetwork is the foundation for good squash, and our training has a heavy emphasis on getting these fundamentals right. We constantly analyze and draw from the latest changes in how the game is played at the highest level, the PSA, and incorporate these concepts into our program.

## Team Page

**Omar Mohy Marei — Head Director of Squash**

Coach Omar hails from Egypt, where he was the top ranked junior for many years before playing professionally on the PSA World Tour.

He was previously part of the coaching team at the storied Wadi Degla club in Cairo, incubator to many of the world's top juniors and PSA players. He was then recruited to the US and coached 3 years at Greenwich Academy, CT (3-time US national high school champions during his tenure) and at LBSquash, working alongside Luke Butterworth.

Omar then returned to Egypt to establish his own junior coaching practice. He mentored and developed Egyptian junior players to achieve success at the highest level worldwide. His students include:
- 2023 British Junior Open winner BU11
- 2023 British Junior Open winner GU11
- 2023 British Junior Open 3rd place GU13
- 2023 British Junior Open 5th place BU15

Omar has also mentored a number of top US junior players, including the 2023 British Junior Open BU13 semi-finalist.

Omar is among the few coaches with PSA level coaching experience: he has worked with a number of professional squash players ranked in the top 20 in the world.

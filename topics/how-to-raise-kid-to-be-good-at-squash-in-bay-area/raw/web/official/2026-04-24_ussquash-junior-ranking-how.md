# Junior National Rankings Overview — US Squash

## Points-Based System
Junior rankings use a points-based approach where "Players earn points by participating in US Squash accredited junior tournaments." Points vary based on tournament level (Gold, Silver, Bronze), division size, and finishing position.

## Ranking Calculation
Once players accumulate sufficient tournament participation, they receive a point average representing "an average of the top 4 points earning tournaments played within the past 11-months."

## Eligibility Requirements
To obtain a junior national ranking, players must satisfy four criteria:

1. **Membership**: Active US Squash membership
2. **Referee Exam**: Passed Age-Specific Online Referee Exam
3. **Citizenship/Residency**: U.S. citizenship or residency matching their profile address
4. **Tournament Participation**: "3 active tournament exposures in the same age group within the past 11-months"

## Referee Exam by Age Division
Players must complete the age-appropriate online referee exam to be listed in the rankings:
- U13: Level 1 referee exam
- U15: Level 2 referee exam
- U17: Level 3 referee exam
- U19: Level 4 referee exam

Players will need to recertify with a new referee exam every two years as they move into older age divisions. The exam is found on a player's ClubLocker profile under "Training" section.

## Tournament Tiers
- **Gold**: Highest level junior tournaments
- **Silver**: Intermediate level, ~80 per year nationally
- **Bronze**: Entry level for establishing ranking, ~100 per year nationally
- **JCT (Junior Championship Tour)**: Top-tier national series

## Division Format
Tournaments offer divisions based on:
- **Age categories**: U11, U13, U15, U17, U19
- **Skill ratings**: U2.5 through 5.5+
- **Format**: Single-sex or co-ed options

Players must be under the age limit through the tournament's final day to be eligible.

## Registration Process
All participants must:
1. Create a ClubLocker account
2. Obtain US Squash membership
3. Pass age-appropriate referee exam
4. Register through the online tournament listing on ClubLocker

# US Squash Junior Programs Overview

## Program Structure

US Squash offers junior programming for players under 19 years old across multiple participation levels:

- **Starting as a Junior**: Entry-level opportunities for children beginning to play
- **Recreational**: Less competitive, skill-development focused programs
- **Competitive**: Advanced programming for serious junior players

## Key Benefits

The organization emphasizes that youth squash participation delivers documented advantages including:

"Physical Health: improved motor skills and coordination, promotes active lifestyle, improved physical fitness, reduced rates of obesity, builds healthy habits which carry over into adulthood."

Mental and educational benefits also include improved concentration, higher academic achievement, and better college attendance rates.

## Competition & Rankings

Junior players can participate through:
- **USR Junior Circuit**: A new competitive pathway launched by US Squash
- **Junior Rankings**: Formalized rating system for competitive players
- **Junior Tournaments**: Sanctioned events across the country
- **Scholastic Team Squash**: School-based team competition

## Special Programs

- **Junior National Team**: Elite player development
- **Preparing for College**: Guidance for juniors pursuing collegiate squash
- **Junior Squash Talk & Parent Corner**: Educational resources for players and families

The organization maintains a comprehensive Player Development Pathway guiding juniors from beginner through advanced competitive levels.

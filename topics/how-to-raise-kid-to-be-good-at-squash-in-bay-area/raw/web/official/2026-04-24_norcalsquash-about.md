# NorCal Squash Overview

## Mission
NorCal Squash aims to "foster squash in Northern California, develop lifelong engagement with the sport, and support teamwork and competition at all levels."

## Leadership Structure
The organization is led by a six-member board:
- Emilie Van Der Hoorn (Chairperson, SF League Commissioner)
- Eric Katerman
- Larry Salzer (Treasurer)
- Lauren Patrizio Xaba
- Michael Corbett (Peninsula League Commissioner)
- Rina Bhagwati

## Legal Entity & Status
NorCal Squash operates under the San Francisco International Squash Federation (SFISF), a registered 501(c)(3) charitable nonprofit corporation. The board members serve dual roles as officers for both organizations.

## Relationship to US Squash
Members join NorCal Squash through US Squash by selecting a NorCal club as their "home club." This creates an integrated membership structure connecting local play to the national governing body.

## Regional Coverage
The organization oversees squash development across Northern California, maintaining league operations in San Francisco and the Peninsula, and coordinating activities across the East Bay, Sacramento, and surrounding areas through partnerships with various clubs and facilities.

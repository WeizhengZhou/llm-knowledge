# US Squash Coaching Certification Levels

## Start Squash (Grassroots Level)
**Duration:** One day (9am-4pm)
**Cost:** $200 ($250 late registration)
**Prerequisites:** Love for squash and community coaching need

This entry-level course trains coaches to teach fundamentals in unconventional settings like parks and handball courts, emphasizing safety and fun-based techniques. Qualifies coach to run beginner community programs, not for formal club coaching.

## Level 1
**Duration:** Weekend (Friday 5pm-8pm, Saturday 8:30am-7pm, Sunday 9am-3pm)
**Cost:** $450 ($500 late registration)
**Prerequisites:** Knowledge of six basic shots; ability to demonstrate and feed for beginners; understanding of rules

Level 1 focuses on "group instruction, with special attention paid to lesson organization, safety, leadership, and general squash coaching knowledge." Coaches teach beginner to low-intermediate players.

**2025-2026 Course:** Level 1 hosted at Specter Center January 9-11, 2026.

## Level 2
**Duration:** Weekend course plus 6-week coaching plan submission
**Cost:** $750 ($800 late registration)
**Prerequisites:** Level 1 certification plus one year experience; detailed shot knowledge; intermediate-level feeding ability

This level emphasizes individual instruction and fault analysis for intermediate/advanced players, introducing physical training principles.

## Level 3
**Duration:** Weekend plus 8-week coursework
**Cost:** $900 ($950 late registration)
**Prerequisites:** Level 2 certification; extensive technical/tactical knowledge; advanced feeding ability

"Level 3 is currently the highest certification that US SQUASH offers." It prepares coaches for regional and national squad development and potential instructor roles. Requires SafeSport training.

In 2025, fourteen professional players earned Level 3 certification.

## What Parents Should Seek
- US Squash certifications are the ONLY official certifications recognized by US Squash in the USA
- Coaches at official tournaments require "Coach Pass" membership
- Level 1 = appropriate for group beginner/low-intermediate instruction
- Level 2 = appropriate for individual lessons with intermediate/advanced players
- Level 3 = highest standard, appropriate for competitive junior development
- HISA assistant directors (Neha Haq, Mahnoor Haq) hold Level 1 certification
- Squash Zone coaches include multiple certified professionals

## Coach Pass Requirement
Coaches who attend US Squash accredited events as coaches must hold a Coach Pass membership in addition to their certification. This is separate from regular membership.

# SquashDrive Overview

**Who They Serve:**
SquashDrive serves "East Bay students" through an integrated program combining academic tutoring, squash instruction, and character development.

**Geographic Location:**
Based in Emeryville, CA (PO Box 99165, Emeryville, CA 94662), they serve the East Bay region.

**Age Groups:**
The organization works with middle and high school students, as evidenced by references to "MS & HS Spring 2025 Program Schedule" and "100% of high school students have college based summer experience" as a goal.

**Programs Offered:**

1. **Academic Support** - "Daily after school academic support" including 115 hours of homework help annually, plus STEM workshops and career exposure

2. **Squash Instruction** - Minimum 100 hours yearly of squash and fitness instruction, with competitive tournament opportunities

3. **Character Building** - Community service projects (10 hours/year required), mentoring, cultural outings, and travel experiences

**Application/Enrollment:**
Contact information is available at /contact. The website provides volunteer opportunities and donation options.

**Key Goal:**
"Help each student achieve success in high school, college, and beyond" through integrated academic, athletic, and personal development.

**Note:** SquashDrive is an urban squash / youth development program, not a fee-based junior competitive program. It specifically serves underserved youth in the East Bay.

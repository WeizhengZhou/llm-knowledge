# Bay Club Squash Program Details

## Program Overview
The Bay Club describes their squash offering as "one of the best in California" featuring "top-of-the-line glass courts" and professional instruction for all skill levels.

## Available Programs
- **Private/Small Group Lessons**: Professional instructors available for players of all experience levels
- **Round Robin Play**: Evening competitions open to all playing abilities
- **Competitive Tournaments**: Year-round events hosted throughout the club system
- **PSA Events**: Annual Professional Squash Association tournaments
- **Junior/Kids Specialty Camps**: Summer/vacation camps for young athletes

## Coaching Staff (Bay Area Locations)
- **Bay Club San Francisco & Bay Club Marin:** Erick Chacon
- **Bay Club Fremont:** Maha Zein, Faisal Riaz, Zarak Khan

## Bay Area Locations with Squash Courts

## Pricing
Specific pricing not publicly available on website. Members access pricing through club portal. Booking through Connect Booking App.

## Key Notes for Junior Programs
- Bay Club Fremont hosted Bay Club Fremont Winter Challenge junior bronze tournament (December 2019) with Dillon Huang (US #1 ranked BU19) as featured player
- Bay Club requires full club membership to access squash courts
- Bay Club membership includes premium fitness facilities in addition to squash
- Bay Club Fremont specifically cited as junior squash hub with 3 coaches on staff

## Membership Type
Bay Club memberships are premium-tier club memberships (not squash-only). Pricing varies by location and membership type; contact individual clubs.

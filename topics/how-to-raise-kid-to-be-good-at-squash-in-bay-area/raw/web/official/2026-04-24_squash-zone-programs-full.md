# Squash Zone - Junior Programs & Facilities Summary

## Facility Details
**Courts:** 9 international singles courts (7 traditional, 1 three-wall glass, 1 all-glass professional court)

**Location:** 3586 Haven Ave., Redwood City, CA 94063

**Hours:** Mon-Fri 9am-9pm; Sat-Sun 9am-5:15pm

## Junior Programming

**Beginner Clinics (ages 5-9):**
- Monday: 4:30-5:30pm
- Saturday: 10:15-11:15am matchplay
- Sunday: 1-1:45pm clinic

**Future Stars Program:**
- Friday: 3:30-4:15pm matchplay
- Sunday: 10:15-11:15am skills + 11:15-11:45am fitness

**Development Clinics (ages 10-14):**
- Tuesday: 5:15-6:45pm (drills, skills, fitness)
- Thursday: 4:30-5:30pm (games/conditions)

**Elite Clinics (2.7-4.5+ rating):**
- Monday & Thursday: 6-7:30pm

**Matchplay Clinics:**
- Saturday: 10:15-11:15am (beginners)
- Sunday: 11:15-12:45pm (advanced)
- Wednesday: 7:30-9pm sanctioned ladder matches

## Pricing
Clinics: "$40-$70 per hour (package/drop in rate)" — membership required

## Mission Statement
The facility aims to "foster a vibrant and inclusive community through the sport of squash" serving "players of all ages and skill levels."

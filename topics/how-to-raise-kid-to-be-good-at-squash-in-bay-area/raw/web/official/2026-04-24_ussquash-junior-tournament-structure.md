# US Squash Junior Tournament Structure

## Tournament Pyramid System

US Squash organizes junior competitions as a pyramid with increasing competition levels. "The lower level tournaments are smaller and generally cater to local players. The higher level tournaments are larger, and attract players from across the country."

## Four Tournament Tiers

**Bronze Tournaments**
One-day local events typically held on weekends. US Squash accredits approximately 100 bronze tournaments annually. These competitions help newer players establish rankings while gaining experience.

**Silver Tournaments**
Regional events spanning two days, designed for intermediate competitors seeking enhanced competition and ranking advancement opportunities. Roughly 80 silver tournaments receive accreditation yearly.

**Gold Tournaments**
Advanced two to three-day competitions attracting highly-ranked players from multiple regions nationwide. About 28 gold tournaments are accredited annually.

**Junior Championship Tour (JCT)**
The highest tier features the nation's top players competing over three full days. US Squash accredits 5 JCT events yearly.

## Player Progression System

The structure features intentional overlap in ranking points between levels. "The top finishers in one level will earn more points than the bottom finishers of the higher-level tournament." This creates mobility allowing ambitious competitors to advance upward through progressive tournament tiers by improving their rankings and performance.

## Age Divisions
U11, U13, U15, U17, U19 — based on player's age at time of tournament.

## Starting Tournaments
Players new to tournaments should start at Bronze level. When competitive with top Bronze players, advance to Silver. Players who excel in Silver may qualify for the US Junior Bronze, Silver, or JCT Championships.

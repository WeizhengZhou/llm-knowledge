# SquashDrive Program Details

## Program Overview
SquashDrive is an after-school program integrating "academic tutoring, squash instruction, and character building" to help East Bay students succeed academically, athletically, and personally.

## Target Population
- East Bay youth, primarily 4th through 11th graders
- Partners with 12 partner schools in Oakland area
- Primary school partner: Berkeley Maynard Academy (Aspire Public School in Oakland) — 70% of students receive free or reduced lunches
- Serves students from lower-income backgrounds

## Academic Component
- **115 hours** of annual homework help and academic enrichment
- Partners with UC Berkeley and Bay Area volunteers for tutoring
- Goals: maintaining 3.0+ GPA, grade-level proficiency, STEM exposure
- "100% of students matriculate to the next grade each year"

## Squash Training
- Minimum **100 hours per year** of squash and fitness instruction
- Competitive tournament opportunities and week-long camps
- Focus on developing discipline, focus, and teamwork
- Elite team members pursue national rankings

## Character Development
- 10 hours of community service annually
- Individual mentoring from staff and volunteers
- "100% of high school students have college based summer experience"
- Cultural outings, travel, and adventure trips

## Cost
Program is free for eligible participants. SquashDrive is a non-profit organization funded by donations.

## How to Access
Contact SquashDrive directly through squashdrive.org for enrollment. The program partners with specific Oakland schools; enrollment typically starts through school partnerships.

## Related National Urban Squash Network
SquashDrive follows model of urban squash programs including:
- CitySquash (New York)
- SquashBusters (Boston)
- SquashSmarts (Philadelphia)

Part of National Urban Squash Education Alliance (NUSEA), making participants eligible for NAO/GO Scholarship Fund for private secondary school and college.

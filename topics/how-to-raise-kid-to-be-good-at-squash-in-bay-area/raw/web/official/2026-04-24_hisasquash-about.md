# HAQ International Squash Academy (HISA) - About

## Contact Information

## Coaching Staff
**Director:** Muhammad Waseem Ul Haq, international squash professional with "world ranking of 131" and extensive competitive experience.
**Assistant Directors:** Neha Haq and Mahnoor Haq, both Level 1 US Squash Certified Coaches.
Management also includes Sadia Waseem Haq.

## Programs Offered
The academy provides lessons, camps, clinics, training, leagues, tournaments, nutrition guidance, courses, and school programs. Additional offerings include a ladder system and "Squash-and-More" activities.

## School Partnerships
- Westmont High School
- Magnolia Science Academy
- Noor Hifz Academy
- Granada Islamic School

## Achievements & Professional History
Waseem Ul Haq won the Kentucky State Championship (1999), earned a silver medal at Pakistan's National Games (1998), and participated in major international tournaments including US Open and three World Opens. He served as Executive Director of Kentucky Squash Racquets Association (1999-2005).

## Junior Development
The academy focuses on youth programming through "complete and unique Squash Management Solution" with dedicated junior tournaments and training initiatives. Haq previously built Kentucky's junior foundation.

## Mission
"An elite squash training academy dedicated to producing the best squash players at both the National and International levels."

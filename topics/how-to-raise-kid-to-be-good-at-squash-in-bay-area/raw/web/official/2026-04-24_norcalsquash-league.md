# NorCal Squash Leagues Overview

## Regional Structure
NorCal Squash operates leagues across four regions: San Francisco, East Bay, South Bay/Peninsula, and Sacramento. Leagues run from September through April.

## Team Organization
Teams are co-ed and divided into divisions 1 through 4. According to the website, "Teams are co-ed and split into Divisions 1 through 4." Detailed information about divisions and scoring structures is available in the League Rules and Regulations document.

## Participating Clubs
**San Francisco:** Bay Club San Francisco, Bay Club Marin, Olympic Club, UCSF Mission Bay, University Club

**East Bay:** Club Sport Fremont, Club Sport San Ramon, Oakwood Athletic Club, UC Berkeley

**South Bay/Peninsula:** Bay Club Redwood Shores, Bay Club Santa Clara, Haq International Squash Academy, Stanford, Squash Zone

## How to Join
Prospective players join through US Squash and designate a NorCal squash club as their "home club." The website directs interested players to the US Squash registration platform.

## League Management
Each region has a dedicated commissioner:
- San Francisco: Emilie van der Hoorn
- East Bay: Waqas Khan
- Peninsula: Michael Corbett

## Match Scoring
The league uses ClubLocker for recording match results and standings calculations. Players must enter actual match scores to influence USSquash ratings.

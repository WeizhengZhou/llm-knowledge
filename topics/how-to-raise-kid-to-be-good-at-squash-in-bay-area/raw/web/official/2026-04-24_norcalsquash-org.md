# NorCal Squash: Organization Overview

## Who They Are
NorCal Squash is the Northern California Squash Association, serving the squash community across Northern California with a focus on promoting the sport at all levels.

## What They Do
The organization facilitates squash participation through multiple avenues:

- **League Operations**: They coordinate competitive leagues including a City League and Peninsula League, with a "Squash Season Kickoff Party" mentioned as an annual event
- **Tournament Hosting**: They organize and promote graded squash tournaments across the region
- **Community Building**: The organization maintains a "Hall of Fame" recognizing significant contributors to the sport
- **Resources**: They provide a directory of playable courts and clubs throughout Northern California

## Junior Programs
While NorCal Squash supports juniors, the primary youth development organization is **SquashDrive**, an urban squash program serving underserved communities in the Bay Area. SquashDrive recently hired Pat Williams as Executive Director in 2020.

## Clubs & Facilities
NorCal Squash maintains partnerships with 20+ facilities across five regions:
- San Francisco (Bay Club, Olympic Club, UCSF, University Club)
- Peninsula (Bay Club locations, Stanford, Squash Zone, Haq Academy)
- East Bay (Bay Club Fremont, ClubSports, Oakwood Athletic Club, UC Berkeley)
- Sacramento (Multiple sports clubs and UC Davis)

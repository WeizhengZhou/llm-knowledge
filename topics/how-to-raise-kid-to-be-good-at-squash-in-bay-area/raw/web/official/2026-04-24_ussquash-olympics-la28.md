# US Squash Drive to LA28 Campaign

## Campaign Purpose
US Squash launched the **Drive to LA28**, building momentum toward squash's Olympic debut at the 2028 Summer Olympics in Los Angeles. Led by CEO Mollie Marcoux Samaan.

## Four Core Pillars

**1. Excellence**
Building a strong talent pipeline by strengthening junior squash and supporting athletes pursuing Team USA and professional careers.

**2. Courts & Coaching**
Expanding nationwide access to squash through public court investment while developing high-quality coaches to meet growing demand.

**3. Scholastic & Collegiate Squash**
Growing participation by strengthening youth and scholastic programs while expanding collegiate opportunities across the country.

**4. Marketing, Media & Visibility**
Elevating squash's profile by reaching new audiences, amplifying stories, and maximizing the 2028 Olympics opportunity.

- National coaching staff expanded

- U.S. Squash Foundation Drive to LA28 Campaign raising funds specifically for development
- National federations leveraging news to secure municipal funding for new courts

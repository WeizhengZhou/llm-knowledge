## How to Register for NorCal Junior Tournaments

### Step-by-Step Registration Process

1. **Get a US Squash membership** at ussquash.org (required for all ranked events)
2. **Create a ClubLocker account** at clublocker.com (linked to US Squash membership)
3. **Find tournaments** on ClubLocker under the US Squash organization tournament listings, filtering by California/NorCal
4. **Enter the tournament** — most junior tournaments have entry fees of $35-75 per draw; paid through ClubLocker
5. **Complete referee exam** — required for ranking eligibility:
   - U13 players: Level 1 referee exam
   - U15 players: Level 2 referee exam
   - U17 players: Level 3 referee exam
   - U19 players: Level 4 referee exam
   - Recertification every 2 years

### Tournament Tiers for Ranking Points
- **JCT (Junior Circuit Tournament):** National ranking points; major events held regionally
- **Gold:** Regional/national ranking points
- **Silver:** Regional ranking points
- **Bronze:** Local ranking points
- **USR Junior Circuit:** Universal Squash Rating junior events (separate from JCT tier)

# Getting Started With Junior Tournaments — US Squash

## Registration Requirements

Players must complete three key steps to participate in accredited junior tournaments:

1. **Create a Club Locker Account**: Required for all participants in US Squash accredited events.

2. **Obtain US Squash Membership**: Players need to be current members.

3. **Pass Age-Appropriate Referee Exam**: All junior tournament players must act as referees for matches following theirs. Exams are accessed through Club Locker profiles under the "Training" section:
   - U13: Level 1
   - U15: Level 2
   - U17: Level 3
   - U19: Level 4

## Division Options

Tournaments may offer divisions based on:
- **Age categories**: U11, U13, U15, U17, U19
- **Skill ratings**: U2.5 through 5.5+
- **Format**: Single-sex or co-ed options

Players must be under the age limit through the tournament's final day to be eligible.

## Practical Tournament Details
- Sign up through the online tournament listing on ClubLocker
- Governing rules are found in the Junior Policies document
- Tournament directors determine which division types are available at each event
- At tournaments, players act as referees for matches following their own matches
- Parents should research which coaches will be present at high-profile tournaments

## NorCal Junior Tournaments (Historical Examples)
From NorCal Squash records, Bay Area tournaments have included:
- **Squash Zone Silver**: 80-103 competitors, Redwood City (March dates)
- **Bay Club Fremont Winter Challenge**: December, junior bronze division
- **Silicon Valley Junior Championships**: Annual event
- **Squash Zone Club Championships**: Annual, juniors division
- **Oakwood Winter Bronze**: Lafayette, East Bay

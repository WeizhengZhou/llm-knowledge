# Bay Club Squash Program Summary

## Program Overview
Bay Club describes their squash program as "one of the best in California," featuring glass courts and professional instructors serving all skill levels from beginners to experienced players.

## Core Offerings
- **Professional Instruction**: Private and small group lessons available from in-club squash professionals experienced with all player levels
- **Competitive Play**: Round Robin evening matches open to all levels, plus year-round competitive tournaments
- **Annual Events**: PSA (Professional Squash Association) events hosted regularly

## Bay Area Locations with Squash
- Bay Club Santa Clara (CA)
- Bay Club Redwood Shores (Redwood City, CA)
- Bay Club Fremont (CA)
- Bay Club San Francisco (CA)
- Bay Club Marin (Corte Madera, CA)

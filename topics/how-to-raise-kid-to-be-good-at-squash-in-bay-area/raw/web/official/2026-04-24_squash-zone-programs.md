# Squash Zone: Comprehensive Program Guide

## Facility Overview
Squash Zone operates 9 international courts in Redwood City, CA, including "7 traditional courts, one 3-wall glass court and an all-glass professional court."

**Location:** 3586 Haven Ave, Redwood City, CA 94063
**Phone:** 650-995-7443
**Hours:** Mon-Fri 9am-9pm; Sat-Sun 9am-5:15pm

## Membership Options
- **Adult:** $205/month + $205 initiation
- **Junior PLUS:** $160/month + $160 initiation
- **Junior Access:** $110/month + $110 initiation

## Lesson Pricing
Seven squash professionals offer private instruction at rates from $70-$85 (30 min) to $165-$175 (60 min). Strength and conditioning sessions with Samy Farrag range from $60-$125 depending on duration and group size.

Clinic costs: "$40-$70 per hour (package/drop in rate)" — membership required

## Guest & Non-Member Access
- Guest fee: $25 plus $40 court booking
- Non-members may take up to two lessons (plus $25 guest fee)
- Court duration: 45 minutes
- Booking: 4 days in advance at 7am
- No-show fee: $15

## Court Requirements
- Non-marking indoor shoes required
- Eye protection mandatory for players 18 and under
- Appropriate sports attire required

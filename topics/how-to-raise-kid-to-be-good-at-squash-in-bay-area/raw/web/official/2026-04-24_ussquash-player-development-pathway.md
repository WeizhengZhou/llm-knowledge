# US Squash Player Development Pathway

## Overview
US Squash has launched a comprehensive Player Development Pathway designed to identify and cultivate future champions. With squash's Olympic debut at the 2028 Los Angeles Games, the organization is implementing a strategic talent development system to prepare American athletes for international competition.

## Core Philosophy
The pathway emphasizes "a strong grassroots foundation that introduces young players to the sport, develops fundamental skills and fosters a lifelong passion for squash." This approach prioritizes age-appropriate training to reduce burnout and injuries while encouraging long-term engagement with the sport.

## National Squash Academy (NSA) Programs

The NSA serves as the centerpiece, offering structured support across seven distinct tiers:

**NSA – Aspire**: Identifies top junior talent and provides high-performance training alongside Team USA athletes.

**NSA – Regional**: Expands access through regional training sessions, reducing travel demands for promising athletes.

**NSA – Performance**: Supports elite juniors competing at the World Junior Championships and Pan American Junior Games.

**NSA – Under 23**: Bridges junior, collegiate, and professional squash, assisting college athletes transitioning to the pro tour.

**NSA – International**: Offers training opportunities for international athletes at the Arlen Specter US Squash Center.

**NSA – Professional**: Supports emerging professionals ranked approximately top 100 on the PSA tour.

**NSA – National Team**: Provides direct support to Team USA elite athletes, including funding and high-performance services.

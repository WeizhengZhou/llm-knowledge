## Key Gaps

- **No East Bay-specific junior squash academy or development program** was found (analogous to Squash Zone or HISA on the Peninsula)
- For serious junior development in the East Bay, families either use Oakwood Athletic Club (private membership required, pricing not public) or travel to Peninsula (Squash Zone, Altius Performance, HISA) or to Bay Club Fremont/Santa Clara
- SquashDrive serves equity-focused youth and is not a general-access program
- The East Bay is meaningfully underserved for competitive junior squash compared to the Peninsula

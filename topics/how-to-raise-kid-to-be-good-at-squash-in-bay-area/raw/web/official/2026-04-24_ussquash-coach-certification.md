# US Squash Coaching Certification Overview

## Certification Levels

US Squash offers four coaching certification tiers:

**Start Squash (Grassroots)**
- One-day program (9am-4pm)
- Cost: $200 ($250 late registration)
- For beginners teaching squash in communities, schools, parks
- Focus on fun-based techniques and group organization

**Level 1**
- Weekend format (Friday 5-8pm, Saturday 8:30am-7pm, Sunday 9am-3pm)
- Cost: $450 ($500 late registration)
- Trains coaches for "beginner and low-intermediate" player instruction
- Emphasizes lesson organization, safety, and leadership

**Level 2**
- Weekend format (same as Level 1)
- Cost: $750 ($800 late registration)
- Prerequisite: Level 1 certification plus one year experience
- Focus on individual instruction and fault analysis for intermediate/advanced players
- Requires 6-week coaching plan submission

**Level 3**
- Weekend format (same as Level 1)
- Cost: $900 ($950 late registration)
- Prerequisite: Level 2 certification for one year
- Advanced player development and regional/national squad training
- Requires 8-week progressive coaching plan
- Highest certification US Squash offers

## Key Requirements

All certified coaches must:
- Complete accredited US Squash courses (only official recognition in USA)
- Pass evaluations across technical, preparation, presentation, and professionalism categories
- Level 3 candidates additionally need SafeSport training, First Aid/CPR, and Level 4 Referee exam

## Coach Pass Membership
All coaches must be Coach Pass members to coach at US Squash accredited junior tournaments and act as tournament director of a US Squash accredited tournament.

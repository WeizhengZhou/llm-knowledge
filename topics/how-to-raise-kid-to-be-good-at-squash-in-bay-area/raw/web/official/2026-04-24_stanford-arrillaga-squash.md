# Stanford University Squash Facilities — Public Access Assessment

Source: Web search "Arrillaga Family Sports Center Stanford squash courts public access non-Stanford families"

---

## Two Separate Facilities at Stanford

Stanford has two distinct squash-relevant facilities, which are often confused:

### 1. Arrillaga Family Sports Center (Athletics)
- **URL:** https://gostanford.com/facilities/arrillaga-family-sports-center
- **Purpose:** Stanford varsity athletics facility (Stanford Cardinal teams)
- **Location:** 641 Campus Dr, Stanford, CA
- **Squash:** Used by Stanford Women's varsity squash program (the only West Coast varsity program)
- **Public access:** NOT generally open to non-Stanford families — varsity athletics venue

### 2. Arrillaga Center for Sports and Recreation (ACSR) — Stanford Recreation
- **URL:** https://rec.stanford.edu / https://stanfordclubsports.com/facilities/arrillaga-center-for-sports-and-recreation-acsr-/4
- **Location:** 341 Galvez St, Stanford, CA (separate building from Family Sports Center)
- **Purpose:** Stanford Recreation & Wellness center for students, faculty, staff
- **Size:** 75,000 square foot recreation facility
- **Squash courts:** 7 squash courts including one glass competition court
- **Access requirement:** Stanford ID required to scan at front desk upon entry

## Public Access Assessment for Non-Stanford Families

**Access restricted by Stanford ID requirement.** Based on search results, ACSR requires a valid Stanford ID to enter. This means:
- Stanford students: full access
- Stanford faculty/staff: full access
- Stanford affiliates (some): possibly access
- Non-Stanford Bay Area families: NOT eligible for standard access

**Guest access:** Stanford Recreation has guest policies, but specifics for non-affiliates were not confirmed in search results. The standard policy requires accompanying a Stanford affiliate. Day passes may be available but are not confirmed.

**Contact for clarification:** (650) 724-9872 (Stanford Recreation)

---

## Key Facts Summary

- **ACSR squash courts:** 7 courts (including 1 glass competition court) — one of the largest squash court concentrations in NorCal
- **Practical access for Bay Area families:** Very limited. Stanford ID required. Non-affiliated families should not plan on using Stanford courts as a training base.
- **Stanford Women's squash:** Only West Coast varsity squash program. Trains at Arrillaga Family Sports Center (separate from ACSR).
- **Alternative nearby courts:** Squash Zone (Redwood City, 3 miles from Stanford campus) is the accessible alternative for Peninsula families
- **Altius Performance** (Redwood City) is the nearby elite junior coaching option
- **Research gap:** Specific day-pass pricing and guest policy for non-affiliates not confirmed. Direct call to (650) 724-9872 required to verify.

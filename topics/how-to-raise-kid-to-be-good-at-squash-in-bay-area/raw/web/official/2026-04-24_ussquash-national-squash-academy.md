# National Squash Academy (NSA) — US Squash

## Overview
The National Squash Academy serves as the centerpiece of US Squash's Player Development Pathway, providing a structured system to support athletes from junior ranks through professional squash. Housed at the Arlen Specter US Squash Center in Philadelphia.

## NSA-Aspire
**Eligibility:** Players from all age groups (U11-U19) showing potential to represent Team USA internationally, selected through "ranking and rating-based qualification."

**Selection:** Sixteen players per age group invited centrally; regional programs include deeper ranking pools based on local player numbers.

**Training:** Squad sessions throughout 2025-2026 season, training alongside Team USA High Performance athletes.

**How to Access:** Invitation-based, not open application. Contact nationalteams@ussquash.org for information.

## NSA-Regional
**Scope:** Subset of NSA-Aspire using a regional approach to minimize travel and missed school days.

**Leadership:** National coaching staff collaborate with appointed regional lead coaches to ensure consistent standards.

**Schedule:** Sessions coordinated with JCT events throughout 2025-2026 season.

**JCT #2 Bay Area connection:** NSA-Regional sessions accompany JCT events, meaning Bay Area juniors can access NSA-Regional at JCT #2 in Fremont, CA (October 11-13, 2025).

## NSA-Performance
**Target:** U19 players representing Team USA at World Junior Championships and Pan American Junior Games.

**Selection:** Top six U.S. citizens by U19 ranking qualify for World Juniors; spots allocated based on professional status and ranking position.

**Training:** Multiple Team USA squad sessions at Specter Center annually, integrated into National Team structure.

## Drive to LA28 Campaign
The U.S. Squash Foundation launched the Drive to LA28 Campaign to build toward squash's Olympic debut at the 2028 Summer Olympics in Los Angeles. Four pillars:

1. **Excellence** — Strengthening junior squash and supporting Team USA athletes
2. **Courts & Coaching** — Expanding nationwide access through public court investment
3. **Scholastic & Collegiate** — Growing youth and scholastic programs, expanding collegiate opportunities
4. **Marketing, Media & Visibility** — Elevating profile via 2028 Olympics

Regional Road Show Events include San Francisco, CA (details TBD as of 2025-2026).

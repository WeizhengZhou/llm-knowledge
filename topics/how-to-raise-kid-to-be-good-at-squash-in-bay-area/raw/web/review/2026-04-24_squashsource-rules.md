# Squash Rules and Game Overview

## Scoring System

Squash matches typically follow a "best-of-five" format, requiring a player to win three games to claim victory. Each individual game reaches 11 points, with the first player to achieve that score winning. If the score becomes tied at 10-10, play continues until someone achieves a two-point lead.

The sport operates on a **point-a-rally (PAR) system**, meaning "you win a point every time you win a rally." This differs from older scoring methods where only the server could score points.

## Match Structure

### Warm-Up
Before competition begins, players warm up the ball together for up to 5 minutes, hitting cross-court shots while alternating sides. A coin flip (racquet spin) determines serving order.

### Serving Requirements
A valid serve must meet these criteria:
- Server keeps one foot in the service box
- Ball strikes the front wall initially
- Ball crosses above the service line
- Ball lands in the opponent's back quarter
- Server receives only one attempt per point

### Playing Points
Players alternate shots until someone wins the rally. A valid shot requires striking the ball before it bounces twice and directing it toward the front wall, though intermediate wall contact is permitted. All shots must remain in-bounds and above the tin (the metal strip at the front wall's base).

## Unique Feature: Interference

Since players share court space, interference situations arise. Three outcomes are possible:
- **No let**: Opponent had no realistic chance to retrieve the ball; they lose the point
- **Let**: Opponent had a realistic retrieval opportunity; the point replays
- **Stroke**: Opponent blocked the swing path or front wall access; interfering player loses the point outright

## Court Layout

The court is an enclosed rectangular room with:
- Front wall, two side walls, back wall
- Service line on front wall
- Outline at top (out of bounds)
- Tin at bottom of front wall (ball must clear this)
- Half-court line on floor dividing left and right service boxes
- Short line on floor (behind which ball must land after serve)

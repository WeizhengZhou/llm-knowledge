# National Squash Academy (NSA)

## Definition

The National Squash Academy (NSA) is US Squash's centralized player development system, housed at the Arlen Specter US Squash Center in Philadelphia. It provides structured support from the junior ranks through professional squash, and is the centerpiece of the Player Development Pathway.

US Squash launched the Player Development Pathway in March 2025 as an Olympic-era initiative.

## Seven NSA Tiers

The US Squash National Squash Academy (NSA) offers seven program tiers: NSA-Aspire, NSA-Regional, NSA-Performance, NSA-Under 23, NSA-International, NSA-Professional, and NSA-National Team.

### NSA-Aspire (Junior Entry Point)

NSA-Aspire invites sixteen players per age group centrally, selected by ranking and rating-based qualification. Aspire is the first tier of national-level recognition for junior players. Players from all age groups (U11-U19) showing potential to represent Team USA internationally are eligible. Aspire sessions take place alongside Team USA High Performance athletes at the Specter Center.

Access is invitation-based, not open application. Contact nationalteams@ussquash.org for information.

### NSA-Regional

According to US Squash, NSA-Regional sessions are coordinated with JCT events throughout the 2025-2026 season. Regional sessions use a subset of NSA-Aspire methodology with appointed regional lead coaches, minimizing travel and missed school days.

**Bay Area access:** NSA-Regional sessions accompany JCT #2 in Fremont, CA (October 11-13, 2025), giving Bay Area juniors direct access without East Coast travel.

### NSA-Performance

NSA-Performance targets U19 players representing Team USA at World Junior Championships and Pan American Junior Games; the top six U.S. citizens by U19 ranking are expected to qualify for World Juniors.

### NSA-Professional

According to US Squash, NSA-Professional supports emerging professionals ranked approximately in the top 100 on the PSA tour.

### NSA-Under 23, NSA-International, NSA-National Team

Upper tiers supporting post-junior development through professional and Team USA representation.

## Drive to LA28

Squash is one of five additional sports approved for the 2028 Los Angeles Olympics, alongside baseball/softball, cricket, flag football, and lacrosse. Squash and flag football are making their Olympic debut.

According to the US Squash LA28 page, the US Squash Foundation launched in May 2024 and the Drive to LA28 campaign was activated in July 2025.

The Drive to LA28 has four pillars: Excellence (junior squash + Team USA), Courts and Coaching (public court expansion), Scholastic and Collegiate (growing youth programs), and Marketing/Media/Visibility (Olympic spotlight).

US Squash Drive to LA28 roadshow confirmed cities include Chicago, IL (May 28) and Boston, MA (June 10); additional cities (Philadelphia, New York, San Francisco, Los Angeles) have dates to be determined.

## How Bay Area Kids Access the NSA

1. **Build a national ranking** through sanctioned tournaments (see [[tournament-rankings]])
2. **Attend JCT #2 in Fremont** — the only West Coast JCT event, which includes NSA-Regional sessions
3. **Reach top 16 in age group** to be considered for NSA-Aspire invitation
4. **Travel to Philadelphia** for centralized NSA-Aspire sessions at the Specter Center

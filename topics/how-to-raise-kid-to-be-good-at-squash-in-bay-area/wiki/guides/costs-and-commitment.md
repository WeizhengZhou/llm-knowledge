# Costs and Time Commitment

## Overview

This guide provides a full cost breakdown for junior squash in the Bay Area at recreational and competitive levels, including membership, coaching, tournaments, equipment, and travel. It also covers time demands at each level and how families balance squash with school.

## Recreational Level ($2,000-$4,000/year)

Based on aggregated estimates, recreational junior squash in the Bay Area costs approximately $2,000-4,000 per year.

### Cost Components (Recreational)

| Item | Annual Cost |
|------|------------|
| Club membership (e.g., [[squash-zone]] Junior Access) | $1,320-$1,430 |
| Group clinics (1-2x/week) | Included or $40-70/hr |
| US Squash membership (if entering tournaments) | $149 |
| Equipment starter kit (year 1) | $130-300 |

Squash Zone Junior Access membership totals $1,430 in the first year ($110/month + $110 initiation) and $1,320 in subsequent years. Junior PLUS totals $2,080 in the first year ($160/month + $160 initiation) and $1,920 in subsequent years.

### Time Commitment (Recreational)

According to a synthesized training guide, competitive squash time demands are approximately: recreational 2-3 hours/week, club competitive 6-10 hours/week, and advanced competitive 12-20 hours/week.

At the recreational level (1-2 sessions per week), squash fits easily into any school schedule.

## Competitive Level ($10,000-$20,000+/year)

Based on aggregated estimates, competitive junior squash (regional/national level) in the Bay Area costs approximately $10,000-20,000+ per year.

### Detailed Competitive Cost Breakdown

| Item | Low Estimate | High Estimate |
|------|-------------|---------------|
| Club membership (Junior PLUS) | $1,920 | $1,920 |
| US Squash membership | $149 | $149 |
| Private lessons (weekly, 60 min) | $7,540 | $9,100 |
| Tournament entries (10-15 tournaments) | $700 | $1,500 |
| Travel for regional/away tournaments | $1,500 | $5,000+ |
| National events travel (JCT, championships) | $1,000 | $3,000 |
| Equipment upgrades | $200 | $500 |
| Summer camp (1 week) | $500 | $1,500 |
| **Annual Total** | **~$13,500** | **~$22,700+** |

### Private Lesson Costs at Squash Zone

According to the Squash Zone website, private lesson rates range from $145-$175 per 60 minutes depending on the coach: Bransten Ming, Aisling Blake, Ricardo Lopez, and Samy Farrag at $165/60 min, $125/45 min, $80/30 min; Shahjahan Khan at $175/60 min, $145/45 min, $85/30 min; Yassin Farrag at $145/60 min, $110/45 min, $70/30 min.

### Other Club Costs

Bay Club requires full club membership to access squash courts; pricing varies by location and is not publicly listed. Oakwood Athletic Club pricing is not publicly listed; private club membership is required. Contact the club directly for current rates. HISA pricing is not publicly available on the website; contact (408) 256-7363 or squash@hisasquash.com for current rates.

## Equipment Costs

A junior squash equipment starter kit (racket, eyeguards, court shoes, balls, bag) costs approximately $130-170 at the budget level or $200-300 at the mid-range level.

| Item | Budget | Mid-Range |
|------|--------|-----------|
| Junior racket | $30-50 | $70-100 |
| Eyeguards (mandatory) | $20 | $35-50 |
| Court shoes | $50 | $80-120 |
| Balls (tube of 3) | $8-12 | $12-15 |
| Bag | $20-30 | $40-60 |
| **Starter Kit Total** | **$130-170** | **$200-300** |

Squash eyeguards must meet or exceed the ASTM-F3164 standard; US Squash mandates eyeguards for all juniors in all sanctioned events. Basic certified eyeguards cost approximately $20-40.

## Tournament Fees

Junior tournament entry fees typically range $35-75 per draw for most junior tournaments; gold and JCT events cost $100-200+. See [[tournament-rankings]] for the full tier breakdown.

## Travel Costs (National Events)

According to a synthesized training guide, national squash events (JCT, championships) may require Thursday-Sunday travel, and East Coast national events require 3-4 days travel from the Bay Area. Most national events are in Philadelphia, so Bay Area families face significant travel costs for the competitive pathway.

## Common Mistakes

- **Underestimating travel costs.** For nationally competitive players, travel is the single largest cost component.
- **Not budgeting for US Squash membership and referee exam.** US Squash membership costs $149 per year and is required for any ranked tournament play.
- **Buying equipment before committing.** Most clubs lend equipment to beginners; invest after 3-4 lessons.

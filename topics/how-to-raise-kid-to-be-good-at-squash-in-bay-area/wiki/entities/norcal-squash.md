## Overview

According to NorCal Squash, the organization operates under the San Francisco International Squash Federation (SFISF), a registered 501(c)(3) charitable nonprofit corporation. NorCal Squash serves the squash community across Northern California through league operations, tournament hosting, community building, and a directory of playable courts and clubs.

Members join NorCal Squash through US Squash by selecting a NorCal club as their 'home club.'

## League Structure

NorCal Squash leagues run from September through April. NorCal Squash league teams are co-ed and divided into Divisions 1 through 4.

According to NorCal Squash, league match format is best of five rally-point games; teams consist of 6-10 players with four players from each team playing per match night. According to NorCal Squash, the league uses ClubLocker for recording match results and standings calculations.

### Participating Clubs by Region

- **San Francisco:** Bay Club San Francisco, Bay Club Marin, Olympic Club, UCSF Mission Bay, University Club
- **East Bay:** Club Sport Fremont, Club Sport San Ramon, [[oakwood-athletic-club]], UC Berkeley
- **Peninsula/South Bay:** Bay Club Redwood Shores, Bay Club Santa Clara, [[hisa-squash-academy]], Stanford, [[squash-zone]]
- **Sacramento:** Multiple sports clubs and UC Davis

According to NorCal Squash, the organization maintains partnerships with 20+ facilities across five regions.

## Junior Programs and High School Squash

According to NorCal Squash, the inaugural NorCal High School Team Championships was held at Bay Club Santa Clara with 12 school teams participating.

No school in NorCal has its own squash courts except Nueva School, which is the only school in Northern California to incorporate squash into its school program. School teams rely on private club court time.

### Notable Bay Area Junior Achievers

According to NorCal Squash, notable Bay Area junior squash achievers include Madison Ho (U-11 National Championship winner, unblemished record 2015-16), Gautham Suresh (multiple NorCal BU11/BU13 wins), Abhinav Athreya (NorCal BU13), Nikhil Prasad (NorCal BU15), and Abhimanyu Gupta (NorCal BU17).

## Common Mistakes

- **Treating NorCal Squash league play as a junior development pathway.** NorCal Squash league divisions (1 through 4) are adult co-ed competitive leagues, not junior programs. Parents of young beginners should not expect their 8-year-old to play in league matches. Junior development happens through club programs ([[squash-zone]], [[hisa-squash-academy]]) and sanctioned US Squash junior tournaments, not through the NorCal league.
- **Trying to join NorCal Squash directly instead of through a home club.** Members join NorCal Squash through US Squash by selecting a NorCal club as their 'home club.' Families who contact NorCal directly without a club affiliation cannot register for league play. Choose a club first (see [[choosing-club-coach]]), then designate it as your home club when registering with US Squash.
- **Not using norcalsquash.org as a tournament calendar resource.** NorCal Squash maintains the most current calendar of NorCal junior tournaments at norcalsquash.org/tournaments/. Parents relying on club-specific announcements alone will miss regional and visiting events. Check the NorCal calendar regularly during the September-April season.

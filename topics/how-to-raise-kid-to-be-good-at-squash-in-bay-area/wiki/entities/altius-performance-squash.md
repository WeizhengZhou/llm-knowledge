## Overview

According to its website, Altius Performance Squash is located at 1197 Oddstad Dr, Redwood City, CA 94063; phone 650.396.9313. Altius Performance is a junior-centric coaching service, not a club or court facility; pricing is not publicly listed and requires direct contact.

Altius was founded to address what its creators describe as "limited options for junior training in the Bay Area." The service offers bespoke one-on-one training, match analysis, and tournament coaching (including court-side support at local and national JCT tournaments).

## Head Coach

According to Altius Performance's website, head coach Omar Mohy Marei is a former top-ranked Egyptian junior and PSA professional who was part of the Wadi Degla club coaching team in Cairo and coached at Greenwich Academy, CT, where the team won 3 US national high school titles during his tenure.

According to Altius Performance's website, coach Omar's students won the 2023 British Junior Open BU11 and GU11, and placed 3rd in GU13 and 5th in BU15.

Omar is among the few coaches with PSA-level coaching experience, having worked with professional players ranked in the top 20 in the world.

## Services

Altius offers three core service areas:

- **Bespoke Training:** One-on-one squash instruction, competitive match play calibrated to player ability, mental training and coaching, personalized strength and conditioning
- **Match Analysis:** Point-by-point dissection, tactics and strategy development, pre-tournament preparation
- **Tournament Coaching:** Court-side coaching and support before, during, and after matches at local and national (JCT) tournaments

## Common Mistakes

- **Enrolling a beginner or unrated child in bespoke 1-on-1 coaching.** Altius positions itself as an elite training service with a former PSA professional coach. Beginners and development-level players (under age 10 or unrated) benefit more from group clinics at [[squash-zone]] where they develop foundational technique alongside peers. Confirm the appropriate entry level with Altius before contacting.
- **Assuming Altius provides court access.** Altius Performance is a coaching service, not a club or court facility. Training takes place at partner courts; parents should clarify where sessions occur and whether separate court booking or club membership is required.
- **Choosing Altius over a club program without understanding the tradeoff.** Bespoke 1-on-1 coaching accelerates technical development but does not provide the competitive match play environment of group clinics and in-house ladders. Most competitive juniors benefit from a combination: club membership for group play and match experience, plus private coaching (whether Altius or another provider) for targeted skill development.

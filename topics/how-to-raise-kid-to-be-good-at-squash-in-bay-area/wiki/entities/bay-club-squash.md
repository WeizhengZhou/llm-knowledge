## Overview

Bay Club locations with squash courts in the Bay Area include Bay Club San Francisco (150 Greenwich Street), Bay Club Marin (Corte Madera), Bay Club Fremont (46650 Landing Parkway, Fremont, CA 94538), Bay Club Santa Clara, and Bay Club Redwood Shores.

Bay Club requires full club membership to access squash courts; pricing varies by location and is not publicly listed. Bay Club memberships are premium-tier club memberships (not squash-only) that include fitness facilities, pools, and other amenities beyond squash.

## Coaching Staff

Bay Club Fremont coaches include Maha Zein, Faisal Riaz, and Zarak Khan. Bay Club San Francisco and Bay Club Marin coach is Erick Chacon.

According to NorCal Squash, Bay Club Redwood Shores coaches are Charlie Johnson, Vanessa Raj, and Bransten Ming; Bay Club Santa Clara coach is Jonathan Perry.

## Junior Programs

Bay Club offers private and small group lessons for all skill levels, junior/kids specialty camps during school breaks, and round robin evening matches. Bay Club Fremont is the primary junior squash hub with three coaches on staff.

According to Bay Club, Bay Club Fremont hosted the Bay Club Fremont Winter Challenge junior bronze tournament in December 2019, featuring Dillon Huang (then US #1 ranked BU19).

## Common Mistakes

- **Joining Bay Club expecting a squash-only membership.** Bay Club requires full club membership to access squash courts; pricing varies by location and is not publicly listed. This is a premium multi-sport membership (fitness facilities, pools, tennis, etc.) — not a squash-specific membership like [[squash-zone]]. A family purely focused on junior squash development should compare Bay Club's full membership cost against Squash Zone's squash-only membership ($110-$160/month) before committing.
- **Assuming all five Bay Club locations are equivalent for junior squash.** Bay Club Fremont has three coaches on staff and is the only Bay Club location with a demonstrated junior development track (including hosting the Bay Club Fremont Winter Challenge and JCT events). Bay Club Marin has one coach and is primarily a convenience option for Marin families. Parents outside Fremont's geography should ask specifically about junior program depth, group clinic schedules, and coach-to-student ratios at their nearest location before joining.
- **Conflating Bay Club camps with a year-round junior program.** The article confirms "junior/kids specialty camps during school breaks" and private lessons, but it is not clear from available sources whether all Bay Club locations run structured year-round junior group clinics analogous to Squash Zone's Beginner/Development/Elite tiers. Ask the specific location whether they offer weekly junior group sessions or only camps and individual lessons.

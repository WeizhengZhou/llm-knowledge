## Overview

According to its website, Oakwood Athletic Club is located at 4000 Mt. Diablo Boulevard, Lafayette, CA 94549; phone 925.283.4000. Oakwood Athletic Club has 3 glass-back wall international-size squash courts and offers youth instruction and private lessons.

Oakwood Athletic Club pricing is not publicly listed; private club membership is required. Contact the club directly for current rates.

## Junior Programs

Oakwood Athletic Club has 3 glass-back wall international-size squash courts and offers youth instruction and private lessons. However, unlike [[squash-zone]] (which publishes detailed age-band group clinics for ages 5-14+ with weekly schedules), Oakwood does not publish a dedicated junior program page. It is not clear from available sources whether Oakwood runs structured junior group clinics or offers only private and semi-private lessons for youth alongside its adult programming.

**What parents should do:** Call 925.283.4000 to confirm: (a) whether structured junior group sessions are offered (with days, times, and age ranges), (b) which coaches lead junior instruction, and (c) whether juniors participate in the in-house league or only adults.

Oakwood is one of very few East Bay club options for junior squash outside of [[squashdrive]] (which serves a different demographic and is eligibility-based). Based on a survey of East Bay squash facilities, the East Bay has no junior squash academy analogous to Squash Zone or HISA on the Peninsula, making it underserved for competitive junior squash development. East Bay families in Oakland, Berkeley, or Fremont may also consider [[bay-club-squash]] Fremont, which has three coaches and a stronger documented junior development track.

## In-House League

According to Oakwood Athletic Club, the in-house squash league has up to 5 divisions (Novice through A Level); formal sessions run Fall (September through early December) and Winter (January through late April).

## League Participation

Oakwood participates in the East Bay Competitive Squash League (see [[norcal-squash]] for full league details). The East Bay Competitive Squash League features team matches between 7 clubs in the East Bay, played on Wednesday evenings in fall and winter.

## Tournaments

According to Oakwood Athletic Club, the Bay Area hosts over ten age-group, skill-level, and open-professional squash tournaments per year. The Oakwood Winter Bronze (February) is among the historical NorCal junior tournament events.

## Common Mistakes

- **Expecting a structured junior development program comparable to Squash Zone.** Oakwood confirms youth instruction and private lessons, but no dedicated junior program page exists. A parent seeking structured weekly junior clinics with age-band groupings (like Squash Zone's Beginner/Development/Elite tiers) should call Oakwood to confirm program depth before joining. If structured group training is the priority, [[bay-club-squash]] Fremont or [[squash-zone]] may be more suitable despite the longer commute.
- **Overlooking the geography constraint for East Bay families.** Oakwood is in Lafayette (Contra Costa County). East Bay families in Oakland, Berkeley, or southern Alameda County may find Bay Club Fremont (46650 Landing Parkway, Fremont) closer and better-resourced for junior squash. Check commute times for after-school practice before committing to a membership.
- **Assuming the in-house league is available for juniors.** According to Oakwood Athletic Club, the in-house squash league has up to 5 divisions (Novice through A Level). It is not stated whether juniors participate in this league or only adults. Confirm with the club whether your child can enter the in-house league at the Novice level.
